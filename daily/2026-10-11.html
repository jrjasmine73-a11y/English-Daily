1. French students protest

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-09 · 原文链接
📖 法国数万名师生走上街头，抗议公立高中长期投入不足：教室爆满、教学楼漏水生锈、教师严重短缺。关键信息点：抗议从巴黎贫困郊区蔓延成全国危机，近三周升级为暴力冲突，一名15岁学生被警方手雷炸断手，法国总统大选仅剩半年。值得读：社会运动的完整链条——经济诉求如何演变成暴力对抗，以及官方与民间两种叙事的分歧，都是新闻英语的高频场景。
¶ 1

Tens of thousands of students and teachers have taken to the streets across France to protest severe under-investment in public high schools.

数万名学生和教师走上法国各地街头，抗议公立高中长期投入严重不足。

take to the streets 短语 走上街头（抗议）e.g. Tens of thousands of students and teachers have taken to the streets across France to protest severe under-investment.
¶ 2

Protesters are demanding immediate action over overcrowded classrooms, decaying 1970s buildings with leaking roofs and rats, and long-term teacher shortages caused by years of budget cuts.

抗议者要求立即采取行动，应对教室过度拥挤、上世纪70年代破旧校舍（屋顶漏水、老鼠横行）以及多年预算削减造成的教师长期短缺。

¶ 3

The movement, which started in deprived Parisian suburbs, has quickly turned into a nationwide crisis. However, the protests have grown increasingly violent over the past three weeks, and resulted in thousands of arrests, damaged schools, and injuries on both sides, including a 15-year-old student who lost his hand to a police grenade.

这场始于巴黎贫困郊区的运动迅速演变成全国性危机。然而，过去三周抗议愈演愈烈，暴力程度不断升级，造成数千人被捕、多所学校被毁，双方都有人受伤——其中包括一名15岁学生，被警方投掷的手雷炸断了手。

¶ 4

Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu strongly condemned the rioting and insisted that destroying schools doesn’t solve funding problems. While police have used tear gas to disperse crowds, demonstrators accuse authorities of using heavy-handed tactics. With a presidential election just six months away, these widespread demonstrations highlight deep social political divisions across the country.

法国总理塞巴斯蒂安·勒科尔尼强烈谴责骚乱行为，坚称破坏学校解决不了经费问题。在警方用催泪瓦斯驱散人群的同时，示威者指责当局手段过激。如今总统大选仅剩半年，这场蔓延全国的示威凸显了法国深层的社会政治分裂。

heavy-handed tactics 短语 强硬手段、铁腕手段e.g. Demonstrators accuse authorities of using heavy-handed tactics.
disperse crowds 短语 驱散人群e.g. Police have used tear gas to disperse crowds.
highlight (deep social divisions) 短语 凸显、突显e.g. These widespread demonstrations highlight deep social political divisions across the country.
¶ 5

Difficult words: under-investment (providing less money or funding than is necessary for something to work properly), deprived (suffering from a lack of necessary resources, money, and good living conditions), heavy-handed (using too much force or authority to control a situation).

难词：under-investment（投入不足：投入的资金或资源少于正常运转所需的量）；deprived（贫困的：缺乏必要的资源、金钱和良好的生活条件）；heavy-handed（强硬的、手段过激的：使用过多的武力或权威来控制局面）。

be caused by 短语 由……引起e.g. long-term teacher shortages caused by years of budget cuts.

2. India’s luxury fashion industry

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-07 · 原文链接
📖 奢侈时尚光鲜的背后，是印度被长期隐藏的角色：许多大牌的高端刺绣和手工活其实在印度完成。关键信息点：一件标价4.3万欧元的夹克，在印度做了2000多小时刺绣，在欧洲只做了不到100小时收尾，却贴着'Made in France'的标签。值得读：揭开'Made in France/Italy'标签的真相，学会议价论证的英语表达，顺带了解时尚产业的伦理争议。
¶ 1

In the world of luxury fashion, India plays a significant, but often hidden, role in the production process.

在奢侈时尚的世界里，印度在生产环节中扮演着重要但常常被隐藏的角色。

play a significant (but hidden) role in 短语 在……中扮演重要（但被隐藏的）角色e.g. India plays a significant, but often hidden, role in the production process.
¶ 2

Despite the industry’s focus on high quality, exclusivity, and European craftsmanship, many luxury fashion houses rely on India for skilled labor and embroidery work. This connection has been concealed for years to maintain a certain image and avoid concerns about working conditions.

尽管该行业标榜高品质、稀缺性和欧洲工匠精神，但许多奢侈品牌都依赖印度的熟练劳动力和刺绣工艺。这种联系多年来一直被掩盖，以维持某种形象，避免引发对劳工条件的担忧。

rely on sb/sth for sth 短语 在某方面依赖……e.g. Many luxury fashion houses rely on India for skilled labor and embroidery work.
be concealed for years 短语 被隐瞒多年e.g. This connection has been concealed for years to maintain a certain image.
¶ 3

While Western luxury brands proudly display ‘Made in France’ or ‘Made in Italy’ labels, much of the intricate embroidery and labor-intensive work is actually done in India. This lack of transparency has raised questions about the ethics of the fashion industry. One example is a jacket that retails for 43,000 Euros and underwent over 2,000 hours of embroidery work in India; however, it received less than 100 hours of finishing touches in Europe, despite bearing a ‘Made in France’ label.

西方奢侈品牌一边骄傲地展示着'Made in France（法国制造）'或'Made in Italy（意大利制造）'的标签，一边把大量繁复的刺绣和劳动密集型工作放在印度完成。这种缺乏透明度的做法，让时尚产业的伦理受到质疑。比如一件标价4.3万欧元的夹克，在印度经历了2000多小时的刺绣加工，但在欧洲只做了不到100小时的收尾工作，却堂而皇之地贴着'Made in France'标签。

bear a label 短语 贴着……标签e.g. It received less than 100 hours of finishing touches in Europe, despite bearing a 'Made in France' label.
raise questions about 短语 引发对……的质疑e.g. This lack of transparency has raised questions about the ethics of the fashion industry.
¶ 4

Despite these practices, some luxury brands are beginning to acknowledge their Indian suppliers and promote India’s artisanal legacy. However, it remains a complex issue in an industry built on exploitation and secrecy.

尽管存在这些做法，一些奢侈品牌开始承认其印度供应商，并宣扬印度的手工传统。不过，在一个建立在剥削和保密之上的行业里，这仍然是个复杂的问题。

¶ 5

Difficult words: exclusivity (the quality of being limited to rich people), embroidery (decorating fabric or other materials with needle and thread, often creating intricate designs), artisanal (made traditionally).

难词：exclusivity（稀缺性：只限于富人享有的特质）；embroidery（刺绣：用针线在织物或其他材料上装饰，常绣出精美图案）；artisanal（手工制作的：以传统方式生产的）。
