1. Why does tech cost more?

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-20 · 原文链接
📖 AI 热潮为什么会让手机变贵？这篇文章讲清了背后的链条：科技巨头争相建设数据中心，专用存储芯片被抢走，普通电子产品闹起了"内存荒"，消费者只好为缩了水的设备付更多的钱。读完你能明白科技涨价背后的供需逻辑，还能顺手学到 boom、shortage 这类高频词。
¶ 1

The traditional tech market bargain where every new gadget offered better performance and storage for a similar price is under pressure due to the global AI boom.

科技市场传统的"划算交易"——每款新设备都以差不多的价格带来更好的性能和更大的存储——正因全球 AI 热潮而受到冲击。

gadget /'gædʒit/ n. 小配件；小玩意；诡计e.g. He loves buying new kitchen gadgets.
performance /pә'fɒ:mәns/ n. 施行；工作情况；成绩；行为；表现；演出e.g. This car's performance is amazing.
boom /bu:m/ n. 繁荣；隆隆声e.g. The city saw a boom in tourism.
under pressure 短语 受到压力；承压e.g. The small company is under pressure from bigger rivals.
¶ 2

As tech companies rush to build massive data centers, they require huge quantities of specialized memory chips. Because chipmakers make much higher profits supplying data centers, consumer electronics manufacturers are facing a severe memory shortage and skyrocketing component costs. To cope, brands are raising prices, cutting down on device memory, or lowering specifications, which means consumers now pay more for devices with fewer features and slower speeds.

随着科技公司争相建设大型数据中心，它们需要巨量的专用存储芯片。由于芯片制造商向数据中心供货的利润要高得多，消费电子厂商正面临严重的内存短缺和飙升的零部件成本。为应对这一局面，各品牌纷纷涨价、削减设备内存或降低配置，这意味着消费者如今要花更多的钱，买到的却是功能更少、速度更慢的设备。

massive /'mæsiv/ a. 大而重的；宽大的；宏伟的e.g. They built a massive shopping mall downtown.
specialized a. 专门的；特别的；专科的；特化的；专化的e.g. She hired a specialized lawyer for the case.
severe /si'viә/ a. 严格的；尖锐的；严肃的；严重的；严厉的；朴素的e.g. The city faced a severe water shortage.
shortage /'ʃɒ:tidʒ/ n. 不足；缺乏e.g. There is a shortage of nurses in hospitals.
component /kәm'pәunәnt/ n. 元件；组件；成分e.g. One component of the machine is broken.
specification /.spesifi'keiʃәn/ n. 规格；详述；详细说明书e.g. Check the specifications before you buy a phone.
rush to do sth 短语 争相/抢着做某事e.g. Fans rushed to buy tickets online.
cope with 短语 应对；应付e.g. She is learning to cope with stress.
cut down on 短语 削减；减少e.g. He decided to cut down on sugar.
¶ 3

Building new semiconductor factories takes years and billions of dollars, and chipmakers are cautious about overinvesting due to historic market crashes. As a result, experts predict high prices and minor upgrades will continue for the next few years. Consumers are adapting by keeping their phones and laptops for much longer or turning to the fast-growing market for cheaper, refurbished devices.

新建半导体工厂需要数年时间并投入数十亿美元，而芯片制造商因历史上市场崩盘的教训，对过度投资十分谨慎。因此专家预测，高价位和微小的升级将在未来几年持续。消费者也在调整策略：把手机和笔记本电脑用得更久，或者转向快速增长的翻新机市场，用更低的价格买设备。

cautious /'kɒ:ʃәs/ a. 谨慎的；小心的e.g. Investors are cautious about the market.
predict /pri'dikt/ v. 预知；预言；预报e.g. Experts predict a hot summer this year.
minor /'mainә/ n. 未成年人；副修科目e.g. There are only minor differences between the two phones.
turn to 短语 转向；求助于e.g. Many students turn to their teachers for advice.
¶ 4

Difficult words: boom (a sudden period of rapid economic growth, activity, or popularity), skyrocketing (increasing very quickly and by a large amount in a short time), refurbished (cleaned, repaired, and tested to work like new before being sold again).

生词：boom（经济活动或人气的突然快速增长时期）、skyrocketing（在短时间内飞速大幅上涨）、refurbished（经清洁、维修和测试，像新的一样再出售）。

2. Sustainable Development Goals not on track, says UN

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-09-21 · 原文链接
📖 联合国给全球可持续发展目标"打了个差评"：2030 年大限将至，只有约三分之一的目标还在正轨上，按现在的速度，实现性别平等还要等 171 年。这篇文章用清晰的数据讲清了进展与短板，读完顺带学会 on track、tackle 这类政策新闻高频表达。
¶ 1

The United Nations has warned that the world is not on track to achieve its Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by 2030. The goals were implemented in 2015 to improve people's lives and protect the planet. They include eliminating poverty, improving education, reducing gender inequality, and tackling climate change. However, the UN has reported that only about one third of the goals are currently on track to be met. Around half are progressing too slowly. Wars, climate change, slow economic growth, rising debt, financing gaps, and other global shocks are making progress difficult. The UN has called on countries to increase their efforts in the run-up to 2030.

联合国警告说，世界在 2030 年前实现可持续发展目标（SDGs）的进程已经偏离了轨道。这些目标于 2015 年制定，旨在改善人们的生活、保护地球。目标包括消除贫困、改善教育、减少性别不平等和应对气候变化。然而联合国报告称，目前只有约三分之一的目标有望实现，约一半目标进展过慢。战争、气候变化、经济增长放缓、债务攀升、资金缺口以及其他全球性冲击，都让进展举步维艰。联合国呼吁各国在 2030 年到来之前加大努力。

¶ 2

The UN did report signs of progress. It noted that one billion more people now have access to drinking water. Internet connectivity has also increased, from around 40 per cent of the world's population in 2015 to about 74 per cent today. Despite these improvements, many serious challenges remain. In particular, there is a considerable lack of progress on gender equality. A UN spokesperson said: "At the current rate of progress, gender equality would take 171 years to achieve." Another official said governments needed to invest more money in infrastructure and institutions. The official also wanted countries to cooperate more. The UN said the goals could still be achieved, but much faster action was needed.

不过，联合国也报告了一些进展迹象。它指出，如今能喝上饮用水的人增加了十亿。互联网接入率也提高了，从 2015 年世界人口的约 40% 上升到今天的约 74%。尽管有这些改善，许多严峻挑战依然存在。特别是性别平等方面的进展严重不足。一位联合国发言人说："按目前的进展速度，实现性别平等还需要 171 年。"另一位官员表示，各国政府需要在基础设施和机构上投入更多资金。该官员还希望各国加强合作。联合国表示，这些目标仍有可能实现，但需要快得多的行动。
