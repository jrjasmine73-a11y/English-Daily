1. 5 Canadian Universities for Indian Students Who Prefer a Smaller City

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-12 · 原文链接
📖 这篇文章给打算去加拿大读大学的学生介绍了五所位于小城市的大学：金斯顿的女王大学、圭尔夫大学、新不伦瑞克大学、特伦特大学和北不列颠哥伦比亚大学。关键信息是：小城市的大学同样有丰富的校园活动、住宿与学术支持，选校时要先确认想读的学位是否在该校区开设、住宿条件如何。文章语言简单实用，满是留学申请和校园生活的高频词，很适合精读。
¶ 1

A student moving from India to a smaller Canadian city may look forward to finding a few favourite places around campus. As the city will be home during term, a campus tour can help them picture where they would spend their time between classes. They can look for nearby outdoor space or ask about a college community and regular activities they could join.

对于从印度来到加拿大较小城市求学的学生来说，他们可能会期待在校园附近找到几处自己喜欢的地方。这座城市将成为学期期间的家，参加一次校园参观可以帮助他们想象课间会在哪里度过时间。他们可以留意附近的户外空间，也可以打听学院社群和可以参加的常规活动。

campus /'kæmpәs/ n. 校园；大学生活e.g. Our school campus has a big library and a small lake.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用，指大学或中学的校园范围；说“在校园里”常用 on campus。
look forward to 短语 期待；盼望e.g. I really look forward to our family trip next month.💡 场合：日常口语和书面语都常用，语气积极、带盼望的感情；后面常接名词或动名词，不能直接接动词原形。
¶ 2

At Queen’s University in Kingston, Campus Life provides information on student clubs, intramurals, recreational activities and volunteering in the city. Students can find activities on campus and ways to meet local people beyond it.

在金斯顿的女王大学，Campus Life（校园生活服务）会提供学生社团、校内体育比赛、娱乐活动和市内志愿服务的信息。学生可以在校园里找到各类活动，也能找到结识校园之外当地人的途径。

intramural /.intrә'mjurәl/ a. 校内的；内部的e.g. He plays intramural football with his classmates every Friday.💡 场合：多用于北美校园场景，指“校内的（比赛/活动）”，正式程度中等；日常口语中常直接说 school games。
volunteering /ˌvɔlənˈtiərɪŋ/ v. 自动提供；自愿效劳( volunteer的现在分词 ); 主动建议（或告诉）; e.g. Volunteering at the library is her favourite weekend activity.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用，语气积极，带有乐于助人的意味；常和 at、in、for 搭配。
meet the requirements 短语 达到要求；符合条件e.g. You need to meet the requirements before you can join the team.💡 场合：正式书面和职场常用，偏中性客观；用于招生、招聘、申请等场景，表示符合明文规定的条件。
¶ 3

The university has broad undergraduate and graduate choices. Applicants still need to choose the level and degree they want and meet its admission requirements.

这所大学的本科和研究生选择范围很广。申请人仍然需要选定自己想要的学历层次和学位，并满足入学要求。

undergraduate /.ʌndә'grædjuit/ n. 大学本科生；大学肄业生e.g. She is an undergraduate in her first year at a university in Beijing.💡 场合：正式书面和教育场景常用；日常口语更常说 college student 或 university student。
graduate /'grædʒueit/ n. 研究生e.g. After four years of college, she became a graduate student in Shanghai.💡 场合：正式书面和教育场景常用；在北美，graduate student 指研究生，graduate 作名词单独用时也常指“毕业生”，要看语境。
admission /әd'miʃәn/ n. 准许进入；入场费；录用；承认e.g. You need good grades to get admission to that school.💡 场合：正式书面和教育场景常用，指“录取、获准入学”；日常口语说“被录取”更常说 get in 或 be accepted。
¶ 4

Queen’s offers incoming international first-year undergraduates early move-in access and dedicated academic and career advising. A recurring activity can give students somewhere to return after orientation.

女王大学为入读本科一年级的新生国际学生提供提前入住宿舍的安排，以及专门的学业与职业咨询。一项定期参加的活动可以让学生在迎新之后有所归属。

orientation /.ɒ:rien'teiʃәn/ n. 新生入学指导e.g. The orientation helped the new students find their classrooms.💡 场合：教育场景常用，指开学前的新生指导活动；日常口语中也指“方向感、定位”。
¶ 5

Student Academic Success Services provides writing and learning-strategy support. Students can discuss unfamiliar academic expectations there as assignments begin to require more independent work.

学生学业成功服务中心提供写作和学习策略方面的支持。学生在那里可以讨论自己不熟悉的学业要求，因为随着课程推进，作业会要求越来越多的独立学习。

¶ 6

Students considering residence should check the conditions in their offer. Residence has Living Learning Communities and interest-based options, but a bed-space commitment does not promise a particular room or building. Those conditions belong alongside the decision to study in Kingston.

考虑住校的学生应当核对录取通知书里的住宿条件。宿舍有“生活学习社区”和按兴趣划分的选择，但床位承诺并不保证能分到某一间具体的房间或某一栋楼。这些条件要和去金斯顿读书的决定一起考虑。

residence /'rezidәns/ n. 住宿（学校宿舍）e.g. Students who want a room in residence should apply early.💡 场合：多用于学校、大学等正式场景，指“住宿（安排）”；日常口语说住学校宿舍更常说 dorm 或 live on campus。
commitment /kә'mitmәnt/ n. 委托；交押；承担义务；赞助e.g. Joining the football team is a big commitment, so think it over first.💡 场合：正式书面和日常口语都常用；既可以指“承诺”，也可以指“投入的时间精力”，语气偏认真。
¶ 7

The campus is in Guelph in southwestern Ontario, with organized activities and nearby outdoor space students can use during the week. The Arboretum connects to campus through trails and forest. Outdoor space is another part of the setting to consider alongside the city’s size.

校园位于安大略省西南部的圭尔夫市，平时有组织好的活动和附近的户外空间供学生使用。植物园通过小径和树林与校园相连。户外空间也是选校时要考虑的因素之一，和城市规模同样重要。

connect with 短语 与……建立联系；结识e.g. The school trip helped me connect with classmates I had never talked to.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用；既可以指人与人建立联系，也可以指交通、网络接通，语气中性偏积极。
¶ 8

At the University of Guelph, GryphLife brings together clubs, events, workshops and co-curricular opportunities. Student Volunteer Connections helps students find community volunteering that suits their interests and availability. A regular activity offers contact with people beyond orientation.

在圭尔夫大学，GryphLife 把社团、活动、讲座和课外机会汇集在一起。学生志愿者联络处帮助学生找到适合自己兴趣和时间的社区志愿服务。一项定期参加的活动可以在迎新之外帮学生结识更多人。

¶ 9

International applicants from India can contact an in-country representative with admissions questions. Global Pre-Arrival Support advisors help new students with registration, housing and arrival matters.

来自印度的国际申请人可以联系学校在印度的招生代表咨询入学问题。全球行前支持顾问会帮助新生处理注册、住宿和抵达相关事宜。

¶ 10

GO Transit serves the campus and provides a connection with the Toronto region. Students considering visits or commuting can check the current timetable from the intended starting point.

GO Transit（公交系统）服务这座校园，并提供通往多伦多地区的交通连接。考虑探访或通勤的学生可以从出发地查询最新的时刻表。

¶ 11

The University of New Brunswick has campuses in Fredericton and Saint John. The Fredericton campus is the option here. Applicants considering Fredericton need to confirm that their intended degree is available there.

新不伦瑞克大学在弗雷德里克顿和圣约翰各有一个校区，这里介绍的是弗雷德里克顿校区。考虑弗雷德里克顿校区的申请人需要确认自己想读的学位在该校区开设。

¶ 12

The International Student Advisor’s Office serves undergraduate and graduate students as well as exchange and other international students. Its transition support includes pre-arrival virtual sessions, in-person workshops and individual advising. Mentorship, cultural activities and social events connect students with peers and New Brunswick communities.

国际学生顾问办公室服务本科生和研究生，也服务交换生和其他国际学生。它的过渡支持包括行前线上会议、面对面讲座和一对一咨询。导师计划、文化活动和社交活动帮助学生结识同学，融入新不伦瑞克省的各个社区。

transition /træn'ziʃәn/ n. 转变；转换；变迁；过渡时期；临时转调e.g. The transition from middle school to high school was hard for him.💡 场合：正式书面和日常口语都常用，指从一种状态到另一种状态的过渡；语气中性。
¶ 13

Students need to log in to access some detailed information. The office can explain where to take a question, but it does not decide application status or provide academic-advising decisions. Keeping admissions and faculty contacts alongside the international contact helps students direct those questions.

学生需要登录后才能查看一些详细信息。该办公室可以告诉学生该去哪里咨询问题，但它不决定申请结果，也不做学业咨询方面的决定。把招生部门和院系的联系方式与国际学生联系方式放在一起，有助于学生把问题问对地方。

keep in mind 短语 记住；记在心里e.g. Please keep in mind that the shop closes at six on Sundays.💡 场合：日常口语和正式场合都常用，语气比 remember 更郑重，常用于提醒对方别忘了重要信息。
¶ 14

At Trent University in Peterborough, college membership includes students living off campus. Members have access to professional and student staff and community activities, so that university group can continue even if housing plans change.

在彼得伯勒的特伦特大学，学院成员身份包括住在校外的学生。成员可以使用专业人员和学生工作人员提供的服务并参加社区活动，因此即使住房计划有变，这个大学社群也不会中断。

¶ 15

The main Peterborough campus is on the Otonabee River. Its Symons Campus colleges are beside the river, while Catharine Parr Traill College is near downtown. Campus maps and tours show the differences between those locations.

彼得伯勒主校区位于奥托纳比河畔。赛蒙斯校区的各学院紧邻河边，而凯瑟琳·帕尔·特雷尔学院则靠近市中心。校园地图和参观活动可以展示这些地点的不同之处。

¶ 16

Trent International provides campus tours, immigration resources and contacts for student-service, career and application enquiries. Broad undergraduate and graduate choices are available, with programs and everyday settings varying by campus. Durham GTA in Oshawa is a separate option and should not be treated as another name for the Peterborough campus. College membership also does not guarantee a residence room.

特伦特国际部提供校园参观、移民资源以及学生服务、职业和申请方面的联系方式。本科和研究生选择范围很广，不同校区的专业和日常生活环境各不相同。位于奥沙瓦的达勒姆大多伦多校区是一个独立的选择，不应被当作彼得伯勒校区的另一个名字。学院成员身份同样不保证能分到宿舍。

¶ 17

The largest campus of the University of Northern British Columbia is in Prince George, in northern British Columbia. The university also has regional and satellite locations, so applicants should confirm Prince George when matching a degree to this setting. The university offers undergraduate and graduate study in several areas. Applicants can consider the location before choosing a specialization.

北不列颠哥伦比亚大学最大的校区位于不列颠哥伦比亚省北部的乔治王子城。该校还有其他地区校区和卫星校区，因此申请人在把学位与校区对应起来时要确认是乔治王子城校区。学校提供多个领域的本科和研究生学习。申请人在选择专业方向之前可以先考虑地理位置。

¶ 18

International undergraduate applicants apply through EducationPlannerBC. They send any remaining documents to international admissions. The university’s country-requirements directory includes a route for applicants from India. It recognizes Class XII credentials and requires Class X and Class XII board marksheets. English-language evidence and program-specific requirements remain part of assessment. A recognized school credential does not assure admission. Applicants should also check the requirements for competitive programs.

国际本科申请人通过 EducationPlannerBC（卑诗省教育规划系统）申请。他们把其余材料寄给国际招生办公室。学校的国家入学要求目录里有针对印度申请人的通道。该校认可印度十二年级学历，要求提供十年级和十二年级的毕业会考成绩单。英语语言证明和专业具体要求仍然是评估的一部分。获得认可的学校学历并不能保证录取。申请人还应查看竞争激烈专业的具体要求。

competitive /kәm'petitiv/ a. 竞争的e.g. It is a very competitive programme, and only a few students get in.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用；既可以说人“好胜的”，也可以说项目、比赛“竞争激烈的”，语气中性。
apply through 短语 通过……（渠道/系统）申请e.g. You can apply through the school website, and it only takes ten minutes.💡 场合：正式书面和办事场景常用，说明申请走的是哪个渠道或系统；日常口语也常说。
¶ 19

A campus map shows whether the intended degree belongs to the main campus, which residence or student centre is relevant and how facilities relate to the surrounding city. Applicants comparing several campuses need a map for each.

校园地图可以显示想读的学位是否属于主校区、哪间宿舍或学生中心与之相关，以及各项设施与周边城市的关系。同时比较几所大学的申请人，每所都需要一张地图。

¶ 20

A student may want outdoor space, a defined college community or a regular volunteering commitment. Keeping two or three likely weekly activities in mind during a virtual tour helps them consider what an ordinary week would be like.

学生可能想要户外空间、一个明确的学院社群，或者一项定期参加的志愿服务。在虚拟参观时，心里先想好两三项每周可能参加的活动，有助于他们想象普通一周的生活会是什么样子。

¶ 21

Before deciding, the applicant can ask an international contact which services begin before arrival and which require an active student account. A family can also discuss when the student hopes to visit friends or relatives elsewhere in Canada and check the relevant transport arrangements. Those travel plans should be considered alongside the smaller-city preference, after confirming the degree and campus.

在做决定之前，申请人可以问问国际学生联系人：哪些服务在抵达前就开始了，哪些需要开通学生账号后才能使用。一家人也可以讨论学生希望何时去加拿大其他地方探望朋友或亲戚，并查询相应的交通安排。在确认学位和校区之后，这些出行计划也应和偏爱小城市的想法一起考虑。

applicant /'æplikәnt/ n. 申请者e.g. Hundreds of applicants sent in their forms for the summer job.💡 场合：正式书面和职场、招生场景常用；日常口语更常说“申请的人”或直接说名字。

1. ____ at the library is her favourite weekend activity.
✓ volunteering　自动提供；自愿效劳( volunteer的现在分词 ); 主动建议（或告诉）;

去图书馆做志愿服务是她最喜欢的周末活动。

✓ orientation　新生入学指导
