1. Labubu maker learns from Disney

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2025-10-03 · 原文链接
📖 中国潮玩公司泡泡玛特凭借 Labubu 风靡全球，市值已超越孩之宝、美泰、三丽鸥等老牌玩具巨头。它正效仿迪士尼的打法：把短期爆款变成能做主题乐园、娱乐和衍生品的长久 IP，目标是再打造 5 到 10 个具有全球潜力的角色。值得读的是一个盲盒品牌如何从“爆款依赖”走向“IP 矩阵”——这是观察中国消费品牌出海最有代表性的一课。
¶ 1

Pop Mart, a Chinese toy company, has achieved global success with its character Labubu.

中国玩具公司泡泡玛特凭借其角色 Labubu 取得了全球性的成功。

a big hit 短语 大热 / 爆款e.g. Pop Mart doesn't want only one big hit.
¶ 2

The company’s strategy is inspired by Disney, aiming to turn short-term popularity into long-term growth. Like Disney’s Mickey Mouse, Labubu is becoming a strong intellectual property with potential for theme parks, entertainment, and merchandise. Pop Mart doesn’t want only one big hit. Instead, it hopes to create five to ten characters with similar global potential. The company is now more valuable than major toy makers like Hasbro, Mattel, and Sanrio.

这家公司的战略深受迪士尼启发，目标是把短期热度转化为长期增长。就像迪士尼的米老鼠一样，Labubu 正在成为一个强大的知识产权（IP），具备进军主题乐园、娱乐产业和衍生品市场的潜力。泡泡玛特不想只有一个爆款；相反，它希望再打造五到十个同样具有全球潜力的角色。该公司的市值如今已超过孩之宝、美泰和三丽鸥等老牌玩具制造商。

be inspired by 短语 受……启发e.g. The company's strategy is inspired by Disney.
turn A into B 短语 把 A 变成 Be.g. aiming to turn short-term popularity into long-term growth
¶ 3

Labubu’s success has also increased competition in China’s art toy industry, where many new companies and artists are trying to enter the market. Pop Mart’s ‘blind box’ model and focus on young women helped its growth, but critics say it still depends too much on Labubu. Analysts note the firm faces challenges if it wants to repeat Disney’s success, but its fast rise shows strong foundations.

Labubu 的成功也加剧了中国潮玩行业的竞争，许多新公司和艺术家正试图进入这个市场。泡泡玛特的“盲盒”模式和对年轻女性消费者的聚焦助力了它的增长，但批评者认为它仍然过度依赖 Labubu。分析师指出，如果泡泡玛特想复制迪士尼的成功，还面临不少挑战；不过，它的快速崛起已经展现出扎实的基础。

enter the market 短语 进入市场e.g. many new companies and artists are trying to enter the market
depend too much on 短语 过度依赖……e.g. critics say it still depends too much on Labubu
¶ 4

Difficult words: intellectual property (ideas, designs, or creations that belong to a person or company), potential (the ability or chance to grow, succeed, or become something in the future), foundation (the main base or starting point of success).

生词：intellectual property（知识产权，指属于某个人或公司的创意、设计或作品）、potential（潜力，指成长、成功或成为某种事物的能力或机会）、foundation（基础，指成功的根基或起点）。

2. Who does Basmati rice belong to?

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-02 · 原文链接
📖 印度和巴基斯坦为“Basmati（印度香米）”这个名字在欧盟打了多年官司——谁拿到“受保护地理标志”地位，谁就垄断欧洲高价值市场。但由于两国缺乏政治合作，至今连联合申请都交不出去。讽刺的是，全球九成挂着 Basmati 名字的米根本不产自印巴。这篇短文把“地理标志”“市场垄断”等经济术语放在真实新闻里讲，值得一读。
¶ 1

Basmati rice is one of the world’s most valuable rice varieties, and sells for up to twice the price of standard white rice. Both India and Pakistan grow this crop in the historical region of Punjab, which is now divided by an international border.

印度香米（Basmati）是世界上最值钱的大米品种之一，售价可达普通白米的两倍。印度和巴基斯坦都在历史上的旁遮普地区种植这种作物，而该地区如今被一条国界一分为二。

¶ 2

For years, both nations have been locked in a legal dispute over the exclusive right to the Basmati name in the EU. Receiving Protected Geographical Indication (PGI) status would grant a valuable market monopoly, but neither country has been able to submit a joint application due to a lack of political cooperation. Although similar disputes, such as Halloumi cheese in divided Cyprus, were resolved through joint EU frameworks, negotiations between India and Pakistan have completely stalled.

多年来，两国一直在为欧盟境内“Basmati”名称的专属权打官司。如果能获得“受保护地理标志”（PGI）地位，就能在市场上获得宝贵的垄断权，但由于缺乏政治合作，两国至今无法提交联合申请。尽管类似的纠纷（比如分裂的塞浦路斯的哈罗米奶酪）曾通过欧盟的联合框架得以解决，印度和巴基斯坦之间的谈判却完全停滞了。

be locked in a dispute 短语 陷入（法律）纠纷e.g. both nations have been locked in a legal dispute over the exclusive right to the Basmati name
exclusive right to 短语 对……的专属权e.g. the exclusive right to the Basmati name in the EU
grant a monopoly 短语 授予垄断权e.g. Receiving PGI status would grant a valuable market monopoly.
due to 短语 由于……e.g. due to a lack of political cooperation
¶ 3

Without an agreement or EU protection, approximately 90% of rice sold globally under the name Basmati comes from other regions. Experts warn that if the EU delays its decision further it may destroy its protection completely.

在没有协议和欧盟保护的情况下，全球以 Basmati 之名销售的大米中，约九成产自其他地区。专家警告，如果欧盟再拖延决定，可能会彻底摧毁这一保护。

¶ 4

Difficult words: exclusive right (the official permission given to only one person or group to do or sell something), monopoly (complete control of a market or product by one single company or country), stall (to stop moving forward or making progress, often unexpectedly).

生词：exclusive right（专属权，只授予某个人或团体做某事或卖某物的官方许可）、monopoly（垄断，指一家公司或一个国家对市场或产品的完全控制）、stall（停滞，指停止前进或取得进展，常常出人意料）。

stall 短语 停滞；使停滞e.g. negotiations between India and Pakistan have completely stalled
