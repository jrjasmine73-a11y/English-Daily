1. Women pay to break things

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-16 · 原文链接
📖 在英国，越来越多的人开始花钱去“发泄屋”砸东西减压——平板电视、瓷器，拿起木槌想砸就砸，而且高达四分之三的顾客是女性。心理学家说，社会期望常常迫使女性压抑怒火，而发泄屋给了她们一个安全可控的出口。这篇新闻不长，话题新奇又贴近生活，能学到不少描述情绪、压力与心理健康的实用表达。
¶ 1

Across the UK, a growing number of people, especially women, are turning to so-called rage rooms to destroy everyday items like flat-screen televisions and crockery with mallets and hammers.

在英国，越来越多的人，尤其是女性，开始走进所谓的“发泄屋”，用木槌和铁锤砸碎平板电视、瓷器等日常物品。

rage /reidʒ/ n. 愤怒；情绪激动；狂暴e.g. He was shaking with rage after the long traffic jam.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都常用；指强烈的怒火，语气比 anger 更强烈。
crockery /'krɒkәri/ n. 陶器；瓦器e.g. She washed the crockery and put it back in the cupboard.💡 场合：英式英语常用词；指盘子、碗、杯子等陶瓷餐具，多用于日常口语。
mallet /'mælit/ n. 木槌；球棍；棒e.g. He hit the tent peg with a mallet.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都可用；指木头做的锤子，常用于木工、露营或砸东西。
turn to 短语 转向、求助于e.g. When work gets too much, I turn to my best friend for a good talk.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都常用；表示遇到困难时转向某种办法或求助于某人。
a surge of adrenaline 短语 一阵肾上腺素飙升e.g. I felt a surge of adrenaline as the roller coaster rushed down.💡 场合：新闻报道和日常口语都可用；形容肾上腺素突然飙升带来的兴奋或紧张感。
¶ 2

Owners report that up to three-quarters of their customers are female. Many use the sessions to cope with everyday stress, child-rearing pressures, or deep emotional trauma such as bereavement. Psychologists note that societal expectations often force women to suppress anger, unlike sports events where men are traditionally permitted to shout freely. Inside a rage room, participants find a safe, controlled environment where they can express frustration without judgment.

发泄屋的老板说，高达四分之三的顾客是女性。许多人用这种方式来应对日常压力、育儿压力，或是丧亲之痛这类深层的情感创伤。心理学家指出，社会期望常常迫使女性压抑怒火，而在体育赛事上，男性却历来可以自由地大喊大叫。在发泄屋里，参与者能找到一个安全可控的环境，毫无顾忌地宣泄自己的沮丧。

bereavement /bi'ri:vmәnt/ n. 丧亲；丧友e.g. She took a week off work after her bereavement.💡 场合：正式书面语，多用于新闻和心理话题；指失去亲人后的悲痛，比 sadness 正式、沉重。
suppress /sә'pres/ vt. 镇压；使止住；禁止；抑制；查禁e.g. She tried to suppress a yawn during the long meeting.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用；表示强行压住情绪、声音或想法。
trauma /'trɒ:mә/ n. 外伤；损伤e.g. The accident left her with trauma that took years to heal.💡 场合：新闻报道和心理话题常用；指身心受到的严重创伤，语气较沉重、正式。
judgment /'dʒʌdʒmәnt/ n. 裁判；宣告；判决书e.g. Don't be too quick to make a judgment about her.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用；指对人或事的看法、判断，也可指法庭判决。
frustration /frʌs'treiʃәn/ n. 挫折；顿挫e.g. He kicked the ball in frustration after missing the goal.💡 场合：日常口语非常常用；指事情不顺时的沮丧、恼火，比 anger 更偏“无奈”。
cope with 短语 应对、处理e.g. Deep breathing helps me cope with exam stress.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用；表示应对压力、困难或难题，语气中性偏正式。
¶ 3

While smashing objects provides an immediate surge of adrenaline and physical relief, experts caution that it doesn’t serve as a permanent therapeutic cure for long-term psychological problems. Instead, they view it as a helpful short-term release of energy, and advise individuals dealing with deep-seated emotional pain to seek professional long-term support.

虽然砸东西能立刻带来一阵肾上腺素飙升和身体上的释放感，但专家提醒，这并不能从根本上治愈长期的心理问题。他们认为，这只是一种有帮助的短期能量释放，并建议那些深受情感痛苦困扰的人寻求专业的长期帮助。

adrenaline n. 肾上腺素e.g. The roller coaster gave me a rush of adrenaline.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都常用；形容紧张、兴奋时身体分泌的激素，口语里常说“肾上腺素飙升”。
therapeutic /.θerә'pju:tik/ a. 治疗的；有益于健康的e.g. Gardening is therapeutic for her — it calms her mind.💡 场合：日常口语和健康话题常用；形容某事对身心有好处、能让人放松。
release /ri'li:s/ n. 释放；发泄；豁免；发行；释放证书e.g. A good cry is a healthy release of sadness.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用；指把积压的情绪、能量释放出来。
seek professional support 短语 寻求专业帮助e.g. After months of feeling low, she decided to seek professional support.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用；指找心理医生、咨询师等专业人士帮忙，语气较正式。
¶ 4

Difficult words: bereavement (suffering the loss of a loved one through death), suppress (to forcibly hold back, control, or prevent the expression of a feeling), judgment (an opinion or decision formed after thought or legal process).

生词：bereavement（丧亲之痛，因失去亲人而遭受的痛苦），suppress（压抑，强行克制或阻止某种情感的表达），judgment（评判，经过思考后形成的看法或决定；也可指法律判决）。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. After months of feeling low, she decided to ____.
✓ seek professional support　寻求专业帮助
After months of feeling low, she decided to seek professional support.
低落了好几个月之后，她决定去寻求专业帮助。
2. Gardening is ____ for her — it calms her mind.
✓ therapeutic　治疗的；有益于健康的
Gardening is therapeutic for her — it calms her mind.
种花对她来说很有疗愈作用——能让她的心平静下来。
3. She took a week off work after her ____.
✓ bereavement　丧亲；丧友
She took a week off work after her bereavement.
遭遇丧亲之痛后，她请了一周假。
4. When work gets too much, I ____ my best friend for a good talk.
✓ turn to　转向、求助于
When work gets too much, I turn to my best friend for a good talk.
工作压力太大时，我会找最好的朋友好好聊聊。
5. The roller coaster gave me a rush of ____.
✓ adrenaline　肾上腺素
The roller coaster gave me a rush of adrenaline.
过山车让我肾上腺素飙升。
6. A good cry is a healthy ____ of sadness.
✓ release　释放；发泄；豁免；发行；释放证书
A good cry is a healthy release of sadness.
痛痛快快哭一场，是释放悲伤的健康方式。
7. She tried to ____ a yawn during the long meeting.
✓ suppress　镇压；使止住；禁止；抑制；查禁
She tried to suppress a yawn during the long meeting.
在漫长的会议上，她努力忍住一个哈欠。
8. He kicked the ball in ____ after missing the goal.
✓ frustration　挫折；顿挫
He kicked the ball in frustration after missing the goal.
射门没进，他沮丧地踢了一脚球。
9. She washed the ____ and put it back in the cupboard.
✓ crockery　陶器；瓦器
She washed the crockery and put it back in the cupboard.
她把瓷器洗干净，放回了碗柜。
10. Don't be too quick to make a ____ about her.
✓ judgment　裁判；宣告；判决书
Don't be too quick to make a judgment about her.
别太快对她下判断。
11. The accident left her with ____ that took years to heal.
✓ trauma　外伤；损伤
The accident left her with trauma that took years to heal.
那场事故给她留下了创伤，花了好几年才愈合。
12. He was shaking with ____ after the long traffic jam.
✓ rage　愤怒；情绪激动；狂暴
He was shaking with rage after the long traffic jam.
堵了半天车之后，他气得浑身发抖。
13. I felt ____ as the roller coaster rushed down.
✓ a surge of adrenaline　一阵肾上腺素飙升
I felt a surge of adrenaline as the roller coaster rushed down.
过山车俯冲而下时，我感到肾上腺素一阵飙升。
14. He hit the tent peg with a ____.
✓ mallet　木槌；球棍；棒
He hit the tent peg with a mallet.
他用木槌把帐篷钉敲进地里。
15. Deep breathing helps me ____ exam stress.
✓ cope with　应对、处理
Deep breathing helps me cope with exam stress.
深呼吸帮我应对考试压力。
