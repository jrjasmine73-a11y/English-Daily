1. Hackers attack French supermarkets

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-19 · 原文链接
📖 黑客入侵了法国多家超市的冷藏系统，远程把冷柜温度调到 50℃，导致数千吨食品变质、损失达数百万美元，甚至引发火灾。这场前所未有的网络攻击利用的是老旧控制软件的漏洞，也让各国开始担心关键基础设施的安全。读这篇文章，可以学到网络安全、损失评估等热点词汇。
¶ 1

Major supermarket chains across France were hit by an unprecedented cyberattack that targeted commercial refrigeration systems.

法国各大连锁超市遭遇了一场前所未有的网络攻击，目标是商用冷藏系统。

cyberattack e.g. The bank lost millions in a cyberattack last year.
unprecedented /.ʌn'presidentid/ a. 空前的e.g. The festival drew an unprecedented crowd this year.
¶ 2

Hackers remotely took control of thermostats in hundreds of stores, and raised temperatures in fridges and freezers to as high as 50°C.

黑客远程控制了数百家门店的恒温器，把冷柜和冷冻柜的温度调高到了 50℃。

remotely adv. 极小地；极细微地e.g. He works remotely and only visits the office once a month.
raise concerns about 短语 引发对……的担忧e.g. The new policy raised concerns about food safety.
¶ 3

The sudden heat caused thousands of tons of fresh and frozen food, including meat, dairy, and juice, to spoil completely. This resulted in millions of dollars in damages and even triggered fires at several locations. The attack exploited security vulnerabilities in outdated control software, which allowed store managers to control appliances remotely. Major retailers like Intermarché and Carrefour were heavily affected, and forced some locations to manually disconnect their refrigeration units to prevent further tampering.

突然升温导致数千吨生鲜和冷冻食品——包括肉类、奶制品和果汁——彻底变质，造成数百万美元损失，还在几个地点引发了火灾。这次攻击利用了老旧控制软件的安全漏洞；正是这套软件让店长可以远程控制电器设备。Intermarché 和 Carrefour 等大型零售商受到严重影响，一些门店被迫手动断开冷藏设备，以防黑客进一步搞破坏。

spoil /spɒil/ n. 战利品；赃物；奖品；变质；次品e.g. Milk spoils quickly if you leave it out of the fridge.
trigger /'trigә/ n. 触发器；扳机e.g. The loud noise triggered the car alarm.
exploit /'eksplɒit/ n. 功绩；勋绩e.g. Some sellers exploit tourists by charging double prices.
disconnect /.diskә'nekt/ vt. 使分离；使不相连；拆开e.g. Please disconnect the charger when the battery is full.
retailer /'ri:teilә/ n. 零售商人；传播的人e.g. Online retailers often offer better prices than physical stores.
result in 短语 导致、造成e.g. Careless driving can result in serious accidents.
exploit vulnerabilities 短语 利用（系统）漏洞e.g. Scammers exploit security holes in phones to steal your money.
¶ 4

French authorities and national cybersecurity agencies have launched an urgent criminal investigation into the incident, and raised serious concerns about the safety and security of essential infrastructure.

法国当局和国家网络安全机构已对这起事件展开紧急刑事调查，并对关键基础设施的安全问题提出了严重关切。

urgent /'ә:dʒәnt/ a. 紧急的；急迫的；催逼的e.g. We need an urgent meeting to fix this problem.
infrastructure /'infrәstrʌktʃә/ n. 基础结构；基础设施e.g. The city is investing in new roads and other infrastructure.
¶ 5

Difficult words: unprecedented (an illegal attempt to damage, or steal data from computer systems or networks), thermostat (a device that automatically controls temperature in an appliance or building), vulnerability (a weakness in a system or software), tamper (to change something in order to cause damage or gain an advantage).

Difficult words：unprecedented（前所未有的；对计算机系统或网络的非法攻击企图），thermostat（恒温器；自动调节电器或建筑温度的装置），vulnerability（漏洞；系统或软件中的弱点），tamper（篡改；为了破坏或谋利而改动某物）。

vulnerability /.vʌlnәrә'biliti/ n. 易受伤；易受责难；易受伤部位e.g. The new lock fixes the security vulnerability in the old one.
tamper /'tæmpә/ vi. 干预；拨弄；贿赂；损害；篡改e.g. Don't tamper with the medicine bottles; it's dangerous.
be hit by 短语 遭遇（攻击、打击等）e.g. The coastal city was hit by a strong typhoon last night.
take control of 短语 控制、掌控e.g. She took control of the meeting and kept everyone on topic.

2. Bus passengers to be fined for playing loud music

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-09-28 · 原文链接
📖 在公交车上用外放听音乐，很多人深受其扰——新加坡出手了：最高可罚 500 新元，脚放座椅上、乱扔垃圾、在车上吃喝也一并打击。司机本人不开罚单，只负责把闹事乘客请下车。读这篇短文，学一学文明出行、公共空间礼仪的地道表达。
¶ 1

The loud music from other passengers on public transport has become a peeve for many people who want to travel in quiet. Singapore has introduced new fines of up to S$500 for blaring out music on buses that annoys other passengers. The new penalties will also be applied to other anti-social behaviour, such as putting feet on seats, littering, and eating or drinking on buses. A news release from Singapore's Land Transport Authority (LTA) said the fines would help create a "safer, more considerate and pleasant public transport environment for all". The LTA added: "Public transport is a shared space, and every commuter has a part to play in ensuring a gracious and pleasant commuting environment."

公交车上其他乘客外放的吵闹音乐，已经成为许多想安静乘车的人的心头之恨。新加坡出台新规：在公交车上大声放音乐、吵到其他乘客，最高罚款 500 新元。新罚则还适用于其他不文明行为，比如脚踩座椅、乱扔垃圾、在车上吃喝。新加坡陆路交通管理局（LTA）发布的新闻稿称，罚款有助于营造"更安全、更体贴、更愉快的公共交通环境"。LTA 还表示："公共交通是共享空间，每位乘客都有责任维护文明愉快的乘车环境。"

¶ 2

The fines will not be issued by bus drivers. The LTA has decided that bus drivers should prioritise passenger safety while driving their vehicles. The LTA said drivers needed to focus on "their primary responsibility of operating buses safely". However, drivers will have the power to ask inconsiderate and disruptive passengers to get off the bus. Many commuters welcomed the new fines. A 29-year-old flight attendant who has to travel to work at Singapore's Changi Airport by bus told the Straits Times newspaper: "I saw the new rules and I thought, 'Thank God'." A 56-year-old commuter was happy. She said: "I just want to get to work without having to listen to other people's music."

罚单不会由公交车司机来开。LTA 决定，公交车司机应该优先保障乘客安全，专心驾驶车辆。LTA 表示，司机需要专注于"安全驾驶这一首要职责"。不过，司机有权要求不文明、扰乱秩序的乘客下车。许多通勤族对新规表示欢迎。一位 29 岁的空姐每天要坐公交车去新加坡樟宜机场上班，她告诉《海峡时报》记者：我看到新规时，心想「谢天谢地」。另一位 56 岁的乘客也很高兴，她说："我只想安安静静去上班，不用听别人的音乐。"
