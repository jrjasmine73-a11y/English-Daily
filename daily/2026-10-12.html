1. My friends are shocked when I speak English

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2025-10-11 · 原文链接
📖 一个在国外餐厅发生的励志小故事：会英语的朋友 Peter 突然被电话叫走，不会点餐的大家瞬间傻眼——关键时刻，主人公靠每天玩游戏积累的英语流利点餐，惊呆了所有朋友。亮点在于它讲的正是“学英语有什么用”的经典场景：点餐、自我介绍式的实用英语。全文简单好读，非常适合作为晨读热身。
¶ 1

This is an inspirational story for people who want to learn English. This could be your story too. I am on holiday abroad. My friends and I have just sat down in a beautiful restaurant with a view of the sea. The staff speak only English. Luckily, our friend Peter knows English, so we feel safe. We can rely on him to order our food.

这是一个关于学英语的励志故事，它也可能是你的故事。我正在国外度假。我和朋友们刚在一家能看到大海的漂亮餐厅坐下。服务员只会说英语。幸运的是，我们的朋友 Peter 会英语，所以我们觉得很安心。我们可以靠他来点餐。

rely on 短语 依赖、依靠e.g. We can rely on him to order our food.（我们可以靠他来点餐。）
¶ 2

Suddenly, Peter’s phone rings. He apologizes and says he has to deal with something urgent. He tells us, “Please order one beer and a steak with baked potatoes for me.” After he leaves, the waiter comes over, smiles, and hands us the menus. Then he asks in English what we would like to drink.

突然，Peter 的电话响了。他道歉说有个急事要处理。他告诉我们：“请帮我点一杯啤酒和一份带烤土豆的牛排。”他离开后，服务员走过来，微笑着递给我们菜单，然后用英语问我们想喝点什么。

deal with 短语 处理、应付（问题/急事）e.g. He has to deal with something urgent.（他有个急事要处理。）
¶ 3

It is a quiet moment. We look at each other. After all, no one except Peter speaks English but I know that this is my moment. I smile, look at the waiter, and say, “I’ll have a glass of wine and some water, please.”

一瞬间安静了下来。我们面面相觑。毕竟，除了 Peter 没人会说英语，但我知道，属于我的时刻到了。我微笑着看向服务员说：“请给我一杯葡萄酒和一些水。”

look at each other 短语 面面相觑、彼此对视e.g. We look at each other.（我们面面相觑。）
¶ 4

Then I ask each friend what they want and tell the waiter their orders.

然后我逐一询问每个朋友想吃什么，再把他们的点餐告诉服务员。

¶ 5

When the waiter leaves, there is silence. My friends look at me in disbelief. They can’t understand what just happened.

服务员离开后，场面一片寂静。朋友们难以置信地看着我，完全不明白刚才发生了什么。

in disbelief 短语 难以置信地e.g. My friends look at me in disbelief.（朋友们难以置信地看着我。）
¶ 6

Then one friend asks, “When did you learn English?”

接着一个朋友问：“你什么时候学会英语的？”

¶ 7

I smile and answer, “I started learning not long ago. I use LangoPlay. It’s a game where you learn English through a story. I play every day and learn a few new words each time. It really works.”

我笑着回答：“我没学多久。我用 LangoPlay。那是一款通过故事学英语的游戏。我每天都玩，每次都能学到几个新单词。真管用。”

not long ago 短语 不久前e.g. I started learning not long ago.（我不久前才开始学。）
¶ 8

There is a short pause, and then another friend says, “Can you show me? Maybe I will try it too.”

短暂沉默之后，另一个朋友说：“能给我演示一下吗？说不定我也会试试。”

¶ 9

I nod, “Sure. LangoPlay is simple. You can start for free and play anytime at www.langoplay.com.”

我点点头：“当然。LangoPlay 很简单。可以免费开始，在 www.langoplay.com 随时玩。”

¶ 10

Difficult words: urgent (very important to do now), silence (a quiet moment), nod (to move your head down and up).

生词：urgent（非常紧急、需立刻处理的事）、silence（寂静、安静的时刻）、nod（点头，把头向下再向上动）。

2. Are people drinking less alcohol?

BBC Learning English · 6 Minute English · 2026-10-12 · 原文链接
📖 BBC 6 分钟英语最新一期：英国饮酒文化正在改变——调查显示 16–24 岁的年轻人比老一辈更可能滴酒不沾，连主持人 Neil 今年都没碰过一杯酒。关键信息点是“反转”：禁酒被贴上自律人设标签后，现在又出现了反弹——名人公开晒喝酒抽烟、不在乎品牌形象。值得读：sober、societal pressure、pushback 都是社会议题类写作的高频词，正反观点兼备，适合积累议论文素材。
¶ 1

GeorgieHi Neil.

你好，Neil。

¶ 2

NeilHi Georgie. Do you want to hear something quite unusual for me?

你好，Georgie。想听一件对我来说很不寻常的事吗？

¶ 3

GeorgieErr, yes!

呃，想！

¶ 4

NeilI haven't had an alcoholic drink this year.

我今年还没喝过一杯含酒精的饮料。

¶ 5

GeorgieWow! That is really impressive. How do you feel? Like a new person?

哇！真了不起。感觉怎么样？像换了个人？

¶ 6

NeilI do feel very good, I have to say.

不得不说，我感觉非常好。

¶ 7

GeorgieI can't say the same – I did have a few alcoholic drinks at the weekend with my friends. But in many parts of the world, people don't drink alcohol at all. In the UK it's very common – that is until recently. Today on 6 Minute English, we're going to find out why many people are choosing to give up or cut down on alcohol, and we'll be asking, "Is this the end of British drinking culture?"

我可说不出这话——周末我还和朋友们喝了几杯。世界上很多地方的人根本不喝酒，在英国喝酒很普遍——但最近情况变了。今天的《6 分钟英语》，我们来聊聊为什么越来越多人选择戒酒或少喝酒，还要问一个问题：“英国的饮酒文化要终结了吗？”

give up / cut down on 短语 戒除 / 减少（某种习惯）e.g. many people are choosing to give up or cut down on alcohol.（越来越多人选择戒酒或少喝酒。）
be the norm 短语 是常态、很普遍e.g. where it was the norm to just join in.（在那里跟着大家一起喝是常态。）
give something away 短语 泄露（秘密/答案）e.g. I'm not going to give it away.（我不打算说出来/泄露答案。）
¶ 8

GeorgieOK. Neil, let's start with a quiz question. Many people in the UK and other countries choose to give up alcohol in the new year for the whole of the month of January, but what is the challenge called? Is it: a)    No-drink New Year

好。Neil，我们先来个小测验。在英国和很多国家，很多人选择在新年的整个一月里戒酒，这个挑战叫什么名字？是：a) 不喝酒新年

¶ 9

b)    Alcohol-free January, orc)    Dry January?

b) 无酒精一月，还是 c) 干一月（Dry January）？

¶ 10

NeilWell, I know the answer to this, but I'm not going to give it away.

嗯，我知道答案，但我不打算说出来。

¶ 11

GeorgieOK. Well, then our listeners can find out the answer at the end of the programme.

好吧，听众朋友们可以在节目最后找到答案。

¶ 12

NeilYes, they can. So, Georgie, I hear all the time that younger people don't drink as much alcohol today as they used to in the past. Is that true?

可以。是的。Georgie，我总听说现在的年轻人比过去喝酒少，这是真的吗？

¶ 13

GeorgieWell, in the UK, surveys suggest that 16 to 24-year-olds are more likely to be non-drinkers than the older generations.

在英国，调查显示 16 到 24 岁的年轻人比老一辈更可能滴酒不沾。

¶ 14

NeilOK. So, why are more young people choosing to be sober? That means not to drink alcohol.

好。那为什么越来越多年轻人选择清醒（sober）生活呢？sober 就是不喝酒的意思。

¶ 15

GeorgieWell, the BBC World Service podcast What in the World have been investigating this. Presenter Hannah Gelbart met three young people who choose to be sober for some alcohol-free drinks to find out more. Here's Daniel's story:

BBC World Service 的播客《What in the World》一直在调查这个问题。主持人 Hannah Gelbart 约了三位选择过无酒精生活的年轻人，一边喝无酒精饮料一边聊聊原因。先来听听 Daniel 的故事：

¶ 16

DanielYeah. So, today I don't drink at all, but when I was in my early 20s, I think I felt maybe a bit of, like, societal pressure – you know, work drinks or being at uni, where it was the norm to just join in. I think you'd probably get a few weird glances if you're like, "Yeah, I'm not going to join in at all." So, I did that and I think Covid lockdown just gave me a bit of confidence to say, "OK, let me step away from this. It's not necessarily something I want to do moving forward."

是的。我现在完全不喝酒了，但二十出头的时候，我觉得有点社会压力（societal pressure）——比如说职场聚餐或在大学里，跟着大家一起喝是常态（the norm）。你要是说“我完全不参加”，估计会收获几个异样的眼神。所以我也跟着喝了。我觉得新冠封锁期给了我一点勇气，让我说：“好吧，我想抽身出来，这不一定是我以后想做的事。”

societal pressure 短语 社会压力（周围人都做，你觉得自己也得做）e.g. I felt societal pressure to drink alcohol.（我感受到了要喝酒的社会压力。）
¶ 17

Hannah GelbartSo, how many years have you been sober for?

那你清醒（不喝酒）多少年了？

¶ 18

DanielI would say it's been six years now.

我想大概六年了。

¶ 19

Hannah GelbartSix years?

六年？

¶ 20

GeorgieSo, Daniel says that when he was younger, he felt societal pressure to drink alcohol. Societal pressure is feeling like you need to do something because lots of people around you are doing it.

Daniel 说他年轻时感受到要喝酒的社会压力。societal pressure 就是一种感觉：因为周围很多人都这么做，你觉得自己也得这么做。

¶ 21

NeilRight. So, Daniel says drinking was the norm at university and after work. The norm is a way of saying that something is normal or common.

对。Daniel 说在大学和下班后喝酒是常态。the norm 就是指某事很正常、很普遍。

¶ 22

GeorgieBut during the Covid-19 pandemic, when people in the UK were unable to socialise and visit pubs and bars, Daniel's attitude to alcohol changed. After that, he decided to be sober and he hasn't had a drink for about six years.

但新冠疫情期间，英国人没法社交、去不了酒吧，Daniel 对酒精的态度改变了。之后他决定戒酒，到现在大概六年没喝过一杯酒了。

¶ 23

NeilSo, Georgie, it seems like many young people don't drink at all and lots of them share their experience of being sober on social media. But recently there's been a bit of a change, hasn't there?

所以，Georgie，看来很多年轻人完全不喝酒，不少人还在社交媒体上分享自己的清醒生活体验。但最近好像有点变化，对吧？

¶ 24

GeorgieYes. Whilst many people use social media to share their experience of drinking less alcohol or no alcohol at all, some people have been saying that they want to go out more and drink and have fun with friends. They argue we're too worried about fitness and wellness, and that drinking some alcohol to have a good time is fine. Here's Millie talking more about this to BBC World Service podcast What in the World:

对。虽然很多人用社交媒体分享少喝或不喝的体验，但也有些人说他们想多出门、多喝酒、和朋友开心玩。他们觉得我们对健身和养生太焦虑了，喝点酒开心一下没什么。来听听 Millie 对 BBC World Service 播客《What in the World》是怎么说的：

¶ 25

MillieI think we had this, like, rise of self-optimisation content. I think unfortunately sobriety, kind of, got swept in with that and it got touted as this way to, you know, get up at 5am, do a HYROX. And, you know, for many of us, that's probably not our reality with not drinking. It's that we do it to feel a bit more sane or, you know, things like that. And so, I think now we are entering this, like, almost pushback era of that content and you're seeing celebrities... Like, it's not just alcohol – you're seeing more celebrities smoking and, like, posting that on their Instagram and not worrying if it's brand-friendly.

我觉得之前兴起了一波自我优化（self-optimisation）内容。不幸的是，清醒生活也被卷进了这波潮流，被吹捧（tout）成一种生活方式：早上 5 点起床、去做 HYROX（高强度体能训练）。但对我们很多人来说，不喝酒的现实根本不是这样的——我们只是想让自己神智清醒一点（sane）之类的。所以我觉得现在进入了一个有点反弹（pushback）的时代，明星们……不只是喝酒——你能看到更多名人在 Instagram 上晒抽烟，根本不在乎这样对品牌形象好不好。

pushback 短语 反弹、强烈反对e.g. we are entering this almost pushback era of that content.（我们正进入对那种内容反弹的时代。）
¶ 26

NeilOK. So, Millie thinks that being sober is associated with certain kinds of self-optimisation content, where people talk about fitness, diet and having big goals for themselves.

好。所以 Millie 认为，清醒生活和某些自我优化内容挂钩了——那些内容讲健身、饮食和给自己定大目标。

¶ 27

GeorgieYes, and Millie says being sober gets touted as a way to get up at 5am to exercise. In other words, people talk about it a lot and try to encourage people to do it.

对，Millie 说清醒生活被吹捧成“早上 5 点起床锻炼”的秘诀。换句话说，人们大谈特谈，还试图说服别人也这么做。

¶ 28

NeilBut Millie says that being sober isn't always about being super fit and healthy. For her, it's about being sane.

但 Millie 说，清醒生活不总是为了超级健康。对她来说，是为了让自己神智清明（sane）。

¶ 29

GeorgieBut still, Millie thinks there's a pushback against self-optimisation content. Pushback means a criticism of the content, and people are pushing back by doing the opposite. For example, lots of celebrities post about themselves drinking and smoking.

不过 Millie 认为，现在出现了对自我优化内容的反弹（pushback）。pushback 就是对某种潮流的强烈批评，人们用做相反的事来反抗。比如很多名人发自己喝酒抽烟的照片。

¶ 30

NeilWell, Georgie, I've decided to try to give up alcohol for a whole year, but I don't think everybody should do that if they don't want to. It's really up to the individual.

嗯，Georgie，我决定试着戒酒一整年，但我觉得不想戒的人也不用戒。这完全是个人选择。

¶ 31

GeorgieYeah, I still enjoy having a few drinks with my friends. I think it's just all about balance, really. OK. Neil, it's time to reveal the answer to this week's question. I asked, "What was the name given for when people give up alcohol in January?"

对，我还是喜欢和朋友们喝几杯。我觉得关键就是平衡。好，Neil，到了揭晓本周问题答案的时候了。我问的是：“人们在一月戒酒的挑战叫什么名字？”

¶ 32

NeilYes. And the answer is Dry January.

是的，答案是 Dry January（干一月）。

¶ 33

GeorgieThat is correct. Well done! Let's recap the vocabulary we learned in this programme, starting with sober, which describes someone who doesn't drink alcohol or take recreational drugs.

答对了！做得好！我们来回顾一下本期学到的词汇，先是 sober，指不喝酒、不吸毒的人。

¶ 34

NeilSocietal pressure is the feeling that you should do something because lots of other people expect you to.

societal pressure 是这样一种感觉：因为周围很多人期望你这么做，你觉得自己应该这么做。

¶ 35

GeorgieSaying something is the norm is a way to say it's usual or common.

说某事是 the norm，就是说它很寻常、很普遍。

¶ 36

NeilSelf-optimisation content means videos and posts about being fit, healthy and achieving big goals.

self-optimisation content 指讲健身、健康饮食和实现大目标的视频和帖子。

¶ 37

GeorgieIf somebody touts an idea or lifestyle, they talk about it a lot and try to convince people to do it.

如果有人 tout 一种观点或生活方式，就是大谈特谈并试图说服别人也去做。

¶ 38

NeilAnd pushback is strong criticism of a trend, and people doing the opposite.

而 pushback 是对某种潮流的强烈批评，人们用做相反的事来反抗。

¶ 39

GeorgieOnce again, our six minutes are up, but don't forget to test what you've learned with the worksheet on our website, bbclearningenglish.com.

我们的六分钟又结束了，别忘了去 bbclearningenglish.com 官网用练习题测测学得怎么样。

¶ 40

NeilThank you for joining us and goodbye.

感谢收听，再见。

¶ 41

GeorgieGoodbye! What do you think of the new version of 6 Minute English? Email us at learningenglish@bbc.co.uk.

再见！你觉得新版《6 分钟英语》怎么样？欢迎发邮件到 learningenglish@bbc.co.uk 告诉我们。
