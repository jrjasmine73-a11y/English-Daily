1. Patrick Stewart, Legendary 'Star Trek' Actor, Dies at 86

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-10-31 · 原文链接
📖 86岁的英国传奇演员帕特里克·斯图尔特去世。从莎士比亚舞台到《星际迷航》舰长，再到《X战警》的X教授，他用一生诠释了什么是大器晚成与跨界长青。读这篇，了解一位演员如何从工人家庭走向世界舞台。
¶ 1

Patrick Stewart, the British actor best known for playing Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek and Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films, has died at 86.

帕特里克·斯图尔特——因在《星际迷航》中饰演让-吕克·皮卡德舰长和在《X战警》系列中饰演查尔斯·泽维尔教授而最为人熟知的英国演员——去世，享年86岁。

be best known for 短语 以……最为人熟知e.g. She is best known for her cooking videos.
be survived by 短语 去世后留下（亲属）e.g. He was survived by his wife and two daughters.
¶ 2

Stewart died on October 28 in Los Angeles, following a brief battle with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia.

斯图尔特于10月28日在洛杉矶去世，此前与阿尔茨海默病和路易体痴呆进行了短暂的抗争。

battle with 短语 与……抗争e.g. He won his long battle with illness and returned to work.
¶ 3

"We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today," his X-Men co-star Hugh Jackman wrote on social media. "And, in my book, a tremendous friend and man."

他的《X战警》搭档休·杰克曼在社交媒体上写道：“今天我们失去了一位舞台和银幕上的伟大演员。”“在我看来，他更是一位了不起的朋友和伟人。”

tremendous /tri'mendәs/ a. 巨大的；非常的；可怕的e.g. She made a tremendous effort to finish the project on time.
stage /steidʒ/ n. 阶段；舞台；场所；戏剧；站；驿站；级；层；脚手架e.g. She has loved the stage since she was a child.
¶ 4

Although Stewart became famous for his screen roles, he spent much of his career onstage.

尽管斯图尔特因银幕角色而出名，但他职业生涯的大部分时间都在舞台上度过。

¶ 5

Born in Mirfield, Yorkshire, in northern England, Stewart grew up in a working-class family, and later spoke openly about witnessing domestic violence as a child. He joined an amateur drama group at 12 and developed a love for Shakespeare.

斯图尔特出生于英格兰北部的约克郡米尔菲尔德，成长于一个工人阶级家庭，后来他公开谈起童年时目睹家庭暴力的经历。他12岁加入了一个业余戏剧团体，并由此爱上了莎士比亚。

amateur /'æmәtә/ n. 业余爱好者；外行；爱好者e.g. He started as an amateur photographer and later went professional.
¶ 6

"The stage would prove to be a safe space, a refuge from real life in which I could inhabit another person, living in another place and time," he later wrote.

他后来写道：“舞台证明是一个安全的空间，是逃离现实生活的避难所，在那里我可以化身另一个人，生活在另一个地方和时代。”

refuge /'refju:dʒ/ n. 避难所；安全地带；避难；庇护e.g. Reading is a refuge for me when life gets busy.
inhabit /in'hæbit/ vt. 居住于；占据；栖息e.g. Good actors can inhabit a character completely.
¶ 7

He attended the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School and spent 14 years with the Royal Shakespeare Company, performing in numerous Shakespeare plays.

他就读于布里斯托老维克戏剧学校，并在皇家莎士比亚剧团度过了14年，出演了众多莎士比亚戏剧。

¶ 8

Stewart's television breakthrough came in 1987 with Star Trek: The Next Generation. He initially expected the series to fail, but instead, it ran for seven seasons and 178 episodes, followed by four feature films.

斯图尔特的电视突破出现在1987年的《星际迷航：下一代》。他起初以为这部剧会失败，但结果它连播了七季、共178集，随后又拍了四部电影。

breakthrough /'breikθru:/ n. 突破e.g. Her first novel was her big breakthrough.
¶ 9

His Shakespearean training helped him portray the starship commander, whose formal manner reminded him of characters he had played onstage.

他的莎士比亚戏剧训练帮助他塑造了这位星舰指挥官——其正式的举止让他想起自己在舞台上演过的角色。

portray /pɒ:'trei/ vt. 描绘；描写；描绘...的肖像e.g. She portrayed a brave teacher in the film.
formal /'fɒ:mәl/ a. 正式的；形式的；礼仪的；拘于礼节的；拘谨的e.g. Please wear formal clothes for the meeting.
commander /kә'mɑ:ndә/ n. 司令官；指挥官e.g. The commander gave clear orders to the whole team.
¶ 10

In 2000, Stewart appeared as Professor Charles Xavier in the first X-Men film. He continued playing the character in several more movies, including 2017's Logan and 2022's Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.

2000年，斯图尔特在首部《X战警》电影中饰演查尔斯·泽维尔教授。此后他在多部电影中继续饰演这一角色，包括2017年的《金刚狼3》和2022年的《奇异博士2：疯狂多元宇宙》。

¶ 11

Throughout his career, Stewart continued working in theater, earning three Olivier Awards and a Tony Award nomination for Macbeth. He also won a Grammy in 1996 for his spoken-word recording of Peter and the Wolf.

在整个职业生涯中，斯图尔特始终没有离开戏剧舞台，凭借《麦克白》获得三座奥利弗奖和一项托尼奖提名。他还凭借《彼得与狼》的朗诵录音于1996年获得格莱美奖。

nomination /.nɒmi'neiʃәn/ n. 提名；任命；提名权e.g. Getting the nomination was already a great honor for her.
¶ 12

He was knighted by Queen Elizabeth II in 2010.

2010年，他被伊丽莎白二世女王册封为爵士。

knighted /naɪt/ n. 骑士；武士；爵士e.g. The writer was knighted for his services to literature.
¶ 13

Stewart also became popular with younger audiences through his humor and social media posts. He appeared in The Simpsons, Futurama and The Emoji Movie.

斯图尔特还凭借幽默感和社交媒体帖子受到年轻观众的喜爱。他曾出现在《辛普森一家》《飞出个未来》和《Emoji大电影》中。

¶ 14

Stewart is survived by his wife, singer and songwriter Sunny Ozell, whom he married in 2013, and his two children.

斯图尔特的遗属包括他的妻子——歌手兼词曲作者桑尼·奥泽尔，两人于2013年结婚——以及他的两个孩子。

legendary /'ledʒәndәri/ a. 传说的；传奇中说的；传奇般的；传说中的；著名的e.g. He became a legendary singer in his country.
