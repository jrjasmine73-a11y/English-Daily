1. A couple sells fake Taylor Swift tickets

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-14 · 原文链接
📖 一对加拿大夫妇假卖泰勒·斯威夫特演唱会门票，骗了100多名粉丝共26.5万美元。他们把近14万美元赃款花在了网络赌博上。如今受害者要求重判，而律师却在为他们争取居家监禁——这起案件值得一读，既能学到诈骗、量刑等实用词汇，也能想想网络购票如何防骗。
¶ 1

A couple from Canada, Denise Tisor and David Blake, went to court after scamming over 100 Taylor Swift fans out of 265 thousand dollars.

一对来自加拿大的夫妇丹尼斯·蒂索和大卫·布莱克，在诈骗100多名泰勒·斯威夫特粉丝共计26.5万美元后出庭受审。

scam somebody out of money 短语 骗走某人的钱e.g. The couple scammed over 100 fans out of 265 thousand dollars.
¶ 2

Tisor sold non-existent tickets for the Eras Tour in Toronto. Many parents bought them as special gifts for their kids, including a sick girl who was seeking relief from medical struggles. Victims only found out the tickets didn’t exist right before the show, and many families were left in tears outside the stadium. Both Tisor and her husband admitted they were guilty. They also confessed to spending nearly 140 thousand dollars of the stolen money on online gambling.

蒂索出售的是根本不存在的多伦多“时代巡回演唱会”门票。许多家长把这些门票当作特殊礼物买给孩子，其中包括一位希望借此暂时摆脱病痛折磨的生病女孩。受害者直到演出开始前才发现门票根本不存在，许多家庭被挡在体育场外，伤心落泪。蒂索和她的丈夫都承认有罪。他们还承认把近14万美元的赃款花在了网络赌博上。

seek relief from something 短语 从某事中寻求解脱/缓解e.g. The sick girl was seeking relief from medical struggles.
be left in tears 短语 伤心落泪e.g. The sad movie left many viewers in tears.
admit to doing something / confess to doing something 短语 承认做了某事e.g. They confessed to spending nearly 140 thousand dollars on online gambling.
¶ 3

Lawyers are asking the judge to give them house arrest instead of sending them to real prison. However, the victims are angry about this idea and think the couple should go to jail. The court will make a final choice about their punishment in December.

律师正在请求法官判处他们居家监禁，而不是把他们送进真正的监狱。然而，受害者对这个提议感到愤怒，认为这对夫妇应该入狱。法院将于12月就他们的惩罚作出最终决定。

house arrest 短语 居家监禁；软禁e.g. Lawyers are asking the judge to give them house arrest instead of prison.
¶ 4

Difficult words: scam (a clever scheme to trick people out of their money), relief (a pleasant feeling when worry, pain, or trouble stops), punishment (a penalty given to someone because they broke a rule or law).

难词：scam（精心设计、骗取他人钱财的骗局），relief（忧虑、疼痛或麻烦停止时那种令人愉快的轻松感），punishment（因违反规则或法律而给予某人的惩罚）。

2. Malaysia’s durian is becoming popular

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-13 · 原文链接
📖 马来西亚正把榴莲从路边小吃打造成全球奢侈品，主攻全球最大的榴莲市场——中国。与泰国提前采摘不同，马来西亚坚持让榴莲自然成熟落地，风味更胜一筹，但保鲜期只有两三天。这篇文章能学到出口、保鲜期、奢侈品等经贸词汇，也能了解“猫山王”背后的商业故事。
¶ 1

Malaysia is making a strong push to turn durian from a simple roadside snack into a luxury global product. The country is targeting China, the world’s biggest durian market.

马来西亚正在大力推动把榴莲从一种普通的路边小吃打造成全球奢侈品。该国瞄准的是全球最大的榴莲市场——中国。

turn something into something 短语 把某物变成/打造成某物e.g. Malaysia is making a strong push to turn durian into a luxury global product.
target a market 短语 瞄准某个市场e.g. The country is targeting China, the world's biggest durian market.
a race against time 短语 与时间赛跑e.g. Getting the vaccine to everyone was a race against time.
¶ 2

Companies in Kuala Lumpur are launching durian-themed items like cakes and ice cream, while a new 2-million-dollar experience centre introduces tourists to Malaysian durian culture. Unlike Thailand, which harvests durians early and controls 81% of China’s market, Malaysia lets its fruit naturally ripen and fall from the tree. This natural method creates superior flavor, but the short shelf life of two to three days creates a difficult race against time.

吉隆坡的公司正在推出榴莲主题产品，如蛋糕和冰淇淋，而一家耗资200万美元新建的体验中心则向游客介绍马来西亚的榴莲文化。与提前采摘榴莲、占据中国市场81%份额的泰国不同，马来西亚让果实自然成熟、从树上掉落。这种自然方式造就了更出色的风味，但两到三天的短暂保鲜期却带来了一场与时间的艰难赛跑。

¶ 3

Since Malaysia only began exporting fresh durians to China in 2024, its export value quadrupled in early 2026. However, sellers note that durian is a luxury item rather than a necessity, which makes sales very sensitive to economic conditions. Thanks to social media, interest in Malaysian durian varieties is growing rapidly among Chinese consumers.

自马来西亚2024年才开始向中国出口新鲜榴莲以来，其出口额在2026年初翻了两番。然而，卖家指出，榴莲是奢侈品而非必需品，这使得销量对经济形势非常敏感。得益于社交媒体，中国消费者对马来西亚榴莲品种的兴趣正在迅速增长。

be sensitive to something 短语 对某事敏感；易受某事影响e.g. Sales are very sensitive to economic conditions.
quadruple 短语 变为四倍；翻两番e.g. Online sales quadrupled during the holiday.
¶ 4

Difficult words: ripen (to become fully grown and ready to eat), superior (better in quality, rank, or value than other things), shelf life (how long food or products stay fresh and safe to eat on a store shelf).

难词：ripen（变得完全成熟、可以食用），superior（在质量、等级或价值上优于其他事物），shelf life（食物或产品在货架上保持新鲜、可食用的时长）。
