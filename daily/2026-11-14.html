1. Pope Leo visits South America

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-13 · 原文链接
📖 教宗良十四世的南美之行在阿根廷掀起人潮：布宜诺斯艾利斯的守夜祈祷吸引了 100 万人，周一的聚会更达 250 万人，创下纪录。值得一读的是，教宗在访问中罕见地公开批评了阿根廷总统的紧缩政策，认为削减开支最伤害穷人——一场宗教之行，也成了一次对经济政策的发声。
¶ 1

Pope Leo drew a crowd of one million people at a nighttime vigil in Buenos Aires on Tuesday during his 12-day South American tour.

教宗良十四世周二在布宜诺斯艾利斯的一场夜间守夜祈祷活动中吸引了 100 万人参加，这是他为期 12 天的南美之行的一部分。

vigil /'vidʒil/ n. 警戒；监视；守夜e.g. Hundreds of people held a vigil for the victims.💡 场合：新闻报道常用词，指为悼念、祈祷或等待而彻夜守候；日常口语里说得少，更多是 stay up all night。
¶ 2

The pontiff addressed the gathered youth, urged them not to be consumed by the pursuit of wealth, physical appearance, or social media popularity, and warned against humiliating others online. This event followed an outdoor Mass in Cordoba earlier that day, which attracted 650,000 attendees, and a record-breaking gathering of 2.5 million people in Buenos Aires on Monday.

教宗向聚集的年轻人发表讲话，劝他们不要被对财富、外貌或社交媒体人气的追求所吞没，并告诫大家不要在网上羞辱他人。这场活动之前，当天早些时候在科尔多瓦举行了一场户外弥撒，吸引了 65 万人参加；而周一在布宜诺斯艾利斯的聚会更是创下纪录，达到 250 万人。

pontiff /'pɒntif/ n. 教皇；主教；大祭司e.g. The pontiff spoke to a huge crowd in the square.💡 场合：正式书面语和新闻报道用词，日常口语一般直接说 the Pope；pontiff 听起来更庄重。
consume /kәn'sju:m/ vt. （被）吞没、占据（本文义）e.g. Don't let your phone consume all your free time.💡 场合：正式和日常都常用；说时间精力被占掉时比 use up 更书面、更强调「被吃掉」的感觉。
pursuit /pә'sju:t/ n. 追踪；追求；追赶；娱乐；职业e.g. She gave up her job in pursuit of her dream.💡 场合：正式书面语常用，the pursuit of + 抽象名词（幸福、成功、梦想）是经典搭配；日常口语更常说 go after。
attendee /æten'di:/ [计] 出席人；参加者；被照顾者；被服务者e.g. Every attendee received a small gift at the meeting.💡 场合：正式场合和新闻报道常用，指会议、活动的出席者；日常口语更常说 people who came / visitors。
popularity /.pɒpju'læriti/ n. 名声；受大众欢迎；流行e.g. The young singer's popularity grew fast after the show.💡 场合：日常和新闻都常用，指人或事物受欢迎的程度；gain / enjoy / lose popularity 是常见搭配。
be consumed by 短语 被……完全占据、吞没e.g. Don't let your free time be consumed by social media.💡 场合：多用于新闻报道和正式书面语，描述人被情绪、欲望或工作完全占据；口语里也可说，但语气比 be busy with 强烈得多，带一点「失控」的意味。
the pursuit of 短语 对……的追求e.g. The pursuit of happiness takes many different forms.💡 场合：正式书面语和新闻报道常用，the pursuit of 后面常接抽象名词，如幸福、成功、梦想；日常口语更常说 go after / chase。
record-breaking 短语 破纪录的e.g. The film had a record-breaking opening weekend.💡 场合：新闻报道高频词，描述成绩、销量、气温等超过历史最高纪录；口语里也可用来形容特别出色的表现。
¶ 3

As the first pope to visit Catholic-majority Argentina since 1987, Leo seeks to revitalize the Church’s presence in the region against growing evangelical competition. Throughout his visit, the US-born pope has also voiced strong criticism of Argentine President Javier Milei’s strict austerity policies. He argued that government spending cuts disproportionately harm the country’s poorest citizens.

作为自 1987 年以来首位访问以天主教徒为主的阿根廷的教宗，良十四世希望在福音派竞争日益激烈的情况下，重振教会在该地区的影响力。在访问期间，这位出生于美国的教宗还强烈批评了阿根廷总统哈维尔·米莱的严格紧缩政策。他认为，政府削减开支对该国最贫困的民众伤害最大。

revitalize /.ri:'vaitәlaiz/ vt. 使恢复生气；使复活；使复兴e.g. The new park revitalized the whole neighbourhood.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面语高频词，用于经济、文化、社区等重振；日常口语更常说 bring back to life。
austerity /ɒ'sterәti/ n. 朴素；苦行；严格；严峻e.g. The new government introduced strict austerity measures.💡 场合：新闻报道和经济类文章高频词，指政府削减开支的政策；日常口语说节俭更常说 live simply / save money。
disproportionately adv. 不匀称；不相称e.g. The tax rise hit low-income families disproportionately hard.💡 场合：正式书面语和新闻报道常用，表示「不成比例地」；日常口语更常说 far more than their share。
evangelical /,i:væn'dʒelikәl/ a. 福音派教会的e.g. Evangelical churches have grown quickly in recent years.💡 场合：新闻报道和宗教类文章用词，指福音派（基督教派别）；日常口语中很少单独使用。
criticism /'kritisizm/ n. 批评；评论；非难e.g. She took the criticism well and rewrote her essay.💡 场合：正式和日常都常用；face criticism（受到批评）、take criticism（接受批评）是高频搭配。
voice criticism of 短语 公开批评……e.g. She voiced criticism of the new school rule at the meeting.💡 场合：多用于新闻报道和正式场合，表示公开表达批评；日常口语更常直接说 criticize。
¶ 4

Difficult words: pontiff (the Pope, who acts as the chief bishop and leader of the Roman Catholic Church), pursuit (chasing or striving to achieve something), austerity (severity in government spending cutbacks and financial discipline).

Difficult words：pontiff（教宗，即罗马天主教会的最高主教和领袖），pursuit（追求；追逐），austerity（财政紧缩；政府削减开支的严厉措施）。

humiliate /hju:'milieit/ vt. 使丢脸；使蒙羞；屈辱e.g. Never humiliate others just to make yourself look funny.💡 场合：正式和日常都常用，多指在公开场合让人难堪；比 embarrass 程度重，带有故意贬低的意味。
draw a crowd 短语 吸引大批人群e.g. The street singer drew a big crowd in the square.💡 场合：新闻报道和日常口语都常用，描述演出、活动或店铺吸引很多人；比 attract people 更形象生动。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. ____ happiness takes many different forms.
✓ the pursuit of　对……的追求
The pursuit of happiness takes many different forms.
对幸福的追求有许多不同的形式。
2. ____ churches have grown quickly in recent years.
✓ evangelical　福音派教会的
Evangelical churches have grown quickly in recent years.
近年来福音派教会发展得很快。
3. She gave up her job in ____ of her dream.
✓ pursuit　追踪；追求；追赶；娱乐；职业
She gave up her job in pursuit of her dream.
她辞了工作，去追逐自己的梦想。
4. The tax rise hit low-income families ____ hard.
✓ disproportionately　不匀称；不相称
The tax rise hit low-income families disproportionately hard.
这次加税对低收入家庭的打击格外沉重。
5. The ____ spoke to a huge crowd in the square.
✓ pontiff　教皇；主教；大祭司
The pontiff spoke to a huge crowd in the square.
教宗在广场上对着一大群人发表了讲话。
6. The new park ____ the whole neighbourhood.
✓ revitalized（revitalize 的变形）　使恢复生气；使复活；使复兴
The new park revitalized the whole neighbourhood.
这个新公园让整个社区重新焕发了活力。
7. She took the ____ well and rewrote her essay.
✓ criticism　批评；评论；非难
She took the criticism well and rewrote her essay.
她虚心接受了批评，重写了作文。
8. Don't let your phone ____ all your free time.
✓ consume　（被）吞没、占据（本文义）
Don't let your phone consume all your free time.
别让手机占掉你全部的空闲时间。
9. Every ____ received a small gift at the meeting.
✓ attendee　出席人；参加者；被照顾者；被服务者
Every attendee received a small gift at the meeting.
每位与会者都在会上收到了一份小礼物。
10. Don't let your free time ____ social media.
✓ be consumed by　被……完全占据、吞没
Don't let your free time be consumed by social media.
别让你的空闲时间被社交媒体吞掉。
11. The young singer's ____ grew fast after the show.
✓ popularity　名声；受大众欢迎；流行
The young singer's popularity grew fast after the show.
那位年轻歌手在节目播出后人气迅速上升。
12. She ____ the new school rule at the meeting.
✓ voiced criticism of（voice criticism of 的变形）　公开批评……
She voiced criticism of the new school rule at the meeting.
她在会上公开批评了这条新校规。
13. Hundreds of people held a ____ for the victims.
✓ vigil　警戒；监视；守夜
Hundreds of people held a vigil for the victims.
数百人为遇难者举行了守夜祈祷。
14. Never ____ others just to make yourself look funny.
✓ humiliate　使丢脸；使蒙羞；屈辱
Never humiliate others just to make yourself look funny.
永远不要为了让自己显得有趣就去羞辱别人。
15. The film had a ____ opening weekend.
✓ record-breaking　破纪录的
The film had a record-breaking opening weekend.
这部电影的首映周末票房破了纪录。
