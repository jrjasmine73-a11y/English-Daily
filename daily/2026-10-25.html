1. Israel’s election and Iran

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-23 · 原文链接
📖 以色列大选临近，对伊朗的安全政策成了竞选的焦点：民调显示多数选民反对停止对伊朗的军事打击，反对派也借此向内塔尼亚胡发难。读这篇可以一次学到 election、opposition 等政治新闻高频词，也能看看外交安全议题是如何左右选情的。
¶ 1

As Israel approaches its national election, security strategy toward Iran has become a central campaign issue.

随着以色列临近全国大选，对伊朗的安全战略已成为竞选的核心议题。

election /i'lekʃәn/ n. 选举；当选；选择权e.g. The election will be held next month.
strategy /'strætidʒi/ n. 战略；策略e.g. We need a better strategy to save money.
campaign /kæm'pein/ n. 战役；运动；竞选运动e.g. She joined the campaign to keep the park clean.
be open to criticism 短语 容易受到批评e.g. If you skip practice, you'll be open to criticism from your coach.
¶ 2

Public opinion polls show that a majority of Israeli voters oppose stopping military attacks on Iran. This leaves Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu open to criticism from opposition rivals who argue he failed to achieve total victory. The lead opposition candidate contends the conflict remains unfinished and highlights that Tehran keeps hundreds of kilograms of highly enriched uranium capable of producing multiple nuclear weapons. Other opposition figures support aggressive military action, including targeting Iranian energy infrastructure to collapse the regime’s economy. While the opposition shares the goal of confronting regional adversaries and expanding diplomatic alliances, critics warn that their proposed policies rely heavily on continuous military dominance. Meanwhile, opposition leaders express concern that Netanyahu might intentionally reignite hostilities with Iran before the vote to change public opinion or disrupt the upcoming election.

民意调查显示，大多数以色列选民反对停止对伊朗的军事打击。这使得总理本雅明·内塔尼亚胡容易受到反对派对手的批评，他们认为他未能取得全面胜利。领先的反对派候选人坚称冲突尚未结束，并指出德黑兰仍持有数百公斤高浓缩铀，足以制造多件核武器。其他反对派人物支持采取激进的军事行动，包括打击伊朗能源基础设施以拖垮该国经济。尽管反对派在对抗地区对手、扩大外交联盟的目标上一致，但批评者警告，他们的政策提议严重依赖持续的军事优势。与此同时，反对派领导人表示担忧，内塔尼亚胡可能在投票前故意重新点燃与伊朗的敌对行动，以改变公众舆论或干扰即将到来的选举。

majority /mә'dʒɒriti/ n. 多数；大半e.g. The majority of students like online classes.
oppose /ә'pәuz/ vt. 反对；以...对抗；抗争e.g. Many parents oppose giving kids too much homework.
criticism /'kritisizm/ n. 批评；评论；非难e.g. He took the criticism calmly and tried to improve.
rival /'raivl/ n. 对手；竞争者e.g. The two shops are old rivals on the same street.
enriched /in'ritʃt/ a. 浓缩的（文中指铀浓缩）e.g. This milk is enriched with vitamins.
infrastructure /'infrәstrʌktʃә/ n. 基础结构；基础设施e.g. The city is fixing its old roads and bridges, part of its infrastructure.
diplomatic /.diplә'mætik/ a. 外交的；老练的e.g. They solved the problem through diplomatic talks.
hostilities n. 战争行动；战争e.g. Both sides agreed to end hostilities and talk peace.
achieve total victory 短语 取得全面胜利e.g. Our team worked hard to achieve total victory in the final match.
rely heavily on 短语 严重依赖e.g. She relies heavily on her notes when she gives a speech.
expand diplomatic alliances 短语 扩大外交联盟e.g. The country hopes to expand diplomatic alliances with its neighbors.
¶ 3

Difficult words: contend (to assert something strongly in an argument), adversary (an opponent or enemy in a conflict), reignite (to restart a conflict).

生词：contend（在争论中坚定地主张）、adversary（冲突中的对手或敌人）、reignite（重新点燃冲突）。

contend /kәn'tend/ vi. 奋斗；斗争；竞争e.g. She contends that hard work matters more than luck.

2. How Good Journalism Fights Bad Science

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-09-03 · 原文链接
📖 好新闻能让科学更干净：一项新研究发现，调查学术不端的科学记者正在推动科研标准提高。文章既讲了他们面临的政治干预和人身威胁，也讲了他们保护公众信任的办法。值得读：既能学到 journalism、fraud 等高频词，也能想想新闻监督到底有什么用。
¶ 1

When journalists do their jobs well, they play a very important role in society, shining light on things the public needs to know.

当记者把本职工作做好时，他们在社会中扮演着非常重要的角色，让公众了解那些需要知道的事情。

journalist /'dʒә:nәlist/ n. 新闻记者；从事新闻杂志业的人e.g. She works as a journalist for a local newspaper.
¶ 2

In addition to exposing things like government and corporate wrongdoing, journalists can even support higher standards in scientific research by reporting on serious issues like malpractice, fraud and business interests manipulating results.

除了揭露政府和企业的不当行为，记者还可以通过报道学术不端、欺诈以及商业利益操纵研究结果等严重问题，来推动科研达到更高的标准。

wrongdoing /'rɒŋ'du:iŋ/ n. 干坏事；坏事e.g. The article exposed wrongdoing in a big company.
fraud /frɒ:d/ n. 欺骗；欺诈；诡计；骗子e.g. He lost his savings to an online fraud.
standards n. 标准；规格（standard的复数）e.g. The school sets high standards for homework.
malpractice /mæl'præktis/ n. 不当治疗；不端行为；渎职e.g. The hospital was blamed for medical malpractice.
in addition to 短语 除了……之外e.g. In addition to English, she can speak a little French.
result in 短语 导致e.g. Careless driving can result in serious accidents.
¶ 3

This was the focus of a study published in Media and Communication that looked at watchdog science journalism — investigative journalism that focuses on scientists and scientific institutions.

这正是一项发表在《媒体与传播》上的研究的主题，该研究关注的是“监督型科学新闻”——即针对科学家和科研机构的调查性新闻报道。

¶ 4

Researchers from the Autonomous University of Barcelona spoke to 15 science journalists from Spain, Portugal and Latin America, all of whom had investigated high-profile cases of scientific malpractice.

来自巴塞罗那自治大学的研究人员采访了15名来自西班牙、葡萄牙和拉丁美洲的科学记者，他们都调查过备受关注的学术不端案件。

¶ 5

These cases included harassment in research centers in Argentina; Saudi universities trying to pay their way into official affiliations with institutions in other countries; and tobacco and pharmaceutical companies interfering with scientific publications in Latin America.

这些案件包括阿根廷研究中心的骚扰事件；沙特大学试图花钱换取与别国机构的正式合作关系；以及烟草公司和制药公司干预拉丁美洲的科学出版物。

harassment /'hærәsmәnt/ n. 困扰；烦扰；烦恼e.g. The school has zero tolerance for harassment.
¶ 6

The interviews with the journalists revealed the challenges of investigating scientific wrongdoing. In addition to things like mental exhaustion and working with limited resources — which are perhaps common to all journalists — the study also noted that watchdog science journalists face political interference with their work, as well as legal and even physical threats.

对记者的采访揭示了调查学术不端所面临的挑战。除了精神疲惫、资源有限这些可能所有记者都会遇到的问题，研究还指出，监督型科学记者还面临政治干预，以及法律甚至人身威胁。

threats /θrets/ n. 威胁；恐吓( threat的名词复数 ); 可能造成威胁的人[事；想法]; 恶兆e.g. The hikers ignored the threats of bad weather.
exhaustion /ig'zɒ:stʃәn/ n. 抽空；耗尽；疲惫；筋疲力尽e.g. After the long trip, she felt total exhaustion.
¶ 7

The journalists also spoke about how they balance critical reporting on poor scientific practices with trying not to erode public trust in science as a whole.

记者们还谈到，他们如何在严厉报道不良科研行为的同时，尽量不损害公众对科学整体的信任。

critical /'kritikәl/ a. 批评的；决定性的；危险的；临界的e.g. She gave a critical review of the new movie.
erode /i'rәud/ vt. 腐蚀；侵蚀e.g. Lies can slowly erode trust between friends.
¶ 8

Their strategies included drawing clear distinctions between scientists who were doing wrong and those who weren't, such as getting respected scholars to offer comments on their investigations. Another approach was to feature positive scientific reporting along with critical investigations, so not everything audiences read about scientists was negative.

他们的策略包括明确区分做错事的科学家和没有做错事的科学家，比如请受人尊敬的学者对调查发表评论。另一个办法是在批评性调查之外同时报道正面的科研新闻，这样读者看到的关于科学家的内容就不全是负面的。

¶ 9

Also, some journalists made a point of explaining research methods and processes instead of just results, so their audience could understand exactly why certain results were not as clear as might be claimed.

此外，一些记者特意讲解研究方法和过程，而不只是公布结果，这样读者就能明白为什么某些结果并不像宣称的那么确凿。

¶ 10

The study also noted that watchdog science journalism had had real effects, resulting in consequences like resignations and new regulations — and this accountability may play a role in restoring public faith in both science and journalists at a time when both are often doubted.

研究还指出，监督型科学新闻确实产生了实际效果，带来了辞职、新规等后果——而这种问责作用可能有助于恢复公众对科学和记者的信心，在这个两者都常常受到质疑的时代尤其如此。

accountability /ә.kauntә'biliti/ n. 负有责任；可说明性e.g. Good leaders accept accountability for mistakes.
regulations n. 章则；条例；规程（regulation的复数）e.g. New regulations protect the river from pollution.
shine light on 短语 揭露；让……大白于天下e.g. This documentary shines light on life in small villages.
play a role in 短语 在……中起作用e.g. Good sleep plays a role in staying healthy.
make a point of doing 短语 特意做某事e.g. He makes a point of calling his mother every Sunday.
