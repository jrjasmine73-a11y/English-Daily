1. A lab worker dies in Russia

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-12 · 原文链接
📖 俄罗斯西伯利亚伊尔库茨克一家研究所传出一名实验室工作人员疑似死于肺鼠疫的消息，在俄欧两地再度引发对大流行的担忧。关键信息：俄卫生部门称死因未确认、未启动紧急措施，但当地居民质疑存在实验室泄漏或官方掩盖。值得一读：短讯浓缩了公共卫生新闻的典型措辞（unconfirmed、cover-up、contained），适合学习如何用英语谈论疫情类事件。
¶ 1

Reports of a lab worker dying from suspected pneumonic plague at a research institute in Irkutsk, Siberia, have raised fresh pandemic fears across Russia and Europe.

据报道，西伯利亚伊尔库茨克一家研究所的一名实验室工作人员疑似死于肺鼠疫，这一消息在俄罗斯和欧洲再度引发了人们对大流行的担忧。

raise (fresh) fears 短语 引发（新的）担忧e.g. Reports of the death raised fresh pandemic fears across Russia and Europe.
a contained incident 短语 一起受控的事件e.g. If no secondary cases appear, this is likely a small, contained incident.
¶ 2

Russian health authorities claim the cause of death is still unconfirmed and insist no special emergency measures have been introduced. However, residents remain divided; while some trust official reports and point out that contacts were quickly isolated, others suspect a laboratory leak or a government cover-up. International experts note that while the plague sounds like a terrifying medieval disease, hundreds of cases occur naturally each year in places like Madagascar and the Democratic Republic of Congo.

俄罗斯卫生部门称死因尚未确认，并坚称没有采取任何特殊的紧急措施。但当地居民看法不一：有人相信官方通报，指出密切接触者已被迅速隔离；也有人怀疑是实验室泄漏或政府掩盖真相。国际专家指出，尽管鼠疫听起来像可怕的中世纪疾病，但马达加斯加、刚果民主共和国等地每年仍会出现数百例自然发生的病例。

remain divided 短语 意见不一、持不同看法e.g. Residents remain divided over whether the official report can be trusted.
point out (that...) 短语 指出（……）e.g. Officials pointed out that all close contacts had been quickly isolated.
¶ 3

Virologists emphasize that because the incident happened inside a laboratory, heightened public worry is understandable, especially after the COVID-19 pandemic. Still, global health organizations suggest that if no secondary cases appear in the coming days, the situation is likely a small, contained incident rather than a widespread health threat.

病毒学家强调，由于事件发生在实验室内部，公众的高度担忧可以理解，尤其是在新冠疫情之后。不过全球卫生组织表示，如果未来几天没有出现继发病例，这很可能只是一起范围有限的小事件，而非广泛的公共卫生威胁。

¶ 4

Difficult words: cover-up (an attempt to hide a mistake, crime, or dangerous truth from the public), medieval (connected to the period of European history between 500 and 1500 AD), secondary (happening as a result of a main or first event), contained (kept within fixed limits and prevented from spreading).

生词：cover-up（掩盖，指试图向公众隐瞒错误、罪行或危险真相的行为）；medieval（中世纪的，指公元 500 年至 1500 年欧洲历史时期相关的）；secondary（继发的，由主要或首发事件引起的）；contained（受控的，被限制在固定范围内、阻止扩散的）。

2. Can apps teach you a language?

BBC Learning English · 6 Minute English · 2026-10-13 · 原文链接
📖 BBC《6 分钟英语》本期探讨“手机应用能教会你一门语言吗”。关键信息：一项研究发现，用多邻国学英语的学生在笔试语法上进步更大，而上课的学生在听力上进步更多——最佳方案是两者结合。
¶ 1

NeilHello and welcome to 6 Minute English from BBC Learning English. I'm Neil.

尼尔：你好，欢迎收听 BBC Learning English 的《6 分钟英语》。我是尼尔。

¶ 2

PippaAnd I'm Pippa. Apps like Duolingo, Babbel and Busuu all aim to help you learn a language, but do they really help? We'll be trying to answer that question in today's episode of 6 Minute English, and as usual we'll be learning some useful new vocabulary too.

皮帕：我是皮帕。像多邻国、Babbel 和 Busuu 这样的语言学习应用都号称能帮你学会一门语言，但它们真的管用吗？在今天的《6 分钟英语》里，我们就来探讨这个问题，照例也会学到一些实用的新词汇。

be surrounded by 短语 被……包围e.g. Children learn their first language by being constantly surrounded by it.
¶ 3

NeilYes, and don't forget to head to our website for a transcript of this episode and a free worksheet to test what you've learnt. It's all at bbclearningenglish.com.

尼尔：没错。别忘了到我们的网站 bbclearningenglish.com 获取本期节目的文字稿和免费练习题，检验你的学习成果。

¶ 4

PippaOK. Neil, let's start with a quiz question. Which of these is not a language you can learn using Duolingo? Is it: a)    Latin,

皮帕：好的，尼尔，我们先来一道小测验。以下哪种语言是你在多邻国上学不到的？a）拉丁语；

¶ 5

b)    Klingon, orc)    Elvish?

b）克林贡语；c）精灵语？

¶ 6

NeilI'm going to say c) Elvish.

尼尔：我选 c）精灵语。

¶ 7

PippaWell, we'll find out at the end of the programme. Now, people might be familiar with the app Duolingo. And then there are other apps which say they can help you learn a language quickly – Bussuu and Babbel, for example.

皮帕：嗯，节目最后揭晓答案。现在大家可能都熟悉多邻国这款应用，还有其他一些号称能帮你快速学会一门语言的应用——比如 Bussuu 和 Babbel。

be familiar with 短语 熟悉、了解e.g. People might be familiar with the app Duolingo.
¶ 8

NeilAnd listeners might be familiar with how they tend to work. You get short lessons which test you on a repeated set of words.

尼尔：听众们也许熟悉它们大致的运行方式：你会得到一些短课程，用一组反复出现的单词来考你。

get exposed to 短语 接触到（某事物）e.g. A child gets constantly exposed to this world of language.
¶ 9

PippaRight. And many of these apps say they focus on teaching language for situations and implicit learning, rather than directly teaching language rules such as grammar. If something is implicit, it's suggested but not communicated directly.

皮帕：没错。许多这类应用声称自己注重“情境语言教学”和“隐性学习”，而不是直接讲授语法这样的语言规则。implicit 指的是“含蓄的、间接的”，即被暗示出来但没有直接明说的。

¶ 10

NeilYes. So, if you think about how children learn to speak their first language, it's mostly implicit – they don't have lessons. But can this work for adults?

尼尔：是的。想想孩子们是怎么学会母语的——基本是隐性学习，他们根本不上课。但这种方法对成年人也管用吗？

¶ 11

PippaBBC Radio 4 programme Sliced Bread has been investigating the evidence behind language learning apps. Presenter Greg Foot spoke to language learning expert Professor Elizabeth Wonnacott, or Liz, about implicit learning.

皮帕：BBC Radio 4 的节目《Sliced Bread》一直在调查语言学习应用背后的科学依据。主持人 Greg Foot 就“隐性学习”采访了语言学习专家伊丽莎白·沃纳科特教授，也就是莉兹。

¶ 12

Professor Elizabeth WonnacottThere is a very big difference between being a child who gets constantly exposed to this world of language, compared with trying to learn a language a few hours a week. There is quite a bit of evidence that some explicit teaching of, for example, grammar and vocabulary is helpful.

沃纳科特教授：孩子持续沉浸在语言世界里，和每周只学几个小时的外语，差别非常大。有相当多的证据表明，适当做一些显性教学，比如直接讲语法和词汇，是有帮助的。

¶ 13

Greg FootGot you. So, it would be helpful to try to mimic what children do, which is full immersion, but realistically you're not going to get to those levels.

格雷格：明白了。所以模仿孩子那种“完全沉浸”的方式会很有帮助，但现实中你很难达到那种程度。

¶ 14

NeilLiz says that while children learn their first language implicitly by being constantly surrounded by the language, adults are often learning for only a few hours a week.

尼尔：莉兹说，孩子因为一直被语言包围，能以隐性方式学会母语，而成年人通常每周只学几个小时。

¶ 15

PippaYes. So, for adults trying to learn a new language, research has shown that some explicit teaching is helpful. Explicit is the opposite of implicit. So, for example, explicit grammar teaching would be somebody explaining a grammar rule to you.

皮帕：是的。所以对于学外语的成年人来说，研究表明适当的显性教学是有帮助的。explicit 是 implicit 的反义词，比如有人直接给你讲一条语法规则，就是显性教学。

¶ 16

NeilRight. So, adults can't realistically achieve full immersion in a language like children do. Immersion means being completely surrounded by something.

尼尔：没错。成年人不可能像孩子那样实现语言的完全沉浸。immersion 就是“沉浸”，指被某样东西彻底包围的状态。

¶ 17

PippaOK. So, a mixture of the implicit teaching of the apps and some explicit teaching – for example, learning grammar rules – is useful. But Neil, there's limited research about just how effective these apps can be at teaching you a language.

皮帕：好。所以，应用提供的隐性教学加上一些显性教学（比如学语法规则），这样的组合是管用的。不过尼尔，关于这些应用到底有多有效，目前的研究还很有限。

¶ 18

NeilIndeed, Liz Wonnacott has seen one study she thinks could be interesting though, which compares two groups of Spanish speakers learning English – one using an app and one attending classes – for a similar amount of time. She explains more to BBC Radio 4 programme Sliced Bread.

尼尔：不过，莉兹·沃纳科特看到了一项她觉得很有意思的研究：两组学英语的西班牙人学习时长相当，一组用 App，一组上课。她在《Sliced Bread》节目中详细讲了这项研究。

¶ 19

Professor Elizabeth WonnacottThe key finding was that in some of the tests, written grammar tests, the Duolingo learners actually improved a bit more, while actually in a test where they were looking at listening the classroom learners appeared to improve more. So, it's really promising, but of course it's one particular language, in one particular app, against one particular school.

沃纳科特教授：关键发现是，在笔试语法类测试中，用多邻国的学习者进步反而更大一些；而在听力测试中，上课那组进步更多。所以这项研究很有希望，但毕竟只涉及一种语言、一个应用和一所学校。

key finding 短语 关键发现、主要结论e.g. The key finding was that app learners improved more on written grammar tests.
¶ 20

PippaLiz says the key finding from the research is that the app users improved more on written tests, but the classroom learners improved more on listening tests. A key finding is the most important conclusion of a piece of research.

皮帕：莉兹说，这项研究的关键发现是：App 用户在笔试中进步更大，课堂学习者在听力中进步更多。key finding 指的是一项研究最重要的结论。

¶ 21

NeilLiz says the study is promising, which means it shows signs of being positive.

尼尔：莉兹说这项研究很有希望（promising），意思是它显示出积极的迹象。

¶ 22

PippaYes. However, Liz points out the limits of the study. It's only looking at one particular language, one particular app and one particular language school. The repetition of one particular here means specific or individual, and Liz would like to see research on more apps, more types of teaching and also more different languages.

皮帕：是的。不过莉兹也指出了这项研究的局限：它只考察了一种特定的语言、一个特定的应用和一所特定的语言学校。这里反复出现的 one particular 意为“特定的、个别的”，莉兹希望未来能看到覆盖更多应用、更多教学方式、更多语言的研究。

point out 短语 指出e.g. Liz points out the limits of the study.
¶ 23

NeilApps can help us learn some parts of a language, but in this study they weren't as successful at improving listening skills, and so Liz concludes that combining learning in apps with some other ways of studying is the best way to try to improve your language skills.

尼尔：应用能帮我们学好语言的某些方面，但这项研究中它们在提高听力方面效果没那么好，因此莉兹的结论是：把 App 学习和其他学习方式结合起来，是提高语言能力最好的办法。

¶ 24

Professor Elizabeth WonnacottProbably moving forwards, some combination of human teaching and these platforms to give us that repetition is probably going to be the right direction.

沃纳科特教授：往前看，真人教学和这些平台相结合——由平台提供重复练习——大概是正确的方向。

¶ 25

NeilAs teachers, we'd also suggest doing things that help you improve different skills – speaking, listening, reading and writing. So, listening to podcasts or trying to practise speaking with a language exchange partner could be things to combine with learning in apps. Find what works for you.

尼尔：作为老师，我们还建议大家做些能锻炼不同技能的事——说、听、读、写。比如听播客，或者找语伴练口语，都可以和 App 学习搭配使用。找到适合自己的方法最重要。

¶ 26

PippaI think it's time to find out the answer to the quiz question, Neil. I asked you which language you can't learn on Duolingo... and you were right. It's c) Elvish, but you can learn Latin and Klingon, the fictional language from the TV series Star Trek, on Duolingo. And if you did want to improve your Elvish, we did find an app specifically for that.

皮帕：尼尔，揭晓测验答案的时候到了。我问的是哪种语言在多邻国上学不到……你答对了，是 c）精灵语！不过在多邻国上可以学拉丁语和克林贡语——克林贡语是电视剧《星际迷航》里虚构的语言。如果你真想练精灵语，我们还找到了一款专门学精灵语的应用呢。

¶ 27

NeilRight. Let's recap the vocabulary we learned in this episode. Implicit means suggested but not communicated directly.

尼尔：好，我们来回顾本期学到的词汇。implicit 意为“含蓄的”，即被暗示出来但没有直接明说。

¶ 28

PippaExplicit is the opposite of implicit. It means something is communicated directly.

皮帕：explicit 是 implicit 的反义词，指“直接明说的”。

¶ 29

NeilImmersion is the state of being fully surrounded by something.

尼尔：immersion 是“沉浸”，指被某样东西完全包围的状态。

¶ 30

PippaThe key finding of a research study is the most important conclusion of the research.

皮帕：一项研究的 key finding 就是它的最重要结论。

¶ 31

NeilAnd the phrase one particular means specific or individual.

尼尔：短语 one particular 意为“特定的、个别的”。

¶ 32

PippaOnce again, our six minutes are up. Test what you've learned with the worksheet on our website.

皮帕：六分钟又到啦。到我们网站上做练习题，检验一下学到了什么。

¶ 33

NeilAnd if you're interested to hear the full episode of Sliced Bread about language-learning apps and the science behind them, there's a link in the notes below this episode. Try listening on 0.5 speed or in short sections if you find it too difficult to keep up.

尼尔：如果你想听《Sliced Bread》关于语言学习应用及其科学依据的完整节目，本期节目下方的备注里有链接。如果跟不上，可以试试 0.5 倍速，或者分小段听。

皮帕：下期见！
