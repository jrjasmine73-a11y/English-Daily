1. Hackers attack Ukrainian and Russian emergency apps

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-06 · 原文链接
📖 乌克兰和俄罗斯的官方应急警报应用近日双双遭到大规模网络攻击，虚假警报引发民众恐慌。文章揭示了数字破坏如何成为现代战争中打击士气、制造混乱的武器，值得一读，了解手机警报应用在战时扮演的关键角色。
¶ 1

Both Ukraine and Russia recently reported that their official emergency alert apps were hit by major cyberattacks, which triggered mass panic through fake notifications.

乌克兰和俄罗斯近日都报告称，各自的官方应急警报应用遭到了大规模网络攻击，虚假通知引发了大规模恐慌。

emergency /i'mә:dʒәnsi/ n. 紧急状况；紧急事件；紧急需要e.g. Call the emergency number if someone is badly hurt.
alert /ә'lә:t/ a. 警觉的；灵敏的；留心的e.g. My phone sent me an alert about heavy rain.
trigger /'trigә/ n. 触发器；扳机e.g. His careless words triggered a big argument.
panic /'pænik/ n. 恐慌；惊慌e.g. Don't panic — help is on the way.
¶ 2

In Kyiv, citizens received terrifying false warnings about nuclear radiation, chemical threats, and active street fighting, and they were told to stay inside. Ukrainian officials quickly blamed Russian hackers and reassured the public that the actual air defense systems were never compromised. Just hours later, Russia’s Ministry of Emergency Situations reported a similar cyberattack on its own app, which sent out fake alerts with Ukrainian phrasing and offensive language. These attacks target popular mobile apps that millions rely on daily as a backup to air raid sirens.

在基辅，市民收到了关于核辐射、化学威胁和街头激战的恐怖虚假警告，并被告知待在室内不要外出。乌克兰官员迅速指责俄罗斯黑客，并向公众保证，实际的防空系统从未被攻破。仅仅几小时后，俄罗斯紧急情况部也报告称，其应用遭到了类似的网络攻击，攻击者发出了带有乌克兰语措辞和侮辱性语言的虚假警报。这些攻击针对的是数百万人每天依赖的热门手机应用，它们是防空警报的备用手段。

cyberattack e.g. The company lost a lot of data in a cyberattack last year.
terrifying /'tәrifaiiŋ/ a. 令人恐惧的；骇人的；极大的e.g. The movie was so terrifying that I couldn't sleep.
radiation /.reidi'eiʃәn/ n. 辐射e.g. Doctors say the radiation from phones is very low.
chemical /'kemikl/ n. 化学药品e.g. Please keep chemical cleaners away from children.
reassure /.ri:ә'ʃuә/ vt. 使...安心；向...再保证e.g. The teacher reassured the worried parents.
compromise /'kɒmprәmaiz/ n. 危及，损害（安全）；攻破e.g. One small mistake can compromise the whole plan.
blame for 短语 把……归咎于e.g. Don't blame me for the broken vase — it wasn't my fault.
rely on 短语 依赖，依靠e.g. Many students rely on their phones to check the time.
¶ 3

Experts point out that both countries have advanced hacking teams, and digital sabotage is a key weapon for wearing down morale, creating chaos, and making ordinary people doubt official safety warnings on both sides of the war.

专家指出，两国都拥有先进的黑客团队，数字破坏是削弱士气、制造混乱、让战争双方的普通民众怀疑官方安全警告的关键武器。

sabotage /'sæbәtɑ:ʒ/ n. 怠工；破坏活动；破坏e.g. Someone tried to sabotage the school play by hiding the costumes.
morale /mɒ'rɑ:l/ n. 士气；道德e.g. A small win can lift the whole team's morale.
wear down 短语 逐渐削弱；耗尽（体力/士气）e.g. The long walk in the hot sun wore us down.
point out 短语 指出e.g. She pointed out a mistake in my homework.
¶ 4

Difficult words: compromised (weakened, damaged, or made vulnerable to unauthorized access or security failure), air raid siren (a loud device used to warn the public of an incoming air attack or missile threat), sabotage (destroying or damaging something on purpose to stop it from working), morale (the confidence, enthusiasm, and discipline of a person or group at a particular time).

生词：compromised（被削弱、被破坏，或因未经授权的访问或安全故障而变得脆弱），air raid siren（防空警报器：一种用于警告公众空袭或导弹来袭的大声装置），sabotage（蓄意破坏或损坏某物以使其停止运转），morale（士气：某个人或群体在特定时期的信心、热情和纪律）。

be hit by 短语 遭到……袭击；受到……打击e.g. The small town was hit by a heavy storm last night.

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. The small town ____ a heavy storm last night.
✓ was hit by（be hit by 的变形）　遭到……袭击；受到……打击
The small town was hit by a heavy storm last night.
昨晚那座小镇遭到了一场暴风雨的袭击。
2. The teacher ____ the worried parents.
✓ reassured（reassure 的变形）　使...安心；向...再保证
The teacher reassured the worried parents.
老师安抚了焦虑的家长们。
3. Doctors say the ____ from phones is very low.
✓ radiation　辐射
Doctors say the radiation from phones is very low.
医生说手机的辐射非常低。
4. The company lost a lot of data in a ____ last year.
✓ cyberattack　
The company lost a lot of data in a cyberattack last year.
去年那家公司在一场网络攻击中丢失了大量数据。
5. A small win can lift the whole team's ____.
✓ morale　士气；道德
A small win can lift the whole team's morale.
一场小胜利能提振整个团队的士气。
6. Many students ____ their phones to check the time.
✓ rely on　依赖，依靠
Many students rely on their phones to check the time.
许多学生靠手机看时间。
7. She ____ a mistake in my homework.
✓ pointed out（point out 的变形）　指出
She pointed out a mistake in my homework.
她指出了我作业里的一个错误。
8. Call the ____ number if someone is badly hurt.
✓ emergency　紧急状况；紧急事件；紧急需要
Call the emergency number if someone is badly hurt.
如果有人受重伤，请拨打急救电话。
9. Someone tried to ____ the school play by hiding the costumes.
✓ sabotage　怠工；破坏活动；破坏
Someone tried to sabotage the school play by hiding the costumes.
有人想通过藏起演出服来破坏学校的演出。
10. One small mistake can ____ the whole plan.
✓ compromise　危及，损害（安全）；攻破
One small mistake can compromise the whole plan.
一个小失误就可能让整个计划功亏一篑。
11. Please keep ____ cleaners away from children.
✓ chemical　化学药品
Please keep chemical cleaners away from children.
请把化学清洁剂放在孩子够不到的地方。
12. The movie was so ____ that I couldn't sleep.
✓ terrifying　令人恐惧的；骇人的；极大的
The movie was so terrifying that I couldn't sleep.
那部电影太吓人了，我睡不着觉。
13. Don't ____ — help is on the way.
✓ panic　恐慌；惊慌
Don't panic — help is on the way.
别慌——救援马上就到。
14. My phone sent me an ____ about heavy rain.
✓ alert　警觉的；灵敏的；留心的
My phone sent me an alert about heavy rain.
我的手机给我发了一条暴雨预警。
15. His careless words ____ a big argument.
✓ triggered（trigger 的变形）　触发器；扳机
His careless words triggered a big argument.
他的话引发了一场大争吵。
