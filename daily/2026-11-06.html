1. Influencers in Mexico work for drug groups

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-03 · 原文链接
📖 墨西哥毒品战争蔓延到了社交媒体：敌对贩毒集团把网红当成炫耀实力、招募年轻人的工具，至少已有19名网红因此丧生。文章揭示了网红经济与有组织犯罪之间危险的交集，值得一读，了解社交平台光鲜背后的阴暗面。
¶ 1

Ever since the Sinaloa Cartel split into rival groups in 2024, social media has turned into a dangerous battleground for Mexico’s drug wars.

自2024年锡那罗亚贩毒集团分裂成敌对派系以来，社交媒体已成为墨西哥毒品战争的危险战场。

battleground /'bætlgraund/ n. 战场；战地e.g. The small town became a battleground during the election.💡 场合：多用于新闻报道，写冲突或竞争最激烈的领域；也可指选举必争之地这类比喻说法
rival /'raivl/ n. 对手；竞争者e.g. The two rival teams will meet in the final on Sunday.💡 场合：新闻和体育报道高频；也常作形容词，如 rival groups 敌对团体、rival fans 对家球迷
turn into 短语 变成，演变为e.g. A small argument can quickly turn into a big fight.💡 场合：日常口语超高频，变成、转变为，后面直接接名词
¶ 2

The competing factions regularly target popular content creators, and use them to show off power, post propaganda, and recruit young people. Local influencers who adopt narco culture for clicks often get caught in the middle and put themselves directly in the sights of rival gangs. On top of that, federal agents are looking into dozens of creators accused of helping cartels clean dirty money through online shops, brand deals, and live donations.

这些相互竞争的派系经常针对热门内容创作者，利用他们炫耀实力、发布宣传内容、招募年轻人。那些为了点击量而追捧毒枭文化的本地网红，常常被卷入其中，直接成为敌对帮派的目标。除此之外，联邦探员正在调查数十名创作者，他们被指控通过网店、品牌合作和直播打赏帮助贩毒集团洗钱。

target /'tɑ:git/ n. 目标；靶子；指标e.g. Scammers often target old people who live alone.💡 场合：作动词是新闻高频词，把……作为攻击或针对的目标；日常也可指广告、销售瞄准某类人群
propaganda /.prɒpә'gændә/ n. 宣传；宣传活动e.g. We should learn to tell facts from propaganda.💡 场合：政治新闻用词，带贬义，指官方为操纵舆论做的宣传；普通推广别用它，用 promotion
recruit /ri'kru:t/ n. 新兵；新手；新会员；补给品e.g. The club is trying to recruit new members this month.💡 场合：招募新成员时用，军队、企业、组织都可；比 hire 更强调主动招揽
adopt /ә'dɒpt/ vt. 追捧，接受（某种文化）e.g. Many families choose to adopt a pet from the shelter.💡 场合：除了领养孩子、宠物，还常指接受新观念、新做法或新潮流，如 adopt a new method；聊生活方式时很常见
creator /kri:'eitә(r)/ n. 上帝；创造者；创作者e.g. The young creator shares cooking videos every week.💡 场合：社交媒体语境的固定说法，content creator 就是内容创作者、网红；比 influencer 更中性
show off 短语 炫耀，卖弄e.g. He likes to show off his new phone in front of his friends.💡 场合：口语常用，带轻微贬义，指炫耀、显摆；炫耀的人叫 show-off
get caught in the middle 短语 被卷入其中，夹在中间e.g. I don't want to get caught in the middle of their argument.💡 场合：被卷入两方争斗、夹在中间左右为难时用，口语和新闻里都常见
on top of that 短语 除此之外，更糟的是e.g. The hotel is expensive. On top of that, the food is terrible.💡 场合：追加信息时用，多用于再补一条坏消息，语气偏负面，类似雪上加霜
¶ 3

This deadly connection has already cost the lives of at least 19 influencers across the country. Weak police work, a lack of real punishment, and slow moderation by tech platforms mean cartels can keep using young creators as easy tools for cash and influence online.

这种致命的联系已经导致全国至少19名网红丧生。警方执法不力、缺乏真正的惩罚，加上科技平台审核缓慢，意味着贩毒集团可以继续把年轻创作者当作轻松赚钱和扩大影响力的工具。

deadly /'dedli/ a. 致命的；致死的e.g. Smoking can cause deadly diseases.💡 场合：新闻里指致命的、致死的；它也能作副词，如 deadly serious 表示非常认真、一点不开玩笑
punishment /'pʌniʃmәnt/ n. 处罚；刑罚；惩罚e.g. The school gave him a serious punishment for cheating.💡 场合：法律和教育话题常用；常见搭配 face punishment 面临处罚、carry a punishment 带有处罚
influence /'influәns/ n. 影响力；权力；势力e.g. Parents have a big influence on their children.💡 场合：日常和媒体都高频；作动词直接带宾语，对……有影响则说 have an influence on…
moderation /.mɒdә'reiʃәn/ n. （内容的）审核e.g. The website needs better moderation to stop hate speech.💡 场合：网络平台语境指内容审核，包括删帖、限流、封号；另一个意思是适度、节制，如 in moderation 适度地
cost the lives of 短语 夺去……的生命e.g. The earthquake cost the lives of hundreds of people.💡 场合：新闻报道句式，指事故、战争、灾害夺去……的生命，主语多是事件本身
¶ 4

Difficult words: faction (a small, organized group within a larger organization or political party), propaganda (information, ideas, or rumors deliberately spread to influence public opinion or promote a political cause), lack (not enough of something).

生词：faction（派系，指一个大型组织或政党内部的小型有组织团体），propaganda（宣传，指为影响公众舆论或宣扬某种政治主张而故意传播的信息、观点或谣言），lack（缺乏，指没有足够的东西）。

faction /'fækʃәn/ n. 小派系；内讧e.g. Different factions inside the company disagreed about the plan.💡 场合：政治和组织内部派系话题的常用词；新闻里多以复数 factions 出现，表示互相敌对的几派

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. We should learn to tell facts from ____.
✓ propaganda　宣传；宣传活动
We should learn to tell facts from propaganda.
我们要学会分辨事实和宣传。
2. Many families choose to ____ a pet from the shelter.
✓ adopt　追捧，接受（某种文化）
Many families choose to adopt a pet from the shelter.
很多家庭选择从收容所领养宠物。
3. Parents have a big ____ on their children.
✓ influence　影响力；权力；势力
Parents have a big influence on their children.
父母对孩子有很大的影响。
4. The earthquake ____ hundreds of people.
✓ cost the lives of　夺去……的生命
The earthquake cost the lives of hundreds of people.
那场地震夺去了数百人的生命。
5. The hotel is expensive. ____, the food is terrible.
✓ on top of that　除此之外，更糟的是
The hotel is expensive. On top of that, the food is terrible.
这家酒店很贵。除此之外，食物也很糟糕。
6. The club is trying to ____ new members this month.
✓ recruit　新兵；新手；新会员；补给品
The club is trying to recruit new members this month.
这个社团这个月正在招募新成员。
7. Scammers often ____ old people who live alone.
✓ target　目标；靶子；指标
Scammers often target old people who live alone.
骗子常常把独居老人作为目标。
8. The school gave him a serious ____ for cheating.
✓ punishment　处罚；刑罚；惩罚
The school gave him a serious punishment for cheating.
学校因他作弊给了他严厉的处罚。
9. Different ____ inside the company disagreed about the plan.
✓ factions（faction 的变形）　小派系；内讧
Different factions inside the company disagreed about the plan.
公司内部不同派系对这个计划意见不一。
10. The two ____ teams will meet in the final on Sunday.
✓ rival　对手；竞争者
The two rival teams will meet in the final on Sunday.
两支劲敌球队将于周日会师决赛。
11. Smoking can cause ____ diseases.
✓ deadly　致命的；致死的
Smoking can cause deadly diseases.
吸烟会导致致命的疾病。
12. He likes to ____ his new phone in front of his friends.
✓ show off　炫耀，卖弄
He likes to show off his new phone in front of his friends.
他喜欢在朋友面前炫耀自己的新手机。
13. The website needs better ____ to stop hate speech.
✓ moderation　（内容的）审核
The website needs better moderation to stop hate speech.
这个网站需要更好的内容审核来阻止仇恨言论。
14. The small town became a ____ during the election.
✓ battleground　战场；战地
The small town became a battleground during the election.
选举期间，这个小镇成了各方争夺的战场。
15. A small argument can quickly ____ a big fight.
✓ turn into　变成，演变为
A small argument can quickly turn into a big fight.
一场小争吵可能很快演变成一场大冲突。
