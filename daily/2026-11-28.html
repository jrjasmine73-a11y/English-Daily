1. Art brings people together

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-27 · 原文链接
📖 2026 文化年之际，卡塔尔、墨西哥和加拿大的艺术家跨越国界交流创作：珍珠遇上天然染料，陶瓷碰撞金属工艺，一张张老照片则讲着父女的故事。这篇短文讲艺术如何拉近不同文化的人，语言简单，适合精读。
¶ 1

As part of the 2026 Year of Culture, artists and designers from Qatar, Mexico, and Canada are collaborating across borders to share traditions, creative techniques, and personal stories.

作为 2026 文化年的一部分，来自卡塔尔、墨西哥和加拿大的艺术家与设计师正在跨越国界开展合作，分享各自的传统、创意技法和个人故事。

technique /tek'ni:k/ n. 技巧；技术；方法e.g. She learned a new cooking technique from her grandma.💡 场合：日常与正式场合都常用，谈学习、运动、艺术、手艺时高频。
across borders 短语 跨越国界；跨国e.g. Music travels across borders without a passport.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面语常用，谈文化、贸易、合作等跨国话题时很自然；日常口语里也可以说 across the border（单数）指越过某一国边境。
¶ 2

Mexican designer Fernando Laposse traveled to Doha for his exhibition, The Sower, which blends Qatari pearls with Mexican natural dyes and explores how ancient farming knowledge and seeds can help communities adapt to harsh climates. Meanwhile, Qatar-based designer Yasamin Shaikhi visited Mexico to work with local artisans. She combines traditional ceramics and metalwork with her experimental designs and discovers surprising similarities in their artistic patterns. At MIHAD, a new contemporary gallery in Doha, Canadian photographer Joanna Johnston is displaying personal photographs of Alberta’s prairies taken during a trip with her late father. She uses visual storytelling to connect with local audiences.

墨西哥设计师费尔南多·拉波塞前往多哈举办展览《播种者》，展览将卡塔尔珍珠与墨西哥天然染料融为一体，探讨古老的农耕知识与种子如何帮助社区适应严酷的气候。与此同时，常驻卡塔尔的设计师亚萨明·沙伊基前往墨西哥，与当地手工艺人合作。她把传统陶瓷与金属工艺融入自己的实验性设计，并惊讶地发现彼此的艺术图案有许多相似之处。在多哈新开的当代画廊 MIHAD，加拿大摄影师乔安娜·约翰斯顿正在展出她与已故父亲同游阿尔伯塔大草原时拍摄的个人照片。她用视觉叙事与当地观众建立联系。

exhibition /.eksi'biʃәn/ n. 表现；展览会；展览品e.g. The photo exhibition opens this weekend.💡 场合：日常与正式场合都常用，谈美术馆、学校、商场里的展览时都合适。
ancient /'einʃәnt/ a. 古代的；古老的；年老的；旧的e.g. They found an ancient coin in the garden.💡 场合：书面与口语通用，谈历史、文物、传统时高频，中性词。
harsh /hɑ:ʃ/ a. 粗糙的；刺耳的；严厉的e.g. Life in the desert is harsh.💡 场合：日常与新闻都常用，形容天气、环境、言语时都很自然，带负面色彩。
artisan /.ɑ:ti'zæn/ n. 工匠；技工e.g. The village is famous for its skilled artisans.💡 场合：多用于新闻、文化、旅游类文章，日常口语里较少，偏正式。
contemporary /kәn'tempәrәri/ n. 同时代的人e.g. She collects contemporary paintings.💡 场合：多用于新闻、艺术、学术等正式语境，日常口语里较少。
storytelling /'stɒ:riteliŋ/ n. 说故事；说谎话e.g. Mom's wonderful storytelling makes bedtime special.💡 场合：日常与新闻都可用，谈讲故事、叙事、演讲时常用，带积极色彩。
blend A with B 短语 把 A 与 B 融合；把 A 和 B 混合e.g. She blends Chinese tea culture with modern design.💡 场合：正式与日常都可用，谈艺术、饮食、音乐、文化融合时很常见，语气积极。
adapt to 短语 适应e.g. It takes time to adapt to a new school.💡 场合：日常口语和书面语都高频，谈新环境、新工作、新生活时最常用，中性词。
¶ 3

Ultimately, these cross-cultural exchanges demonstrate that art can bridge geographical divides and create lasting inspiration and friendships long after the exhibitions close.

归根结底，这些跨文化交流表明，艺术能够跨越地理上的距离，在展览落幕很久之后，依然带来持久的灵感与友谊。

demonstrate /'demәnstreit/ vt. 示范；证明e.g. The study demonstrates the power of reading.💡 场合：正式书面与新闻高频，学术、演讲中常用；日常口语多用 show 或 prove。
geographical /dʒiә'græfikl/ a. 地理学的；地理的e.g. The island's geographical position is very special.💡 场合：多用于新闻、学术、正式书面语，日常口语较少。
inspiration /.inspә'reiʃәn/ n. 灵感；鼓舞人心的人或物；妙计；吸气e.g. Travel always brings me new inspiration.💡 场合：日常与正式场合都高频，谈创作、学习、生活感悟时常用，带积极色彩。
bridge divides 短语 弥合分歧；跨越隔阂e.g. Good food can bridge divides between strangers.💡 场合：多用于新闻、演讲等正式语境，谈文化、国家、人群之间的隔阂时常用，带积极色彩。
long after 短语 在……很久之后e.g. Long after the show ended, people still talked about it.💡 场合：书面与口语通用，强调某事的影响或记忆持续很久，语气带感慨。
¶ 4

Difficult words: blend (to mix or combine different substances, styles, or ideas smoothly so they form a unified whole), contemporary (modern in style or design), ultimately (finally).

生词：blend（把不同的物质、风格或想法顺滑地混合或结合，形成一个整体），contemporary（风格或设计上现代的），ultimately（最终）。

collaborate /kә'læbәreit/ vi. 合作；勾结e.g. Artists from three countries collaborate on one show.💡 场合：正式与日常都可用，职场、学校、新闻里谈合作时很常见，语气中性偏积极。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. She learned a new cooking ____ from her grandma.
✓ technique　技巧；技术；方法
She learned a new cooking technique from her grandma.
她从奶奶那里学了一种新的烹饪技巧。
2. It takes time to ____ a new school.
✓ adapt to　适应
It takes time to adapt to a new school.
适应一所新学校需要时间。
3. The study ____ the power of reading.
✓ demonstrates（demonstrate 的变形）　示范；证明
The study demonstrates the power of reading.
这项研究证明了阅读的力量。
4. Good food can ____ between strangers.
✓ bridge divides　弥合分歧；跨越隔阂
Good food can bridge divides between strangers.
美食能拉近陌生人之间的距离。
5. ____ the show ended, people still talked about it.
✓ long after　在……很久之后
Long after the show ended, people still talked about it.
演出结束很久之后，人们还在谈论它。
6. The village is famous for its skilled ____.
✓ artisans（artisan 的变形）　工匠；技工
The village is famous for its skilled artisans.
这个村子以手艺精湛的手工艺人闻名。
7. Life in the desert is ____.
✓ harsh　粗糙的；刺耳的；严厉的
Life in the desert is harsh.
沙漠里的生活很严酷。
8. The island's ____ position is very special.
✓ geographical　地理学的；地理的
The island's geographical position is very special.
这座岛的地理位置非常特殊。
9. She collects ____ paintings.
✓ contemporary　同时代的人
She collects contemporary paintings.
她收藏当代绘画作品。
10. Travel always brings me new ____.
✓ inspiration　灵感；鼓舞人心的人或物；妙计；吸气
Travel always brings me new inspiration.
旅行总能带给我新的灵感。
11. The photo ____ opens this weekend.
✓ exhibition　表现；展览会；展览品
The photo exhibition opens this weekend.
这场摄影展本周末开幕。
12. Mom's wonderful ____ makes bedtime special.
✓ storytelling　说故事；说谎话
Mom's wonderful storytelling makes bedtime special.
妈妈精彩的讲故事让睡前时光变得特别。
13. Artists from three countries ____ on one show.
✓ collaborate　合作；勾结
Artists from three countries collaborate on one show.
三个国家的艺术家合作办了一场展览。
14. Music travels ____ without a passport.
✓ across borders　跨越国界；跨国
Music travels across borders without a passport.
音乐不需要护照就能跨越国界。
15. They found an ____ coin in the garden.
✓ ancient　古代的；古老的；年老的；旧的
They found an ancient coin in the garden.
他们在花园里发现了一枚古钱币。
