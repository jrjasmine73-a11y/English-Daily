1. Is the internet dead?

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-09 · 原文链接
📖 互联网是否正在被 AI“杀死”？这篇短文介绍了“死亡互联网理论”：机器人流量已占全网六成以上，AI 搜索直接“盗取”人类记者的摘要，人们不再点击原网站，新闻机构纷纷倒闭。它关系到你每天看到的每一条新闻是否可信，值得细读。
¶ 1

Do you ever feel like the internet is getting weirder and less useful? You are not alone. More and more people worry AI is slowly killing the web by flooding it with cheap, machine-made articles.

你是否觉得互联网越来越奇怪、越来越没用？你并不孤单。越来越多的人担心，AI 正用大量廉价的机器生成内容淹没网络，慢慢“杀死”互联网。

flood A with B 短语 用 B 淹没 Ae.g. AI is flooding the web with cheap, machine-made articles.
¶ 2

Right now, bots make up over 60% of all web traffic, and AI writes a huge chunk of new pages. It is part of the dead internet theory, the idea that real human content is vanishing. Even big tech bosses know there is a problem. Their smart AI search tools steal summaries from human writers, so people stop clicking on actual websites. That means real newsrooms lose money and go bust. Once human writers disappear, AI will start learning from its own low-quality content and create lots of mistakes and bad advice. To save the web, we need to click on original sources, check who wrote what, and support real human journalists.

目前，机器人占据了 60% 以上的全部网络流量，AI 也撰写了新增网页中的很大一部分。这就是“死亡互联网理论”的一部分——认为真实的人类内容正在消失。连科技巨头们都知道问题严重：他们的智能 AI 搜索工具从人类作者那里“偷走”摘要，人们就不再点击真实网站了。于是，真正的新闻机构失去收入、纷纷倒闭。一旦人类作者消失，AI 将开始学习自己生成的低质量内容，制造出大量错误和糟糕的建议。要拯救互联网，我们需要点击原创来源、查证每篇文章的作者、支持真正的人类记者。

make up 短语 构成；占据（比例）e.g. Bots make up over 60% of all web traffic.
steal (summaries) from human writers 短语 从人类作者那里“盗取”（摘要）e.g. AI search tools steal summaries from human writers.
go bust 短语 破产，被迫倒闭e.g. Real newsrooms lose money and go bust.
learn from its own low-quality content 短语 学习自己生成的低质量内容（自我退化）e.g. AI will start learning from its own low-quality content and create lots of mistakes.
¶ 3

Difficult words: chunk (a large piece or part of something), vanish (to disappear completely and suddenly), go bust (to lose all money and be forced to close a business).

难词：chunk（一大块、很大的一部分），vanish（完全、突然地消失），go bust（破产，被迫倒闭）。

2. Disney Animal Kingdom

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-08 · 原文链接
📖 迪士尼动物王国开园 25 年，不仅是个主题乐园，更是全球动物保护的“大本营”：它运营着世界最大的兽医院之一、每天为动物送出 1 万磅食物，迪士尼保护基金已投入 1.2 亿美元拯救野生动物。读完它，你会记住 conservation、charismatic、pollinator garden 等实用的保护主题词汇。
¶ 1

For 25 years, Disney’s Animal Kingdom has been leading worldwide conservation efforts to increase the populations of threatened animals and restore their habitats.

25 年来，迪士尼动物王国一直引领全球动物保护工作，增加受威胁动物的数量、恢复它们的栖息地。

lead conservation efforts 短语 引领保护工作e.g. The park has been leading worldwide conservation efforts for 25 years.
restore their habitats 短语 恢复它们的栖息地e.g. Conservation aims to increase animal populations and restore their habitats.
be created for 短语 专为……而建e.g. Wishing Star Lake was created for struggling bird populations.
¶ 2

With one of the world’s largest veterinarian hospitals, the park cares for all animal species, not just the big charismatic ones. The Animal Nutrition Center staff oversees 1,500 different diets and delivers 10,000 pounds of food to animals every day. The Disney Conservation Fund has directed 120 million dollars to help save wildlife. The team designs wildlife and animal habitats in the parks, such as Wishing Star Lake, created for struggling bird populations in Shanghai Disney Resort. The hope is that the threatened and endangered butterflies, including the monarch, will thrive in pollinator gardens across all the Disney properties.

公园拥有世界最大的兽医院之一，照顾所有动物物种，而不仅是那些最有“明星相”的大动物。动物营养中心的员工管理着 1500 种不同的食谱，每天为动物送去 1 万磅食物。迪士尼保护基金已投入 1.2 亿美元拯救野生动物。团队还为公园设计了野生动物栖息地，比如上海迪士尼的“许愿星湖”，就是为濒临困境的鸟类而建的。他们希望，濒危的蝴蝶（包括帝王蝶）能在迪士尼各乐园的传粉花园里茁壮成长。

direct money to 短语 把资金投入到……e.g. The Disney Conservation Fund has directed 120 million dollars to help save wildlife.
¶ 3

On Earth Day, the conservation experts encourage everyone to grow a pollinator garden to help care for their local animal kingdom.

在地球日，保护专家呼吁每个人都种一个传粉花园，为自己身边的“动物王国”尽一份力。

encourage everyone to (do sth) 短语 呼吁每个人都……e.g. Experts encourage everyone to grow a pollinator garden.
¶ 4

Difficult words: conservation (protecting earth’s resources for future generations), charismatic (likable and naturally drawing attention), pollinator garden (a garden designed to grow specific nectar and pollen-producing plants).

难词：conservation（保护地球资源，造福后代），charismatic（有魅力的、自然吸引人的），pollinator garden（专门种植产蜜产粉植物的花园，传粉花园）。
