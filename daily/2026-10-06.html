1. The Swiss vote on neutrality

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-05 · 原文链接
📖 瑞士选民近日以超过三分之二的票数否决了一项提案：该提案要求把瑞士传统的中立地位写入硬性规定，包括禁止与北约的军事合作、对交战国实施制裁也将被禁止。这次公投结果意味着选民支持政府在俄乌战争后走的务实路线——中立不等于与世隔绝。读完你能用英文聊聊“中立国”这件事，顺便学会 status quo、momentum 这几个高频表达。
¶ 1

Swiss voters have clearly rejected a proposal that wanted to enforce super strict rules on the country’s traditional neutrality.

瑞士选民以压倒性多数否决了一项提案，该提案要求对瑞士传统的中立地位实施极其严格的规定。

¶ 2

Backed by a right-wing party, the plan tried to ban military cooperation with groups like NATO and stop Switzerland from placing sanctions on nations at war. However, over two-thirds of voters said no. They prefer to give the government room to maneuver. The result really shows people are happy with the status quo rather than looking for a big political shift. Voters basically support how the government reacted after Russia invaded Ukraine. Switzerland has kept close ties with European neighbors and backed sanctions against Russia. While this result doesn’t mean a total defeat for the right-wing party, it shows their momentum has stalled. Switzerland’s defense system works for now; however, an attack on a NATO country could still force everyone to rethink Swiss neutrality in the future.

这项提案由一个右翼政党发起，试图禁止瑞士与北约等组织进行军事合作，并禁止瑞士对交战国家实施制裁。然而，超过三分之二的选民投了反对票。他们更希望给政府留下回旋的空间。这一结果清楚表明，人们更满意维持现状，而不是寻求重大的政治转变。选民基本上支持俄罗斯入侵乌克兰后政府的应对方式。瑞士一直与欧洲邻国保持密切关系，并支持对俄罗斯的制裁。虽然这一结果并不意味着右翼政党的彻底失败，但它显示出该党的上升势头已经停滞。就目前而言，瑞士的防御体系行之有效；不过，如果某个北约国家遭到攻击，所有人都可能被迫重新思考瑞士的中立地位。

status quo 短语 现状；维持不变的状态e.g. Some people just want to keep the status quo.
room to maneuver 短语 回旋/周旋的空间e.g. Voters prefer to give the government room to maneuver.
¶ 3

Difficult words: maneuver (to move or act skillfully to manage a tricky situation or get an advantage), status quo (the current state of things, without any changes), momentum (the force or speed that keeps a process or movement growing stronger over time).

重点词汇：maneuver（熟练应对棘手局面、化被动为主动的行动或策略），status quo（现状，保持不变的当前状态），momentum（势头，事物不断发展壮大的力量或速度）。

gain/lose momentum 短语 获得/失去势头e.g. The result shows the right-wing party's momentum has stalled.
be backed by 短语 由……支持/发起e.g. Backed by a right-wing party, the plan tried to ban military cooperation with NATO.

2. First Germany-Russia meeting since 2022

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-02 · 原文链接
📖 俄乌战争全面爆发四年多来，德俄两国外长首次面对面交谈——德国外长瓦德富尔与拉夫罗夫在联合国大会间隙进行了20分钟的短暂会晤。背景很微妙：德国刚刚指控俄罗斯策划了莱比锡机场的无人机袭击。这次会晤象征意义大于实际成果，值得读它来理解“外交重启”的措辞：downplay、orchestrate、on the sidelines 都是新闻英语的高频词。
¶ 1

German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul and Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov held a brief 20-minute meeting on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York.

德国外长约翰·瓦德富尔与俄罗斯外长谢尔盖·拉夫罗夫在纽约联合国大会的间隙举行了一次简短的20分钟会晤。

on the sidelines 短语 （会议等）场外、间隙e.g. They held a 20-minute meeting on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.
¶ 2

This marked the first direct diplomatic encounter between top German and Russian officials since Moscow’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine more than four years ago.

这是自俄罗斯四年前对乌克兰发动全面入侵以来，德俄高级官员之间的首次直接外交接触。

¶ 3

The meeting took place despite escalating tensions, following German accusations that Russia orchestrated a drone attack at Leipzig/Halle airport. During the talks, Wadephul urged Moscow to abandon its dangerous path of escalation toward Germany, Europe, and NATO. While Lavrov downplayed the event’s importance and claimed Germany made no position changes, diplomats noted the symbolic weight of reopening a direct channel. Both sides disputed who initiated the exchange.

尽管紧张局势不断升级——德国此前指控俄罗斯策划了莱比锡/哈雷机场的无人机袭击——这次会晤仍如期举行。会谈中，瓦德富尔敦促莫斯科放弃对德国、欧洲和北约的危险升级路线。拉夫罗夫则轻描淡写了这次会晤的重要性，声称德国没有改变任何立场；但外交官们指出，重新开通直接沟通渠道本身就具有象征意义。双方还就到底是谁先发起这次会晤各执一词。

downplay sth 短语 淡化；轻描淡写e.g. Lavrov downplayed the event's importance.
orchestrate sth 短语 精心策划（事件/阴谋）e.g. Germany accused Russia of orchestrating a drone attack at the airport.
symbolic weight 短语 象征意义/分量e.g. Their handshake carried great symbolic weight.
¶ 4

Despite Germany’s primary role as a strategic supporter of Ukraine rather than a mediator, the meeting reflects broader shifts in diplomatic discussions as international leaders search for ways toward future negotiations.

尽管德国主要是乌克兰的战略支持者而非调停者，但这次会晤反映出各国领导人正为未来的谈判寻找出路，外交讨论的格局也在发生变化。

¶ 5

Difficult words: sideline (a place or occasion outside the main event where unofficial talks happen), orchestrate (to plan and organize a complex event or secret action carefully), downplay (to make something seem less important, serious, or bad than it actually is).

重点词汇：sideline（场边/场外，在主要活动之外的非正式场合），orchestrate（精心策划和组织复杂的事件或秘密行动），downplay（淡化，使某事显得没那么重要、严重或糟糕）。

escalate tensions / a path of escalation 短语 升级紧张局势e.g. Careless words can escalate tensions quickly.
