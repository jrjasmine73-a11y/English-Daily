1. China agrees to halve hybrid car exports to EU

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-10-12 · 原文链接
📖 中国同意在未来四年内将向欧盟出口的混合动力汽车减半。这项在激烈谈判后达成的里程碑式协议，有望阻止数百万辆汽车进入欧洲市场，避免一场有害的贸易战。值得读：它把贸易谈判、汽车产业竞争和“公平贸易”这些大话题讲得很清楚，还能学到不少谈判与经贸表达。
¶ 1

China has agreed to halve its exports of hybrid cars to the European Union over the next four years. The landmark deal followed intense negotiations, as both sides sought to avoid a harmful trade war. The EU's trade commissioner, Maros Sefcovic, said the agreement could prevent millions of vehicles from entering the European market. He said: "This package proves that negotiations bring results." European carmakers have struggled to compete with cheaper Chinese imports. China's rise has raised fears of factory closures and job losses in Europe. Despite these concerns, Mr Sefcovic was pleased with the talks. He said the deal was "only a first step" towards more equitable trade relations.

中国已同意在未来四年内将向欧盟出口的混合动力汽车减半。这项具有里程碑意义的协议是在激烈的谈判之后达成的，双方都希望避免一场有害的贸易战。欧盟贸易专员马罗什·谢夫乔维奇表示，该协议可以阻止数百万辆汽车进入欧洲市场。他说：“这一揽子协议证明，谈判能够带来成果。”欧洲汽车制造商一直难以与更便宜的中国进口汽车竞争。中国的崛起引发了人们对欧洲工厂关闭和失业的担忧。尽管存在这些担忧，谢夫乔维奇先生对谈判结果感到满意。他说，这项协议只是朝着更公平的贸易关系迈出的“第一步”。

landmark /'lændmɑ:k/ n. 里程碑式的e.g. This deal is a landmark moment for both sides.
negotiation /ni.gәuʃi'eiʃәn/ n. 谈判；磋商；交涉e.g. The negotiation took three long days.
commissioner /kә'miʃәnә/ n. 委员；理事；行政长官e.g. The trade commissioner spoke to reporters.
prevent /pri'vent/ v. 预防；防止；阻止；妨碍e.g. A good umbrella can prevent you from getting wet.
struggle /'strʌgl/ n. 斗争；努力；奋斗e.g. I struggled to finish the run, but I made it.
compete /kәm'pi:t/ vi. 竞争；对抗e.g. Our team will compete with the best schools.
closure /'klәuʒә/ n. 关闭e.g. The closure of the small shop surprised everyone.
equitable /'ekwitәbl/ a. 公平的，公正的e.g. We want an equitable deal for everyone.
halve its exports 短语 将出口减半e.g. The factory halved its exports last year.
avoid a trade war 短语 避免贸易战e.g. Both leaders want to avoid a trade war.

¶ 2

China has rapidly become a major player in the global car industry. Chinese manufacturers have established a foothold in many markets worldwide. China's auto companies have become world leaders in electric and hybrid technology. Chinese cars are now very popular with motorists across the globe. Makers such as BYD, Xiaomi, and Geely offer competitively priced vehicles that are reliable and have good build quality. Consumers see these cars as more affordable alternatives to European and Japanese brands. Sales of Chinese cars in the EU now make up around 10 per cent of the new car market. In the UK, this figure has steadily risen to nearly 20 per cent.

中国已迅速成为全球汽车行业的主要参与者。中国制造商已在世界许多市场站稳脚跟。中国汽车公司已成为电动和混合动力技术领域的世界领先者。中国汽车如今深受全球驾驶者的欢迎。比亚迪、小米和吉利等厂商提供的汽车价格具有竞争力，可靠且制造质量良好。消费者认为这些汽车是欧洲和日本品牌更实惠的替代品。中国汽车在欧盟的销量目前约占新车市场的10%。在英国，这一比例已稳步上升至近20%。

manufacturer /.mænju'fæktʃәrә/ n. 制造业者；厂商e.g. The manufacturer offers a two-year warranty.
reliable /ri'laiәbl/ a. 可靠的；可信赖的e.g. My old bike is still very reliable.
affordable [计] 普及型e.g. This phone is affordable for most students.
establish a foothold in 短语 在……站稳脚跟e.g. The brand is trying to establish a foothold in Asia.

2. India’s big river project

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-16 · 原文链接
📖 印度中部村民正在抗议一项连接两条大河的大型政府工程：工程想帮数百万农民解决干旱缺水，却已淹没村庄、房屋和一座寺庙。关键信息点：环保专家警告，新大坝将破坏著名老虎保护区的森林和珍稀野生动物。值得读：发展、民生与环保的冲突就在眼前，语言简单，贴近生活。
¶ 1

Villagers in central India are protesting against a massive government project to link two major rivers in order to help millions of local farmers.

印度中部的村民正在抗议政府一项连接两条主要河流的大型工程，该工程旨在帮助数百万当地农民。

protest /prә'test/ n. 抗议；反对；抗议书；断言e.g. Many people protested against the new plan.
massive /'mæsiv/ a. 大而重的；宽大的；宏伟的e.g. The project is massive in size.
link /liŋk/ n. 环；连结物；链接；火把e.g. This road links the two towns.
protest against 短语 抗议……e.g. Villagers are protesting against a massive government project.
link two rivers 短语 把两条河连接起来e.g. The project will link two major rivers to help local farmers.
¶ 2

The plan aims to build a large dam and move water to areas facing severe drought; however, construction work has already caused rising waters to flood nearby villages, homes, and a local temple. Angry residents say the government is not giving them enough money or a new place to rebuild their lives. Some protesters have even taken extreme actions, like lying on fake funeral fires to show their anger.

该计划旨在修建一座大坝，把水引到面临严重干旱的地区；然而，施工已经导致水位上涨，淹没了附近的村庄、房屋和一座当地寺庙。愤怒的居民说，政府没有给他们足够的钱，也没有给他们新的地方重建生活。一些抗议者甚至采取了极端行动，比如躺在假的葬礼柴堆上以表达愤怒。

dam /dæm/ n. 水坝；障碍e.g. The dam holds back the river water.
drought /draut/ n. 干旱；缺乏e.g. Farmers suffer when drought lasts too long.
flood /flʌd/ n. 洪水；大量之水；涨潮e.g. Heavy rain may flood the village.
resident /'rezidәnt/ n. 居民；常驻程序；居住者；留鸟e.g. Local residents want more help.
extreme /ik'stri:m/ n. 极端；末端e.g. Lying on fake fires was an extreme action.
rebuild their lives 短语 重建生活e.g. They need a new place to rebuild their lives.
¶ 3

At the same time, environmental experts warn that the new dam will destroy large areas of forest and harm rare wildlife inside a famous tiger reserve. While government officials claim the project will solve long-term water shortages and bring power to the region, critics argue the scientific plans are flawed and urge leaders to protect both local communities and nature.

与此同时，环境专家警告说，新大坝将摧毁大片森林，并伤害著名老虎保护区内的珍稀野生动物。政府官员声称该工程将解决长期的缺水问题并为该地区带来电力，而批评者则认为科学规划存在缺陷，并敦促领导人同时保护当地社区和自然环境。

environmental /in.vaiәrәn'mentәl/ a. 周围的；环境的e.g. Environmental experts worry about the forest.
destroy /di'strɒi/ vt. 破坏；毁坏；消灭e.g. Fire can destroy a forest in hours.
rare /rєә/ a. 稀罕的；罕有的；珍奇的；稀薄的；半熟的；非常好的e.g. Pandas are rare animals.
flawed a. 有缺陷的e.g. His plan is flawed, so we need a better one.
solve long-term water shortages 短语 解决长期缺水问题e.g. Desalination plants may solve long-term water shortages in coastal cities.
¶ 4

Difficult words: rare (not common or frequent; very unusual), shortage (a situation where there is not enough of something that people need), flawed (not perfect).

生词：rare（不常见；非常罕见），shortage（短缺；人们需要的东西不够用的状况），flawed（不完美；有缺陷的）。
