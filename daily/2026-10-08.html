1. A woman still lives after an execution

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-07 · 原文链接
📖 美国田纳西州的一次失败行刑（botched execution）后，50岁的死刑犯克里斯塔·派克在经历两次致命注射后仍然活了下来，被紧急送往医院救治。她本是该州200多年来首位将被处决的女性，此事立即引发国际舆论哗然，联合国人权官员称其"残忍且令人发指"。州长已暂停今年剩余所有死刑并启动全面调查。这则新闻重燃了美国关于死刑制度与行刑药物可靠性的大辩论，时效性强，值得一读。
¶ 1

A botched execution in Tennessee has left 50-year-old death row prisoner Christa Pike alive in hospital after surviving two lethal injections.

田纳西州一次失败的行刑，让 50 岁的死刑犯克里斯塔·派克（Christa Pike）在经历两次致命注射后依然活了下来，目前她正在医院接受救治。

a botched execution 短语 一次搞砸了的行刑e.g. A botched execution in Tennessee has left 50-year-old death row prisoner Christa Pike alive in hospital.（原文）
¶ 2

Pike, who was sentenced to death in 1996 for a brutal murder, was set to become the first woman executed in the state in over 200 years. However, witnesses reported seeing her continue breathing and snoring long after the pentobarbital injections were administered. Following an emergency court motion, prison officials rushed her to hospital to receive life-saving care. The horrific incident sparked immediate international outrage and prompted UN human rights officials to call the failed execution cruel and abhorrent.

派克因 1996 年的一起残忍谋杀案被判死刑，原本将成为该州 200 多年来第一位被处决的女性。然而据目击者称，注射戊巴比妥之后很久，她仍在呼吸，甚至发出鼾声。经过紧急法院动议，监狱官员立即将她送往医院抢救。这起骇人的事件瞬间引发国际舆论哗然，联合国人权官员称这次失败的行刑"残忍且令人发指"。

be sentenced to death (for …) 短语 被判死刑（因……）e.g. Pike was sentenced to death in 1996 for a brutal murder.（原文改编）
spark (international) outrage 短语 引发（国际）愤怒 / 哗然e.g. The horrific incident sparked immediate international outrage.（原文）
cruel and abhorrent 短语 残忍且令人发指的e.g. UN human rights officials called the failed execution cruel and abhorrent.（原文改编）
¶ 3

In response, Tennessee Governor Bill Lee launched a comprehensive investigation into the procedure and issued a temporary hold on all remaining executions scheduled for this year. The controversy has reopened major public debates across the United States regarding capital punishment and the reliability of lethal execution drugs.

作为回应，田纳西州州长比尔·李（Bill Lee）对行刑程序启动了全面调查，并暂停了今年原定执行的其余所有死刑。这场争议让美国公众围绕死刑制度以及致命行刑药物的可靠性的大辩论再度升温。

issue a (temporary) hold on … 短语 对……发布（临时）暂停令e.g. The governor issued a temporary hold on all remaining executions scheduled for this year.（原文改编）
¶ 4

Difficult words: botched (done very poorly or carelessly), lethal (deadly), pentobarbital (a strong sedative drug used in medical treatments and lethal injections), abhorrent (disgusting or shocking).

难词：botched（搞砸的，做得非常糟糕或马虎的）；lethal（致命的）；pentobarbital（戊巴比妥，一种用于医疗和致命注射的强效镇静剂）；abhorrent（令人厌恶的、骇人的）。

2. Gen Z and money

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-06 · 原文链接
📖 年轻一代正集体推迟买房、组建家庭等人生大事——超过 70% 的 Z 世代把原因归于高得离谱的房价和房租，在英国，一套房的价格已达平均年薪的 12 倍。关键信息：经济学家预计 Z 世代将比父辈更穷，但年轻人并未躺平，他们学习理财、走上街头抗议，要求真正的政治变革。这篇文章直击住房、通胀、社交媒体焦虑等热点话题，与你我的生活息息相关，值得精读。
¶ 1

Younger generations are finding it harder than ever to reach classic adult milestones like buying a home or starting a family. Statistics show over 70% of Gen Z have delayed these major life goals due to sky-high house prices, high rent,

年轻一代发现，实现买房、组建家庭这些经典人生里程碑，比以往任何时候都更难。数据显示，超过 70% 的 Z 世代因高得离谱的房价、高昂的房租……而推迟了这些人生大事。

reach adult milestones 短语 实现成年的人生里程碑（买房、成家等）e.g. Younger generations are finding it harder than ever to reach classic adult milestones like buying a home.（原文）
¶ 2

In places like the UK, buying a property now costs up to twelve times the average salary, which makes home ownership feel like a distant dream. On top of that, social media constantly exposes young people to unrealistic standards of wealth, creates unnecessary pressure, and leads to poor financial decisions.

在英国这样的地方，一套房的价格已高达平均年薪的 12 倍，让拥有自己的房子仿佛遥不可及。除此之外，社交媒体不断把年轻人置于不切实际的财富标准之下，带来不必要的压力，还导致他们做出糟糕的财务决策。

feel like a distant dream 短语 感觉遥不可及e.g. …which makes home ownership feel like a distant dream.（原文）
on top of that 短语 除此之外；雪上加霜e.g. On top of that, social media constantly exposes young people to unrealistic standards of wealth.（原文）
¶ 3

Economic experts point out that Gen Z is projected to be less wealthy than their parents due to high interest rates and inflation. However, rather than giving up, young people are adapting, learning about personal finance, and taking to activism or public protests to demand real political change for their future.

经济学家指出，由于高利率和通货膨胀，Z 世代预计将比父辈更穷。但年轻人并没有放弃——他们正在适应现实，学习个人理财知识，甚至走上街头参与抗议，要求为他们的未来带来真正的政治变革。

be projected to … 短语 预计将……e.g. Gen Z is projected to be less wealthy than their parents.（原文）
¶ 4

Difficult words: milestone (an important event or stage in life or development), stagnant (not growing, changing, or developing over time), projected (expected or estimated to happen or be a certain level in the future).

难词：milestone（里程碑，人生或发展中的重要事件 / 阶段）；stagnant（停滞的，不增长、不变化的）；projected（预计的，预期会发生的）。

take to activism / public protests 短语 投身行动 / 走上街头抗议e.g. …young people are taking to activism or public protests to demand real political change.（原文改编）
