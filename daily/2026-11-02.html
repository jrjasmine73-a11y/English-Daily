1. Study finds majority of pilots nod off mid-flight

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-11-02 · 原文链接
📖 一项针对 32 个国家近 7200 名飞行员的调查发现，飞行员疲劳问题普遍存在且常被隐瞒：超过四分之三的人曾在飞行中短暂打盹，近四分之一的人即使太累仍继续飞行。这篇短文用词清晰、数据具体，适合积累"疲劳、报告、后果"一类职场安全表达。
¶ 1

A new report has raised serious concerns about pilot fatigue. The European Cockpit Association (ECA) conducted a survey of nearly 7,200 pilots across 32 countries. It discovered that exhaustion in the cockpit is a widespread and underreported problem. More than three-quarters of respondents said they had briefly dozed off while operating an aircraft in the month before the survey. Almost a quarter of them admitted they continued flying even when they were too tired. Furthermore, half said they had operated a flight when they should have declared themselves unfit for duty. Tiredness can result from long working hours, insufficient rest, and unpredictable schedules.

一份新报告引发了人们对飞行员疲劳的严重担忧。欧洲驾驶舱协会对 32 个国家的近 7200 名飞行员进行了调查。调查发现，驾驶舱内的疲惫是一个普遍存在且被低报的问题。超过四分之三的受访者表示，在调查前一个月里，他们曾在驾驶飞机时短暂打盹。近四分之一的人承认，即使太累了仍继续飞行。此外，一半的人表示，他们曾在应当申报自己不适合执勤时仍然执行了航班任务。疲惫可能源于工作时间长、休息不足和排班不规律。

fatigue /fә'ti:g/ n. 疲乏；疲劳e.g. I felt a lot of fatigue after the night shift.
survey /sә'vei/ n. 纵览；视察；测量；俯瞰；调查e.g. Our school did a survey about students' reading habits.
widespread /'waidspred/ a. 充分伸展的；广布的；普及的；流传广的e.g. Online learning is now widespread in our city.
exhaustion /ig'zɒ:stʃәn/ n. 抽空；耗尽；疲惫；筋疲力尽e.g. Exhaustion made it hard for him to focus.
unpredictable /.ʌnpri'diktәbl/ a. 不可预知的e.g. Her work schedule is unpredictable this week.
insufficient /.insә'fiʃәnt/ a. 不够的；不能胜任的；不充足的e.g. Insufficient sleep makes me grumpy in the morning.
result from 短语 由……造成、源于e.g. His headache resulted from too little sleep.

respond to 短语 对……作出反应、回应e.g. Please respond to my message as soon as you can.
¶ 2

The pilots were very frank in their answers to the survey questions. Many said they were reluctant to report fatigue because they feared punishment or other negative reactions from their bosses. Captain Coen George, a director of the ECA, warned: "When pilots fear career consequences, fatigue goes unreported and the risk returns to the cockpit." Aviation safety experts say exhaustion can impair judgement, slow reaction times and prevent pilots from responding effectively to unexpected in-flight situations. The experts urged airlines to foster a culture in which pilots can report tiredness without fear of repercussions. Carriers should ensure that passenger safety is their top priority.

飞行员在回答调查问题时非常坦率。许多人表示不愿报告疲劳，因为他们害怕受到惩罚或遭到上司的其他负面反应。欧洲驾驶舱协会负责人科恩·乔治机长警告说："当飞行员害怕职业后果时，疲劳就得不到报告，风险又回到了驾驶舱。"航空安全专家表示，疲惫会损害判断力、减慢反应速度，并使飞行员无法有效应对飞行中的突发情况。专家敦促航空公司营造一种文化，让飞行员可以无惧后果地报告疲惫。航空公司应确保乘客安全是首要任务。

judgement /'dʒʌdʒmәnt/ n. 审判；判决；判断e.g. Tiredness can affect your judgement on the road.
impair /im'pєә/ vt. 损害；减少；削弱e.g. Lack of sleep can impair your memory.
reaction /ri'ækʃәn/ n. 反应；反作用；反动e.g. His quick reaction saved the game.
repercussion /.ri:pә'kʌʃәn/ n. 弹回；反响；反射e.g. She worried about the repercussions of telling the truth.
priority /prai'ɒriti/ n. 优先权；优先e.g. Safety must always be the top priority.

2. Salmon farming and tech

¶ 3

Fish farming relies heavily on chemicals and antibiotics to treat parasites that can weaken or kill the fish. A large farm in Norway, Norlock, uses freshwater baths and chemical treatments to combat salmon lice, but these methods can also stress the fish and lead to further health issues. One innovative solution at Norlock involves using laser technology to remove the parasites, reducing infection rates. Also, farms in open sea environments benefit from ocean currents, which can help wash away waste and reduce diseases.

养鱼业高度依赖化学品和抗生素来治疗可能削弱或杀死鱼类的寄生虫。挪威一家大型养殖场诺洛克使用淡水浴和化学处理来对付三文鱼虱，但这些方法也可能给鱼带来压力并导致进一步的健康问题。诺洛克的一项创新办法是使用激光技术去除寄生虫，从而降低感染率。此外，开放海域环境中的养殖场受益于洋流，洋流有助于冲走废物、减少疾病。

Chile’s salmon farms, which are heavily dependent on antibiotics, are also under scrutiny for their environmental impact. New methods like closed aquaculture systems, which keep farmed fish in tanks and prevent contamination, offer hope for a more sustainable future in salmon farming.

智利的三文鱼养殖场高度依赖抗生素，其环境影响也受到密切关注。封闭式水产养殖系统等新方法把养殖鱼养在水箱里、防止污染，为三文鱼养殖更可持续的未来带来了希望。
