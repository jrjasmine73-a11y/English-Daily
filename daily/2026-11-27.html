1. COP31 brings no deal

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-26 · 原文链接
📖 联合国 COP31 气候大会在土耳其落幕，却没能达成新的重要协议——这是历史上耗时最长的气候大会。分歧集中在化石燃料和气候资金上，富国与发展中国家各执一词。读这篇短新闻，可以顺便学会 transition away from、a serious blow to 等新闻高频表达。
¶ 1

The United Nations COP31 climate talks in Turkey failed to reach a major new agreement to tackle global warming.

联合国第31届气候大会（COP31）在土耳其举行，但未能就应对全球变暖达成新的重大协议。

reach an agreement 短语 达成协议e.g. After a long talk, the two sides finally reached an agreement.💡 场合：新闻、职场和正式场合通用，中性表达；口语里也可说 reach a deal，意思相近但更随意。
¶ 2

Although negotiations were extended late into Sunday, which made it the longest UN climate conference in history, deep divisions over fossil fuels and financial aid prevented a deal.

尽管谈判一直延长到周日深夜，使其成为历史上持续时间最长的联合国气候大会，但围绕化石燃料和资金援助的严重分歧阻碍了协议的达成。

negotiation /ni.gәuʃi'eiʃәn/ n. 谈判；磋商；交涉e.g. The negotiation went on until midnight.💡 场合：正式场合常用，指双方商量以达成一致；日常口语多说 talk 或 discussion。
extend /ik'stend/ v. 扩充；延伸；伸展；扩大e.g. Can we extend our lunch break by ten minutes?💡 场合：日常和正式场合都常用，可指延长时间、延长假期，也可指伸出手臂等具体动作。
division /di'viʒәn/ n. 分；分开；除法；部门(如部、处、系等)；师e.g. The division in the class made group work difficult.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用，指意见或立场上的分裂；日常口语更常说 disagreement。
prevent /pri'vent/ v. 预防；防止；阻止；妨碍e.g. Wearing a helmet can prevent serious injuries.💡 场合：日常和正式场合都常用；后面常接 from + 动名词，表示阻止某事发生。
deep divisions over 短语 在……问题上的严重分歧e.g. There are still deep divisions over the new homework rules.💡 场合：新闻和正式讨论中常用，描述双方立场差距大、很难达成一致，多用于政策、规则等议题。
¶ 3

The EU, the UK, and vulnerable Pacific island nations rejected the final draft text because oil-producing countries blocked any mention of transitioning away from fossil fuels, the primary driver of climate change. At the same time, African and developing nations objected to the lack of clear financial commitments from wealthy countries to help them adapt to extreme weather and rising sea levels.

欧盟、英国和脆弱的太平洋岛国拒绝了最终草案文本，因为产油国阻止了任何提及逐步摆脱化石燃料的内容——化石燃料是气候变化的主要驱动因素。与此同时，非洲和发展中国家反对富国没有作出明确的资金承诺来帮助他们适应极端天气和海平面上升。

vulnerable /'vʌlnәrәbl/ a. 易受伤害的；有弱点的；易受影响的；脆弱的；成局的e.g. Little kids are more vulnerable to the cold.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用，描述容易受伤害的人或群体；口语里也可形容人感情上脆弱。
reject /ri'dʒekt/ n. 被拒之人；被弃之物；不合格品；次品e.g. He rejected the job because the pay was too low.💡 场合：正式和日常都常用，语气比 refuse 稍正式，多用于拒绝提议、申请或方案。
block /blɒk/ n. 阻止；堵住e.g. A big truck blocked the narrow street.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻都常用，可指堵住道路，也可指阻止某事发生。
commitment /kә'mitmәnt/ n. 委托；交押；承担义务；赞助e.g. She made a commitment to run every morning.💡 场合：正式书面和职场常用，指郑重的承诺或投入；日常口语多说 promise。
transition away from 短语 逐步摆脱；从……过渡转型e.g. Our city is trying to transition away from coal.💡 场合：常见于新闻报道和政策讨论，语气正式，多用于能源、产业转型等话题。
adapt to 短语 适应e.g. It took me two weeks to adapt to the new school.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用，中性词；后面常接新环境、新规则、新生活等。
¶ 4

Conference leaders and campaigners warned that the collapse represents a serious blow to global cooperation, and urged wealthy nations to show stronger leadership, limit the influence of fossil fuel lobbyists, and deliver real climate finance.

大会领导人和活动人士警告说，此次谈判破裂是对全球合作的一次严重打击，并敦促富国展现更强的领导力，限制化石燃料游说者的影响，拿出真正的气候资金。

collapse /kә'læps/ n. 崩溃；倒塌；虚脱e.g. The collapse of the old bridge shocked the whole town.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面常用，可指建筑倒塌，也可指谈判、计划、身体垮掉等抽象崩溃。
finance /fai'næns/ n. 财政；财务e.g. The school needs more finance for new books.💡 场合：新闻、经济和正式场合常用；作「资金」讲时不可数，作「金融」学科讲时也常用。
influence /'influәns/ n. 影响力；权力；势力e.g. My teacher has a big influence on my English.💡 场合：日常和正式场合都常用；have an influence on 是固定搭配。
a serious blow to 短语 对……的严重打击e.g. The rain was a serious blow to our picnic plans.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面语常用，语气较重，多用于谈挫折、失败对某人或某事的影响。
¶ 5

Difficult words: vulnerable (likely to be harmed, damaged, or negatively affected), primary driver (the most important factor or force that causes something to happen or develop), lobbyist (a person who takes part in an organized attempt to influence legislators or officials to support a particular interest or industry).

难词：vulnerable（容易受到伤害、损害或负面影响的），primary driver（导致某事发生或发展的最重要因素或力量），lobbyist（参与有组织的活动、试图影响立法者或官员以支持某一利益或行业的人）。

lobbyist /'lɔbiist/ n. 说客e.g. The lobbyist spent the day talking to lawmakers.💡 场合：新闻和政治话题专用词，日常口语很少用；常带一点负面色彩，指为某行业利益游说的人。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. After a long talk, the two sides finally ____.
✓ reached an agreement（reach an agreement 的变形）　达成协议
After a long talk, the two sides finally reached an agreement.
经过长时间商谈，双方终于达成了协议。
2. A big truck ____ the narrow street.
✓ blocked（block 的变形）　阻止；堵住
A big truck blocked the narrow street.
一辆大卡车堵住了狭窄的街道。
3. The ____ of the old bridge shocked the whole town.
✓ collapse　崩溃；倒塌；虚脱
The collapse of the old bridge shocked the whole town.
老桥的倒塌震惊了全镇。
4. Little kids are more ____ to the cold.
✓ vulnerable　易受伤害的；有弱点的；易受影响的；脆弱的；成局的
Little kids are more vulnerable to the cold.
小孩子更容易受凉。
5. The school needs more ____ for new books.
✓ finance　财政；财务
The school needs more finance for new books.
学校需要更多资金来买新书。
6. He ____ the job because the pay was too low.
✓ rejected（reject 的变形）　被拒之人；被弃之物；不合格品；次品
He rejected the job because the pay was too low.
他拒绝了这份工作，因为工资太低了。
7. My teacher has a big ____ on my English.
✓ influence　影响力；权力；势力
My teacher has a big influence on my English.
我的老师对我的英语影响很大。
8. The ____ in the class made group work difficult.
✓ division　分；分开；除法；部门(如部、处、系等)；师
The division in the class made group work difficult.
班里的分歧让小组合作变得很难。
9. There are still ____ the new homework rules.
✓ deep divisions over　在……问题上的严重分歧
There are still deep divisions over the new homework rules.
关于新的作业规定，大家仍然存在严重分歧。
10. Our city is trying to ____ coal.
✓ transition away from　逐步摆脱；从……过渡转型
Our city is trying to transition away from coal.
我们城市正在努力逐步摆脱煤炭。
11. Wearing a helmet can ____ serious injuries.
✓ prevent　预防；防止；阻止；妨碍
Wearing a helmet can prevent serious injuries.
戴头盔可以防止严重受伤。
12. It took me two weeks to ____ the new school.
✓ adapt to　适应
It took me two weeks to adapt to the new school.
我花了两周才适应新学校。
13. She made a ____ to run every morning.
✓ commitment　委托；交押；承担义务；赞助
She made a commitment to run every morning.
她承诺每天早上跑步。
14. Can we ____ our lunch break by ten minutes?
✓ extend　扩充；延伸；伸展；扩大
Can we extend our lunch break by ten minutes?
我们能把午休延长十分钟吗？
15. The ____ spent the day talking to lawmakers.
✓ lobbyist　说客
The lobbyist spent the day talking to lawmakers.
这位说客花了一整天与立法者交谈。
