1. Scotland has a big problem with drugs

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-01 · 原文链接
📖 苏格兰正面临严重的毒品危机：过去十年超过 11,000 人死于毒品相关原因，被称为"欧洲毒品死亡之都"；仅 2025 年就有 1,133 人死亡，新型合成阿片类药物尼塔嗪的蔓延让形势更加严峻。文章追溯了这场危机背后的工业衰落、贫困与代际创伤，以及"减害政策"等争议性治理手段，读完能顺手积累一批公共政策话题的高频词汇。
¶ 1

Scotland faces a severe national crisis. It recorded over 11,000 drug-related deaths in the past decade, which made it the drug death capital of Europe.

苏格兰正面临一场严重的全国性危机。过去十年间，该国录得超过 11,000 例与毒品相关的死亡，使其成为“欧洲毒品死亡之都”。

drug-related deaths 短语 与毒品相关的死亡e.g. Scotland recorded over 11,000 drug-related deaths in a decade.
¶ 2

In 2025 alone, 1,133 people died, driven by an increasingly dangerous supply of synthetic opioids like nitazenes.

仅 2025 年一年，就有 1,133 人死亡，罪魁祸首是日益危险的合成阿片类药物供应，比如尼塔秦类物质。

¶ 3

The origins of this crisis trace back to 20th-century industrial decline, poverty, and intergenerational trauma. Additionally, political decisions worsened the situation, including controversial 2015 funding cuts to treatment services by the Scottish government, for which officials have since apologized. To combat the epidemic, Scotland is expanding its national drug testing facilities to identify rapid market changes and provide scientific evidence for public health policies.

这场危机的根源可以追溯到 20 世纪的工业衰落、贫困以及代际创伤。此外，政治决策也让情况雪上加霜，包括苏格兰政府 2015 年对戒毒治疗服务有争议的资金削减，官员们已为此道歉。为了对抗这场“流行病”，苏格兰正在扩大全国药物检测设施，以便识别毒品市场的快速变化，并为公共卫生政策提供科学依据。

trace back to 短语 追溯到；根源在于e.g. The crisis traces back to industrial decline and poverty.
¶ 4

Police continue seizing tens of millions of kilograms worth of illicit substances, while politicians advocate for harm-reduction measures, including safer consumption rooms and expanded rehabilitation spaces. Some facilities offer critical recovery programs, which give long-term users a safe environment to overcome addiction and rebuild their lives.

警方持续查获大量非法药物，与此同时，政界人士主张推行减害措施，包括设立更安全的吸毒场所和扩大康复空间。一些机构提供关键的康复项目，为长期吸毒者提供安全的环境，帮助他们戒除毒瘾、重建生活。

advocate for 短语 主张；提倡e.g. Politicians advocate for harm-reduction measures.
¶ 5

Difficult words: intergenerational trauma (psychological pain or emotional stress passed down from parents or grandparents to their children and future generations), seize (to officially take control of illegal items or property using legal authority), illicit (forbidden by law, rules, or custom).

难词：intergenerational trauma（代际创伤：从父母或祖父母传给子女及后代的心理痛苦或情感压力），seize（查获：动用法律权力正式没收非法物品或财产），illicit（非法的：被法律、规则或习俗禁止的）。

be driven by 短语 由……驱动；因……导致e.g. The deaths were driven by a dangerous supply of synthetic opioids.
seize 短语 查获；没收e.g. Police seized millions of dollars' worth of illicit substances.

2. Tokyo micro-apartments

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-09-30 · 原文链接
📖 东京兴起 9 平方米的"超迷你公寓"，面积不足普通单身公寓的一半，却因免押金、免"礼金"、月租仅 380-610 美元而受到初入职场的年轻人追捧。9 平米也能安放一种生活方式，折射出东京年轻人的真实居住处境；key money、amenity 等租房场景词也非常实用。
¶ 1

Micro-apartments are places that are very small, have very few amenities, very little space, and not many of the home comforts that you’d associate with living in a bigger apartment and certainly in a house.

超迷你公寓面积非常小，设施极少，空间局促，几乎没有你在大公寓乃至独栋房子里能想到的那些居家舒适感。

associate with 短语 联想到；与……联系起来e.g. the comforts you'd associate with a bigger apartment
¶ 2

However, they’re becoming increasingly popular in the Japanese capital, particularly among younger people who’re just starting in the workplace and can’t afford rents for ordinary places, or they may be moving into Tokyo from out of town and want a cheap place to stay.

然而，这种公寓在日本首都越来越受欢迎，尤其受刚步入职场、租不起普通房子的年轻人青睐；也有人是从外地搬到东京，只想找个便宜的落脚处。

¶ 3

Each micro-apartment measures just nine square meters, which is under half the size of a typical studio apartment in Tokyo. The benefit’s that there’s zero deposit and no ‘key money’, a nonrefundable gift to the landlord. Monthly rents range from 380 to 610 dollars, depending on where in Tokyo you are.

每套超迷你公寓只有 9 平方米，不到东京普通单身公寓的一半大。好处是零押金，也没有“礼金”（给房东的一笔不可退还的钱）。月租在 380 到 610 美元之间，具体看在东京的哪个地段。

key money 短语 礼金（租房时给房东的不可退还费用）e.g. There's no key money with these micro-apartments.
range from A to B 短语 在 A 到 B 之间e.g. Monthly rents range from 380 to 610 dollars.
¶ 4

Difficult words: amenity (a desirable or useful feature or facility of a building or place), nonrefundable (money that you pay and can’t get back).

难词：amenity（便利设施：建筑物或场所中令人向往或实用的设施），nonrefundable（不可退还的：付了就拿不回来的钱）。

become increasingly popular 短语 越来越受欢迎e.g. Micro-apartments are becoming increasingly popular among young people.
