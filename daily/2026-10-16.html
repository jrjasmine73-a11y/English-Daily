1. 2026 Chemistry Nobel Prize

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-15 · 原文链接
📖 2026年诺贝尔化学奖授予了破解分子“镜像之谜”的两位科学家。关键信息：药物分子的两个镜像版本可能一个治病、一个致命，这项发现让制药业能只生产安全有效的那一半。值得读：短短几段讲清了“手性”这个听起来高深、却关系到每颗药片安全的概念。
¶ 1

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry has been awarded to Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai for solving a long-standing scientific mystery regarding molecular mirror images.

2026年诺贝尔化学奖授予了亨利·卡根和健二·索艾，以表彰他们解开了一个关于分子镜像的长期科学之谜。

mystery /'mistәri/ n. 秘密；神秘；奥秘e.g. The old house holds a mystery no one can solve.
mirror /'mirә/ n. 镜子；写真；典范e.g. She looked at herself in the mirror before going out.
long-standing 短语 长期存在的；由来已久的e.g. They solved a long-standing scientific mystery.
be critical for 短语 对……至关重要e.g. Clean water is critical for health.
¶ 2

Many chemical molecules exist in two mirror-image forms, much like a person’s left and right hands. In nature, living things almost always use only one version, while the other form is absent or inactive. Producing just the helpful version has historically been a huge challenge for chemical manufacturing. Controlling this balance is critical for medicine because two mirror-image molecules can have vastly different effects on the human body. For instance, while one version of a drug acts as a helpful painkiller, its mirror image can be inactive or even highly dangerous, as seen in historical medical tragedies.

许多化学分子都存在两种互为镜像的形式，就像人的左手和右手。在自然界中，生物几乎总是只使用其中一种，而另一种则不存在或不起作用。只生产有用的那一种，历来是化学制造中的巨大挑战。控制这种平衡对医药至关重要，因为两个互为镜像的分子对人体可能产生截然不同的影响。例如，某种药物的一种版本是有效的止痛药，而它的镜像版本可能毫无作用，甚至极其危险，历史上的医疗悲剧就是例证。

version /'vә:ʒәn/ n. 一种描述；版本；译文e.g. I prefer the original version of this song.
absent /'æbsәnt/ a. 缺席的；不在的；缺乏的；漫不经心的e.g. He was absent from school yesterday because he was sick.
inactive /in'æktiv/ a. 不活动的；停止的；怠惰的；闲置的e.g. My old phone has been inactive for months.
critical /'kritikәl/ a. 批评的；决定性的；危险的；临界的e.g. Drinking enough water is critical for your health.
vastly /'vɑ:stli/ adv. 广大地；许多；巨大e.g. The new phone is vastly better than the old one.
mirror image 短语 镜像；镜中像e.g. Many molecules exist in two mirror-image forms, like your left and right hands.
have an effect on 短语 对……产生影响e.g. Loud music can have an effect on your hearing.
¶ 3

The duo’s discovery of unique chemical processes allows scientists to selectively produce the safe, effective version of a molecule. This breakthrough has revolutionized the pharmaceutical industry and made everyday medicines far safer and more effective.

这两位科学家的发现揭示了独特的化学过程，使科学家能够有选择地生产安全有效的分子版本。这一突破彻底改变了制药行业，让日常药物变得更加安全有效。

effective /i'fektiv/ a. 有效的；有力的；实际的e.g. This method is simple but very effective.
pharmaceutical /,fɑ:mә'sju:tikәl/ a. 药学的；制药的；药用的；药物的；药剂师的；药师的e.g. She works for a pharmaceutical company.
duo /'dju:әu/ n. 二重唱e.g. The singing duo became famous overnight.
¶ 4

Difficult words: absent (not present or not existing in a place), duo (two people who work or perform together), revolutionize (to completely change the way something is done or made).

难词：absent（不存在的；缺席的），duo（两人组合；二人搭档），revolutionize（彻底变革；使发生革命性变化）。

revolutionize /.revә'lu:ʃәnaiz/ vt. 向...鼓吹革命；使发生革命性剧变e.g. Smartphones revolutionized the way we live.
tragedy /'trædʒidi/ n. 悲剧；惨案；悲剧作品e.g. The accident was a terrible tragedy for the whole family.

2. A women’s village

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-14 · 原文链接
📖 叙利亚东北部有个只住女性的村庄 Jinwar，收留了因战争和家暴流离失所的妇女。关键信息：村里不许男性居住，但妇女们种地、开面包房、办学校，过上了不受干涉的生活。值得读：篇幅短、故事性强，还能学到 refuge、interference 这类高频表达。
¶ 1

Jinwar is a village in the Kurdish region in northeast Syria where a group of women displaced by war and domestic violence found a safe refuge and rebuilt their lives.

金瓦尔是叙利亚东北部库尔德地区的一个村庄，一群因战争和家庭暴力而流离失所的妇女在这里找到了安全的避难所，重新开始了生活。

refuge /'refju:dʒ/ n. 避难所；安全地带；避难；庇护e.g. The small town became a refuge for people fleeing the war.
displaced /dis'pleist/ a. 位移的；无家可归的；被取代的e.g. The flood left thousands of families displaced.
¶ 2

Jinwar means ‘women’s space’ in the Kurdish language. The village was built in 2018, and it has 30 houses hosting 20 families of women and children. Jinwar has a school, a training academy, a bakery, and fields cultivated by women. Men aren’t allowed to live in Jinwar; however, they can visit their relatives living there. Boys who live with their mothers can stay in the village until they get married.

Jinwar 在库尔德语中意为“女性的空间”。这个村庄建于 2018 年，有 30 栋房屋，住着 20 个妇女和儿童家庭。金瓦尔有一所学校、一所培训学院、一家面包房，还有由妇女耕种的田地。男性不允许住在金瓦尔，但可以来探望住在这里的亲属。和母亲一起生活的男孩可以在村里住到结婚。

cultivate /'kʌltiveit/ vt. 培养；耕作e.g. My grandma cultivates vegetables in her garden.
relative /'relәtiv/ n. 亲戚；关系词e.g. We visited our relatives during the holiday.
academy /ә'kædәmi/ n. 学院；院校；学会e.g. She joined a dance academy to improve her skills.
¶ 3

The head of the village says women and children are always the victims in conflicts like the civil war that broke out in 2011. Women in the village spend their days working and evenings singing and dancing. Many say that they have goals and ambitions, and they can live their lives without any interference from anyone or without being controlled. They become stronger and they aren’t viewed as being confined to the household and its duties anymore.

村长说，在像 2011 年爆发的内战这样的冲突中，妇女和儿童永远是受害者。村里的妇女白天工作，晚上唱歌跳舞。许多人说，她们有了目标和抱负，可以过不受任何人干涉、不受任何人控制的生活。她们变得更坚强，不再被视为只能被局限在家庭及其家务之中。

victim /'viktim/ n. 受害人；牺牲者；牺牲品e.g. She was a victim of the scam, but she reported it bravely.
conflict /'kɒnflikt/ n. 战斗；冲突；矛盾；争执e.g. They solved the conflict by talking it out calmly.
ambition /æm'biʃәn/ n. 野心；志向e.g. Her ambition is to become a doctor one day.
interference /.intә'fiәrәns/ n. 冲突；干涉e.g. I enjoy working alone, without any interference.
be displaced by 短语 因……而流离失所e.g. Many families were displaced by the war.
be confined to 短语 被局限于；被限制在e.g. They aren't viewed as being confined to the household anymore.
without interference from 短语 不受……的干涉e.g. The kids played freely without interference from adults.
¶ 4

Difficult words: interference (getting involved annoyingly), confine (to keep or restrict someone).

难词：interference（令人讨厌的干涉；介入），confine（限制；禁闭）。

rebuild /ri'bild/ vt. 改建；重建；改造e.g. After the fire, they had to rebuild their home from scratch.
confine /kәn'fain/ vt. 限制；使不外出；禁闭e.g. Don't confine yourself to one way of thinking.
find refuge 短语 找到避难所；找到安身之所e.g. The women found a safe refuge in Jinwar and rebuilt their lives.
break out 短语 （战争、冲突等）爆发e.g. The civil war broke out in 2011.
