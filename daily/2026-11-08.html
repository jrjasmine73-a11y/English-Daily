1. Parents take children out of big schools

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-06 · 原文链接
📖 丹麦越来越多家长让孩子离开主流学校，因为传统教育对神经多样性儿童太过僵化。文章介绍了家长自发组织的替代学习俱乐部，以及教育研究者对学校改革的呼吁。值得一读：它提供了一个思考"教育应该适应孩子，还是孩子适应教育"的真实案例。
¶ 1

More Danish parents are pulling their children out of mainstream schools. They argue that the traditional education system is far too rigid for neurodivergent kids.

越来越多的丹麦家长正让孩子离开主流学校。他们认为，传统的教育体系对神经多样性儿童来说过于僵化。

mainstream /'meinstri:m/ n. 主流e.g. Most kids go to mainstream schools in our town.
rigid /'ridʒid/ a. 坚硬的；刚性的；严格的；精密的；刻板的e.g. A rigid daily plan leaves no time for fun.
neurodivergent e.g. Neurodivergent kids often learn best in their own way.
pull out of 短语 退出；使退出e.g. Some parents pull their kids out of big schools to find a better fit.
¶ 2

Children with autism or attention disorders, like 12-year-old Olav, often struggle with noisy classrooms and fixed daily schedules. Because public schools lack resources and specialized programs, many families have turned to alternative support systems, such as parent-led clubs. These spaces allow children to learn at their own pace through practical activities, like organic farming, while giving them a safe environment free from pressure.

患有自闭症或注意力障碍的儿童，比如12岁的奥拉夫，常常难以适应嘈杂的教室和固定不变的日常安排。由于公立学校缺乏资源和专门的课程，许多家庭转向了替代性支持体系，比如由家长主导的俱乐部。这些空间让孩子们可以通过有机农耕等实践活动，按自己的节奏学习，同时提供一个没有压力的安全环境。

struggle /'strʌgl/ n. 斗争；努力；奋斗e.g. He struggles to finish his homework on time.
schedule /'skedʒuәl/ n. 时间表；一览表；计划表；议事日程e.g. I keep a simple schedule on my phone.
resource /ri'sɒ:s/ n. 资源；财力；办法；策略；急智；消遣e.g. Our school needs more resources for special classes.
alternative /ɒ:l'tә:nәtiv/ n. 两者择一；供替代的选择e.g. Some families look for alternative ways to learn.
practical /'præktikl/ a. 实际的；现实的；实用性的e.g. Gardening is a practical way to learn about nature.
pressure /'preʃә/ n. 压；榨；按；强制；压力；压迫；压强e.g. Too much pressure can make kids hate school.
at one's own pace 短语 按自己的节奏e.g. She likes to learn English at her own pace, a little every day.
turn to 短语 转向；求助于e.g. When I feel stressed, I turn to music to relax.
free from 短语 没有……的；不受……影响e.g. I love a quiet morning free from phone calls.
¶ 3

While politicians worry about falling academic standards in reading and math, educational researchers argue that the school system itself needs urgent reform rather than forcing children to fit into outdated models. These alternative clubs aim to rebuild confidence and help kids rebuild their relationship with learning.

政客们担心阅读和数学的学业水平下降，而教育研究者则认为，学校体系本身亟需改革，而不是强迫孩子去适应过时的模式。这些替代俱乐部旨在重建孩子们的信心，帮助他们重建与学习的关系。

confidence /'kɒnfidәns/ n. 信心e.g. Small wins help children build confidence.
reform /ri'fɒ:m/ n. 改革；改正；改造e.g. Many people want to reform the old school system.
outdated /.aut'deitid/ a. 旧式的；落伍的；过时的e.g. This old rule feels outdated now.
fit into 短语 适应；融入e.g. It takes time for a new student to fit into a new class.
¶ 4

Difficult words: rigid (unable to be changed or adapted; strictly set), neurodivergent (having a brain that processes information differently than what is considered typical), academic standards (the levels of quality, knowledge, and achievement expected from students in school).

生词：rigid（无法改变或调整的；严格规定的）、neurodivergent（大脑处理信息的方式与通常认为的典型方式不同）、academic standards（学校对学生应达到的质量、知识和成绩水平的要求）。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. Most kids go to ____ schools in our town.
✓ mainstream　主流
Most kids go to mainstream schools in our town.
我们镇上大多数孩子都上主流学校。
2. I love a quiet morning ____ phone calls.
✓ free from　没有……的；不受……影响
I love a quiet morning free from phone calls.
我喜欢没有电话打扰的安静早晨。
3. Many people want to ____ the old school system.
✓ reform　改革；改正；改造
Many people want to reform the old school system.
许多人希望改革旧的教育体系。
4. A ____ daily plan leaves no time for fun.
✓ rigid　坚硬的；刚性的；严格的；精密的；刻板的
A rigid daily plan leaves no time for fun.
死板的日常计划一点玩的时间都不留。
5. It takes time for a new student to ____ a new class.
✓ fit into　适应；融入
It takes time for a new student to fit into a new class.
新生需要一段时间才能融入新班级。
6. He ____ to finish his homework on time.
✓ struggles（struggle 的变形）　斗争；努力；奋斗
He struggles to finish his homework on time.
他总是很难按时完成作业。
7. When I feel stressed, I ____ music to relax.
✓ turn to　转向；求助于
When I feel stressed, I turn to music to relax.
我感到压力大时，会听音乐来放松。
8. Our school needs more ____ for special classes.
✓ resources（resource 的变形）　资源；财力；办法；策略；急智；消遣
Our school needs more resources for special classes.
我们学校需要更多资源来开设特殊课程。
9. ____ kids often learn best in their own way.
✓ neurodivergent　
Neurodivergent kids often learn best in their own way.
神经多样性儿童常常按自己的方式学得最好。
10. Small wins help children build ____.
✓ confidence　信心
Small wins help children build confidence.
小小的成功能帮助孩子建立自信。
11. Some families look for ____ ways to learn.
✓ alternative　两者择一；供替代的选择
Some families look for alternative ways to learn.
一些家庭在寻找替代的学习方式。
12. I keep a simple ____ on my phone.
✓ schedule　时间表；一览表；计划表；议事日程
I keep a simple schedule on my phone.
我在手机上记了一个简单的日程表。
13. This old rule feels ____ now.
✓ outdated　旧式的；落伍的；过时的
This old rule feels outdated now.
这条旧规定现在感觉已经过时了。
14. Too much ____ can make kids hate school.
✓ pressure　压；榨；按；强制；压力；压迫；压强
Too much pressure can make kids hate school.
太大的压力会让孩子讨厌上学。
15. Gardening is a ____ way to learn about nature.
✓ practical　实际的；现实的；实用性的
Gardening is a practical way to learn about nature.
种花种菜是了解大自然的一种实用方式。
