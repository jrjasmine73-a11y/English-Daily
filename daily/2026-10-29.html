1. Sydney must close its beaches

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-28 · 原文链接
📖 悉尼东部热门海滩因一头幼年座头鲸搁浅死亡而对公众关闭。关键信息：鲸鱼尸体很可能把鲨鱼引到海岸边，而且由于尸体高度腐烂、海况恶劣，打捞工作困难重重。值得读：看一次野生动物事件如何同时牵动公共安全、环境保护和原住民文化，顺便学一组与海洋和自然相关的表达。
¶ 1

Popular beaches in eastern Sydney were closed to the public after a dead young humpback whale washed ashore in a rocky bay.

悉尼东部热门海滩在发现一头死亡的幼年座头鲸被冲上岩石海湾后，对公众关闭。

humpback /'hʌmpbæk/ n. 驼背(者)；细鳞大马哈鱼e.g. Humpback whales can sing beautiful songs under the sea.
rocky /'rɒki/ a. 岩石的；多石的；像岩石的；无情的；摇动的；摇晃的e.g. The path to the beach is rocky, so wear good shoes.
wash ashore 短语 被冲上岸e.g. After the storm, a lot of seaweed washed ashore.
¶ 2

Local authorities shut down swimming and surfing spots as a safety precaution and warned that the whale carcass is likely to attract sharks to the coastline. Emergency teams and local rangers face significant challenges trying to remove the body because the animal is in an advanced state of decomposition, and the rough ocean conditions make the area inaccessible for boats or heavy machinery. Community leaders and experts noted that with growing whale populations along the coast, such events are becoming more common. Indigenous rangers plan to perform cultural ceremonies to honor the whale, while scientists advise the public to stay out of the water and let nature take its course if retrieval remains impossible.

当地政府出于安全考虑关闭了游泳和冲浪点，并警告鲸鱼尸体很可能把鲨鱼引到海岸边。应急队伍和当地护林员在移走尸体时面临巨大挑战，因为这头鲸已经高度腐烂，而且恶劣的海况使船只和重型机械无法进入该区域。社区领袖和专家指出，随着沿岸鲸鱼数量不断增长，此类事件正变得越来越常见。原住民护林员计划举行文化仪式来缅怀这头鲸，而科学家建议公众远离海水，如果打捞确实无法进行，就顺其自然。

authorities n. 当局；官方（authority的复数）e.g. Local authorities closed the road after the heavy rain.
precaution /pri'kɒ:ʃәn/ n. 预防；留心；警戒e.g. I took an umbrella as a precaution, though it didn't rain.
carcass /'kɑ:kәs/ n. 尸体e.g. Workers removed the carcass of a dead bird from the park.
coastline /'kәustlain/ n. 海岸线e.g. We drove along the coastline and enjoyed the sea view.
significant /sig'nifikәnt/ a. 重要的；有效的；有含义的；暗示的；值得注意的e.g. She made significant progress in English this term.
decomposition /.di:kɒmpә'ziʃәn/ n. 分解；腐败；变质e.g. Decomposition turns fallen leaves into rich soil.
inaccessible /.inæk'sesәbl/ a. 难得到的；难接近的；难达成的e.g. The mountain village was inaccessible after the snowstorm.
machinery /mә'ʃi:nәri/ n. 机器；机械装置；机构e.g. The factory bought new machinery this year.
indigenous /in'didʒinәs/ a. 本土的；国产的；固有的e.g. Indigenous people have lived in this area for thousands of years.
ceremonies /ˈseriməniz/ n. 典礼( ceremony的名词复数 ); 仪式; 礼节; 礼仪e.g. The wedding ceremony was simple but beautiful.
shut down 短语 关闭e.g. The library shuts down at 9 pm every night.
as a safety precaution 短语 出于安全考虑；为安全起见e.g. As a safety precaution, we always wear helmets when we ride bikes.
attract ... to ... 短语 把……吸引到……e.g. Bright lights attract insects to the garden at night.
take its course 短语 顺其自然；自然发展e.g. Don't worry too much; sometimes you just have to let things take their course.
¶ 3

Difficult words: precaution (an action taken in advance to prevent something dangerous, unpleasant, or inconvenient from happening), carcass (the dead body of an animal, especially a large one), retrieval (getting something back or removing it from a specific location).

生词：precaution（为防止危险、不愉快或不便的事情发生而预先采取的行动），carcass（动物的尸体，尤指大型动物），retrieval（取回某物或把某物从特定地点移走）。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（4 题）
1. Local ____ closed the road after the heavy rain.
✓ authorities　当局；官方（authority的复数）
Local authorities closed the road after the heavy rain.
大雨过后，当地政府关闭了这条路。
2. ____, we always wear helmets when we ride bikes.
✓ as a safety precaution　出于安全考虑；为安全起见
As a safety precaution, we always wear helmets when we ride bikes.
为安全起见，我们骑车时总是戴头盔。
3. ____ whales can sing beautiful songs under the sea.
✓ humpback　驼背(者)；细鳞大马哈鱼
Humpback whales can sing beautiful songs under the sea.
座头鲸能在海底唱出动听的歌声。
4. The factory bought new ____ this year.
✓ machinery　机器；机械装置；机构
The factory bought new machinery this year.
这家工厂今年买了新机器。
