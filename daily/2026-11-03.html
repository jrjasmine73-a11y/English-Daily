1. The longest train

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-02 · 原文链接
📖 南非国营物流公司 Transnet 成功测试了一列 7.6 公里长的货运列车，声称打破世界纪录：696 节空车厢、860 公里路程，由电力和柴油机车共同牵引。关键信息点是：这一纪录目前只来自公司自述，尚待外部核实。短文用词清晰，适合积累物流、运输类词汇和“打破纪录”一类表达。
¶ 1

South Africa’s state-owned logistics company, Transnet, has successfully tested a 7.6-kilometre freight train and claimed to set a new world record.

南非国营物流公司 Transnet 成功测试了一列 7.6 公里长的货运列车，并声称创下了新的世界纪录。

logistics /lәu'dʒistiks/ n. 后勤学；后勤e.g. She studies logistics and wants to work in shipping.
freight /'freit/ n. 船货；运费；货运e.g. Freight costs went up after the holiday.
set a record 短语 打破纪录、创纪录e.g. Our class set a record for reading the most books this term.
¶ 2

In a test journey, the colossal train, powered by a combination of electric and diesel locomotives, consisted of 696 empty wagons and covered 860 kilometers between a western coastal port and an inland iron ore mine. According to company officials, the test run surpassed the previous record established in 2001 by a 7.2-kilometre freight train operating in Western Australia.

在这次测试行程中，这列庞大的列车由电力机车和柴油机车共同牵引，由 696 节空车厢组成，行驶了 860 公里，往返于西部沿海港口与内陆铁矿之间。据公司官员称，这次测试超过了 2001 年在西澳大利亚创下的 7.2 公里货运列车的前纪录。

colossal /kә'lɒsәl/ a. 巨大的；巨像似的e.g. The new library has a colossal reading room.
locomotive /.lәukә'mәutiv/ n. 火车头；机车e.g. The old locomotive in the museum still looks powerful.
wagon /'wægәn/ n. 四轮马车；货车e.g. The farmer loaded apples onto the wagon.
surpass /sә'pɑ:s/ vt. 超越；凌驾；胜过e.g. She hopes to surpass her best score this term.
coastal /'kәustәl/ a. 海岸的；沿海的；沿岸的e.g. We spent the weekend in a small coastal town.
consist of 短语 由……组成e.g. This lunch box consists of rice, vegetables, and an egg.
surpass the record 短语 超过纪录e.g. He trained hard to surpass the record next season.
¶ 3

Transnet presented the successful trial as a significant breakthrough for the nation’s freight transport network, which relies heavily on efficient rail systems to export raw minerals. However, industry observers note that the record claim currently originates directly from Transnet itself, and they await external verification.

Transnet 把这次成功的试验称为该国货运网络的重大突破，该国货运网络高度依赖高效的铁路系统来出口原材料。然而，业内观察人士指出，目前这一纪录说法直接来自 Transnet 本身，他们正在等待外部核实。

breakthrough /'breikθru:/ n. 突破e.g. After years of hard work, they made a real breakthrough.
efficient /i'fiʃәnt/ a. 有效率的；能干的e.g. Taking the subway is an efficient way to cross the city.
verification /.verifi'keiʃәn/ n. 证实；查证；证明属实e.g. We are waiting for verification before we pay the bill.
trial /'traiәl/ n. 审判；试验；艰苦；麻烦事；考验e.g. The school ran a trial of the new timetable last week.
originate from 短语 源自、来自e.g. This festival originates from an old harvest celebration.
¶ 4

Difficult words: colossal (extremely large), iron ore (a natural rock or mineral from which metallic iron can be extracted), verification (establishing the truth, accuracy, or validity of something).

生词：colossal（极其巨大的），iron ore（可从中提炼金属铁的天然岩石或矿物），verification（证实某事的真实性、准确性或有效性）。

mineral /'minәrәl/ n. 矿物；无机物；苏打水e.g. This bottle of water contains several useful minerals.
rely heavily on 短语 严重依赖、高度依靠e.g. Small towns here rely heavily on buses to get to the city.

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. She hopes to ____ her best score this term.
✓ surpass　超越；凌驾；胜过
She hopes to surpass her best score this term.
她希望这学期超过自己的最好成绩。
2. He trained hard to ____ next season.
✓ surpass the record　超过纪录
He trained hard to surpass the record next season.
他刻苦训练，希望下个赛季超过这一纪录。
3. The old ____ in the museum still looks powerful.
✓ locomotive　火车头；机车
The old locomotive in the museum still looks powerful.
博物馆里的旧机车看起来依然很有力量。
4. This festival ____ an old harvest celebration.
✓ originates from（originate from 的变形）　源自、来自
This festival originates from an old harvest celebration.
这个节日源自古老的丰收庆典。
5. She studies ____ and wants to work in shipping.
✓ logistics　后勤学；后勤
She studies logistics and wants to work in shipping.
她学物流专业，想从事航运工作。
6. Taking the subway is an ____ way to cross the city.
✓ efficient　有效率的；能干的
Taking the subway is an efficient way to cross the city.
坐地铁是穿城而过的便捷方式。
7. Small towns here ____ buses to get to the city.
✓ rely heavily on　严重依赖、高度依靠
Small towns here rely heavily on buses to get to the city.
这里的小镇去城里严重依赖公交车。
8. The school ran a ____ of the new timetable last week.
✓ trial　审判；试验；艰苦；麻烦事；考验
The school ran a trial of the new timetable last week.
学校上周试行了新的课程表。
9. This bottle of water contains several useful ____.
✓ minerals（mineral 的变形）　矿物；无机物；苏打水
This bottle of water contains several useful minerals.
这瓶水含有几种有益的矿物质。
10. We spent the weekend in a small ____ town.
✓ coastal　海岸的；沿海的；沿岸的
We spent the weekend in a small coastal town.
我们在一个沿海小镇度过了周末。
11. The new library has a ____ reading room.
✓ colossal　巨大的；巨像似的
The new library has a colossal reading room.
新图书馆有一个巨大的阅览室。
12. The farmer loaded apples onto the ____.
✓ wagon　四轮马车；货车
The farmer loaded apples onto the wagon.
农夫把苹果装上了货车车厢。
13. ____ costs went up after the holiday.
✓ freight　船货；运费；货运
Freight costs went up after the holiday.
假期过后货运费用上涨了。
14. We are waiting for ____ before we pay the bill.
✓ verification　证实；查证；证明属实
We are waiting for verification before we pay the bill.
我们在付款之前正在等待核实结果。
15. This lunch box ____ rice, vegetables, and an egg.
✓ consists of（consist of 的变形）　由……组成
This lunch box consists of rice, vegetables, and an egg.
