1. Oil pollution in the sea

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-24 · 原文链接
📖 波斯湾正遭遇严重的石油污染：冲突中受损的船只不断泄漏，500 多处浮油已经漂上多国海岸，珊瑚礁、海龟和濒危鲸鱼都受到威胁。文章讲清了这场环境灾难是怎么发生的、为什么油污可能在海里滞留数年。值得一读：一场看似遥远的海上事故，如何变成沿岸国家的共同难题；词汇多与海洋、环保相关。
¶ 1

Severe oil pollution from damaged ships during the conflict involving Iran is threatening marine life and coastal communities across the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz.

伊朗卷入的冲突中受损船只造成的严重石油污染，正威胁着波斯湾和霍尔木兹海峡一带的海洋生物与沿岸社区。

severe /si'viә/ a. 严格的；尖锐的；严肃的；严重的；严厉的；朴素的e.g. The storm caused severe damage to the old bridge.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面常用，日常口语里更常说 serious / bad；语气较重，表示程度很深。
marine /mә'ri:n/ n. 舰队；水兵；海景画e.g. We saw many marine animals at the aquarium.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，日常口语里说 sea 的更多；多用于 marine life、marine animals 这类固定搭配。
threaten /'θretn/ vt. 恐吓；威胁；预示...的凶兆e.g. Smoking threatens your health more than you think.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面常用，日常口语里也说；表示对人或事构成危险，语气较重。
coastal /'kәustәl/ a. 海岸的；沿海的；沿岸的e.g. We spent the holiday in a small coastal town.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面常用，日常口语里也说；多用于 coastal areas、coastal cities。
¶ 2

According to the International Maritime Organization, 136 maritime incidents have been confirmed in the region, while Greenpeace reports that satellite monitoring has detected more than 500 oil slicks since the war began. One recently spotted slick covers around 300 square kilometers, including a thick, dark section of heavy oil. Environmental experts warn that because the Gulf consists of shallow, semi-enclosed waters with limited exchange with the open ocean, trapped oil can remain in the environment for years. The spills have already washed onto coastlines in Iran, Oman, and the United Arab Emirates, and some beaches had to be closed temporarily. Conservationists are deeply concerned about vulnerable marine habitats, coral reefs, and rare species such as sea turtles and the endangered Arabian Sea humpback whale.

据国际海事组织称，该地区已确认发生 136 起海上事故；绿色和平组织则报告称，自战争开始以来，卫星监测已发现 500 多处浮油。一处最近发现的浮油覆盖约 300 平方公里，其中包括一大片浓黑的重油。环境专家警告说，由于海湾水域浅、半封闭，与外海的水体交换有限，被困住的石油可能在环境中存留数年。泄漏的石油已经冲上伊朗、阿曼和阿拉伯联合酋长国的海岸，一些海滩不得不暂时关闭。自然保护人士对脆弱的海洋栖息地、珊瑚礁以及海龟、濒危的阿拉伯海座头鲸等珍稀物种深感担忧。

confirm /kәn'fә:m/ vt. 证实；确定；批准；使巩固e.g. Please confirm your seat before Friday.💡 场合：职场、新闻报道和日常口语都常用；表示核实某事属实或确认安排，偏正式。
detect /di'tekt/ vt. 发现；察觉；探测e.g. The camera can detect movement at night.💡 场合：正式书面、科技新闻常用，日常口语里更常说 find / notice；表示用仪器或检查发现不易察觉的东西。
slick /slik/ a. 浮油；水面上的油层e.g. Workers are cleaning up the oil slick near the beach.💡 场合：新闻报道和环保话题常用词；日常口语里很少说，一般直接说 oil on the water。
vulnerable /'vʌlnәrәbl/ a. 易受伤害的；有弱点的；易受影响的；脆弱的；成局的e.g. Young children are more vulnerable to the flu.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，日常口语里也说；表示容易受到伤害或影响，语气较正式。
endangered /in'deindʒәd/ a. 有生命危险的；濒临灭绝的e.g. The giant panda is an endangered animal in China.💡 场合：正式书面、科普和新闻报道常用，日常口语里也说；多用于 endangered animals / species。
conservationist /,kәnsә'veiʃәnist/ n. 自然资源保护论者e.g. Conservationists are working to save the coral reefs.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，日常口语里更常说 people who protect nature；多用于环保、动物保护话题。
temporarily /'tempәrәrәli/ adv. 暂时；一时；临时e.g. The shop is closed temporarily for repairs.💡 场合：新闻报道、职场和日常口语都常用；表示“短时间地、暂时地”，中性。
wash onto 短语 （水流）把……冲上（岸/路面）e.g. The waves washed the bottles onto the beach.💡 场合：新闻报道和日常口语都常用，描述水流把东西带到岸上或某处，中性。
be concerned about 短语 对……深感担忧e.g. Many parents are concerned about the new school rules.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，日常口语里更常说 worry about；语气比 worry 更正式、更克制。
consist of 短语 由……组成e.g. Our English group consists of six teachers and two students.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，日常口语里更常说 be made up of；中性，偏正式。
remain in 短语 滞留在……中；一直留在e.g. The smell of the paint remained in the room for days.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，表示“继续留在某处/某种状态”，中性，偏正式。
¶ 3

Difficult words: slick (a film or layer of oil floating on water, especially one that has leaked from a ship), semi-enclosed (partially surrounded or shut in by land or barriers, limiting connection to the open sea), habitat (the natural home or environment of an animal, plant, or other organism).

生词：slick（漂浮在水面上的油膜，尤指从船上泄漏的油层）；semi-enclosed（半封闭的：部分被陆地或屏障包围、与外海连通有限的）；habitat（栖息地：动植物等生物的天然家园或生存环境）。

habitat /'hæbitæt/ n. 栖息地；居留地；自生地；聚集处e.g. The forest is the natural habitat of many birds.💡 场合：正式书面、科普和新闻报道常用，日常口语里更常说 home 或 living place；多用于动物、植物的生存环境。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. The storm caused ____ damage to the old bridge.
✓ severe　严格的；尖锐的；严肃的；严重的；严厉的；朴素的
The storm caused severe damage to the old bridge.
这场暴风雨给那座旧桥造成了严重损坏。
2. The smell of the paint ____ the room for days.
✓ remained in（remain in 的变形）　滞留在……中；一直留在
The smell of the paint remained in the room for days.
油漆味在房间里滞留了好几天。
3. We spent the holiday in a small ____ town.
✓ coastal　海岸的；沿海的；沿岸的
We spent the holiday in a small coastal town.
我们在一个沿海小镇度了假。
4. The forest is the natural ____ of many birds.
✓ habitat　栖息地；居留地；自生地；聚集处
The forest is the natural habitat of many birds.
这片森林是许多鸟类的天然栖息地。
5. Our English group ____ six teachers and two students.
✓ consists of（consist of 的变形）　由……组成
Our English group consists of six teachers and two students.
我们的英语小组由六位老师和两名学生组成。
6. ____ are working to save the coral reefs.
✓ Conservationists（conservationist 的变形）　自然资源保护论者
Conservationists are working to save the coral reefs.
自然保护人士正在努力拯救珊瑚礁。
7. Smoking ____ your health more than you think.
✓ threatens（threaten 的变形）　恐吓；威胁；预示...的凶兆
Smoking threatens your health more than you think.
吸烟对健康的威胁比你想象的更大。
8. Please ____ your seat before Friday.
✓ confirm　证实；确定；批准；使巩固
Please confirm your seat before Friday.
请在周五之前确认你的座位。
9. Workers are cleaning up the oil ____ near the beach.
✓ slick　浮油；水面上的油层
Workers are cleaning up the oil slick near the beach.
工人们正在清理海滩附近的浮油。
10. Many parents ____ the new school rules.
✓ are concerned about（be concerned about 的变形）　对……深感担忧
Many parents are concerned about the new school rules.
很多家长对新的校规深感担忧。
11. Young children are more ____ to the flu.
✓ vulnerable　易受伤害的；有弱点的；易受影响的；脆弱的；成局的
Young children are more vulnerable to the flu.
小孩子更容易得流感。
12. We saw many ____ animals at the aquarium.
✓ marine　舰队；水兵；海景画
We saw many marine animals at the aquarium.
我们在水族馆看到了许多海洋动物。
13. The camera can ____ movement at night.
✓ detect　发现；察觉；探测
The camera can detect movement at night.
这台摄像机能在夜间探测到动静。
14. The giant panda is an ____ animal in China.
✓ endangered　有生命危险的；濒临灭绝的
The giant panda is an endangered animal in China.
大熊猫是中国的一种濒危动物。
15. The shop is closed ____ for repairs.
✓ temporarily　暂时；一时；临时
The shop is closed temporarily for repairs.
这家店因维修暂时关闭。
