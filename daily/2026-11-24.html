1. U.S. brings an end to production of one-cent coin

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-11-23 · 原文链接
📖 美国正式停止生产一分钱硬币：造一枚要花 3.69 美分，亏本买卖终于叫停，每年能省 8500 万美元。不过硬币仍可继续流通，商家找零要凑整到五美分。这篇文章带你回望一分钱 200 多年的历史，看它如何从日常零钱变成"负担"。
¶ 1

The United States has officially brought an end to the production of its one-cent coin. President Donald Trump signed the Common Cents Act on Friday. However, the coin will remain legal tender and continue circulating. The US Mint had actually stopped manufacturing the penny (the nickname for the coin) in November 2025. Businesses now have to round cash transactions up or down to the nearest five cents when exact change is unavailable. The decision to discontinue minting the penny was an economic one. In 2024, each coin cost 3.69 cents to produce. The bill's author, Lisa McClain, said: "This legislation will save $85 million per year by permanently ending penny production. It's common sense."

美国已正式停止生产一分钱硬币。特朗普总统于周五签署了《通用美分法案》（Common Cents Act）。不过，这种硬币仍是法定货币，可以继续流通。美国铸币局实际上早在 2025 年 11 月就已停止铸造一分钱（penny 是这种硬币的昵称）。如今商家在无法找零时，必须把现金交易金额向上或向下凑整到最接近的五美分。停产一分钱的决定是出于经济考虑。2024 年，每枚硬币的生产成本为 3.69 美分。该法案的起草人丽莎·麦克莱恩说："这项立法将通过永久停止生产一分钱，每年节省 8500 万美元。这是常识。"

tender /'tendә/ a. 法定货币（用于 legal tender）e.g. This old coin is no longer legal tender in our country.💡 场合：正式书面和法律、金融语境；日常口语很少单独说 tender，多说 cash / money。
discontinue /.diskәn'tinju:/ vi. 中断；终止；停止e.g. The shop discontinued that shampoo because few people bought it.💡 场合：正式书面和商业语境常用，指产品停产、服务终止；口语多说 stop making / selling。
legislation /.ledʒis'leiʃәn/ n. 立法；法律e.g. New legislation will protect workers from long hours.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面高频词；日常口语多说 law。
round up / round down 短语 向上 / 向下凑整e.g. The shop rounds prices up to the nearest dollar.💡 场合：日常口语和商业场景常用，数学、购物、账单中都常见；round up 还可表示"召集、聚集"。
common sense 短语 常识e.g. Wearing a helmet when you ride a bike is just common sense.💡 场合：日常口语常用，语气肯定，有时带一点"这还用说吗"的意味。
¶ 2

The penny's history dates back to the founding years of the United States. Congress established the US Mint in 1792. The following year, the mint distributed more than 11,000 one-cent coins. The penny underwent numerous design changes. Early designs included an image of Liberty, a Native American chief's headdress, and an eagle. In 1909, it featured a portrait of former President Abraham Lincoln to commemorate the centenary of his birth. The last major redesign was in 2010. Rising manufacturing costs gradually undermined the coin's viability, consigning it to history. Wikipedia stated that the coin was eventually "viewed as a burden to businesses, banks, government, and the public".

一分钱的历史可以追溯到美国建国之初。国会于 1792 年设立了美国铸币局。次年，铸币局发行了 1.1 万多枚一分钱硬币。一分钱经历了多次设计变更。早期设计包括自由女神像、印第安酋长头饰和雄鹰图案。1909 年，为纪念林肯总统诞辰 100 周年，硬币上印上了他的肖像。最近一次重大改版是在 2010 年。不断上涨的制造成本逐渐损害了这种硬币的实用价值，最终把它送进了历史。维基百科称，这枚硬币最终被"视为企业、银行、政府和公众的负担"。

distribute /di'stribju:t/ vt. 分配；散布；分发e.g. Teachers distribute the exam papers before the test starts.💡 场合：正式书面和工作场景常用；日常口语多说 give out / hand out。
undergo /.ʌndә'gәu/ vt. 遭受；经历；忍受e.g. The old library is undergoing a big repair this year.💡 场合：正式书面常用，多用于经历变化、治疗、检查；口语中较少用。
commemorate /kә'memәreit/ vt. 纪念e.g. Every year, people gather here to commemorate the heroes.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用；日常口语多说 remember / honour。
viability /.vaiә'biliti/ n. 生存能力；发育能力e.g. High costs made people question the viability of the small shop.💡 场合：正式书面和商业、政策语境；日常口语多说 whether it can work / survive。
burden /'bә:dn/ n. 负担；重载；担子；责任e.g. Paying for two homes at once was a heavy burden for the family.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常用，常与 heavy 搭配；也可作动词"使负担沉重"。
bring an end to 短语 结束、终止（某事）e.g. The school brought an end to the old lunch rule last month.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用，比 stop 更正式，多用于政策、制度或长期习惯的终结。
date back to 短语 追溯到……（起源于）e.g. This old bridge dates back to the 1800s.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用，讲历史、起源时使用，比 come from 更书面。
be viewed as 短语 被看作、被视为e.g. Working from home is now viewed as normal in many companies.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面常用，表达公众或普遍看法；口语中更常用 people think...。

1. They ____ a small school in the village ten years ago.
✓ established（establish 的变形）　建立；确立；制定
They established a small school in the village ten years ago.
他们十年前在村里建了一所小学。
2. This company ____ toys for children all over the world.
✓ manufactures（manufacture 的变形）　产品；制造
This company manufactures toys for children all over the world.
这家公司为世界各地的孩子生产玩具。
3. The shop ____ that shampoo because few people bought it.
✓ discontinued（discontinue 的变形）　中断；终止；停止
The shop discontinued that shampoo because few people bought it.
这家店停售了那款洗发水，因为买的人很少。
4. Every year, people gather here to ____ the heroes.
✓ commemorate　纪念
Every year, people gather here to commemorate the heroes.
每年人们都会聚集在这里纪念英雄们。
5. The factory cut ____ of old phones this year.
✓ production　制造；生产；产物
The factory cut production of old phones this year.
这家工厂今年削减了旧款手机的产量。
6. This old coin is no longer legal ____ in our country.
✓ tender　法定货币（用于 legal tender）
This old coin is no longer legal tender in our country.
这种旧硬币在我们国家已不再是法定货币。
7. Teachers ____ the exam papers before the test starts.
✓ distribute　分配；散布；分发
Teachers distribute the exam papers before the test starts.
考试开始前，老师会分发试卷。
8. Paying for two homes at once was a heavy ____ for the family.
✓ burden　负担；重载；担子；责任
Paying for two homes at once was a heavy burden for the family.
同时供两套房子的开销对这个家庭来说是个沉重的负担。
9. High costs made people question the ____ of the small shop.
✓ viability　生存能力；发育能力
High costs made people question the viability of the small shop.
高昂的成本让人们质疑这家小店能否维持下去。
10. This old bridge ____ the 1800s.
✓ dates back to（date back to 的变形）　追溯到……（起源于）
This old bridge dates back to the 1800s.
这座老桥可以追溯到 19 世纪。
11. Old banknotes still ____ in small towns.
✓ circulate　(使)流通；(使)循环；(使)传播
Old banknotes still circulate in small towns.
旧版纸币在小城镇里仍在流通。
12. The old library is ____ a big repair this year.
✓ undergoing（undergo 的变形）　遭受；经历；忍受
The old library is undergoing a big repair this year.
这座老图书馆今年正在经历一次大修。
13. New ____ will protect workers from long hours.
✓ legislation　立法；法律
New legislation will protect workers from long hours.
新立法将保护工人免受长时间工作之苦。
14. Wearing a helmet when you ride a bike is just ____.
✓ common sense　常识
Wearing a helmet when you ride a bike is just common sense.
骑车戴头盔就是常识。
