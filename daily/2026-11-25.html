1. India has too much rice

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-24 · 原文链接
📖 印度如今大米多到装不下——这本是现代农业的胜利，却成了政府的财政和政治难题。文章讲清了"丰收过剩"如何变成负担：仓库爆满、补贴高企、地下水告急，还引发了贸易摩擦。值得一读：一个看似"好消息"背后的连锁反应，词汇也多与经济、农业相关。
¶ 1

Thanks to modern plant science and generous government subsidies, India has become one of the world’s largest rice producers.

得益于现代植物科学和政府慷慨的补贴，印度已成为世界上最大的大米生产国之一。

generous /'dʒenәrәs/ a. 慷慨的；有雅量的；大量的；丰富的e.g. She is a very generous neighbour — she always shares her food with us.💡 场合：日常口语和书面都常用，夸人"大方"时最自然的词，褒义。
be reluctant to do sth 短语 不愿做某事e.g. He is reluctant to start his homework on his own.💡 场合：正式书面和日常口语都常见，语气比 don't want 更委婉、更书面。
¶ 2

Decades ago, India struggled to feed its population, so the government introduced guaranteed minimum prices, and scientists created new rice plants that survive heavy floods. As a result, India’s annual rice production has grown by 50 percent in ten years, which created a massive surplus that makes up 40 percent of global rice exports.

几十年前，印度还难以养活本国人口，于是政府推出了有保障的最低收购价，科学家也培育出了能在严重洪涝中存活的新水稻品种。结果，印度的大米年产量在十年里增长了 50%，形成了巨大的过剩，占全球大米出口量的 40%。

surplus /'sә:plәs/ n. 剩余；过剩；盈余e.g. We have a big surplus of rice at home.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面常用，日常口语里更常说"剩了好多/多余的"。
guaranteed a. 有保证的；；有人担保的e.g. I bought a phone with a guaranteed one-year repair service.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都常见，购物、合同、政策里高频，表示"有保证的"。
flood /flʌd/ n. 洪水；大量之水；涨潮e.g. The heavy rain caused a flood in the village.💡 场合：新闻报道高频词，日常口语里说"发大水/被水淹了"更常见。
production /prә'dʌkʃәn/ n. 制造；生产；产物e.g. The factory's production has doubled this year.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面高频词，说"产量"时用；日常口语里常说"产了多少"。
make up 短语 占（比例）；构成e.g. Rent makes up almost half of my monthly spending.💡 场合：新闻报道和日常口语都常用，表示"占（比例）/构成"，中性。
struggle to do sth 短语 费力做某事；勉强做到e.g. He struggled to carry the heavy desk upstairs by himself.💡 场合：日常口语和书面都常见，表示"费力做某事"，中性偏书面。
¶ 3

However, this big success is now causing serious financial and political problems. Warehouses are overflowing, and the government spends over 24 billion dollars a year on food subsidies. This means Indian taxpayers are effectively paying for cheap food sold abroad. Rival countries like the United States and Thailand complain that India’s cheap exports hurt their own farmers.

然而，这一巨大成功如今却带来了严重的财政和政治问题。仓库已经装不下了，政府每年在食品补贴上的花费超过 240 亿美元。这意味着印度纳税人实际上是在为销往国外的廉价食品买单。美国、泰国等竞争国家抱怨，印度廉价出口损害了本国农民的利益。

warehouse /'wєәhaus/ n. 仓库；货栈；大商店e.g. The goods are stored in a huge warehouse outside the city.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都常用，网购、物流场景高频词。
taxpayer /'tækspeiә/ n. 纳税人e.g. Taxpayers pay for the new roads and schools.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面高频词，日常口语里常说"交税的人/老百姓的钱"。
rival /'raivl/ n. 对手；竞争者e.g. The two brands are rivals in the same market.💡 场合：新闻报道和日常口语都常用，商业、体育、学习竞争场景都合适。
pay for 短语 为……付钱；为……付出代价e.g. In the end, someone always has to pay for very cheap prices.💡 场合：日常口语常用，也可用于新闻评论；说"付出代价"时带一点无奈或批评的语气。
¶ 4

Meanwhile, growing so much rice strains groundwater supplies and leaves less land for other essential crops like oilseeds and pulses. Yet politicians remain reluctant to cut farmer bonuses because they fear losing rural votes.

与此同时，种这么多大米消耗了大量地下水，还挤占了油料作物、豆类等其他重要作物的耕地。然而，政客们却不愿削减给农民的补贴，因为他们担心失去农村选票。

reluctant /ri'lʌktәnt/ a. 不情愿的；勉强的e.g. He was reluctant to get out of bed on a cold morning.💡 场合：正式书面和日常口语都常见，语气比 don't want 更委婉、更书面。
strain /strein/ n. 使紧张；消耗（供应）e.g. Working late every night strains my eyes and my back.💡 场合：正式书面和日常口语都常见；说身体、资源、关系"吃不消"时用，比 stress 更具体。
essential /i'senʃәl/ n. 要素；要点；本质e.g. Water and medicine are essential for a long trip.💡 场合：日常口语和正式书面都高频，比 necessary 语气更强，表示"本质上不可或缺"。
¶ 5

Difficult words: subsidy (money paid by a government or organization to help keep the price of a product or service low, or to support an industry like farming), surplus (an amount of something more than what is needed or used), reluctant (unwilling and hesitant).

生词：subsidy（政府或组织为保持某种产品或服务价格低廉、或扶持农业等产业而支付的钱）、surplus（超出需要或使用量的数量）、reluctant（不情愿的、犹豫的）。

subsidy /'sʌbsidi/ n. 补助金；津贴e.g. The government gives farmers a subsidy to keep growing rice.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻报道常用词，日常口语里更常说"政府给的钱/补助"。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（14 题）
1. In the end, someone always has to ____ very cheap prices.
✓ pay for　为……付钱；为……付出代价
In the end, someone always has to pay for very cheap prices.
到头来，超低的价格总要有人为之买单。
2. Rent ____ almost half of my monthly spending.
✓ makes up（make up 的变形）　占（比例）；构成
Rent makes up almost half of my monthly spending.
房租几乎占了我每月开销的一半。
3. Working late every night ____ my eyes and my back.
✓ strains（strain 的变形）　使紧张；消耗（供应）
Working late every night strains my eyes and my back.
天天熬夜加班，眼睛和后背都吃不消了。
4. The factory's ____ has doubled this year.
✓ production　制造；生产；产物
The factory's production has doubled this year.
这家工厂今年的产量翻了一番。
5. The two brands are ____ in the same market.
✓ rivals（rival 的变形）　对手；竞争者
The two brands are rivals in the same market.
这两个品牌是同一市场里的竞争对手。
6. The goods are stored in a huge ____ outside the city.
✓ warehouse　仓库；货栈；大商店
The goods are stored in a huge warehouse outside the city.
货物都存在城外一个巨大的仓库里。
7. She is a very ____ neighbour — she always shares her food with us.
✓ generous　慷慨的；有雅量的；大量的；丰富的
She is a very generous neighbour — she always shares her food with us.
她是个非常慷慨的邻居，总和我们分享她做的饭菜。
8. The government gives farmers a ____ to keep growing rice.
✓ subsidy　补助金；津贴
The government gives farmers a subsidy to keep growing rice.
政府给农民发补贴，让他们继续种水稻。
9. I bought a phone with a ____ one-year repair service.
✓ guaranteed　有保证的；；有人担保的
I bought a phone with a guaranteed one-year repair service.
我买的手机带有一年保修服务。
10. The heavy rain caused a ____ in the village.
✓ flood　洪水；大量之水；涨潮
The heavy rain caused a flood in the village.
暴雨在村里引发了洪水。
11. Water and medicine are ____ for a long trip.
✓ essential　要素；要点；本质
Water and medicine are essential for a long trip.
长途旅行，水和药是必不可少的。
12. He was ____ to get out of bed on a cold morning.
✓ reluctant　不情愿的；勉强的
He was reluctant to get out of bed on a cold morning.
大冷天早上，他一点儿也不想起床。
13. ____ pay for the new roads and schools.
✓ Taxpayers（taxpayer 的变形）　纳税人
Taxpayers pay for the new roads and schools.
纳税人出钱修了新路和新学校。
14. We have a big ____ of rice at home.
✓ surplus　剩余；过剩；盈余
We have a big surplus of rice at home.
家里剩了好多大米。
