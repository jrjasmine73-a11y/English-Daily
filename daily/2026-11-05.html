1. Japan Launches New Agency to Deal with Disasters

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-11-05 · 原文链接
📖 日本新成立国家灾害管理厅，专职应对地震、海啸等灾害。关键信息：日本位于太平洋“火环”地带，却一直没有专门的灾害管理机构，如今面对南海海槽大地震等潜在威胁，终于补上这一短板。值得读：了解一个灾害大国如何系统性提升防灾能力，也能学到不少防灾应急的实用表达。
¶ 1

Japan has launched a new disaster management agency to improve the country's preparedness for major earthquakes, tsunamis and volcanic eruptions.

日本新成立了一个灾害管理机构，旨在提升国家应对大地震、海啸和火山爆发的准备能力。

disaster /di'zɑ:stә/ n. 灾祸；不幸；彻底失败e.g. The flood was a terrible disaster for the village.
preparedness /pri'pєәdnis/ n. 准备状态；战备状态e.g. Good preparedness can save lives in an emergency.
¶ 2

The National Disaster Management Agency, which began operating on November 2, has more than 350 staff members responsible for disaster preparedness, emergency response and reconstruction efforts.

国家灾害管理厅于11月2日开始运行，拥有350多名工作人员，负责灾害预防、应急响应和灾后重建工作。

reconstruction /.ri:kәn'strʌkʃәn/ n. 改造；再建e.g. After the storm, reconstruction of the town took two years.
be prepared for 短语 为……做好准备e.g. We should always be prepared for a power cut during a storm.
be responsible for 短语 负责……e.g. Our team is responsible for checking the fire alarms every month.
¶ 3

Japan, which lies along the Pacific "Ring of Fire," is one of the world's most disaster-prone countries but has never had an agency dedicated to disaster management. Instead, the country has relied on temporary response teams assembled from different government ministries and departments.

日本位于太平洋“火环”地带，是世界上最容易遭受灾害的国家之一，但此前从未设立专门的灾害管理机构，而是依赖由不同政府部门临时组建的应对小组。

lie along 短语 位于……沿线e.g. The town lies along the river, so it floods easily in summer.
¶ 4

As part of the launch, the agency urged local governments and residents to check the quake resistance of their homes, make sure their furniture is safely anchored, evaluate their evacuation procedures and stock up on emergency supplies.

机构成立之际，呼吁地方政府和居民检查房屋的抗震性能，确保家具固定牢靠，评估疏散流程，并储备应急物资。

evacuation /i.vækju'eiʃәn/ n. 撤退；疏散e.g. The evacuation went smoothly because everyone followed the plan.
anchor /'æŋkә/ n. 锚e.g. Please anchor the bookshelf to the wall so it won't fall over.
stock up on 短语 储备……e.g. Before winter comes, we stock up on warm clothes and canned food.
¶ 5

The agency was not established in response to any specific threat, but amid concerns about Japan's ability to cope with another crisis on the scale of the 2011 quake and tsunami, which killed nearly 20,000 people, and triggered the Fukushima nuclear disaster.

该机构的成立并非针对某一具体威胁，而是源于人们对日本能否应对另一场2011年地震海啸规模危机的担忧——那场灾难造成近2万人死亡，并引发了福岛核事故。

cope with 短语 应对……e.g. A good plan helps a small town cope with a sudden flood.
¶ 6

Experts have warned of the possibility of major quakes in the coming decades and officials want people to be prepared.

专家警告，未来几十年内可能发生大地震，官员希望民众做好准备。

¶ 7

The biggest worry is a megaquake along the Nankai Trough, an undersea trench off Japan's southeastern coast. According to government estimates, such a disaster could kill 298,000 people, force 6.5 million to evacuate and destroy 2.35 million homes.

最令人担忧的是沿南海海槽发生的大地震，这是一条位于日本东南沿海的海底沟槽。据政府估计，这样的灾难可能造成29.8万人死亡，迫使650万人撤离，并摧毁235万所房屋。

estimate /'estimeit/ n. 估计；判断e.g. Can you estimate how much time we need for this trip?
¶ 8

A major earthquake in metropolitan Tokyo could cause another 18,000 deaths, while an eruption of Mount Fuji could affect 790,000 people and disrupt transportation, power lines and other infrastructure.

东京都会区的一场大地震可能再造成1.8万人死亡，而富士山喷发可能影响79万人，并扰乱交通、电力线路等基础设施。

eruption /i'rʌpʃәn/ n. 爆发；喷出物；出疹e.g. The volcano's eruption forced thousands of people to leave their homes.
infrastructure /'infrәstrʌktʃә/ n. 基础结构；基础设施e.g. The city is investing in better infrastructure, like roads and bridges.
disrupt /dis'rʌpt/ a. 分裂的；中断的e.g. Heavy rain disrupted the train service this morning.
¶ 9

Japan has long been criticized for its poorly equipped evacuation centers, where people are typically crowded into gymnasiums, community centers and classrooms that lack adequate privacy, bedding, clean toilets or hot food.

日本长期以来因避难所条件简陋而受到批评，人们通常挤在体育馆、社区中心和教室里，缺乏足够的隐私、被褥、干净的厕所和热食。

adequate /'ædikwәt/ a. 适当的；足够的e.g. Make sure you get adequate sleep before the exam.
criticized /ˈkritisaizd/ vt. 批评(criticize的过去式)e.g. The plan was criticized for being too expensive.
¶ 10

The new agency aims to change that by bringing in clean portable toilets, kitchens serving hot food, bathing facilities and cardboard beds.

新机构希望通过引入干净的移动厕所、提供热食的厨房、洗浴设施和纸板床来改变这一状况。

portable /'pɒ:tәbl/ a. 可携带的；可搬运的；可移动的e.g. We bought a portable heater for our camping trip.
¶ 11

The importance of better shelters became clear after the 2011 disaster, when {{prn:3,810|three thousand eight hundred and ten}} people died from causes linked to harsh living conditions following the earthquake and tsunami.

改善避难所的重要性在2011年灾后凸显出来，当时有3810人死于与地震海啸后恶劣生活条件相关的原因。

2. New EU deforestation law

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-04 · 原文链接
📖 欧盟反森林砍伐法年底生效在即，却在执法环节迎来首场考验。关键信息：调查发现多家国际大牌购买的棕榈油涉嫌来自印尼非法砍伐的种植园。值得读：看懂一部环保法规如何从纸面走向现实，也能学到环保与贸易议题的常用表达。
¶ 1

The EU’s anti-deforestation law faces an enforcement test ahead of its launch on 30 December 2026.

欧盟的反森林砍伐法在2026年12月30日正式实施前，正面临一场执法考验。

enforcement /in'fɒ:smәnt/ n. 执行；强制e.g. Strict enforcement of the law protects wildlife.
deforestation /di:.fɒ:ri'steiʃәn/ n. 采伐森林；森林开伐e.g. Deforestation harms animals that live in the forest.
¶ 2

Under the new rules, companies selling goods such as palm oil, coffee, and cocoa must prove their products are not linked to land cleared after 2020.

根据新规，销售棕榈油、咖啡和可可等商品的公司必须证明其产品与2020年之后被砍伐的土地无关。

¶ 3

However, recent investigations by Indonesian environmental organizations reveal that major international consumer brands risk breaching these regulations. Reports show that companies like PepsiCo, Unilever, and Ferrero have bought palm oil from Indonesian plantations accused of recent illegal deforestation. Despite heavy political pressure and lobbying from major exporting nations like the United States and Indonesia to delay or soften the legislation, environmental activists insist the EU must strictly enforce the rules.

然而，印尼环保组织的最新调查显示，一些大型国际消费品牌有违反这些规定的风险。报告指出，百事可乐、联合利华和费列罗等公司从被指控近期非法砍伐森林的印尼种植园购买了棕榈油。尽管美国和印尼等主要出口国施加了巨大的政治压力并进行游说，要求推迟或放宽该法案，环保活动人士仍坚持欧盟必须严格执行这些规定。

Indonesia has lost vast areas of forest to illegal logging and agricultural clearing, which increased its vulnerability to severe wildfires. Experts emphasize that strict enforcement by European authorities will be essential to hold global corporations accountable and halt worldwide forest destruction.

印尼已因非法伐木和农业开垦失去了大片森林，这增加了该国遭受严重野火的脆弱性。专家强调，欧洲当局的严格执法对于追究全球大公司的责任、遏制全球森林破坏至关重要。
