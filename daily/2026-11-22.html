1. Strict rules for students in South Korea

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-20 · 原文链接
📖 韩国高考在即，50 多万考生同场竞技，而一场作弊丑闻让官方祭出史上最严防作弊手段——严查智能眼镜、屏蔽公共 Wi-Fi。短短三段，带你读懂高压之下的韩国高考，以及藏在考场背后的教育公平之争。
¶ 1

South Korea is introducing strict anti-cheating measures ahead of its national college entrance exam, where over 500,000 students compete for scores that shape their future universities, careers, and social standing.

韩国正在推行严格的反作弊措施，全国高考在即，50 多万考生将为决定未来大学、职业和社会地位的分数展开竞争。

measure /'meʒә/ n. 尺寸；量度器；量度标准；测量；量具；程度；范围；限度；分寸；措施；方法e.g. The company took measures to protect its workers from the heat.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用，日常口语更常说 step 或 action；常与 take 搭配。
compete /kәm'pi:t/ vi. 竞争；对抗e.g. Ten students will compete for the three places on the school team.💡 场合：日常口语和正式场合都常用，常与 for（争夺某物）或 with / against（与某人竞争）搭配。
shape /ʃeip/ n. 形状；形态；外形；形式；身材e.g. Reading every day has shaped the way I think.💡 场合：正式和日常都可用，常用于“经历塑造性格、决定塑造未来”这类抽象搭配，比 decide 更形象。
shape one's future 短语 决定某人的未来e.g. The books you read as a child can shape your future.💡 场合：正式和日常都可用，常用于谈教育、选择对人生的深远影响；比 decide 更有画面感。
¶ 2

The government’s crackdown follows a major scandal in which the top scorer of a national tax accountant exam confessed to using a hidden camera, a smartphone, and generative AI to obtain answers. Officials are now targeting smart glasses and hidden recording devices, deploying specially trained supervisors across 1,300 test sites to inspect students’ eye wear, and warning that cheaters face criminal prosecution.

此次严打源于一起重大丑闻：一名全国税务会计师考试的状元承认，他用隐藏摄像头、智能手机和生成式人工智能获取答案。官方如今正严查智能眼镜和隐藏录音设备，在 1300 个考点部署了受过专门培训的监考员，检查学生的眼镜，并警告作弊者将面临刑事起诉。

crackdown /'krækdaun/ n. 制裁；镇压；痛击e.g. The city launched a crackdown on illegal parking last month.💡 场合：新闻报道常用，语气正式且强硬，常与 on 搭配（a crackdown on...），指官方针对某种不良行为的集中整治。
scandal /'skændәl/ n. 丑闻；中伤；耻辱；反感；流言蜚语e.g. The company tried to hide the scandal, but reporters found out.💡 场合：新闻报道常用，语气较重，指公众人物或机构的丑闻；日常聊天说八卦时也可用，略带调侃。
confess /kәn'fes/ v. 承认；坦白；忏悔；供认e.g. The boy confessed that he had broken the window.💡 场合：日常和正式都可用，语气比 admit 更重，常指承认错误或罪行；也可用于表白心意（confess one's love），语气郑重。
obtain /әb'tein/ vt. 获得；达到e.g. You need a letter from your school to obtain a student visa.💡 场合：正式书面用语，新闻、考试、公文中常见，意思是“得到、获得”；日常口语一般用 get，写作用 obtain 更显正式。
deploy /di'plɒi/ v. 展开；配置e.g. The company deployed fifty workers to fix the road overnight.💡 场合：正式用语，新闻和职场常见，指“调派、部署”人力或设备；科技领域也指“部署（系统、软件）”，日常口语很少用。
inspect /in'spekt/ vt. 检查；检阅；检验e.g. Please inspect the goods before you sign for them.💡 场合：正式和日常都可用，指“仔细检查”，常用于官方检查、验货、安检；比 check 更正式、更仔细。
prosecution /.prɒsi'kju:ʃәn/ n. 执行；经营；起诉e.g. Selling fake medicine can lead to prosecution.💡 场合：法律术语，新闻报道常用，指“起诉、检控”，语气非常正式；日常口语一般说“告上法庭”（take sb. to court）。
crack down on 短语 严厉打击；大力取缔e.g. The government is cracking down on fake news on the Internet.💡 场合：新闻和正式场合常用，语气强硬，指官方对某种违法或不良行为采取严厉行动；日常口语也可用。
face criminal prosecution 短语 面临刑事起诉e.g. Anyone who sells fake medicine will face criminal prosecution.💡 场合：新闻和法律语境专用，非常正式；日常口语说“要被起诉”（face prosecution 就够了），criminal 强调刑事性质。
¶ 3

Despite intense academic pressure and calls to delay the test, the education ministry refused to postpone it. However, analysts doubt whether basic steps like blocking public Wi-Fi will stop tech-savvy cheaters, and note that expensive private tutoring already creates an uneven playing field in South Korea’s highly competitive education system.

尽管学业压力巨大，也有人呼吁推迟考试，但教育部拒绝延期。不过，分析人士怀疑，屏蔽公共 Wi-Fi 这类基本措施能否拦住精通技术的作弊者，并指出，昂贵的课外补习已经让韩国竞争激烈的教育体系变得不公平。

postpone /pәust'pәun/ vt. 延迟；使延期；缓办；搁延e.g. The football match was postponed because of the heavy rain.💡 场合：日常和正式都常用，指“推迟、延期”，比 delay 更正式一点；常用于会议、考试、活动。
uneven /.ʌn'i:vәn/ a. 不均等的（机会、条件不公平）e.g. The old road is uneven, so please watch your step.💡 场合：日常和正式都可用，既指地面、桌子“不平”，也指机会、分配“不均等”；新闻里常用后一种意思。
an uneven playing field 短语 不公平的竞争环境e.g. Small shops face an uneven playing field when they compete with big online stores.💡 场合：新闻评论和正式讨论常用，是比喻说法，指竞争双方起点、条件不平等；日常口语也可用于聊教育、职场竞争。
¶ 4

Difficult words: crackdown (severe action taken by authorities to stop illegal or unwanted behavior), tech-savvy (well-informed about or proficient in the use of modern technology, especially computers and digital devices), uneven (not equal, fair, or balanced in opportunity, quality, or distribution).

生词：crackdown（严厉打击；当局为制止非法或不良行为而采取的严厉行动），tech-savvy（精通技术的；对现代技术，尤其是电脑和数码设备非常了解或运用熟练），uneven（不均等的；机会、质量或分配上不平等、不公平）。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. Reading every day has ____ the way I think.
✓ shaped（shape 的变形）　形状；形态；外形；形式；身材
Reading every day has shaped the way I think.
每天阅读塑造了我的思维方式。
2. The company tried to hide the ____, but reporters found out.
✓ scandal　丑闻；中伤；耻辱；反感；流言蜚语
The company tried to hide the scandal, but reporters found out.
公司想掩盖这起丑闻，但记者还是发现了。
3. Small shops face ____ when they compete with big online stores.
✓ an uneven playing field　不公平的竞争环境
Small shops face an uneven playing field when they compete with big online stores.
小商店和大型网店竞争时，处境并不公平。
4. Selling fake medicine can lead to ____.
✓ prosecution　执行；经营；起诉
Selling fake medicine can lead to prosecution.
售卖假药可能会被起诉。
5. Ten students will ____ for the three places on the school team.
✓ compete　竞争；对抗
Ten students will compete for the three places on the school team.
十名学生将争夺校队仅有的三个名额。
6. You need a letter from your school to ____ a student visa.
✓ obtain　获得；达到
You need a letter from your school to obtain a student visa.
你需要学校的一封证明信才能拿到学生签证。
7. The government is ____ fake news on the Internet.
✓ cracking down on（crack down on 的变形）　严厉打击；大力取缔
The government is cracking down on fake news on the Internet.
政府正在严厉打击网上的假新闻。
8. The company ____ fifty workers to fix the road overnight.
✓ deployed（deploy 的变形）　展开；配置
The company deployed fifty workers to fix the road overnight.
公司连夜调派了五十名工人去修路。
9. Please ____ the goods before you sign for them.
✓ inspect　检查；检阅；检验
Please inspect the goods before you sign for them.
签收之前请先仔细检查货物。
10. The company took ____ to protect its workers from the heat.
✓ measures（measure 的变形）　尺寸；量度器；量度标准；测量；量具；程度；范围；限度；分寸；措施；方法
The company took measures to protect its workers from the heat.
公司采取了措施保护工人免受高温影响。
11. The football match was ____ because of the heavy rain.
✓ postponed（postpone 的变形）　延迟；使延期；缓办；搁延
The football match was postponed because of the heavy rain.
足球比赛因大雨推迟了。
12. The old road is ____, so please watch your step.
✓ uneven　不均等的（机会、条件不公平）
The old road is uneven, so please watch your step.
这条老路坑坑洼洼的，走路请小心。
13. Anyone who sells fake medicine will ____.
✓ face criminal prosecution　面临刑事起诉
Anyone who sells fake medicine will face criminal prosecution.
任何售卖假药的人都将面临刑事起诉。
14. The boy ____ that he had broken the window.
✓ confessed（confess 的变形）　承认；坦白；忏悔；供认
The boy confessed that he had broken the window.
那个男孩承认是他打碎了窗户。
15. The city launched a ____ on illegal parking last month.
✓ crackdown　制裁；镇压；痛击
The city launched a crackdown on illegal parking last month.
上个月，这座城市开展了一次针对违章停车的严厉整治。
