1. Russell Brand goes to prison

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-11 · 原文链接
📖 英国喜剧演员拉塞尔·布兰德因强奸和性侵被判有罪，即将面临长期监禁。伦敦警方表示，成功的判决往往能鼓励更多受害者站出来举报。这篇新闻用词简洁，集中出现了定罪、裁决、举报等高频法律词汇，适合学习新闻英语里的法律表达。
¶ 1

Following the high-profile conviction of British comedian and actor Russell Brand for rape and sexual assault, UK police chiefs expect more victims to step forward.

继英国喜剧演员拉塞尔·布兰德因强奸和性侵被判有罪之后，英国警方负责人预计会有更多受害者站出来。

conviction /kәn'vikʃәn/ n. 定罪；信服；坚信e.g. The court announced his conviction on Friday.💡 场合：新闻报道和法律语境常用，指法庭正式判定有罪；日常口语里更常说 be found guilty。
assault /ә'sɒ:t/ n. 攻击；袭击e.g. The woman reported the assault to the police.💡 场合：新闻和法律语境，指对他人身体的攻击或侵犯；sexual assault 特指性侵犯，语气严肃。
step forward 短语 主动站出来e.g. More victims stepped forward to report what happened to them.💡 场合：新闻语境高频，指受害者或证人主动站出来举报、作证；日常也可用于主动请缨。
¶ 2

London’s Metropolitan Police Commissioner stated that successful court verdicts often give additional survivors the confidence to report past abuse, knowing they will be believed. Brand, who previously starred in Hollywood films and was once married to pop singer Katy Perry, faces a lengthy prison sentence after a London court found him guilty of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others. He also faces ongoing investigations regarding additional allegations, including one involving a 16-year-old girl at the time of the incident.

伦敦警察局局长表示，成功的法庭判决常常能让更多幸存者有信心举报过去遭受的虐待，因为他们知道自己会被相信。布兰德曾主演好莱坞电影，也曾与流行歌手凯蒂·佩里结婚，如今伦敦一家法庭裁定他强奸一名女性、性侵另外三名女性，他将面临长期监禁。他还面临与更多指控有关的持续调查，其中包括一起事发时受害者年仅 16 岁的案件。

verdict /'vә:dikt/ n. 裁决；判决；判断性意见；定论；结论e.g. The jury gave a guilty verdict after three days.💡 场合：法律和新闻语境，指陪审团或法庭的正式裁决；日常可引申为“（对某事的）判断、结论”。
survivor /sә'vaivә/ n. 幸存者；挺过来的受害者e.g. The survivor now helps other people in the same situation.💡 场合：新闻和正式语境常用，比 victim 更强调“挺过来、活下来”；日常口语多用于灾难、事故后。
abuse /ә'bju:s.ә'bju:z/ n. 虐待e.g. She reported years of abuse to the police.💡 场合：新闻和正式语境，指对人或权力的伤害性使用；日常口语中说 abuse 常指虐待，语气较重。
lengthy /'leŋθi/ a. 冗长的；漫长的e.g. The lengthy meeting left everyone feeling tired.💡 场合：正式和新闻语境常用，形容时间或篇幅过长，略带“拖沓”的意味；日常口语多说 long。
guilty /'gilti/ a. 犯罪的；有过失的；自觉有错的；心虚的e.g. The court found him guilty of theft.💡 场合：法律语境指“有罪的”，日常口语多指“内疚的”；两种意思都很常用。
be found guilty of 短语 被判犯有……罪e.g. He was found guilty of stealing from the shop.💡 场合：法律和新闻语境固定搭配；日常聊天说“被判有罪”时也用它。
give someone the confidence to do something 短语 给某人信心去做某事e.g. My words gave him the confidence to walk onto the stage.💡 场合：日常和正式语境都常用，表达“鼓励某人去做某事”；口语里也很自然。
¶ 3

British Prime Minister praised the victims for their courage in bringing the horrific crimes to light. As one of Britain’s most prominent public figures convicted of sex offenses in recent years, Brand remains in custody and is awaiting his formal sentencing hearing.

英国首相称赞受害者们勇敢地揭露了这些骇人的罪行。作为近年来英国被判性犯罪的最知名的公众人物之一，布兰德目前仍被羁押，等待正式量刑听证会。

courage /'kʌridʒ/ n. 勇气；胆量e.g. It took a lot of courage for her to speak on stage.💡 场合：通用词，正式和日常都常用；常和 have / take / show 搭配。
horrific /hɒ'rifik/ a. 令人毛骨悚然的；感到恐惧的e.g. The news showed horrific pictures of the accident.💡 场合：新闻和正式语境，指令人震惊的惨状；语气比 terrible 更重，日常口语较少用。
prominent /'prɒminәnt/ a. 卓越的；显著的；突出的；凸出的e.g. The prominent doctor answered questions about the new treatment.💡 场合：正式和新闻语境，指有名望、有地位的人或显眼的位置；日常口语多说 famous。
custody /'kʌstәdi/ n. 羁押e.g. The police took the man into custody last night.💡 场合：法律和新闻语境，指被警方拘留；日常也指孩子的监护权（child custody）。
bring to light 短语 揭露；使真相大白e.g. Her report brought the company's cheating to light.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面语，指把隐藏的事公之于众；日常口语多说 reveal / expose。
in custody 短语 被羁押e.g. The suspect is in custody and waits for his trial.💡 场合：新闻和法律语境，指人被警方扣留；日常口语很少用。
¶ 4

Difficult words: conviction (a formal declaration by a court of law that someone is guilty of a criminal offense), allegation (a claim that someone has done something illegal or wrong, typically one made without immediate proof), courage (the ability to do something frightening or difficult without showing fear).

难词：conviction（法庭正式判定某人犯有刑事罪行）、allegation（指控某人做了违法或错误的事，通常指未经立即证实的说法）、courage（做可怕或困难的事时不表现恐惧的能力）。

allegation /.æli'geiʃәn/ n. 断言；主张；申辩e.g. The actor faces new allegations from several people.💡 场合：新闻和法律语境高频词，指未经证实的指控；比 charge 更正式，语气上“尚未证实”。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. She reported years of ____ to the police.
✓ abuse　虐待
She reported years of abuse to the police.
她向警方举报了多年的虐待行为。
2. More victims ____ to report what happened to them.
✓ stepped forward（step forward 的变形）　主动站出来
More victims stepped forward to report what happened to them.
更多受害者站出来，举报了发生在自己身上的事。
3. The jury gave a guilty ____ after three days.
✓ verdict　裁决；判决；判断性意见；定论；结论
The jury gave a guilty verdict after three days.
经过三天的讨论，陪审团作出了有罪裁决。
4. The news showed ____ pictures of the accident.
✓ horrific　令人毛骨悚然的；感到恐惧的
The news showed horrific pictures of the accident.
新闻播出了事故现场可怕的画面。
5. The ____ meeting left everyone feeling tired.
✓ lengthy　冗长的；漫长的
The lengthy meeting left everyone feeling tired.
这场漫长的会议让每个人都累了。
6. It took a lot of ____ for her to speak on stage.
✓ courage　勇气；胆量
It took a lot of courage for her to speak on stage.
她鼓起很大勇气才站上舞台讲话。
7. The court announced his ____ on Friday.
✓ conviction　定罪；信服；坚信
The court announced his conviction on Friday.
法庭在周五宣布了对他的定罪。
8. The actor faces new ____ from several people.
✓ allegations（allegation 的变形）　断言；主张；申辩
The actor faces new allegations from several people.
这位演员面临几个人的新指控。
9. The woman reported the ____ to the police.
✓ assault　攻击；袭击
The woman reported the assault to the police.
这名女子向警方报了案，称自己遭到袭击。
10. The ____ now helps other people in the same situation.
✓ survivor　幸存者；挺过来的受害者
The survivor now helps other people in the same situation.
这位幸存者现在帮助处境相同的人。
11. The ____ doctor answered questions about the new treatment.
✓ prominent　卓越的；显著的；突出的；凸出的
The prominent doctor answered questions about the new treatment.
这位著名的医生回答了有关新疗法的问题。
12. The suspect is ____ and waits for his trial.
✓ in custody　被羁押
The suspect is in custody and waits for his trial.
嫌疑人正被羁押，等待审判。
13. The police took the man into ____ last night.
✓ custody　羁押
The police took the man into custody last night.
警方昨晚把这名男子拘留了。
14. He ____ stealing from the shop.
✓ was found guilty of（be found guilty of 的变形）　被判犯有……罪
He was found guilty of stealing from the shop.
他被判犯有从商店偷窃的罪行。
15. The court found him ____ of theft.
✓ guilty　犯罪的；有过失的；自觉有错的；心虚的
The court found him guilty of theft.
法庭判定他犯有盗窃罪。
