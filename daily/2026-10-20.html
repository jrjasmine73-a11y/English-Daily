1. Magnitude 7.1 Earthquake Hits Japan's Kumamoto

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-07-29 · 原文链接
📖 7月28日，日本九州熊本县发生7.1级强震，造成至少13人死亡、数百人受伤，购物中心坍塌、列车脱轨，超过26万人被建议撤离。值得读，因为这篇报道浓缩了灾难新闻的核心词汇——从 tsunami advisory（海啸预警）到 evacuate（疏散），学完就能看懂突发新闻的英文报道。
¶ 1

A 7.1 magnitude earthquake shook Kumamoto prefecture on Japan's southern island of Kyushu on July 28, leaving at least 13 people dead and hundreds more injured. Many people are also missing after part of a shopping center and a huge chimney at a paper factory collapsed. A tsunami advisory was issued but quickly lifted.

7月28日，一场7.1级地震袭击了日本南部九州岛的熊本县，造成至少13人死亡、数百人受伤。购物中心局部坍塌、造纸厂的一根巨大烟囱倒塌后，还有许多人下落不明。日本曾发布海啸预警，但很快解除。

magnitude /'mægnitju:d/ n. 巨大；重大；重要；大小；数量；星等(指星的亮度)e.g. The magnitude of the problem is hard to measure.
tsunami /tsu'nɑ:mi/ n. 海啸e.g. A tsunami warning was issued after the earthquake.
advisory /әd'vaizәri/ a. （文中）预警，警示e.g. The weather service issued a travel advisory for the area.
¶ 2

In Kashima Town, the quake collapsed the second floor of the Aeon Mall shopping center, trapping an unknown number of people there. Japan's NHK national television said the collapse came after an explosion, believed to have been triggered by a gas leak.

在嘉岛町，地震震塌了永旺梦乐城购物中心（Aeon Mall）的二楼，不知多少人被困其中。日本NHK国家电视台称，坍塌发生前曾出现爆炸，据信是由燃气泄漏引发的。

collapse /kә'læps/ n. 崩溃；倒塌；虚脱e.g. The old bridge collapsed in the flood.
explosion /ik'splәuʒәn/ n. 爆发；激增；爆炸(声)e.g. The explosion shattered windows nearby.
trigger /'trigә/ n. 触发器；扳机e.g. A gas leak triggered the fire.
be trapped underneath 短语 被困在……下面，动弹不得e.g. The cat was trapped underneath the fallen bookshelf.
be suspended for safety checks 短语 暂停运营以进行安全检查e.g. All flights were suspended for safety checks after the warning.
¶ 3

At least eight people have been pulled out of the rubble, but others remain unaccounted for, the Kumamoto prefectural emergency response team said, as the search and rescue effort continued.

熊本县应急响应小组表示，救援人员已从废墟中救出至少8人，但仍有其他人下落不明，搜救工作仍在继续。

rubble /'rʌbl/ n. 粗石；碎砖；粗石堆e.g. Workers cleared the rubble from the street.
remain unaccounted for 短语 下落不明e.g. After the storm, two hikers remained unaccounted for.
¶ 4

At the Nippon Paper Industries' Yatsushiro factory, 11 people were trapped underneath a collapsed chimney. Two are believed to be dead, while two others sustained serious injuries. The other seven remain unaccounted for.

在日本制纸公司（Nippon Paper Industries）八代工厂，有11人被压在倒塌的烟囱下。据信其中2人已经死亡，另有2人重伤，其余7人仍下落不明。

sustain serious injuries 短语 受重伤e.g. He sustained serious injuries in a car crash last year.
¶ 5

The Fire and Disaster Management Agency said more than 260,000 people were advised to evacuate, most of them in Kumamoto prefecture, but also in neighboring Nagasaki prefecture.

日本消防厅（Fire and Disaster Management Agency）表示，超过26万人被建议撤离，其中大多在熊本县，邻近的长崎县也有部分居民。

evacuate /i'vækjueit/ v. 疏散；撤出；排泄e.g. Residents were told to evacuate the building at once.
¶ 6

Japan's Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi told journalists there were reports of damage to roads, bridges and buildings, as well as blackouts and fires, though details were unclear.

日本首相高市早苗告诉记者，已接到道路、桥梁和建筑物受损以及停电、火灾的报告，但具体细节尚不清楚。

¶ 7

According to NHK, more than 250 people have been treated at local hospitals and several hospitals have been hit by power outages.

据NHK报道，已有超过250人在当地医院接受治疗，多家医院遭遇停电。

¶ 8

Shinkansen bullet trains and local trains in Kyushu were suspended for safety checks, and the runway at Aso Kumamoto Airport was also closed.

九州的新干线子弹头列车和普通列车已暂停运营进行安全检查，阿苏熊本机场的跑道也已关闭。

¶ 9

The Japanese Defense Ministry said it sent military aircraft to the area to assess the situation.

日本防卫省表示，已派出军用飞机前往灾区评估情况。

assess /ә'ses/ vt. 估定；对...征税；评定e.g. We need time to assess the damage.
assess the situation 短语 评估情况e.g. Let's assess the situation before we make a decision.
¶ 10

A train derailed and fell on its side at Yatsushiro Station, and stone walls were damaged at Kumamoto Castle. The castle was badly damaged in a previous quake in 2016 and is still being repaired, Kyodo News said.

一列火车在八代站脱轨侧翻，熊本城的石墙也受损。据共同社报道，熊本城在2016年的地震中曾严重受损，至今仍在修复中。

derail /di'reil/ vt. 使出轨e.g. The train derailed, but luckily no one was hurt.
¶ 11

Kumamoto was hit with a deadly quake in 2016 that killed at least 50 people.

熊本在2016年曾遭遇致命地震，造成至少50人死亡。

¶ 12

Shinji Kiyomoto with the Japan Meteorological Agency urged residents to be cautious over the next two to three days.

日本气象厅的清本真二（Shinji Kiyomoto）敦促居民在未来两到三天内保持警惕。

cautious /'kɒ:ʃәs/ a. 谨慎的；小心的e.g. Be cautious when you drive at night.

2. The best countries to retire

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-19 · 原文链接
📖 一项新的全球退休指数出炉：乌拉圭超越葡萄牙，成为全世界最适合海外退休的国家。报告比较了46个国家的退休项目——欧洲在生活品质上遥遥领先，却在税收上丢分不少。值得读：全是 lifestyle 类高频词（retire abroad、quality of life、taxation），正好为“你想去哪儿养老”这类口语话题攒表达弹药。
¶ 1

In a new global retirement index, Uruguay ranked as the best place in the world to retire abroad, and took the top spot from Portugal.

在一项新的全球退休指数中，乌拉圭被评为全世界最适合海外退休的地方，从葡萄牙手中夺走了榜首位置。

retire /ri'taiә/ n. 隐居e.g. My dad plans to retire at sixty.
abroad /ә'brɒ:d/ adv. 往国外；到室外；到处e.g. She studied abroad for two years.
index /'indeks/ n. （文中）指数，排行榜e.g. The price index went up last month.
rank /ræŋk/ n. 等级；排；横列；队伍；阶级e.g. Our city ranks third for air quality.
rank high for 短语 在……方面排名靠前e.g. This school ranks high for student happiness.
¶ 2

The study compared 46 retirement and passive-income programs across several areas, including quality of life, travel freedom, citizenship routes, taxation, and costs. While Uruguay leads overall due to balanced scores, Europe remains the most represented region. Six spots are in the top ten, including Spain, Portugal, Latvia, Andorra, Italy, and Greece.

这项研究比较了46个退休和被动收入项目，涵盖生活品质、旅行自由度、入籍途径、税收和生活成本等多个方面。乌拉圭凭借各项均衡的得分领跑，但欧洲仍是上榜最多的地区：前十名中有六席，包括西班牙、葡萄牙、拉脱维亚、安道尔、意大利和希腊。

compare /kәm'pєә/ vt. 比较；比喻；对照e.g. It's unfair to compare kids with each other.
route /ru:t/ n. （文中）途径e.g. Is there a faster route to the station?
taxation /tæk'seiʃәn/ n. 课税；征税；抽税；税款；估定的税额e.g. High taxation can slow down small businesses.
represent /.repri'zent/ vt. 表现；表示；描绘；讲述；代表；象征；回忆；再赠送；再上演e.g. Each color on the map represents a different region.
across several areas 短语 在多个方面，涵盖多个领域e.g. We need to cut costs across several areas.
¶ 3

European nations excel in living standards, strong passports, and safety, but often lose points for higher taxes and complex rules. For example, Spain ranks high for quality of life but lowest for taxes, while Portugal slipped to fifth place after increasing its citizenship requirement to ten years. Meanwhile, other regions offer different perks, such as tax benefits in Mauritius and the Middle East, or great affordability and fast citizenship across the Americas.

欧洲国家在生活水准、护照含金量和安全方面表现出色，但常因较高的税收和复杂的规则丢分。例如，西班牙在生活品质上排名靠前，却在税收一项排名垫底；葡萄牙将入籍居住要求提高到十年后，跌到了第五名。与此同时，其他地区则有不同的优势，比如毛里求斯和中东地区的税收优惠，或美洲国家的高性价比与快速入籍。

excel /ik'sel/ vt. 胜过；优于；擅长e.g. He excels at fixing old bikes.
slip /slip/ n. 滑；滑行；事故；溜；差错；滑台；下降；插条；后裔；板条；瘦长的年轻人e.g. She slipped to third place in the final round.
lose points for 短语 因……丢分e.g. He lost points for messy handwriting in the exam.
excel in 短语 在……方面表现出色e.g. She excels in speaking but needs to work on her writing.
¶ 4

Difficult words: taxation (the system of collecting taxes from citizens and businesses by the government), excel (to be extremely good at something or perform much better than others), perk (an extra advantage or benefit given in addition to regular pay or status).

难词：taxation（政府向公民和企业征税的制度），excel（在某方面极其出色，表现远超他人），perk（除常规薪资或身份之外的额外好处、福利）。

perk /pә:k/ vi. 昂首；振作；举止高傲；神气活现；滤煮e.g. Free lunch is a nice perk of this job.
take the top spot 短语 夺得榜首e.g. Our team took the top spot in the singing contest.
