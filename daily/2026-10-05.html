1. An artist paints on water

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2025-10-08 · 原文链接
📖 突尼斯艺术家 Zohra Zarougui 用古老的水拓画技艺 Ebru 在水面上作画，这是突尼斯从未有人教授的艺术，她为此远赴海外求学多年才将这门技艺带回祖国。一篇温暖的文化新闻，适合学习描述艺术创作和坚持追梦的表达。
¶ 1

Zohra Zarougui, a Tunisian artist, paints on water using an ancient technique of marbling called Ebru.

突尼斯艺术家 Zohra Zarougui 用一种叫做 Ebru 的古老水拓技艺在水面上作画。

¶ 2

This art is not taught in Tunisia, so Zarougui had to travel and study abroad. She got a master’s degree, and after years of research and experimentation, she returned to Tunisia.

突尼斯国内没有教授这门艺术的学校，因此 Zarougui 不得不远赴海外求学。她获得了硕士学位，经过多年研究与实践后，回到了突尼斯。

¶ 3

The artist creates pieces from patterns from old cities in the Arab world. She sprinkles brightly colored dyes into pans of oily water and then transfers the patterns onto a piece of paper canvas. According to Zarougui, it takes years of studying to be able to bring out the history of this art and its techniques and methods. Her experience lasted several years outside the country, and it took a lot of courage and sacrifice.

这位艺术家的作品取材于阿拉伯世界古老城市的纹样。她将色彩鲜艳的染料洒进盛满油质水面的盘中，再把图案转印到纸画布上。据 Zarougui 说，要真正展现这门艺术的历史及其技法，需要多年的研习。她的海外求学经历持续了好几年，需要极大的勇气和付出。

be not taught in … 短语 在……没有教授/不被教授e.g. This art is not taught in Tunisia, so Zarougui had to travel and study abroad.
transfer … onto … 短语 把……转印/转移到……上e.g. She sprinkles dyes into pans of oily water and then transfers the patterns onto a piece of paper canvas.
bring out the history of … 短语 展现……的历史e.g. It takes years of studying to be able to bring out the history of this art and its techniques.
take courage and sacrifice 短语 需要勇气和付出e.g. Her experience lasted several years outside the country, and it took a lot of courage and sacrifice.
¶ 4

The art of Ebru first appeared and evolved in the 15th century in Persia and Ottoman Turkey. Some of the methods use colors which float on the top of a bath of liquid mucilage.

Ebru 这种艺术最早于 15 世纪出现在波斯和奥斯曼土耳其，并在那里发展演变。有些技法使用的颜料漂浮在一池黏稠胶液的表面。

¶ 5

Difficult words: dye (a substance that people use to change the color of clothes, hair, and so on), sacrifice (to give up something that you like or want to keep to get something else or help someone), mucilage (a thick gooey substance that’ comes from plants and some microorganisms).

难词：dye（染料，人们用来改变衣服、头发等颜色的物质），sacrifice（为了得到别的东西或帮助他人而放弃自己喜欢或想要的东西；牺牲），mucilage（黏液，一种来自植物和某些微生物的浓稠黏性物质）。

2. First Germany-Russia meeting since 2022

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-02 · 原文链接
📖 德国外长 Wadephul 与俄罗斯外长 Lavrov 在联合国大会期间举行了 20 分钟的短暂会晤，这是 2022 年俄乌冲突全面升级以来德俄高层官员的首次直接接触。关键看点：会晤发生在德国指责俄罗斯策划莱比锡机场无人机事件、局势持续升级的背景下，象征着直接对话渠道的重新开启。
¶ 1

German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul and Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov held a brief 20-minute meeting on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York.

德国外长 Johann Wadephul 与俄罗斯外长 Sergei Lavrov 在纽约联合国大会期间举行了一场简短的 20 分钟会晤。

on the sidelines of … 短语 在……的场外/间隙e.g. The two ministers held a brief meeting on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York.
¶ 2

This marked the first direct diplomatic encounter between top German and Russian officials since Moscow’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine more than four years ago.

这是自四年多前俄罗斯对乌克兰发动全面入侵以来，德俄两国高层官员的首次直接外交接触。

¶ 3

The meeting took place despite escalating tensions, following German accusations that Russia orchestrated a drone attack at Leipzig/Halle airport. During the talks, Wadephul urged Moscow to abandon its dangerous path of escalation toward Germany, Europe, and NATO. While Lavrov downplayed the event’s importance and claimed Germany made no position changes, diplomats noted the symbolic weight of reopening a direct channel. Both sides disputed who initiated the exchange.

此次会晤在紧张局势不断升级的背景下举行——德国此前指责俄罗斯策划了莱比锡/哈雷机场的无人机袭击事件。会谈中，Wadephul 敦促莫斯科放弃针对德国、欧洲和北约的危险升级之路。Lavrov 则淡化了这次会晤的重要性，并称德国并未改变立场，但外交人士指出，重新开启直接沟通渠道具有象征意义。双方还就谁是会晤发起方各执一词。

downplay the importance of … 短语 淡化……的重要性e.g. While Lavrov downplayed the event's importance, diplomats noted the symbolic weight of reopening a direct channel.
abandon one's path of escalation 短语 放弃升级之路e.g. Wadephul urged Moscow to abandon its dangerous path of escalation toward Germany, Europe, and NATO.
¶ 4

Despite Germany’s primary role as a strategic supporter of Ukraine rather than a mediator, the meeting reflects broader shifts in diplomatic discussions as international leaders search for ways toward future negotiations.

尽管德国的角色主要是乌克兰的战略支持者而非调停者，但这次会晤反映了外交讨论的广泛转变——国际领导人正在为未来的谈判寻找出路。

search for ways toward … 短语 为……寻找出路e.g. International leaders search for ways toward future negotiations.
¶ 5

Difficult words: sideline (a place or occasion outside the main event where unofficial talks happen), orchestrate (to plan and organize a complex event or secret action carefully), downplay (to make something seem less important, serious, or bad than it actually is).

难词：sideline（场外，正式活动之外非官方会谈的场所或时机），orchestrate（精心策划、组织复杂的事件或秘密行动），downplay（轻描淡写，使某事显得不如实际那样重要、严重或糟糕）。
