1. Good news of October

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-30 · 原文链接
📖 这是一份十月份全球好消息的盘点：亚马孙森林砍伐大幅下降、野生老虎在消失 70 年后重返哈萨克斯坦、眼部植入物帮助患者恢复视力、艾滋病毒感染降至 30 年低点。好消息横跨环保、医学和科技，篇幅短、信息密，适合快速阅读并积累"积极进展"类表达。
¶ 1

A roundup of positive global developments highlights encouraging progress across conservation, medicine, and technology.

一份全球积极进展的盘点，突显了自然保护、医学和科技领域鼓舞人心的进步。

roundup /'raundʌp/ n. 驱集；集拢；综述e.g. Here's a quick roundup of today's sports news.
highlight /'hailait/ n. 加亮区；精彩场面e.g. Let me highlight the three most important points for you.
encouraging /in'kʌridʒiŋ/ a. 鼓励的；促进的e.g. Your progress this month is really encouraging.
conservation /.kɒnsә'veiʃәn/ n. 保护；保存e.g. We all need to do more for wildlife conservation.
¶ 2

Environmental reports reveal that deforestation in the Brazilian Amazon dropped significantly, and lead to the lowest fire activity in decades, while wild tigers returned to Kazakhstan after 70 years. In Canada, Indigenous nations partnered with the federal government to establish a massive marine reserve.

环境方面的报告显示，巴西亚马孙地区的森林砍伐大幅下降，火灾活动降至几十年来的最低水平，而野生老虎在消失 70 年后重返哈萨克斯坦。在加拿大，原住民部落与联邦政府合作建立了一个大面积的海洋保护区。

significantly adv. 值得注目地；意味深长地e.g. My English has improved significantly this year.
establish /i'stæbliʃ/ vt. 建立；确立；制定e.g. They plan to establish a new library in our town.
massive /'mæsiv/ a. 大而重的；宽大的；宏伟的e.g. It was a massive party with over two hundred guests.
lead to 短语 导致、引起e.g. Too much screen time can lead to poor sleep.
partner with 短语 与……合作e.g. We will partner with a local farm to grow vegetables.
return to 短语 回到、重返e.g. After ten years away, he finally returned to his hometown.
¶ 3

Medical breakthroughs feature a world-first eye implant that restores vision for patients with age-related macular degeneration, a fast 10-second liquid treatment stopping tooth decay in children, and a new mRNA flu vaccine showing improved effectiveness. Meanwhile, global public health initiatives reached historic milestones. HIV infections have fallen to a 30-year low, and digital tracking helps health workers vaccinate over 200,000 previously unreached children in Nigeria. Finally, European regulations now require digital watermarks on AI, and global electric vehicle sales surged to nearly 30 percent of total market share.

医学突破包括世界首例眼部植入物，帮助老年性黄斑变性患者恢复视力；一种只需 10 秒的液体疗法可以阻止儿童龋齿；还有一种新型 mRNA 流感疫苗，效果更好。与此同时，全球公共卫生行动也取得了历史性里程碑：艾滋病毒感染降至 30 年来的最低水平，数字追踪帮助卫生工作者为尼日利亚 20 多万此前未覆盖到的儿童接种了疫苗。最后，欧洲出台法规，要求给人工智能内容加上数字水印，全球电动汽车销量也大幅增长，接近总市场份额的 30%。

breakthrough /'breikθru:/ n. 突破e.g. Scientists made a big breakthrough in cancer treatment.
restore /ri'stɒ:/ vt. 回复；恢复；归还；修补；修复e.g. The operation helped restore his vision.
effectiveness /i'fektivnis/ n. 效力e.g. The new medicine shows great effectiveness.
historic /hi'stɒrik/ a. 历史上著名的；有历史性的e.g. It was a historic day for our school.
surge /sә:dʒ/ n. 巨涌；汹涌；澎湃e.g. Sales of electric cars surged last year.
reach a milestone 短语 达到一个里程碑e.g. She reached a big milestone when she finished her first book.
¶ 4

Difficult words: implant (a medical device, tissue, or object that is placed inside the body through surgery), macular degeneration (an eye disease that causes vision loss in the center of the field of vision as people age), watermark (a hidden or visible digital mark in a file or image to identify its source).

生词：implant（植入物：通过手术置入体内的医疗器械、组织或物体），macular degeneration（黄斑变性：一种随年龄增长导致中心视野丧失的眼病），watermark（水印：文件中用于标明来源的隐藏或可见的数字标记）。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. It was a ____ day for our school.
✓ historic　历史上著名的；有历史性的
It was a historic day for our school.
那是我们学校具有历史意义的一天。
2. Here's a quick ____ of today's sports news.
✓ roundup　驱集；集拢；综述
Here's a quick roundup of today's sports news.
这里是今天体育新闻的快速盘点。
3. Scientists made a big ____ in cancer treatment.
✓ breakthrough　突破
Scientists made a big breakthrough in cancer treatment.
科学家在癌症治疗方面取得了重大突破。
4. My English has improved ____ this year.
✓ significantly　值得注目地；意味深长地
My English has improved significantly this year.
我的英语今年进步了很多。
5. The operation helped ____ his vision.
✓ restore　回复；恢复；归还；修补；修复
The operation helped restore his vision.
这次手术帮助他恢复了视力。
6. The new medicine shows great ____.
✓ effectiveness　效力
The new medicine shows great effectiveness.
这种新药效果很好。
7. After ten years away, he finally ____ his hometown.
✓ return to　回到、重返
After ten years away, he finally returned to his hometown.
离开十年后，他终于回到了家乡。
8. It was a ____ party with over two hundred guests.
✓ massive　大而重的；宽大的；宏伟的
It was a massive party with over two hundred guests.
那是一场有两百多位客人的盛大聚会。
9. They plan to ____ a new library in our town.
✓ establish　建立；确立；制定
They plan to establish a new library in our town.
他们计划在我们镇上建一座新图书馆。
10. Let me ____ the three most important points for you.
✓ highlight　加亮区；精彩场面
Let me highlight the three most important points for you.
让我为你强调最重要的三点。
11. We all need to do more for wildlife ____.
✓ conservation　保护；保存
We all need to do more for wildlife conservation.
我们都需要为野生动物保护多做一点。
12. We will ____ a local farm to grow vegetables.
✓ partner with　与……合作
We will partner with a local farm to grow vegetables.
我们将与当地一家农场合作种菜。
13. Sales of electric cars ____ last year.
✓ surge　巨涌；汹涌；澎湃
Sales of electric cars surged last year.
去年电动汽车的销量大幅增长。
14. Your progress this month is really ____.
✓ encouraging　鼓励的；促进的
Your progress this month is really encouraging.
你这个月的进步真的很鼓舞人心。
15. Too much screen time can ____ poor sleep.
✓ lead to　导致、引起
Too much screen time can lead to poor sleep.
看屏幕时间太长会导致睡眠不好。
