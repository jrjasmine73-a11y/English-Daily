1. Musk slows down internet for Ukraine

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-27 · 原文链接
📖 报道称，马斯克亲自批准放缓乌克兰的星链服务，而这一卫星网络对乌克兰的军事防御至关重要。关键信息：这一限制影响了无人机与导弹拦截，华盛顿方面要求彻查。值得读：看单个私营企业主如何在战时对军事通信产生巨大影响，以及随之而来的权力集中之争。
¶ 1

Recent news reports reveal that Elon Musk personally authorized a slowdown of Starlink, the military-grade satellite network crucial to Ukraine’s defense against Russia.

近期新闻报道披露，埃隆·马斯克亲自批准放缓星链（Starlink）服务——这一军用级卫星网络对乌克兰抵御俄罗斯的防御至关重要。

crucial /'kru:ʃәl/ a. 决定性的；重要的；严厉的e.g. Water is crucial for our health.
authorize a slowdown 短语 批准放缓e.g. The manager authorized a slowdown in production for safety checks.
¶ 2

The restriction severely hampered Ukrainian military operations, affected drone capabilities and missile interceptions. While some US officials suggest Musk acted on his own, other sources indicate he discussed the decision with President Trump and administration members. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed that Starlink service remains limited, though American officials promised to help resolve the issue.

这一限制严重妨碍了乌克兰的军事行动，影响了无人机能力和导弹拦截。一些美国官员认为马斯克是自行其是，另一些消息来源则称他曾与特朗普总统及政府成员讨论过这一决定。乌克兰总统泽连斯基证实，星链服务目前仍受限，不过美国官员承诺帮助解决这一问题。

restriction /ri'strikʃәn/ n. 限制；限定；约束e.g. The new rule is a restriction on late-night noise.
hampered /ˈhæmpəd/ v. 妨碍；束缚；限制( hamper的过去式和过去分词 )e.g. Heavy rain hampered our travel plans.
capabilities /ˌkeɪpəˈbɪlɪti:z/ n. 能力( capability的名词复数 ); 可能; 容量; [复数]潜在能力e.g. This phone has amazing camera capabilities.
officials /ə'fɪʃls/ n. 行政官员( official的复数形式 )e.g. Local officials visited the school today.
confirmed /kәn'fә:md/ a. 被证实的；根深蒂固的；坚定的e.g. She confirmed the meeting time by text.
act on one's own 短语 自行其是；独自行动e.g. She decided to act on her own and book the tickets.
¶ 3

The incident triggered strong outrage in Washington, and lawmakers call for a full investigation into potential contract violations and illegal interference. Commentators and politicians expressed deep concern over the dangerous concentration of geopolitical power in the hands of a single private citizen during wartime.

这一事件在华盛顿引发强烈愤慨，议员们呼吁对可能违反合同和非法干预的行为展开全面调查。评论员和政客们对战时单个普通公民手中集中了危险的地缘政治权力深表担忧。

investigation /in.vesti'geiʃәn/ n. 调查；审查e.g. Police opened an investigation into the fire.
interference /.intә'fiәrәns/ n. 冲突；干涉e.g. Please work without any interference.
concentration /.kɒnsәn'treiʃәn/ n. 集中；专心e.g. The concentration of power worried many people.

concentration of power 短语 权力集中e.g. Too much concentration of power in one office is risky.
¶ 4

Difficult words: hamper (to delay something or prevent it from progressing), interception (stopping or catching something before it reaches its destination), violation (breaking a rule, law, or agreement).

难词：hamper（拖延某事或阻碍其进展），interception（在某物到达目的地之前将其拦截或截获），violation（违反规则、法律或协议）。

2. Mummy removed from exhibition over 'lack of consent'

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-10-26 · 原文链接
📖 苏格兰格拉斯哥一家博物馆把古埃及木乃伊遗骸撤出展览，理由是无法证明逝者生前同意展出。关键信息：此举引发了“政治正确走过头”的批评，博物馆则以当代博物馆伦理为自己辩护。值得读：看“尊重逝者”与“展示历史”两种立场如何交锋，顺便学一组博物馆与伦理话题的表达。
¶ 1

A museum in Scotland has removed the remains of an ancient Egyptian mummy from an exhibition because there is no documented evidence that the deceased gave consent for their remains to be displayed. Glasgow's Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum took away the remains of a priest named Nakht, a mummified male head, and other body parts. In place of the exhibits, visitors found signs explaining that the remains had been removed "as a mark of respect". The museum said the decision "reflects contemporary museum practice". It added: "In the absence of consent, we believe removing ancient Egyptian human remains from display is more respectful to the deceased individuals."

苏格兰一家博物馆已将一具古埃及木乃伊的遗骸撤出展览，因为没有书面证据表明逝者曾同意将其遗骸展出。格拉斯哥的凯尔文格罗夫美术馆与博物馆移走了祭司纳赫特的遗骸、一颗木乃伊化的男性头颅和其他身体部位。展品原处换上了标牌，向参观者解释遗骸已被移走，“以示尊重”。博物馆表示，这一决定“反映了当代博物馆的做法”。它还补充道：“在没有征得同意的情况下，我们认为将古埃及人类遗骸撤出展出，是对逝者个人更尊重的做法。”

remains /ri'meins/ n. 剩余物；废墟；残余e.g. The remains of the old castle stand on the hill.
exhibition /.eksi'biʃәn/ n. 表现；展览会；展览品e.g. We visited a photo exhibition last weekend.
documented a. 备有证明文件的e.g. Every step of the trip was well documented.
evidence /'evidәns/ n. 根据；证据；迹象e.g. There is no evidence that he broke the rule.
deceased /di'si:st/ a. 已故的e.g. We should respect the wishes of the deceased.
consent /kәn'sent/ n. 同意；许可e.g. The teacher asked for parents' consent first.
as a mark of respect 短语 以示尊重e.g. We stood in silence as a mark of respect.
in the absence of 短语 在缺乏……的情况下e.g. In the absence of rain, farmers watered the fields by hand.
¶ 2

The decision has provoked widespread criticism. Scottish politician Murdo Fraser called the move "woke madness". He argued that museums should showcase history rather than hide it. A museum visitor complained that his daughter was upset at not being able to see the exhibits. An education campaigner said the decision "represents the triumph of educational stupidity and ignorance". He added: "It is not possible to seek permission from those who died thousands of years ago." However, the museum defended its action. It cited the International Council of Museums' Code of Ethics, which says museums "should uphold the inherent dignity of human remains".

这一决定引发了广泛批评。苏格兰政客默多·弗雷泽称此举是“觉醒的疯狂”。他认为，博物馆应当展示历史，而不是隐藏历史。一名博物馆参观者抱怨说，他的女儿因为看不到展品而感到难过。一名教育活动人士称，这一决定“代表了教育愚蠢与无知的胜利”。他补充说：“不可能去征求几千年前去世的人的许可。”不过，博物馆为自己的做法辩护。它引用了国际博物馆协会的《职业道德准则》，该准则称博物馆“应当维护人类遗骸固有的尊严”。

provoked /prəˈvəukt/ v. 激起( provoke的过去式和过去分词 ); 惹怒; 引起; 引发e.g. His rude words provoked a loud argument.
criticism /'kritisizm/ n. 批评；评论；非难e.g. The film received a lot of criticism online.
complained /kəmˈpleind/ v. 抱怨；诉苦；投诉( complain的过去式和过去分词 ); 申诉；控诉；抗议;e.g. She complained about the cold food.
campaigner /kæm'peinә/ n. 从军者；老兵；竞选者e.g. The young campaigner speaks for clean rivers.
dignity /'digniti/ n. 尊严；高贵e.g. Treat every patient with dignity.
give consent 短语 表示同意；给予许可e.g. You must give consent before the doctor starts treatment.
showcase history 短语 展示历史e.g. The small museum showcases local history with old photos.
