1. Chef fired for refusing to put pineapple on pizza

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-11-09 · 原文链接
📖 一位意大利厨师因拒绝在披萨上放菠萝而被解雇，还为此打起了官司。关键点：仲裁机构因他错过申诉期限而驳回了申请，他本人则认为菠萝披萨是对意大利文化的“侮辱”。这篇短文语言简单、话题有趣，很适合练习“争议话题”的常用表达。
¶ 1

An Italian chef in Brisbane, Australia, has been fired for refusing to put pineapple on pizzas, among other reasons. He went to the Fair Work Commission to fight his dismissal, but failed to get his job back. The chef stated that topping pizzas with the tropical fruit was an "insult to his culture". He claimed he was sacked from a restaurant in May after repeatedly refusing his boss's instructions to make pineapple and ham pizzas for diners. His employer contended that the refusal was one of several workplace issues. The commission rejected the chef's application because it was submitted after the deadline. The chef has since found a job at another Italian restaurant.

澳大利亚布里斯班的一位意大利厨师因拒绝在披萨上放菠萝等原因被解雇了。他向公平工作委员会申诉，试图讨回工作，但未能如愿。这位厨师表示，在披萨上放这种热带水果是对他文化的“侮辱”。他声称，自己在5月份被一家餐厅解雇，原因是多次拒绝老板的要求，不肯为顾客做菠萝火腿披萨。他的雇主则辩称，拒绝做菠萝披萨只是多项工作问题之一。委员会驳回了厨师的申请，因为他的申请是在截止日期之后提交的。此后，这位厨师已在另一家意大利餐厅找到了工作。

fired n. 因祸得福（电影名）e.g. He got fired last month, but he soon found a better job.💡 场合：职场口语里被解雇最常用的说法就是 be / get fired，比 dismissed 随意得多
dismissal /dis'misәl/ n. 免职；解雇e.g. His dismissal came as a shock to everyone in the office.💡 场合：新闻和法律语境的正式说法，指解雇或驳回；劳资纠纷报道里常见
contend /kәn'tend/ vi. 奋斗；斗争；竞争e.g. She contends that the plan will save the company a lot of money.💡 场合：正式书面词，新闻里指声称、主张，后接 that 从句；也有竞争、争夺的意思
tropical /'trɒpikl/ a. 热带的；热情的e.g. Tropical fruits like mango and papaya are my favourites.💡 场合：地理、气候、饮食话题用词，热带的；tropical fruit 就是菠萝、榴莲这类热带水果
an insult to 短语 对……的侮辱e.g. Leaving the meeting early felt like an insult to the whole team.💡 场合：表示对……的侮辱、冒犯，语气很强；文化争论里常见，日常慎用
fight one's dismissal 短语 为被解雇一事申诉；抗争e.g. He decided to fight his dismissal and asked a lawyer for help.💡 场合：职场法律新闻用语，指对解雇决定不服、申诉打官司讨说法
¶ 2

The question of whether pineapple belongs on pizza is a controversial one. It continues to divide public opinion. Pizza purists are aghast at the idea, but it is an established menu staple in many countries. The Hawaiian pizza was reportedly invented in Canada in 1962 after a chef used ham and pineapple as toppings. The restaurant owner expressed his opinion on it. He admitted that while the pizza was not his "absolute favourite," he would not "turn his nose up at it". Many restaurateurs insist that customers should be free to enjoy whatever ingredients and toppings they choose. Although the Hawaiian pizza is popular internationally, many Italians regard it as "culinary heresy".

菠萝到底该不该放在披萨上，这个问题颇具争议，至今仍让公众意见分裂。披萨纯粹主义者对这个想法感到震惊，但在许多国家，菠萝披萨早已是菜单上的固定菜品。据报道，夏威夷披萨是1962年在加拿大发明的，当时一位厨师用火腿和菠萝做配料。这家餐厅的老板也表达了自己的看法。他承认，虽然这款披萨不是他的“最爱”，但他也不会“嗤之以鼻”。许多餐馆老板坚持认为，顾客应该可以自由享用自己喜欢的任何配料。尽管夏威夷披萨在国际上很受欢迎，许多意大利人仍视之为“美食异端”。

refuse /ri'fju:z/ vt. 拒绝；谢绝e.g. She refused to eat the soup because it was too salty.💡 场合：日常高频，refuse to do sth. 就是拒绝做某事；注意名词 refuse 重音在前，指垃圾、废弃物，别混
controversial /.kɒntrә'vә:ʃәl/ a. 争论的；论争的；被议论的e.g. Smoking in public places is a controversial topic in many countries.💡 场合：谈论争议话题的必备词，新闻评论高频；名词是 controversy 争议、争论
purist /'pjuәrist/ n. 纯粹主义者e.g. As a coffee purist, he never adds sugar or milk.💡 场合：美食、文化、语言话题里指坚持传统、不容改动的纯粹主义者，常带一点调侃意味
aghast /ә'gɑ:st/ a. 惊骇的；吓呆的e.g. We were aghast when we heard the price of the ticket.💡 场合：书面和新闻用词，表示惊骇、吓呆了；日常口语里一般说 shocked 就够了
staple /'steipl/ n. 主要产物；常用品；主要要素；原料；订书钉；钩环e.g. Rice is a staple food in many parts of Asia.💡 场合：饮食话题指主食、日常必备品；也可泛指某个地方或行业的标志性事物
reportedly /ri'pɒ:tidli/ adv. 根据传说；根据传闻；据报道e.g. The singer reportedly left the concert early last night.💡 场合：新闻报道专用词，表示据报道、据说，暗示消息还没有证实；写新闻英语必备
heresy /'herәsi/ n. 异端；异教e.g. To some fans, changing the old song is close to heresy.💡 场合：本指宗教异端；日常多是夸张调侃，把公认规矩反着来就是 heresy，美食争论里很常见
insist /in'sist/ v. 坚持；坚决主张；强调e.g. He insists on walking to work every day, rain or shine.💡 场合：强调坚持要求或主张时用：insist on doing、insist that…，语气比 say 强得多
turn one's nose up at 短语 对……嗤之以鼻；瞧不起e.g. She didn't turn her nose up at the cheap noodles; she finished every bite.💡 场合：口语习语，对……不屑一顾、看不上，鄙视语气很明显，聊天里用很传神
divide public opinion 短语 让公众意见分裂e.g. The new rule divides public opinion, and everyone seems to have a view on it.💡 场合：新闻评论的固定搭配，指让公众意见两极分化、吵成两派
insist that 短语 坚持认为e.g. My mum insists that I call her when I get home late.💡 场合：新闻引述某人立场时高频：他坚持认为……；后面接完整的句子

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. Smoking in public places is a ____ topic in many countries.
✓ controversial　争论的；论争的；被议论的
Smoking in public places is a controversial topic in many countries.
在许多国家，在公共场所吸烟都是一个有争议的话题。
2. The new rule ____, and everyone seems to have a view on it.
✓ divides public opinion（divide public opinion 的变形）　让公众意见分裂
The new rule divides public opinion, and everyone seems to have a view on it.
这条新规定让公众意见分裂，似乎人人都有自己的看法。
3. He ____ on walking to work every day, rain or shine.
✓ insists（insist 的变形）　坚持；坚决主张；强调
He insists on walking to work every day, rain or shine.
他坚持每天步行上班，风雨无阻。
4. She ____ to eat the soup because it was too salty.
✓ refused（refuse 的变形）　拒绝；谢绝
She refused to eat the soup because it was too salty.
她拒绝喝那碗汤，因为太咸了。
5. Rice is a ____ food in many parts of Asia.
✓ staple　主要产物；常用品；主要要素；原料；订书钉；钩环
Rice is a staple food in many parts of Asia.
在亚洲许多地方，大米是主食。
6. As a coffee ____, he never adds sugar or milk.
✓ purist　纯粹主义者
As a coffee purist, he never adds sugar or milk.
作为一个咖啡纯粹主义者，他从不加糖或牛奶。
7. His ____ came as a shock to everyone in the office.
✓ dismissal　免职；解雇
His dismissal came as a shock to everyone in the office.
他被解雇的消息让办公室里的每个人都很震惊。
8. We were ____ when we heard the price of the ticket.
✓ aghast　惊骇的；吓呆的
We were aghast when we heard the price of the ticket.
听到票价时，我们都惊呆了。
9. To some fans, changing the old song is close to ____.
✓ heresy　异端；异教
To some fans, changing the old song is close to heresy.
对一些歌迷来说，改动这首老歌简直是异端。
10. My mum ____ I call her when I get home late.
✓ insists that（insist that 的变形）　坚持认为
My mum insists that I call her when I get home late.
我妈妈坚持让我晚回家时给她打个电话。
11. ____ fruits like mango and papaya are my favourites.
✓ tropical　热带的；热情的
Tropical fruits like mango and papaya are my favourites.
芒果、木瓜这类热带水果是我的最爱。
12. She ____ that the plan will save the company a lot of money.
✓ contends（contend 的变形）　奋斗；斗争；竞争
She contends that the plan will save the company a lot of money.
她认为这个计划会为公司省下很多钱。
13. The singer ____ left the concert early last night.
✓ reportedly　根据传说；根据传闻；据报道
The singer reportedly left the concert early last night.
据报道，这位歌手昨晚提前离开了演唱会。
14. He got ____ last month, but he soon found a better job.
✓ fired　因祸得福（电影名）
He got fired last month, but he soon found a better job.
他上个月被解雇了，不过很快就找到了一份更好的工作。
15. Leaving the meeting early felt like ____ the whole team.
✓ an insult to　对……的侮辱
Leaving the meeting early felt like an insult to the whole team.
提前离开会议感觉像是对整个团队的侮辱。
