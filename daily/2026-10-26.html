1. Beijing Restaurant Serves Food Fit for Emperors

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-08-31 · 原文链接
📖 北京仿膳饭庄的宫廷点心：从慈禧太后的一场梦说起，讲手工糕点背后的老手艺与满汉风味。读这篇可以学到 preserve、delicate 等词，也能想想传统味道该怎么留下来。
¶ 1

During the final decades of imperial China, Empress Dowager Cixi is said to have dreamed about a pastry. The next morning, the same treat appeared at breakfast.

在清朝最后几十年，据说慈禧太后梦见了一种糕点。第二天早上，同样的点心就出现在了早餐桌上。

imperial /im'piәriәl/ a. 帝王的；宗主国的；至尊的；壮丽的e.g. The palace keeps many imperial treasures.
pastry /'peistri/ n. 油酥点心；面粉糕饼e.g. I bought a sweet pastry for breakfast.
¶ 2

Delighted, she rewarded the chef with a silver ingot. The dish, previously called "minced pork pancake," became known as "dream pastry."

她非常高兴，赏了厨师一锭银子。这道菜以前叫“肉末饼”，从此被称为“梦糕”。

delighted /di'laitid/ a. 高兴的；快乐的e.g. She was delighted to hear the good news.
be committed to 短语 致力于e.g. She is committed to learning English every day.
be known as 短语 被称为e.g. The lake is known as a perfect place for fishing.
be worthy of 短语 值得；配得上e.g. This movie is worthy of a second watch.
¶ 3

More than a century later, visitors can still try it at Fangshan Restaurant in Beijing.

一个多世纪后的今天，游客仍可以在北京的仿膳饭庄尝到它。

¶ 4

Fangshan was established in 1925 by former chefs from the Qing imperial court. Located in Beihai Park, a former imperial garden, it is one of a handful of restaurants specializing in food once served to emperors.

仿膳创建于1925年，创办者是清宫的前御厨。它位于北海公园——一座昔日的皇家园林，是少数几家专做皇帝曾经享用的菜肴的餐馆之一。

¶ 5

The restaurant's name means "imitating imperial cuisine," but its chefs do more than copy old recipes. They also preserve the stories and traditional skills behind them.

饭庄的名字意思是“仿照宫廷菜”，但厨师们做的远不止复制古方。他们还保存着这些菜背后的故事和传统手艺。

preserve /pri'zә:v/ vt. 保护；保持；保存；维持；腌；禁猎e.g. We should preserve old photos with care.
traditional /trә'diʃәnl/ a. 传统的；惯例的e.g. They celebrate the festival in a traditional way.
¶ 6

Pastry chef Zhou Tong learned his craft from a master who had learned it from another mentor. At Fangshan, pastries are prepared entirely by hand, without electric blenders or food processors.

糕点厨师周桐的手艺师承一位师傅，而那位师傅也是从另一位师傅那里学来的。在仿膳，糕点全部手工制作，不用电动搅拌机或食品加工机。

¶ 7

Chefs use sieves to turn beans into powder and prepare certain flours in custom-made copper pots. Zhou said machines may be faster, but they cannot create the delicate texture expected of imperial pastries.

厨师们用筛子把豆子筛成粉，还在特制的铜锅里准备某些面粉。周桐说，机器也许更快，但做不出宫廷糕点应有的细腻口感。

delicate /'delikәt/ a. 细致优雅的；微妙的；美味的e.g. This cup is delicate, so hold it gently.
¶ 8

Two of the restaurant's most popular treats are pea flour cakes and red bean rolls. During busy periods, customers order more than 200 pea flour cakes a day.

饭庄最受欢迎的两种点心是豌豆黄和芸豆卷。生意好的时候，顾客一天要点200多份豌豆黄。

popular /'pɒpjulә/ a. 通俗的；流行的；受欢迎的；大众的；人民的；普及的e.g. This small shop is very popular with students.
¶ 9

Executive chef Fan Liangliang also had to learn slower, more careful methods when he joined Fangshan. One technique, called "Nizihuo," involves mincing fish, shrimp or chicken into a smooth paste and shaping it into frogs, birds or flowers.

行政总厨范亮亮加入仿膳时，也不得不学习更慢、更精细的做法。一种叫“泥子活”的技法，是把鱼、虾或鸡肉剁成细腻的糊，再塑成青蛙、小鸟或花朵的形状。

technique /tek'ni:k/ n. 技巧；技术；方法e.g. She learned a new cooking technique last week.
involve doing something 短语 包括做某事e.g. The job involves talking with customers every day.
¶ 10

"Every dish had to be innovative, visually beautiful, nutritious and worthy of presentation on the imperial dining table," Fan said.

范亮亮说：“每道菜都要新颖、美观、营养，而且配得上摆上皇帝的餐桌。”

innovative /'inәjveitiv/ a. 革新的；创新的；富有革新精神的e.g. His innovative idea saved us a lot of time.
nutritious /nju:'triʃәs/ a. 滋养的；营养的e.g. This soup is simple but very nutritious.
¶ 11

Some ingredients traditionally used in imperial cooking, including bear paws, are no longer served. Others have been replaced because they are difficult to obtain or do not suit modern tastes.

宫廷菜传统上用的一些食材，包括熊掌，如今已不再供应。另一些食材因为难以获得或不符合现代口味，也被替换掉了。

suit modern tastes 短语 符合现代口味e.g. This light soup suits my taste on hot days.
¶ 12

However, Fan said the restaurant remains committed to preserving the cuisine's character, including its blend of Manchu and Han traditions.

不过范亮亮说，饭庄仍致力于保持这门菜系的特色，包括满汉交融的风味。

committed a. 献身于某种事业的e.g. He is committed to finishing the project on time.
character /'kærәktә/ n. 个性；字符；人物；性质；品格；资格e.g. The old street keeps its special character.
¶ 13

"When people dine at Fangshan Restaurant, I hope they experience more than just a meal," he said. "They experience the weight of history."

他说：“我希望人们在仿膳吃饭时，感受到的不只是一顿饭，而是历史的厚重。”

2. Why we say “God bless you”

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2025-10-29 · 原文链接
📖 打喷嚏时为什么要说“God bless you”？从古人的灵魂说到中世纪的瘟疫，再到今天的礼貌习惯，短短一篇讲清一个日常用语的来历，顺便记住 sneeze、plague 几个词。
¶ 1

Why do we say “God bless you” when someone sneezes? People cannot say exactly when or where the response started; however, we have some theories.

我们为什么在别人打喷嚏时说“上帝保佑你”？人们说不清这句回应到底何时、何地开始，不过我们有几种说法。

sneeze /sni:z/ n. 喷嚏e.g. I always sneeze when I smell pepper.
bless /bles/ vt. 祝福；祈佑；使神圣化e.g. Her grandmother always blesses her before a trip.
response /ri'spɒns/ n. 反应；回答；响应e.g. His response to the question was very short.
¶ 2

A long time ago, people thought that when someone sneezed, snot and the soul escaped, too, and to say “God bless you” was to help. Another theory is from the 6th century when a plague struck Europe, and Pope Gregory I formally ordered people shout out blessings to protect someone who sneezed. Similarly, in the 14th century during the bubonic plague, sneezing was a sign that a person might be sick with the plague.

很久以前，人们认为打喷嚏时鼻涕和灵魂也会一起跑出来，说一句“上帝保佑你”能帮上忙。另一种说法来自6世纪：当时瘟疫席卷欧洲，教皇格里高利一世正式下令，人们要大声喊出祝福，以保护打喷嚏的人。同样，在14世纪黑死病期间，打喷嚏是一个人可能染上瘟疫的征兆。

theory /'θiәri/ n. 理论；学说；原理；意见；推测e.g. She has a theory about why cats sleep so much.
soul /sәul/ n. 灵魂；心灵；精神；精髓；人；化身；典型；鬼魂e.g. Music can comfort the soul after a long day.
plague /pleig/ n. 瘟疫；天罚；麻烦；灾祸e.g. The plague spread quickly in old cities.
formally /'fɒ:mәli/ adv. 正式地；形式上e.g. The teacher formally welcomed the new students.
protect /prә'tekt/ vt. 防卫；保护；警戒e.g. A good coat can protect you from the cold.
shout out 短语 大声喊出e.g. He shouted out the answer before anyone else.
a sign that 短语 ……的征兆；……的迹象e.g. Dark clouds are a sign that rain is coming.
a long time ago 短语 很久以前e.g. A long time ago, people wrote letters by hand.
¶ 3

Today, the reaction is mostly ingrained, and people think it is polite. However, people don’t use it worldwide. For example, in Spanish speaking countries, people say “salud”, which can be translated to “good health”.

如今，这句回应大多已根深蒂固，人们认为它是一种礼貌。不过世界各地并不都这么说。比如在西班牙语国家，人们说“salud”，可以译为“身体健康”。

ingrained /in'greind/ a. 根深蒂固的；彻底的；深染的e.g. Drinking water in the morning is an ingrained habit for her.
polite /pә'lait/ a. 有礼貌的；文雅的；客气的；有教养的e.g. It is polite to say thank you after dinner.
worldwide /'wә:ldwaid/ a. 全世界的e.g. This song is popular worldwide.
translate to 短语 译为e.g. How do you translate this sentence to French?
¶ 4

Difficult words: snot (liquid that comes from someone’s nose when he has a bad cold or sneezes), bubonic plague (the most common type of plague in humans), ingrained (firmly established and difficult to change).

生词：snot（感冒或打喷嚏时从鼻子里流出的液体）、bubonic plague（人类最常见的鼠疫类型）、ingrained（根深蒂固、难以改变）。
