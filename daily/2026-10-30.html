1. The UK says yes to drilling in the North Sea

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-29 · 原文链接
📖 英国政府批准在北海一处颇具争议的气田钻探，尽管环保团体强烈反对。这篇文章呈现了支持与反对两方的理由：一边强调能源安全，另一边质疑化石燃料能否真正降低家庭账单。读它可以顺便学到能源与气候议题的常用表达。
¶ 1

The British government has approved drilling at a controversial North Sea gas field, despite strong opposition from environmental groups.

英国政府已经批准在一处颇具争议的北海气田进行钻探，尽管环保团体强烈反对。

controversial /.kɒntrә'vә:ʃәl/ a. 争论的；论争的；被议论的e.g. The new road plan is controversial in our town.
drilling /'driliŋ/ n. 演练e.g. Drilling for oil can harm sea animals.
opposition /.ɒpә'ziʃәn/ n. 反对；敌对；相反；在野党e.g. The plan met strong opposition from local people.
environmental /in.vaiәrәn'mentәl/ a. 周围的；环境的e.g. Environmental groups protect forests and rivers.
¶ 2

Energy companies welcomed the decision and predicted the project could supply over six percent of the nation’s gas at peak production. Officials argue that developing domestic resources is necessary to boost national energy security while global conflicts drive up fuel costs. The prime minister stated that although the country remains committed to long-term climate targets, oil and gas will still be needed during the transition to renewable energy.

能源公司对这一决定表示欢迎，并预测该项目在产量高峰期可供应全国6%以上的天然气。官员们认为，在全球冲突推高燃料成本的背景下，开发国内资源对提升国家能源安全是必要的。首相表示，尽管英国仍致力于实现长期气候目标，但在向可再生能源过渡期间，石油和天然气仍将是必需的。

predicted /priˈdiktid/ v. 预言；预测；预示( predict的过去式和过去分词 )e.g. The weather report predicted rain for tomorrow.
domestic /dәu'mestik/ a. 家庭的；国内的；驯养的e.g. Domestic flights are usually shorter than international ones.
security /si'kjuriti/ n. 安全；安全性；防护物；保安；可靠性；担保人；抵押品；保证金e.g. Energy security matters most in cold winters.
transition /træn'ziʃәn/ n. 转变；转换；变迁；过渡时期；临时转调e.g. The transition to online classes took some time.
renewable /ri'nju(:)әbl/ a. 可更新的；可恢复的；可继续的；可换新的；可重复的；可重说的e.g. Solar power is a kind of renewable energy.
drive up 短语 推高e.g. The holiday season usually drives up hotel prices.
energy security 短语 能源安全e.g. Countries want energy security so that homes stay warm in winter.
transition to 短语 向……过渡e.g. Our town is in transition to greener buses.
¶ 3

However, climate activists and politicians heavily criticized the approval and warned that increased fossil fuel reliance won’t lower household energy bills because prices are dictated by international markets. They stress that true security requires a faster shift toward clean energy rather than expanding fossil fuel infrastructure.

然而，气候活动人士和政界人士严厉批评了这一批准，并警告说，增加对化石燃料的依赖并不会降低家庭能源账单，因为价格由国际市场决定。他们强调，真正的安全需要更快地转向清洁能源，而不是扩大化石燃料基础设施。

criticized /ˈkritisaizd/ vt. 批评(criticize的过去式)e.g. Many people criticized the plan for being too costly.
reliance /ri'laiәns/ n. 信任；信赖；受信赖的人(或事)e.g. Our reliance on cars makes traffic worse.
infrastructure /'infrәstrʌktʃә/ n. 基础结构；基础设施e.g. Good roads are part of a country's infrastructure.
fossil fuel 短语 化石燃料e.g. Cars that burn fossil fuel add to air pollution.
clean energy 短语 清洁能源e.g. Wind and solar are two common forms of clean energy.
¶ 4

Difficult words: controversial (causing a lot of angry public discussion and strong disagreement), dictate (to state, order, or control authoritatively how something must be done or decide), reliance (dependence on someone or something for help or support).

难词：controversial（引起大量愤怒的公开讨论和强烈分歧的）、dictate（权威地规定、命令或控制某事必须如何进行或做出决定）、reliance（依赖某人或某事的帮助或支持）。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. ____ flights are usually shorter than international ones.
✓ domestic　家庭的；国内的；驯养的
Domestic flights are usually shorter than international ones.
国内航班通常比国际航班短。
2. Our town is in ____ greener buses.
✓ transition to　向……过渡
Our town is in transition to greener buses.
我们镇正在向更环保的公交车过渡。
3. Wind and solar are two common forms of ____.
✓ clean energy　清洁能源
Wind and solar are two common forms of clean energy.
风能和太阳能是两种常见的清洁能源。
4. The weather report ____ rain for tomorrow.
✓ predicted　预言；预测；预示( predict的过去式和过去分词 )
The weather report predicted rain for tomorrow.
天气预报预测明天会下雨。
5. ____ for oil can harm sea animals.
✓ drilling　演练
Drilling for oil can harm sea animals.
钻探石油可能会伤害海洋动物。
6. Good roads are part of a country's ____.
✓ infrastructure　基础结构；基础设施
Good roads are part of a country's infrastructure.
好的道路是一个国家基础设施的一部分。
7. The plan met strong ____ from local people.
✓ opposition　反对；敌对；相反；在野党
The plan met strong opposition from local people.
该计划遭到了当地居民的强烈反对。
8. Many people ____ the plan for being too costly.
✓ criticized　批评(criticize的过去式)
Many people criticized the plan for being too costly.
很多人批评这个计划成本太高。
9. Solar power is a kind of ____ energy.
✓ renewable　可更新的；可恢复的；可继续的；可换新的；可重复的；可重说的
Solar power is a kind of renewable energy.
太阳能是一种可再生能源。
10. Energy ____ matters most in cold winters.
✓ security　安全；安全性；防护物；保安；可靠性；担保人；抵押品；保证金
Energy security matters most in cold winters.
能源安全在寒冷的冬天最重要。
11. Cars that burn ____ add to air pollution.
✓ fossil fuel　化石燃料
Cars that burn fossil fuel add to air pollution.
燃烧化石燃料的汽车会加重空气污染。
12. Countries want ____ so that homes stay warm in winter.
✓ energy security　能源安全
Countries want energy security so that homes stay warm in winter.
各国都希望保障能源安全，这样冬天家里才能保持温暖。
13. ____ groups protect forests and rivers.
✓ environmental　周围的；环境的
Environmental groups protect forests and rivers.
环保团体保护森林和河流。
14. The ____ to online classes took some time.
✓ transition　转变；转换；变迁；过渡时期；临时转调
The transition to online classes took some time.
向线上课程的过渡花了一些时间。
15. Our ____ on cars makes traffic worse.
✓ reliance　信任；信赖；受信赖的人(或事)
Our reliance on cars makes traffic worse.
我们对汽车的依赖让交通更糟。
