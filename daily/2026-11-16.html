1. Wire upgrade at fault for grounding all US flights

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-11-16 · 原文链接
📖 11月13日星期五，美国遭遇了一场全国性的航空大停摆：空中交通管制系统突发通信故障，联邦航空局下令全国停飞，3500多架飞机滞留空中，数千架次航班取消。调查发现，罪魁祸首竟然是一台过载的路由器——故障可能源于把几十年前的铜线换成光纤的维护工作。这篇新闻不长，却能让你看到美国老旧基建的真实一面，也能学到不少描述事故与基础设施的实用表达。
¶ 1

Thousands of flights across the USA were grounded on Friday the 13th following a major communications failure in the country's air traffic control system. The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) ordered a nationwide ground stop after problems with radar and communications equipment. More than 3,500 airplanes were in the air at the time. Thousands of flights were cancelled, leaving passengers stranded at airports nationwide. The problem was eventually traced to an overloaded router, possibly as a result of maintenance work to replace decades-old copper wires with fibre-optic cables. The FAA lifted the ground stop almost five hours later. Operations may take days to return to normal.

11月13日星期五，美国空中交通管制系统发生重大通信故障，全国数千架次航班被迫停飞。美国联邦航空局（FAA）在雷达和通信设备出现问题后，下令全国停飞。当时有3500多架飞机正在空中飞行。数千架次航班被取消，乘客滞留在全国各地的机场。问题最终被追溯到一台过载的路由器，可能是更换几十年历史的铜线、改用光纤电缆的维护工作所致。近五个小时后，联邦航空局解除了停飞令。航班运营可能需要数天才能恢复正常。

stranded a. 处于困境的；进退两难的e.g. I was stranded at the airport for six hours with no hotel to go to.💡 场合：新闻报道和日常口语都常用，描述因交通中断、天气等原因被困在某地，语气比 stuck 更正式。
router /'ru:tә(r), 'raj-/ [计] 路由器e.g. I restarted the router, and the Wi-Fi came back in a minute.💡 场合：科技、日常口语都常用，家里和办公室的网络设备；新闻里常出现在网络故障报道中。
nationwide /'neiʃәnwaid/ a. 全国性的e.g. The supermarket is holding a nationwide sale this weekend.💡 场合：新闻报道常用，形容范围覆盖全国的活动、调查或问题；日常口语里也常用来形容连锁促销、统一考试。
maintenance /'meintәnәns/ n. 维护；保持；维修；生活费用；坚持；扶养e.g. I take my car in for regular maintenance to keep it running well.💡 场合：日常口语和正式场合都可用，指对设备、车辆、房屋等的维护保养；新闻里常出现在设施故障报道中。
trace back to 短语 追溯到；起因是……e.g. Doctors traced the illness back to the food the family ate last night.💡 场合：正式与日常都可用，常用于解释原因、查源头，新闻报道里很常见。
return to normal 短语 恢复正常e.g. It took the school a whole week to return to normal after the flood.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都常用，中性表达，描述生活、工作或秩序恢复原样。
¶ 2

The incident sparked widespread concern. Officials quickly reassured the public that it was not another 9/11. The US homeland security secretary said there had been no indication of a cyberattack or national security threat. He described the incident as a "telecommunications outage". Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed the failure on outdated infrastructure. He said: "We have old switches and old routers. We use 1980s little copper lines." He added: "It's an old system that went down. It went down everywhere. It got overloaded." Duffy warned that systems would "continue to break" unless Congress allocated extra funds. He said: "I can't build things if I don't have money."

这起事件引发了广泛关注。官员们迅速向公众保证，这不是另一起9·11事件。美国国土安全部部长表示，没有迹象表明这是网络攻击或国家安全威胁。他把这起事件称为一次“电信中断”。交通部长肖恩·达菲把故障归咎于过时的基础设施。他说：“我们用的还是老交换机和老路由器，用的是20世纪80年代的小铜线。”他还说：“这是一套老系统，它瘫痪了，全国都瘫痪了，它超载了。”他警告说，除非国会拨出更多资金，否则系统还会“继续出故障”。他说：“没有钱，我什么也建不起来。”

outage /'autidʒ/ n. （服务、供应）中断e.g. A power outage hit our building, and we had to climb ten floors in the dark.💡 场合：新闻报道常用，指水、电、网络等服务突然中断；日常口语里说停电更常用 power cut。
infrastructure /'infrәstrʌktʃә/ n. 基础结构；基础设施e.g. The city is spending a lot on new infrastructure, like roads, bridges and power lines.💡 场合：新闻、政策讨论和正式书面语常用，指一个国家或地区的道路、桥梁、电力、网络等基础建设；日常口语较少用。
outdated /.aut'deitid/ a. 旧式的；落伍的；过时的e.g. My old phone is too outdated to run the newest apps.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻都常用，形容东西、观念、方法跟不上时代，略带贬义。
allocate /'ælәukeit/ vt. 分派；分配e.g. We allocate part of our salary to savings and part to the children's education.💡 场合：正式书面和职场常用，指分配资金、时间、资源；日常口语里更常说 set aside 或 give。
reassure /.ri:ә'ʃuә/ vt. 使...安心；向...再保证e.g. She hugged her son and reassured him that the storm would pass soon.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻都常用，表示安抚别人、消除对方的担心，语气温和。
incident /'insidәnt/ n. 事件；事变；小事e.g. A small incident in class — a student knocked over a bottle of water.💡 场合：新闻报道常用，指发生的事件，常指不愉快的事故或冲突；日常口语里说“小事”更常用 thing。
spark /spɑ:k/ n. 引发，激起e.g. Her question sparked a lively debate in class.💡 场合：新闻和日常口语都常用，比喻“引发”讨论、争议或兴趣，比 cause 更生动；作名词时是“火花”。
concern /kәn'sә:n/ n. 关心；忧虑e.g. Her biggest concern is that her child spends too much time on the phone.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻都高频，既可以指“担忧”这种情绪，也可以指“令人担心的事”；正式书面也常用。
be grounded 短语 （飞机）停飞；被禁足e.g. The airline grounded every flight after the storm hit the airport.💡 场合：航空新闻和日常口语都常用；作“禁足”讲时多用于家长对孩子的惩罚，语气较严厉。
blame ... on ... 短语 把……归咎于……e.g. She blamed the delay on the heavy traffic.💡 场合：日常口语高频短语，常带推卸责任的语气；正式场合也可用。
go down 短语 （系统、设备、网站）瘫痪；停止运行e.g. The school website went down just before we had to hand in our homework.💡 场合：日常口语和科技新闻常用，指电脑、网络、网站突然不能用；也可以指物价、温度下降，要看语境。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（13 题）
1. She hugged her son and ____ him that the storm would pass soon.
✓ reassured（reassure 的变形）　使...安心；向...再保证
She hugged her son and reassured him that the storm would pass soon.
她抱住儿子，安慰他说暴风雨很快就会过去。
2. A power ____ hit our building, and we had to climb ten floors in the dark.
✓ outage　（服务、供应）中断
A power outage hit our building, and we had to climb ten floors in the dark.
一次停电波及了我们整栋楼，我们只好摸黑爬了十层楼。
3. I take my car in for regular ____ to keep it running well.
✓ maintenance　维护；保持；维修；生活费用；坚持；扶养
I take my car in for regular maintenance to keep it running well.
我每隔一段时间就把车送去保养，让它保持良好状态。
4. The supermarket is holding a ____ sale this weekend.
✓ nationwide　全国性的
The supermarket is holding a nationwide sale this weekend.
这家超市本周末正在搞全国大促销。
5. I restarted the ____, and the Wi-Fi came back in a minute.
✓ router　路由器
I restarted the router, and the Wi-Fi came back in a minute.
我重启了路由器，不到一分钟 Wi-Fi 就恢复了。
6. Her question ____ a lively debate in class.
✓ sparked（spark 的变形）　引发，激起
Her question sparked a lively debate in class.
她的一个问题在班上引发了一场热烈的讨论。
7. Her biggest ____ is that her child spends too much time on the phone.
✓ concern　关心；忧虑
Her biggest concern is that her child spends too much time on the phone.
她最担心的是孩子花太多时间在手机上。
8. The city is spending a lot on new ____, like roads, bridges and power lines.
✓ infrastructure　基础结构；基础设施
The city is spending a lot on new infrastructure, like roads, bridges and power lines.
这座城市正在把大笔钱花在道路、桥梁和电力线路等新基础设施上。
9. My old phone is too ____ to run the newest apps.
✓ outdated　旧式的；落伍的；过时的
My old phone is too outdated to run the newest apps.
我的旧手机太过时了，装不了最新的应用。
10. We ____ part of our salary to savings and part to the children's education.
✓ allocate　分派；分配
We allocate part of our salary to savings and part to the children's education.
我们把一部分工资存起来，另一部分留作孩子的教育费用。
11. I was ____ at the airport for six hours with no hotel to go to.
✓ stranded　处于困境的；进退两难的
I was stranded at the airport for six hours with no hotel to go to.
我在机场滞留了六个小时，没地方可去。
12. A small ____ in class — a student knocked over a bottle of water.
✓ incident　事件；事变；小事
A small incident in class — a student knocked over a bottle of water.
课堂上出了个小插曲——一个学生打翻了一瓶水。
13. It took the school a whole week to ____ after the flood.
✓ return to normal　恢复正常
It took the school a whole week to return to normal after the flood.
洪水过后，学校花了一整周才恢复正常。
