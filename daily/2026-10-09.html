1. Are wind turbines safe for birds?

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-08 · 原文链接
📖 随着海上风电在欧洲快速扩张，科学家用热成像相机和 AI 研究风力涡轮机对迁徙鸟类的影响。关键信息是：一项为期一年的试点研究发现，在数百万次鸟类飞行中仅确认两起撞击，超过 99.8% 的鸟能成功避开叶片，威胁远比此前预想的要低。值得读：科研团队在黑暗夜空下“看见”鸟类的方法很有意思，文中的科技术语和自然保护词汇也很实用。
¶ 1

As offshore wind energy rapidly expands across Europe, scientists are using thermal cameras and AI to study the impact of wind turbines on migrating birds.

随着海上风能在欧洲迅速扩张，科学家们正利用热成像相机和人工智能，研究风力涡轮机对迁徙鸟类的影响。

¶ 2

Millions of birds cross the North Sea every year; however, it is difficult to measure collision risks at sea due to dark night conditions and remote locations.

每年有数百万只鸟飞越北海；然而，由于海上夜间黑暗、地点偏远，测量鸟类撞击涡轮机的风险十分困难。

¶ 3

In a year-long pilot study at a Dutch wind farm, researchers placed sixteen thermal cameras on a turbine to monitor bird activity day and night. Out of millions of bird movements recorded over two migration seasons, the team identified only two confirmed collisions. Other studies used radar technology and similarly showed that over 99.8% of migrating birds successfully avoid turbine blades.

在荷兰一家风电场开展的为期一年的试点研究中，研究人员在一台涡轮机上安装了 16 台热成像相机，全天候监测鸟类活动。在连续两个迁徙季记录到的数百万次鸟类飞行中，团队只确认了两起撞击事件。其他采用雷达技术的研究也显示，超过 99.8% 的迁徙鸟类成功避开了涡轮叶片。

successfully avoid 短语 成功避开e.g. Over 99.8% of migrating birds successfully avoid turbine blades.（超过 99.8% 的迁徙鸟类成功避开了涡轮叶片。）
a year-long pilot study 短语 为期一年的试点研究e.g. In a year-long pilot study at a Dutch wind farm, researchers placed sixteen thermal cameras on a turbine.（在一项为期一年的试点研究中，研究人员在荷兰一家风电场的涡轮机上安装了 16 台热成像相机。）
monitor ... day and night 短语 昼夜监测……e.g. ...to monitor bird activity day and night.（……以昼夜监测鸟类活动。）
¶ 4

While these initial findings suggest that offshore wind farms pose a lower threat than previously feared, conservationists emphasize that careful site selection, smart technology, and protective measures remain important to protect endangered seabird populations during the clean energy transition.

虽然这些初步发现表明，海上风电场对鸟类的威胁可能比人们此前担心的要小，但自然资源保护主义者强调，在清洁能源转型过程中，审慎选址、智慧技术和防护措施仍然至关重要，以保护濒危海鸟种群。

pose a threat to 短语 对……构成威胁e.g. While these initial findings suggest that offshore wind farms pose a lower threat than previously feared...（尽管初步发现表明海上风电场构成的威胁比预想的要小……）
remain important to 短语 对……仍然重要e.g. Careful site selection, smart technology, and protective measures remain important to protect endangered seabird populations.（审慎选址、智慧技术和防护措施对保护濒危海鸟种群仍然重要。）
¶ 5

Difficult words: turbine (a machine with blades that are turned by water, wind, or steam to produce power), blade (a wide, flat arm on a propeller or wind turbine that spins in the air), conservationist (a person who works to protect animals, plants, and natural environments).

难词：turbine（涡轮机：利用水流、风或蒸汽推动叶片转动来发电的机器）；blade（叶片：螺旋桨或风力涡轮机上在空中旋转的宽而扁的臂）；conservationist（自然资源保护主义者：致力于保护动物、植物和自然环境的人）。

2. Brazilians have problems with money

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-05 · 原文链接
📖 巴西大选临近，巨额债务问题已大到无法忽视：超过八成家庭深陷债务，分期消费、400% 以上的信用卡利率、银行 App 快贷，加上爆发式增长的线上体育博彩，把普通家庭拖入财务困境。关键信息是：连政府自己也寅吃卯粮，利息支出占预算 8% 以上，谁上台都避不开艰难的财政改革。值得读：全文充满实用的财经表达，如 deep in debt、drown in unpaid bills，对读懂经济新闻大有帮助。
¶ 1

As Brazil gets closer to its next election, the massive debt problem is getting way too big to ignore.

随着巴西大选临近，巨大的债务问题已经大到无法忽视。

¶ 2

Over 80% of households are deep in debt, mostly thanks to paying for everything in monthly installments, credit card rates shooting over 400%, and quick loans on banking apps.

超过 80% 的家庭深陷债务，这主要归因于分期付款的消费方式、高达 400% 以上的信用卡利率，以及银行 App 上的快贷。

deep in debt 短语 深陷债务e.g. Over 80% of households are deep in debt.（超过 80% 的家庭深陷债务。）
thanks to 短语 归因于（此处为贬义：由于）e.g. ...mostly thanks to paying for everything in monthly installments, credit card rates shooting over 400%...（……这主要归因于分期付款的消费方式、飙到 400% 以上的信用卡利率……）
¶ 3

On top of that, a huge surge in online sports betting has made financial stress even worse for regular families. Small businesses and farmers are also drowning in unpaid bills. Meanwhile, the government itself is struggling under heavy debt, with interest payments consuming over 8% of the national budget alongside mandatory spending on pensions and social programs.

除此之外，线上体育博彩的爆发式增长让普通家庭的财务压力雪上加霜。小企业和农民也同样被拖欠的账单压得喘不过气。与此同时，政府自身也背负沉重债务：利息支出占国家预算 8% 以上，还有养老金和社会项目的法定支出。

drown in unpaid bills 短语 被拖欠的账单压得喘不过气e.g. Small businesses and farmers are also drowning in unpaid bills.（小企业和农民也同样被拖欠的账单压得喘不过气。）
¶ 4

President Lula wants to keep spending money to help poor families, while his main rival Flávio Bolsonaro is promising cuts without offering detailed plans. Analysts warn that if whoever wins doesn’t implement difficult financial reforms, the whole economy could easily end up in a real slowdown.

总统卢拉希望继续花钱帮助贫困家庭，而他的主要竞争对手弗拉维奥·博索纳罗则承诺削减开支，却没有给出详细方案。分析人士警告，如果获胜者不推行艰难的财政改革，整个经济很可能陷入真正的衰退。

end up in 短语 最终落得……/以……告终e.g. ...the whole economy could easily end up in a real slowdown.（……整个经济很可能最终陷入真正的衰退。）
implement (financial) reforms 短语 推行（财政）改革e.g. If whoever wins doesn't implement difficult financial reforms...（如果获胜者不推行艰难的财政改革……）
¶ 5

Difficult words: installment (one of several regular payments made over time to pay for something), mandatory (required by law or a rule), slowdown (a period when economic activity or growth becomes slower than before).

难词：installment（分期付款：为支付某物而分多次定期支付的款项之一）；mandatory（强制性的：法律或规则要求的）；slowdown（放缓：经济活动或增长变得比以前慢的时期）。
