1. Serbia's Vucic Concedes Defeat to Student-Led Movement

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-10-27 · 原文链接
📖 塞尔维亚议会选举出现历史性转折：执政14年的前进党败给了由学生发起的“学生名单”。关键点在于，学生自己并不参选，而是推举各行各业的代表，主打重振经济、恢复民主。值得读：看一场由校园抗议发展而来的运动如何改写一个国家的政治走向。
¶ 1

Serbia's Aleksandar Vucic has conceded defeat in a parliamentary election, after a student-led movement challenged his party's 14-year grip on power.

塞尔维亚的亚历山大·武契奇已在一场议会选举中承认失败，此前一场由学生领导的运动挑战了他所在政党长达14年的执政地位。

parliamentary /.pɑ:lә'mentәri/ a. 国会的；议会的；议会制度的e.g. The parliamentary debate lasted all afternoon.💡 场合：正式新闻与政治报道用语，日常口语几乎不用，专门修饰议会相关的会议、辩论和选举。
election /i'lekʃәn/ n. 选举；当选；选择权e.g. The local election will take place in spring.💡 场合：中性常用词，新闻、班级和社区生活里都高频出现，凡是投票选人的正式场合都用它。
concede defeat 短语 承认失败e.g. After a close game, our team conceded defeat with good grace.💡 场合：体育报道和新闻里常见，日常下棋、比赛时也可以大方地用，显得输得起。
grip on power 短语 对权力的掌控e.g. The old mayor finally lost his grip on power after thirty years.💡 场合：新闻政治报道用语，日常借用到公司、社团的小圈子里时，多带一点调侃意味。
¶ 2

Following the October 25 vote, Vucic said the Students' List was projected to win between 135 and 140 of parliament's 250 seats. His right-wing Serbian Progressive Party (SNS) was expected to take around 105. That would give the opposition a majority.

10月25日投票结束后，武契奇表示，学生名单预计将赢得议会250个席位中的135至140席，而他领导的右翼塞尔维亚前进党预计获得约105席。这将使反对派获得多数席位。

majority /mә'dʒɒriti/ n. 多数；大半e.g. The party won a clear majority in the election.💡 场合：正式与日常都常用，新闻报道里尤其高频，表示超过半数的那一部分。
¶ 3

Vucic had resigned as president in September to lead his party's campaign, hoping to become prime minister.

武契奇已于9月辞去总统职务，率领本党参加竞选，希望出任总理。

resigned /ri'zaind/ a. 服从的；听任的；已辞职的e.g. He resigned from his job to start his own business.💡 场合：辞职义多见于新闻和职场语境；作形容词表示无奈顺从时，常见于日常叙事。
¶ 4

News of his defeat brought crowds onto the streets of Belgrade and other cities. Supporters of the student movement sang, honked car horns and celebrated a result that few had expected him to accept so quickly.

败选消息传出后，贝尔格莱德和其他城市的民众走上街头。学生运动的支持者们唱歌、鸣汽车喇叭，庆祝这一几乎没人预料到他会如此迅速接受的结果。

celebrated /'selibreitid/ a. 驰名的；有名的e.g. We celebrated her birthday with cake and music.💡 场合：日常高频词，过生日、过节、庆祝好消息时都用它，口语书面都自然。
¶ 5

The movement grew out of anger over a disaster in November 2024, when a concrete canopy collapsed at a train station in Novi Sad, killing 16 people. Protesters blamed corruption during renovation work for the tragedy and demanded accountability.

这场运动源于2024年11月的一场灾难：诺维萨德一座火车站的混凝土雨棚坍塌，造成16人死亡。抗议者指责翻修工程中的腐败导致了这场悲剧，并要求追责。

tragedy /'trædʒidi/ n. 悲剧；惨案；悲剧作品e.g. The flood was a terrible tragedy for the village.💡 场合：新闻报道惨事时高频，语气沉重；文学里指悲剧作品，日常口语偶尔夸张借用。
accountability /ә.kauntә'biliti/ n. 负有责任；可说明性e.g. Good leaders accept accountability for their mistakes.💡 场合：正式书面与新闻用语，强调出了事要有人负责并给出交代，日常闲聊很少说。
¶ 6

The demonstrations developed into a wider challenge to Vucic's rule. Students accused his government of weakening democracy and built support among people frustrated with established politicians. Vucic dismissed the movement as a foreign-backed effort to remove him from power.

抗议活动逐渐演变为对武契奇统治的更广泛挑战。学生们指责他的政府削弱民主，并在对建制派政客感到失望的人群中争取到了支持。武契奇则称这场运动是外国支持的、旨在把他赶下台的行动。

challenge /'tʃælindʒ/ n. 挑战；盘问e.g. Young people challenged the old rules bravely.💡 场合：日常与职场高频，既可指难对付的事，也可作动词表示质疑或向人下战书。
¶ 7

Although university students organized the Students' List, none stood as candidates themselves. Instead, they backed experts, athletes, farmers and artists, campaigning to rebuild the economy and restore democracy.

虽然组织学生名单的是大学生，但他们自己并没有参选。相反，他们支持专家、运动员、农民和艺术家参选，竞选主张是重振经济、恢复民主。

restore democracy 短语 恢复民主e.g. People marched in the streets to restore democracy and free speech.💡 场合：新闻政治报道专用短语，指恢复民主制度，日常生活中极少使用，了解即可。
¶ 8

To reach voters beyond the cities, students walked and cycled across the country, visiting rural areas where Vucic had traditionally enjoyed strong support.

为了接触到城市以外的选民，学生们徒步、骑行走遍全国，走访了武契奇传统上颇受支持的农村地区。

¶ 9

Monitors estimated that turnout could reach around 70%, a level not seen in Serbia since 2000. However, they also reported suspected vote buying and pressure on voters, following a campaign marked by detentions and concerns about government control of the media.

观察员估计投票率可能达到约70%，这是塞尔维亚自2000年以来未见的水平。不过他们也报告了疑似买票和向选民施压的情况，此次竞选期间还出现了拘留事件，以及对政府控制媒体的担忧。

turnout /'tә:naut/ n. 聚集的人群；出席者；产量e.g. Voter turnout was high despite the rain.💡 场合：新闻里多指投票率，日常活动报道里指出席人数，常和多少、高低搭配着说。
suspected /sә'spektid/ a. 有嫌疑的e.g. Police arrested a suspected thief near the station.💡 场合：新闻报道色彩浓，指只是被怀疑、尚未证实；日常口语里人们更常用 maybe 或 I think。
¶ 10

The European Union closely watched the vote in Serbia, which is seeking membership. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen congratulated the student movement and offered support for efforts to protect media freedom, fight corruption and strengthen democratic institutions.

欧盟密切关注塞尔维亚的这次投票，塞尔维亚正在寻求加入欧盟。欧盟委员会主席乌尔苏拉·冯德莱恩向学生运动表示祝贺，并表示支持保护媒体自由、打击腐败、加强民主制度的努力。

strengthen /'streŋθәn/ vt. 加强；变坚固e.g. Reading every day will strengthen your vocabulary.💡 场合：日常与书面都常用，健身、学习、维修、关系这些话题里都能自然地用上。
fight corruption 短语 打击腐败e.g. The new law aims to fight corruption in public spending.💡 场合：新闻与官方报道用语，指打击贪污受贿，日常口语里很少直接这样说。
¶ 11

For Students' List candidate Radomir Saicic, the result was "a victory over fear."

对学生名单候选人拉多米尔·赛伊契奇来说，这个结果是“战胜恐惧的胜利”。

candidate /'kændideit/ n. 候选人；投考者e.g. Each candidate gave a short speech before the vote.💡 场合：选举和招聘两大场景的常用词，新闻与职场都高频。
¶ 12

"But victory carries responsibility," he said.

“但胜利也意味着责任，”他说。

grow out of 短语 源于；由……发展而来e.g. Her love of cooking grew out of childhood summers in her grandmother's kitchen.💡 场合：日常口语常用短语，聊孩子成长、坏习惯和兴趣来源时特别自然。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. We ____ her birthday with cake and music.
✓ celebrated　驰名的；有名的
We celebrated her birthday with cake and music.
我们用蛋糕和音乐庆祝了她的生日。
2. Reading every day will ____ your vocabulary.
✓ strengthen　加强；变坚固
Reading every day will strengthen your vocabulary.
每天阅读会扩充你的词汇量。
3. Young people ____ the old rules bravely.
✓ challenged（challenge 的变形）　挑战；盘问
Young people challenged the old rules bravely.
年轻人勇敢地挑战了旧规则。
4. The party won a clear ____ in the election.
✓ majority　多数；大半
The party won a clear majority in the election.
该党在选举中赢得了明显的多数席位。
5. After a close game, our team ____ with good grace.
✓ conceded defeat（concede defeat 的变形）　承认失败
After a close game, our team conceded defeat with good grace.
经过一场势均力敌的比赛，我们队虽败犹荣地认了输。
6. The new law aims to ____ in public spending.
✓ fight corruption　打击腐败
The new law aims to fight corruption in public spending.
这部新法律旨在打击公共开支中的腐败行为。
7. Police arrested a ____ thief near the station.
✓ suspected　有嫌疑的
Police arrested a suspected thief near the station.
警方在车站附近逮捕了一名疑似小偷。
8. Voter ____ was high despite the rain.
✓ turnout　聚集的人群；出席者；产量
Voter turnout was high despite the rain.
尽管下着雨，选民投票率还是很高。
9. The old mayor finally lost his ____ after thirty years.
✓ grip on power　对权力的掌控
The old mayor finally lost his grip on power after thirty years.
当了三十年市长后，这位老市长终于失去了对权力的掌控。
10. He ____ from his job to start his own business.
✓ resigned　服从的；听任的；已辞职的
He resigned from his job to start his own business.
他辞职去创办自己的公司了。
11. The flood was a terrible ____ for the village.
✓ tragedy　悲剧；惨案；悲剧作品
The flood was a terrible tragedy for the village.
这场洪水对这个村庄来说是一场可怕的悲剧。
12. Good leaders accept ____ for their mistakes.
✓ accountability　负有责任；可说明性
Good leaders accept accountability for their mistakes.
好的领导者会为自己的错误承担责任。
13. The local ____ will take place in spring.
✓ election　选举；当选；选择权
The local election will take place in spring.
地方选举将在春天举行。
14. The ____ debate lasted all afternoon.
✓ parliamentary　国会的；议会的；议会制度的
The parliamentary debate lasted all afternoon.
议会辩论持续了一整个下午。
15. People marched in the streets to ____ and free speech.
✓ restore democracy　恢复民主
People marched in the streets to restore democracy and free speech.
人们走上街头，要求恢复民主和言论自由。

2. A man attacks a politician in Australia

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-26 · 原文链接
📖 澳大利亚独立议员海伦·海恩斯在选区办公室外遭遇车辆袭击，伤势危重，一条腿已被截肢。关键信息：一位路过的理发师当场制服了嫌疑人，直到警察赶到。值得读：这起事件让澳大利亚开始认真讨论民选官员的人身安全问题。
¶ 1

Independent Australian lawmaker Helen Haines remains in critical condition following a shocking vehicle attack outside her electorate office in Wangaratta, Victoria.

澳大利亚独立议员海伦·海恩斯在维多利亚州旺加拉塔的选区办公室外遭遇一起令人震惊的车辆袭击，目前仍情况危急。

independent /.indi'pendәnt/ n. 独立自主者；无党派者e.g. She works as an independent artist, not for a company.💡 场合：日常高频词，夸孩子自立、说人经济上不靠别人、自由职业时都用它。
shocking /'ʃɒkiŋ/ a. 令人震惊的；极坏的；不正当的e.g. It was shocking news for the whole town.💡 场合：新闻和日常都能用，口语里常带夸张语气，表示太离谱、太让人吃惊。
remain in critical condition 短语 情况危急e.g. The injured driver remains in critical condition in hospital.💡 场合：医疗新闻专用短语，语气严肃，日常只在谈及重病亲友时才会用到。
¶ 2

A 43-year-old suspect allegedly rammed his car into her while she was standing near the trunk of her vehicle, and then backed up to strike her again. A local barber disarmed and held the suspect until police arrived. Authorities have charged the suspect, while counterterrorism units and federal police assist with the investigation. The attack resulted in the amputation of one of the woman’s legs, and surgeons are working to save her other leg.

一名43岁的嫌疑人据称开车撞向站在自己车辆后备箱旁的她，随后又倒车再次撞她。一位当地理发师制服并控制住嫌疑人，直到警察赶到。当局已对嫌疑人提出指控，反恐部门和联邦警察也协助调查。此次袭击导致这名女性的一条腿被截肢，外科医生正在努力保住她的另一条腿。

allegedly /ә'ledʒidli/ adv. 依其申述e.g. He allegedly took money from the office safe.💡 场合：新闻和法律报道专用，表示据称如此但未经证实，日常聊天用它会显得一本正经地调侃。
suspect /sә'spekt/ n. 被怀疑者；嫌疑犯e.g. Police caught the suspect within an hour.💡 场合：新闻案件报道用语，指嫌疑人；作动词表示怀疑时，日常口语也常用。
disarmed v. 武器解除；缴械（disarm的过去式）e.g. The guard disarmed the attacker quickly.💡 场合：本义见于军事和案件报道；引申为被真诚或魅力软化时，日常叙事里很生动。
amputation /.æmpju'teiʃәn/ n. 切断；截肢术e.g. The amputation changed his life completely.💡 场合：医学和新闻报道专用词，日常生活中很少主动使用，谈论时语气平实尊重。
surgeons /'sɜ:dʒənz/ n. 外科医生; 外科医生( surgeon的复数形式 )e.g. The surgeon worked through the night to save the patient.💡 场合：医疗新闻和日常就医话题常用，专指做手术的外科医生。
back up 短语 倒车e.g. Please back up a little so I can open the door.💡 场合：日常口语高频短语，开车、电脑备份、替人撑腰三个场景最常见。
assist with 短语 协助；帮助处理e.g. Local police assisted with the rescue work all night.💡 场合：正式场合和书面语常用，志愿服务和官方协助的报道里尤其多，日常口语更常说 help。
¶ 3
