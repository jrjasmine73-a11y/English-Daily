1. Pumpkin festival

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-22 · 原文链接
📖 比利时一座历史悠久的城堡即将迎来一年一度的秋季南瓜节，预计将吸引约 5 万名游客。除了满眼的大小南瓜，最大的有 772 公斤——甚至有人把它当皮划艇来玩。秋意十足的轻松话题，词汇亲民，非常适合作为晨读热身。
¶ 1

Thousands of visitors are expected to attend Belgium’s annual autumn pumpkin festival at the historic Groot-Bijgaarden castle near Brussels.

数千名游客预计将前往布鲁塞尔附近历史悠久的 Groot-Bijgaarden 城堡，参加比利时一年一度的秋季南瓜节。

festival /'festәvәl/ a. 节日的；喜庆的；快乐的e.g. The town holds a food festival every spring.
be expected to 短语 预计将……e.g. The new store is expected to open next week.
¶ 2

Organizers anticipate around 50,000 people will visit the event, which celebrates the diverse and creative uses of squashes and pumpkins.

主办方预计约有 5 万人会来参加这次活动，这个节日庆祝南瓜和瓠瓜多种多样、富有创意的用途。

anticipate /æn'tisipeit/ vt. 预期；占先；加速；提前使用e.g. We anticipate a busy weekend at the shop.
diverse /dai'vә:s/ a. 不同的；变化多的e.g. The city has a diverse food culture.
creative /kri:'eitiv/ a. 有创造力的；创作的；产生的e.g. She has a lot of creative ideas for the party.
¶ 3

The displays feature thousands of vegetables sourced from across Belgium as well as distant countries like Portugal and Germany, with sizes ranging from small varieties to a massive 772-kilogram giant. Despite a challenging growing season caused by a hot summer, the festival offers far more than just a harvest exhibition. Attendees can participate in pumpkin carving workshops ahead of Halloween and even watch some of the largest pumpkins being used as makeshift kayaks.

展品有数千种蔬菜，来自比利时各地，也来自葡萄牙、德国等远方国家，个头从小巧的品种到 772 公斤的巨无霸都有。尽管炎热的夏天让种植季格外艰难，这个节日远不止是一场丰收展览。游客可以在万圣节到来之前参加南瓜雕刻工坊，甚至还能看到一些最大的南瓜被当作临时小船来用。

massive /'mæsiv/ a. 大而重的；宽大的；宏伟的e.g. They bought a massive TV for the living room.
challenging /'tʃælindʒiŋ/ a. 挑战的；引起争论的e.g. Raising a kid is challenging but fun.
exhibition /.eksi'biʃәn/ n. 表现；展览会；展览品e.g. There is a free photo exhibition at the museum.
participate /pɑ:'tisipeit/ vi. 参加；分享；参与；带有e.g. Everyone is welcome to participate in the game.
makeshift /'meikʃift/ n. 权宜之计；临时代用品e.g. They built a makeshift tent with a blanket.
¶ 4

The popular seasonal celebration successfully brings together families and gardening enthusiasts to enjoy autumn traditions and appreciate the remarkable versatility of this large vegetable.

这场深受欢迎的季节性庆祝活动成功地把家庭和园艺爱好者聚在一起，共同享受秋天的传统，感受这种大型蔬菜令人惊叹的多功能性。

remarkable /ri'mɑ:kәbl/ a. 不平常的；值得注意的；显著的e.g. He made a remarkable recovery after the surgery.
versatility /.vә:sә'tiliti/ n. 多用途；多才多艺；易变e.g. I like this bag for its versatility.
bring together 短语 把……聚集在一起e.g. This festival brings together people from all over the country.
¶ 5

Difficult words: attendee (a person who officially attends or is present at an event, meeting, or festival), makeshift (serving as a temporary substitute for something else), versatility (being able to adapt to many different functions or activities).

难词：attendee（参加者；正式出席活动、会议或节日的人），makeshift（临时的；临时替代某物的），versatility（多功能性；能适应多种不同功能或活动）。

attendee /æten'di:/ [计] 参加者e.g. How many attendees are coming to the party?
range from A to B 短语 从 A 到 B 不等e.g. Prices at this market range from 10 to 100 yuan.
take part in 短语 参加（活动）e.g. He took part in a photo contest last year.

2. CEO loses case to get back gifts from ex-girlfriend

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-09-14 · 原文链接
📖 新加坡一名公司 CEO 在追求前女友期间送出价值 36.9 万美元的豪华礼物和现金“贷款”，分手后把她告上法庭想把钱都要回来——结果打输了：法官认定这些全是出于爱意赠予的，不用还。一段“恋爱脑”引发的官司，词句生动，顺带学学法庭新闻的英语。
¶ 1

A man in Singapore has lost a court case to get back hundreds of thousands of dollars of gifts that he had showered on his ex-girlfriend. The man, Mr X, is a company CEO. His ex, Ms Y, was a flight attendant. The two met during a flight in 2019. Mr X became besotted with Ms Y and started giving her lavish gifts to win her affection. He said he also gave her cash "loans". The court said the value of the presents and additional loans amounted to $369,200. The presiding judge said that Mr X had "displayed a consistent pattern of showering [Ms Y] with lavish gifts". The judge said there had been no expectation that Ms Y would have to repay any of the money or return the gifts.

新加坡一名男子打输了一场官司——他想把之前砸在前女友身上的数十万美元礼物要回来。这名男子被称作 X 先生，是一家公司的首席执行官；他的前任 Y 小姐曾是空姐。两人在 2019 年的一次航班上相识。X 先生被 Y 小姐迷得神魂颠倒，开始送她豪华礼物以博取她的芳心。他还说给过她现金“贷款”。法庭认定，礼物加上额外贷款的总价值为 36.92 万美元。主审法官说，X 先生“持续不断地用豪华礼物宠爱 [Y 小姐]”。法官还表示，Y 小姐并不需要偿还任何钱款或归还礼物。

lavish /'læviʃ/ a. 大方的；丰富的；浪费的e.g. They had a lavish wedding with 500 guests.
besotted /bi'sɒtid/ a. 愚蠢的；糊涂的e.g. He was besotted with her from day one.
shower /'ʃauә/ n. 大量给予（与词典第一义“淋浴”不同）e.g. The company showered new workers with welcome gifts.
presiding /pri'zaidiŋ/ a. 主审的e.g. The presiding judge asked both sides to sit down.
expectation /.ekspek'teiʃәn/ n. 期待；指望；展望e.g. I had high expectations for the movie.
repay /ri'pei/ v. 偿还；报答；报复e.g. She repaid the loan in six months.
shower sb with sth 短语 把某物大量送给某人e.g. She showered her kids with gifts on their birthdays.
be besotted with sb 短语 被某人迷得神魂颠倒e.g. He is completely besotted with his new girlfriend.
¶ 2

The couple were involved in a romantic relationship for just over a year. They split up after Mr X suspected Ms Y of having an affair. He argued in court that his ex-partner had requested interest-free loans to repay her credit cards and life insurance premiums, to pay for overseas trips, and to seek the advice of a feng shui master to assess her apartment. However, there was no documentary evidence to prove this. Ms Y said all gifts were "made out of love and affection". The judge concluded that Mr X had been "smitten" by Ms Y. He added: "Unfortunately, when their relationship ended on a sour note, [Mr X] became embittered and was determined to extract a price from [Ms Y]."

两人在一起只交往了一年多就分手了，分手的原因是 X 先生怀疑 Y 小姐出轨。他在法庭上辩称，是前任要求免息贷款来还信用卡和寿险保费、支付海外旅行费用，还要请风水大师给她的公寓看风水。然而，没有书面证据能证明这一点。Y 小姐说，所有礼物都是“出于爱和感情”送的。法官最后认定，X 先生是被 Y 小姐迷住了。他还补了一句：“不幸的是，当他们的感情以不欢收场时，[X 先生] 心生怨恨，铁了心要让 [Y 小姐] 付出代价。”

embittered /emˈbɪtəd/ v. 使怨恨；激怒( embitter的过去式和过去分词 )e.g. Losing the job left him embittered.
extract /ik'strækt/ n. 榨出物；精汁；摘录；选段e.g. He tried to extract more money from his parents.
sour /'sauә/ a. 不愉快的（文中“ended on a sour note”=不欢而散）e.g. The milk has gone sour.
affair /ә'fєә/ n. 外遇，风流韵事e.g. The movie is about a secret affair.
documentary /.dɒkju'mentәri/ n. 书面的（与词典第一义“纪录片”不同）e.g. Do you have documentary proof of the payment?
conclude /kәn'klu:d/ vt. 结束；作结论；推断e.g. We concluded the meeting at five.
lose a court case 短语 打输官司e.g. If he loses the case, he will have to pay a big fine.
out of love 短语 出于爱e.g. She did it out of love, not for the money.
