1. The strange business of Roman Abramovich

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-16 · 原文链接
📖 最新调查揭露，前切尔西老板阿布拉莫维奇涉嫌用伪造、倒签的财务文件隐藏财富、逃避纳税，涉及超过6亿美元奢侈资产。这篇报道带你看清离岸金融、制裁与税务欺诈的真实运作，还能学到 oligarch、fraud 等高频词。
¶ 1

A new investigation has revealed that former Chelsea FC owner Roman Abramovich used fake, backdated financial documents to hide his wealth and avoid paying taxes.

一项新的调查揭露，前切尔西足球俱乐部老板罗曼·阿布拉莫维奇曾使用伪造的、倒签日期的财务文件来隐藏自己的财富并逃避纳税。

investigation /in.vesti'geiʃәn/ n. 调查；审查e.g. The investigation took two years to finish.
former /'fɒ:mә/ a. 从前的；前者的e.g. My former teacher still lives nearby.
fake /feik/ n. 假货；欺骗；诡计e.g. Don't buy fake tickets online.
wealth /welθ/ n. 财富；资源；财产；丰富；富裕；大量e.g. True wealth is good health and good friends.
avoid paying taxes 短语 逃避纳税e.g. He used fake documents to avoid paying taxes.
¶ 2

The leak shows that Cyprus-based financial firms, along with major global accounting advisers, helped rewrite company records to secret away over 600 million dollars in luxury assets, including superyachts, private jets, and luxury homes. These firms also changed bank records to help the Russian oligarch avoid paying millions of dollars in local taxes on a 2 billion dollar fortune.

这次泄密事件显示，总部位于塞浦路斯的金融公司连同全球大型会计顾问公司，帮助改写公司记录，把价值超过6亿美元的奢侈资产藏匿起来，包括超级游艇、私人飞机和豪华住宅。这些公司还篡改了银行记录，帮助这位俄罗斯寡头就其20亿美元的财富逃避缴纳数百万美元的地方税。

fortune /'fɒ:tʃәn/ n. 财富；运气；兴隆；大量财产；好运；命运e.g. He spent a fortune on that house.
luxury /'lʌkʃәri/ n. 奢侈；豪华e.g. The hotel offers luxury rooms at fair prices.
oligarch /'ɒligɑ:k/ n. 寡头政治执政者e.g. The oligarch owns several football clubs.
secret away 短语 偷偷藏匿；秘密转移e.g. The firms helped secret away over 600 million dollars in luxury assets.
¶ 3

Since Russia invaded Ukraine, Abramovich has faced heavy international sanctions over his close ties to Vladimir Putin, which forced him to move his massive superyachts to safe waters in Turkey. While legal experts argue that these fake records amount to clear tax fraud that should face criminal prosecution, Abramovich’s legal team strongly denies any wrongdoing and insists he simply followed professional financial advice.

自俄罗斯入侵乌克兰以来，阿布拉莫维奇因与普京关系密切而面临严厉的国际制裁，这迫使他把巨型超级游艇转移到土耳其的安全水域。法律专家认为，这些伪造记录明显构成税务欺诈，应受到刑事起诉；而阿布拉莫维奇的律师团队则坚决否认任何不当行为，坚称他只是听从了专业财务建议。

sanction /'sæŋkʃәn/ n. 核准；制裁；处罚；约束力e.g. The country faces new sanctions from abroad.
fraud /frɒ:d/ n. 欺骗；欺诈；诡计；骗子e.g. That email is a fraud — don't click it.
prosecution /.prɒsi'kju:ʃәn/ n. 执行；经营；起诉e.g. The case may lead to prosecution.
massive /'mæsiv/ a. 大而重的；宽大的；宏伟的e.g. The ship is massive — bigger than a football field.
professional /prә'feʃәnl/ n. 专业人才e.g. Ask a professional for financial advice.
face sanctions 短语 面临制裁e.g. Since the invasion, he has faced heavy international sanctions.
amount to 短语 相当于；等同于e.g. Experts argue these fake records amount to clear tax fraud.
¶ 4

Difficult words: secret away (to hide or place something in a secret location so others cannot find or see it), oligarch (a very rich and powerful business leader who often has strong connections to political leaders), fraud (the crime of tricking people or changing information to illegally get money or power).

难词：secret away（把某物藏到秘密地点，使他人无法找到或看到），oligarch（非常富有且有权势的商业领袖，通常与政治领袖关系密切），fraud（通过欺骗他人或篡改信息来非法获取金钱或权力的犯罪行为，即欺诈）。

deny any wrongdoing 短语 否认任何不当行为e.g. His legal team strongly denies any wrongdoing.

2. Young people and concussion

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-15 · 原文链接
📖 数据显示，每年有170万至300万青少年在运动中遭受脑震荡。最新研究颠覆了“静养最好”的旧观念：少缺课、回归日常生活反而恢复得更好。家长和老师都该了解的脑震荡新处理方式，还能学到 lingering、socialization 等实用词。
¶ 1

According to data, 1.7 million to 3 million young people suffer concussions each year while playing sports.

据数据显示，每年有170万至300万青少年在运动时遭受脑震荡。

suffer concussions 短语 遭受脑震荡e.g. Millions of young people suffer concussions each year while playing sports.
¶ 2

Previously, the treatment for concussions in young people was rest. However, researchers at Children’s National Hospital in Washington, D.C. suggest that rest may no longer be the best treatment for young people with concussions. Pediatric neuropsychologist Dr. Chris Vaughan said that there was a time when a diagnosis involved a couple of fingers and if the patient could stand, they could return to play.

过去，青少年脑震荡的治疗方法是休息。然而，位于华盛顿特区的儿童国家医院的研究人员认为，休息可能不再是青少年脑震荡患者的最佳治疗方式。儿科神经心理学家克里斯·沃恩博士说，曾几何时，诊断就是伸出两根手指问患者看得见几根，如果患者能站起来，就可以重返赛场。

treatment /'tri:tmәnt/ n. 治疗；待遇；处理e.g. The treatment worked well for her.
researcher /ri:'sә:tʃә/ n. 研究人员e.g. Researchers found a better way to help kids recover.
diagnosis /.daiәg'nәusis/ n. 诊断e.g. The doctor's diagnosis was a mild concussion.
return to play 短语 重返赛场e.g. After his injury, he hopes to return to play next month.
¶ 3

Researchers found that children who missed less school after a concussion had fewer lingering symptoms, indicating that returning to school and daily life may be therapeutic for the brain. The study suggests that parents with children who suffer concussions should push socialization, reduce school-related stress, maintain a normal sleep/wake schedule, and encourage light-to-moderate physical activity. The findings reinforce the idea that there are things that can be done to help treat concussions in children and that it is not a wait-and-see type of injury.

研究人员发现，脑震荡后缺课较少的孩子，持续存在的后遗症状更少，这表明重返学校和日常生活可能对大脑有治疗作用。这项研究建议，家有脑震荡孩子的父母应鼓励孩子社交、减少与学业相关的压力、保持正常的睡眠/起床时间表，并鼓励轻度到中度的体育活动。研究结果进一步证实：有一些方法可以帮助治疗儿童脑震荡，这不是一种只能“等等看”的伤病。

concussion /kәn'kʌʃәn/ n. 剧烈摇动；震荡e.g. He got a concussion after falling off his bike.
symptom /'simptәm/ n. 症状；征候；征兆e.g. A headache is a common symptom of a concussion.
lingering /'liŋɡәriŋ/ a. 拖延的e.g. He still has a lingering cough after the cold.
therapeutic /.θerә'pju:tik/ a. 治疗的；有益于健康的e.g. Walking in the park is therapeutic for me.
socialization /.sәuʃәlai'zeiʃәn/ n. 社会化；社会主义化e.g. School is important for children's socialization.
reinforce /.ri:in'fɒ:s/ vt. 加强；增援；补充e.g. This study reinforces what doctors already believed.
schedule /'skedʒuәl/ n. 时间表；一览表；计划表；议事日程e.g. Keep a normal sleep schedule every night.
moderate /'mɒdәrәt/ a. 适度的；稳健的；中等的；节制的e.g. Light-to-moderate exercise is good after a concussion.
injury /'indʒәri/ n. 伤害；侮辱e.g. Rest is not the only way to treat this injury.
miss school 短语 缺课e.g. Children who missed less school had fewer lingering symptoms.
push socialization 短语 鼓励社交e.g. Parents should push socialization after a concussion.
wait-and-see 短语 等等看；静观其变e.g. A concussion is not a wait-and-see type of injury.
¶ 4

Difficult words: lingering (lasting for a long time), socialization (the activity with others), reinforce (to strengthen or support).

难词：lingering（持续很久的；久久不散的），socialization（与他人交往的活动；社交），reinforce（加强；巩固；进一步证实）。
