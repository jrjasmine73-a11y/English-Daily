1. Lithuania and nuclear weapons

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-13 · 原文链接
📖 立陶宛议会迈出历史性一步，决定修改宪法、取消长期以来禁止外国核武器驻扎本国领土的禁令。关键信息：这一动议紧跟特朗普表态愿在立陶宛设立永久美军基地，而俄罗斯已放出严厉威胁称一旦部署将把核武瞄准立陶宛。值得一读：短文密集使用安全政治类措辞（deterrence、sovereignty、deploy），是学习国际新闻用语的好素材。
¶ 1

Lithuania’s parliament has taken a historic step to change its constitution, which aims to remove a long-standing ban on hosting foreign nuclear weapons on its soil.

立陶宛议会已经迈出历史性一步，决定修改宪法，目的是取消长期以来禁止外国核武器驻扎本国领土的禁令。

take a historic step 短语 迈出历史性一步e.g. Lithuania's parliament has taken a historic step to change its constitution.
aim to do sth 短语 旨在做某事e.g. The move aims to remove a long-standing ban on hosting foreign nuclear weapons.
¶ 2

Originally introduced after the fall of the Soviet Union to speed up the departure of Russian troops, the constitutional ban is now seen as outdated given current security threats.

该禁令最初是在苏联解体后出台的，目的是加快俄军撤离；但在当前的安全威胁下，它如今被认为已经过时。

¶ 3

Bordered by Russia’s Kaliningrad region and Russian ally Belarus, both believed to host nuclear warheads, Lithuania seeks to strengthen its position within NATO. The political move comes as US President Donald Trump expressed openness to establishing a permanent US military base in the country, an idea strongly welcomed by Lithuania’s prime minister to deter potential Russian aggression.

立陶宛与俄罗斯的加里宁格勒州和俄罗斯盟友白俄罗斯接壤，两地都被认为部署了核弹头，立陶宛希望借此强化在北约中的地位。美国总统特朗普表示对在该国设立永久美军基地持开放态度，立陶宛总理对这一想法表示热烈欢迎，认为这有助于威慑俄罗斯可能发动的侵略。

deter sb from (doing) sth / deter aggression 短语 威慑、阻止某人（做某事）e.g. The base is meant to deter potential Russian aggression.
be bordered by 短语 与……接壤e.g. Lithuania is bordered by Russia's Kaliningrad region and Belarus.
¶ 4

However, Russia reacted with severe threats and warned it will target nuclear weapons at Lithuania if warheads are deployed there. Experts view Lithuania’s vote as a strong political signal that the nation is determined to defend its sovereignty and secure NATO corridors like the Suwalki Gap.

然而，俄罗斯对此作出了严厉威胁，警告如果在立陶宛部署核弹头，它将把核武器瞄准立陶宛。专家认为，立陶宛的这次投票是一个强烈的政治信号，表明该国决心捍卫主权，并确保苏瓦乌基走廊等北约通道的安全。

a strong political signal 短语 强烈的政治信号e.g. Experts view Lithuania's vote as a strong political signal of its determination.
¶ 5

Difficult words: outdated (old-fashioned and no longer useful or suitable for the current time), deter (to discourage or prevent someone from doing something by making them fear the consequences), sovereignty (the full power and right of a country to govern itself without outside control).

难词：outdated（过时的，不再有用或适应当下的）、deter（威慑，吓阻，通过让对方害怕后果来阻止其行动）、sovereignty（主权，一个国家不受外部干涉、自主治理的完整权力与权利）。

2. Indonesia’s Vice President has problems with school papers

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-12 · 原文链接
📖 印度尼西亚最高法院驳回了针对副总统吉布兰高中学历资格的诉讼。关键信息：吉布兰是前总统佐科的长子，反对者质疑他的高中文凭不满足参选资格，但九名法官一致以原告无诉讼资格为由驳回起诉——尽管没人真正出示过他的毕业证。值得一读：文章涵盖选举法程序措辞（dismiss、petitioners、be sworn in），适合学习法律政治新闻的表达。
¶ 1

Indonesia’s top court has thrown out a case against Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka over his high school credentials.

印度尼西亚最高法院已经驳回了针对副总统吉布兰·拉卡布明·拉卡的高中学历资格提起的诉讼。

throw out a case 短语 驳回诉讼e.g. Indonesia's top court has thrown out a case against the vice president.
¶ 2

Gibran is the oldest son of former President Joko Widodo. Critics claimed his high school diploma didn’t meet national requirements for office and wanted to remove him. Under Indonesian election laws, anyone running for vice president must finish high school or must hold a qualification equivalent to it. All nine judges dismissed the case for legal reasons. They said petitioners didn’t have the right to do so because they were not candidates in the 2024 election.

吉布兰是前总统佐科·维多多的长子。批评者声称他的高中文凭不符合参选的国家要求，希望把他赶下台。根据印尼选举法，任何竞选副总统的人都必须完成高中学业，或持有同等学历。九名法官全部以法律理由驳回了诉讼：他们表示起诉方没有起诉资格，因为他们并不是 2024 年大选的候选人。

meet requirements / meet national requirements 短语 符合要求e.g. Critics claimed his diploma didn't meet national requirements for office.
hold a qualification (equivalent to) 短语 拥有（等同于……的）资格e.g. A candidate must hold a qualification equivalent to a high school diploma.
be sworn in 短语 宣誓就职e.g. Since Gibran has already been sworn in, it is too late to change the results.
¶ 3

Also, since Gibran has already been sworn in, it is too late to change the election results. However, the judges pointed out that nobody actually showed a graduation certificate to prove his foreign credentials match local standards. So while Gibran remains in power, this issue might cause more trouble for him later on.

而且，由于吉布兰已经宣誓就职，现在更改选举结果也为时已晚。然而，法官们指出，实际上并没有人出示过毕业证书来证明他的海外学历符合本地标准。所以，虽然吉布兰仍然掌权，但这个问题日后可能会给他带来更多麻烦。

cause sb trouble / spell trouble 短语 给某人带来麻烦e.g. This issue might cause more trouble for him later on.
¶ 4

Difficult words: credentials (official documents or achievements that prove your skills, background, or education), equivalent (having the same value, amount, or importance as something else), swear (to make a serious formal promise to tell the truth or perform official duties).

难词：credentials（学历证明，证明技能、背景或教育程度的官方文件或成就）、equivalent（等同的，具有相同价值、数量或重要性的）、swear（宣誓，郑重承诺说真话或履行公职）。
