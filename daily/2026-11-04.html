1. Canada and Europe want to work closer together

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-03 · 原文链接
📖 蒙特利尔峰会释放信号：加拿大与欧盟正越走越近。关键信息：在美国贸易与地缘压力下，加拿大已加入欧洲联合防务采购等实际合作。值得读：看清中等国家如何抱团降低对大国的依赖。
¶ 1

A summit in Montreal has highlighted a growing push to deepen political and economic ties between Canada and the EU.

蒙特利尔的一场峰会凸显了加拿大与欧盟之间加深政治和经济联系的势头日益增强。

summit /'sʌmit/ n. 顶点；最高阶层；最高级会议e.g. World leaders will meet at a summit next month to discuss climate change.
highlight /'hailait/ n. 加亮区；精彩场面e.g. This report highlights how fast online shopping is growing.

deepen ties 短语 加深联系e.g. The two cities want to deepen ties through student exchanges and joint projects.
¶ 2

Motivated by trade disputes, geopolitical friction, and economic pressures from the United States, many Canadians are actively favoring European trade over American imports. While European Commission leadership proposed the idea of making Canada an associate member, major member states like France and Germany remain hesitant due to treaty limits and undefined legal terms.

受贸易争端、地缘政治摩擦以及来自美国的经济压力推动，许多加拿大人正积极青睐欧洲贸易而非美国进口商品。尽管欧盟委员会领导层提出了让加拿大成为联系成员的想法，但法国、德国等主要成员国因条约限制和法律术语不明确而仍持犹豫态度。

dispute /dis'pju:t/ n. 争论e.g. The two neighbors settled their dispute over the fence peacefully.
friction /'frikʃәn/ n. 摩擦；摩擦治疗；不和e.g. There was some friction between the two teams after the match.
hesitant /'hezitәnt/ a. 迟疑的；踌躇的；犹豫不定的e.g. He felt hesitant to speak up in the meeting.
treaty /'tri:ti/ n. 条约；谈判e.g. The two countries signed a treaty to protect the shared river.
favor A over B 短语 青睐 A 胜过 Be.g. Many shoppers now favor local markets over big supermarkets.
remain hesitant 短语 仍持犹豫态度e.g. She remained hesitant about moving abroad until she visited the city.
¶ 3

Despite these political hurdles, practical integration is already accelerating across some sectors. Canada has joined joint defense procurement programs and is exploring agreements covering energy, critical raw materials, and student exchanges. Both Canadian and European leaders view this closer partnership as essential for protecting national sovereignty, reducing dangerous dependencies on global superpowers, and securing critical supply chains in an increasingly unstable global economy.

尽管存在这些政治障碍，一些领域的实际一体化已经在加速。加拿大已加入联合防务采购项目，并正在探讨涵盖能源、关键原材料和学生交流的协议。加拿大和欧洲领导人都认为，这种更紧密的伙伴关系对于保护国家主权、减少对全球大国危险的依赖以及在一个日益不稳定的全球经济中保障关键供应链至关重要。

hurdle /'hә:dl/ n. 障碍；跳栏；临时活动篱笆e.g. Money was the biggest hurdle for her small business.
procurement /prә'kjuәmәnt/ n. 获得e.g. The company improved its procurement process to save money.
sovereignty /'sɒvrәnti/ n. 主权；独立国e.g. Every nation wants to protect its sovereignty and make its own decisions.
dependencies n. 依赖性；相关性；管理e.g. Our dependency on phones grows a little more every year.
unstable /.ʌn'steibl/ a. 不稳定的e.g. The weather has been unstable all week, sunny one hour and rainy the next.
¶ 4

Difficult words: friction (disagreement, conflict, or tension between people or groups with different views), associate member (a member of an organization or group who has limited rights or status compared to full members), procurement (obtaining or buying goods and services, especially for a government or organization).

生词：friction（意见分歧、冲突或持不同观点的群体之间的紧张关系），associate member（组织或团体中权利或地位有限的成员，区别于正式成员），procurement（获取或购买商品和服务，尤其指政府或机构的采购行为）。

2. Patrick Stewart, Legendary Star Trek Actor, Dies at 86

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-10-31 · 原文链接
📖 英国传奇演员帕特里克·斯图尔特以 86 岁辞世。关键信息：他从皇家莎士比亚剧团的舞台走向《星际迷航》和《X 战警》，成为几代观众的共同记忆。值得读：看一位舞台演员如何用一生诠释经典角色。
¶ 1

Patrick Stewart, the British actor best known for playing Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek and Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films, has died at 86.

英国演员帕特里克·斯图尔特以 86 岁辞世，他最著名的角色是在《星际迷航》中饰演让-吕克·皮卡德舰长，以及在《X 战警》系列电影中饰演查尔斯·泽维尔教授。

best known for 短语 以……最为人所知e.g. This town is best known for its beautiful beaches and fresh seafood.
¶ 2

Stewart died on October 28 in Los Angeles, following a brief battle with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia.

斯图尔特于 10 月 28 日在洛杉矶去世，此前曾短暂与阿尔茨海默病和路易体痴呆作斗争。

¶ 3

"We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today," his X-Men co-star Hugh Jackman wrote on social media. "And, in my book, a tremendous friend and man."

他的《X 战警》搭档休·杰克曼在社交媒体上写道：“我们今天失去了一位舞台和银幕上的伟大演员。”“在我看来，他更是一位了不起的朋友和了不起的人。”

¶ 4

Although Stewart became famous for his screen roles, he spent much of his career onstage.

尽管斯图尔特因银幕角色而成名，但他职业生涯的大部分时间都在舞台上度过。

¶ 5

Born in Mirfield, Yorkshire, in northern England, Stewart grew up in a working-class family, and later spoke openly about witnessing domestic violence as a child. He joined an amateur drama group at 12 and developed a love for Shakespeare.

斯图尔特 1940 年出生于英格兰北部的约克郡米尔菲尔德，在一个工人阶级家庭长大，后来他公开谈起自己童年时目睹家庭暴力的经历。他 12 岁加入了一个业余戏剧团体，并由此爱上了莎士比亚。

amateur /'æmәtә/ n. 业余爱好者；外行；爱好者e.g. He started as an amateur singer before joining a band.
¶ 6

"The stage would prove to be a safe space, a refuge from real life in which I could inhabit another person, living in another place and time," he later wrote.

他后来写道：“舞台证明是一个安全的空间，是逃离现实生活的避难所，在那里我可以化身为另一个人，生活在另一个地方和时代。”

refuge /'refju:dʒ/ n. 避难所；安全地带；避难；庇护e.g. The quiet park is a refuge from the busy city streets.
inhabit /in'hæbit/ vt. 居住于；占据；栖息e.g. Thousands of birds inhabit this island in summer.
a safe space 短语 一个安全的空间e.g. The library is a safe space where students can read and relax.
¶ 7

He attended the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School and spent 14 years with the Royal Shakespeare Company, performing in numerous Shakespeare plays.

他就读于布里斯托老维克戏剧学校，并在皇家莎士比亚剧团度过了 14 年，出演了众多莎士比亚戏剧。

numerous /'nju:mәrәs/ a. 很多的；数目众多的；多数的e.g. We have received numerous messages from our readers.
¶ 8

Stewart's television breakthrough came in 1987 with Star Trek: The Next Generation. He initially expected the series to fail, but instead, it ran for seven seasons and 178 episodes, followed by four feature films.

斯图尔特的电视突破出现在 1987 年的《星际迷航：下一代》。他起初以为这部剧会失败，但结果它连播七季、共 178 集，随后还拍了四部电影。

breakthrough /'breikθru:/ n. 突破e.g. Her first novel was a real breakthrough for her career.
feature /'fi:tʃә/ n. 面孔的一部分(如眼、口等)；特征；容貌；特色；特写e.g. The festival will feature several short films this weekend.
¶ 9

His Shakespearean training helped him portray the starship commander, whose formal manner reminded him of characters he had played onstage.

他的莎士比亚训练帮助他塑造了这位星舰舰长，这个举止正式的角色让他想起自己在舞台上演过的角色。

portray /pɒ:'trei/ vt. 描绘；描写；描绘...的肖像e.g. The young actor will portray a brave soldier in the new film.
formal /'fɒ:mәl/ a. 正式的；形式的；礼仪的；拘于礼节的；拘谨的e.g. Please wear formal clothes for the wedding dinner.
¶ 10

In 2000, Stewart appeared as Professor Charles Xavier in the first X-Men film. He continued playing the character in several more movies, including 2017's Logan and 2022's Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.

2000 年，斯图尔特在第一部《X 战警》电影中饰演查尔斯·泽维尔教授。此后他又在多部电影中继续饰演这一角色，包括 2017 年的《金刚狼 3》和 2022 年的《奇异博士 2：疯狂多元宇宙》。

¶ 11

Throughout his career, Stewart continued working in theater, earning three Olivier Awards and a Tony Award nomination for Macbeth. He also won a Grammy in 1996 for his spoken-word recording of Peter and the Wolf.

在整个职业生涯中，斯图尔特始终坚持舞台演出，赢得三座奥利弗奖，并因《麦克白》获得托尼奖提名。1996 年，他还凭借《彼得与狼》的有声朗读录音获得格莱美奖。

nomination /.nɒmi'neiʃәn/ n. 提名；任命；提名权e.g. Her nomination for best actress surprised everyone.
earn an award 短语 获得奖项e.g. She earned an award for her excellent teaching last year.
¶ 12

He was knighted by Queen Elizabeth II in 2010.

2010 年，他由伊丽莎白二世女王授予爵士头衔。

knighted /naɪt/ n. 骑士；武士；爵士e.g. The famous scientist was knighted for his work in medicine.
¶ 13

Stewart also became popular with younger audiences through his humor and social media posts. He appeared in The Simpsons, Futurama and The Emoji Movie.

斯图尔特还凭借幽默感和社交媒体帖子受到年轻观众的喜爱。他曾出现在《辛普森一家》《飞出个未来》和《表情奇幻冒险》中。

¶ 14

Stewart is survived by his wife, singer and songwriter Sunny Ozell, whom he married in 2013, and his two children.

他的遗属包括妻子、歌手兼词曲作者桑尼·奥泽尔（两人于 2013 年结婚），以及他的两个孩子。

legendary /'ledʒәndәri/ a. 传说的；传奇中说的；传奇般的；传说中的；著名的e.g. The singer is already a legendary figure in pop music.
survive /sә'vaiv/ vt. 比...活得长；生存；生还；幸免于e.g. Only a few old houses survived the big fire.
grow up 短语 长大；成长e.g. She grew up in a small village before moving to the city.
speak openly about 短语 公开谈论e.g. It's good to speak openly about your feelings with close friends.
