1. Czechs protest to keep free TV and radio

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-20 · 原文链接
📖 捷克爆发了自1989年天鹅绒革命以来最大规模的抗议——多达40万人走上布拉格街头，只为保住公共电视台和电台的独立性。政府想取消收视费、改由国家预算直接拨款，还要削减15%的经费，民众担心这会让政客捏住新闻的命脉。一篇195词的短新闻，带你读懂一场为媒体自由而起的抗议。
¶ 1

Up to 400,000 people gathered at Letná Park in Prague, Czech Republic, on Sunday in what organizers call the largest demonstration since the 1989 Velvet Revolution.

周日，多达40万人在捷克共和国首都布拉格的莱特纳公园集会，组织者称这是自1989年天鹅绒革命以来规模最大的一次示威。

gather /'gæðә/ n. 聚集；集合e.g. Every morning, parents gather at the school gate to wait for their kids.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻都常用；既可说人集合，也可说收集东西（gather information）。
demonstration /.demәn'streiʃәn/ n. 示威游行；演示e.g. The workers held a peaceful demonstration in front of the city hall.💡 场合：新闻常用词；作“演示”解时多用于产品或教学场景（a cooking demonstration）。
¶ 2

Protesters opposed Prime Minister Andrej Babiš’s government plan to abolish public license fees and fund Czech Television and Czech Radio directly from the state budget while cutting their funding by 15 percent. Critics and media experts warn that direct state financing gives politicians financial leverage over newsrooms and threatens editorial independence, and draw comparisons to government pressure on media in Hungary and Slovakia.

抗议者反对总理安德烈·巴比什的政府计划：取消公共收视费，改由国家预算直接为捷克电视台和捷克电台提供资金，同时削减15%的经费。批评者和媒体专家警告说，国家直接出资会让政客在财政上拿捏新闻编辑部，威胁编辑独立性，他们还拿匈牙利和斯洛伐克政府向媒体施压的情况作比较。

abolish /ә'bɒliʃ/ vt. 正式废除e.g. The school abolished the old rule about no phones in class.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用，比 cancel 更正式，专指废除制度、法律、习俗。
leverage /'levәridʒ/ n. 影响力；杠杆；可借助的力量e.g. Good English gives you leverage when you look for a better job.💡 场合：职场和新闻常用，偏正式；既可指谈判筹码，也可指可以利用的优势。
threaten /'θretn/ vt. 威胁；危及e.g. Heavy rain threatened the outdoor school sports day.💡 场合：新闻和日常都常用；既可指人发出威胁，也可指某事危及另一件事。
independence /.indi'pendәns/ n. 独立；自主e.g. After years of hard work, she finally enjoys the independence of running her own shop.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用；既可指国家独立，也可指个人的自主、经济独立。
pressure /'preʃә/ n. 压力e.g. The new rule puts a lot of pressure on young teachers.💡 场合：日常和新闻都高频；既可指心理压力，也可指外界施加的压力。
funding /'fʌndiŋ/ [经] 资金；经费e.g. The school needs more funding for new computers.💡 场合：新闻和正式场合常用；日常口语里更常说 money。
draw comparisons to 短语 拿……作比较；与……相提并论e.g. People often draw comparisons to their school days when they talk about work.💡 场合：新闻报道和正式书面语常用，比日常口语的 compare with 更正式，常用于指出两件事的异同。
¶ 3

The bill was pushed rapidly through parliament at 4:36 AM, just ahead of the November 17th anniversary, and it became a catalyst for wider anger against the ruling coalition’s approach to culture, defense, and democratic institutions. Babiš is facing intense public pressure, and he announced he would skip Velvet Revolution celebrations to ease tensions and signaled openness to a compromise funding model.

这项法案在凌晨4点36分被火速推过议会，正赶在11月17日纪念日之前，它成了导火索，点燃了民众对执政联盟在文化、国防和民主制度方面做法的更广泛愤怒。巴比什面临巨大的公众压力，他宣布将不出席天鹅绒革命庆祝活动以缓和紧张局势，并表示愿意接受一种折中的经费模式。

catalyst /'kætәlist/ n. 催化剂；导火索e.g. The new library became a catalyst for reading in our neighborhood.💡 场合：新闻、正式书面和演讲中常用，偏书面；日常口语里更常说 trigger 或 turning point。
rapidly /'ræpidli/ adv. 迅速地；很快地e.g. The rain came rapidly, and we all ran inside.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面常用；日常口语里更常说 quickly 或 fast。
compromise /'kɒmprәmaiz/ n. 折中方案；妥协e.g. After a long talk, we reached a compromise on the holiday plan.💡 场合：新闻、职场和日常都常用；作动词时指“妥协、让步”，作名词指“折中方案”。
signal /'signl/ n. （动词）表示；发出信号e.g. Her smile signaled that she was happy with the result.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面常用；日常口语里更常说 show 或 mean。
ease tensions 短语 缓和紧张局势；缓解紧张气氛e.g. A walk together after dinner can ease tensions between parents and teenagers.💡 场合：新闻和正式场合常用，日常口语里也可说，但比 relax the atmosphere 更书面。
push through 短语 强行通过（法案、决定等）；硬推e.g. The school pushed through the new phone rule without asking any students.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面语常用，带“不顾反对、仓促强行”的意味；日常口语也可用于推行计划或决定。
signal openness to 短语 表示愿意（接受/考虑）……；释放开放信号e.g. After weeks of silence, she finally signaled openness to a new way of teaching.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面语常用，比 say yes 更委婉、正式，表示“态度上开了口子、还没完全答应”。
¶ 4

Difficult words: abolish (to formally put an end to a system, practice, or institution), leverage (the power or ability to influence people, events, or decisions and get the outcome you want), catalyst (a person or event that quickly causes a big change or action).

难词：abolish（正式废除某一制度、做法或机构），leverage（影响他人、事件或决定并得到自己想要结果的权力或能力），catalyst（迅速引发重大变化或行动的人或事件）。

come under pressure 短语 受到压力；面临压力e.g. Small bookshops often come under pressure from big online stores.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面常用；日常口语里也可说，比 be stressed 更正式、更客观。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. The school needs more ____ for new computers.
✓ funding　资金；经费
The school needs more funding for new computers.
学校需要更多经费来买新电脑。
2. The school ____ the new phone rule without asking any students.
✓ pushed through（push through 的变形）　强行通过（法案、决定等）；硬推
The school pushed through the new phone rule without asking any students.
学校没征求任何学生的意见，就强行通过了新的手机管理规定。
3. Every morning, parents ____ at the school gate to wait for their kids.
✓ gather　聚集；集合
Every morning, parents gather at the school gate to wait for their kids.
每天早上，家长们都聚集在校门口等孩子。
4. Her smile ____ that she was happy with the result.
✓ signaled（signal 的变形）　（动词）表示；发出信号
Her smile signaled that she was happy with the result.
她的微笑表示她对结果很满意。
5. A walk together after dinner can ____ between parents and teenagers.
✓ ease tensions　缓和紧张局势；缓解紧张气氛
A walk together after dinner can ease tensions between parents and teenagers.
晚饭后一起散散步，可以缓和父母和青春期孩子之间的紧张关系。
6. The new library became a ____ for reading in our neighborhood.
✓ catalyst　催化剂；导火索
The new library became a catalyst for reading in our neighborhood.
新图书馆成了我们小区阅读风气的催化剂。
7. The rain came ____, and we all ran inside.
✓ rapidly　迅速地；很快地
The rain came rapidly, and we all ran inside.
雨下得很快，我们都跑进了屋里。
8. The school ____ the old rule about no phones in class.
✓ abolished（abolish 的变形）　正式废除
The school abolished the old rule about no phones in class.
学校废除了不准带手机进课堂的旧规定。
9. Good English gives you ____ when you look for a better job.
✓ leverage　影响力；杠杆；可借助的力量
Good English gives you leverage when you look for a better job.
找更好的工作时，好的英语会给你带来优势。
10. After a long talk, we reached a ____ on the holiday plan.
✓ compromise　折中方案；妥协
After a long talk, we reached a compromise on the holiday plan.
经过长谈，我们就假期计划达成了一个折中方案。
11. The workers held a peaceful ____ in front of the city hall.
✓ demonstration　示威游行；演示
The workers held a peaceful demonstration in front of the city hall.
工人们在市政厅前举行了一场和平示威。
12. After weeks of silence, she finally ____ a new way of teaching.
✓ signaled openness to（signal openness to 的变形）　表示愿意（接受/考虑）……；释放开放信号
After weeks of silence, she finally signaled openness to a new way of teaching.
沉默了几周之后，她终于表示愿意尝试一种新的教学方法。
13. The new rule puts a lot of ____ on young teachers.
✓ pressure　压力
The new rule puts a lot of pressure on young teachers.
新规定给年轻老师带来了很大的压力。
14. People often ____ their school days when they talk about work.
✓ draw comparisons to　拿……作比较；与……相提并论
People often draw comparisons to their school days when they talk about work.
人们谈起工作时，常常会拿自己的学生时代来作比较。
15. Heavy rain ____ the outdoor school sports day.
✓ threatened（threaten 的变形）　威胁；危及
Heavy rain threatened the outdoor school sports day.
大雨威胁着学校户外运动会的举行。
