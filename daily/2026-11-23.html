1. Young people lose hearing

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-18 · 原文链接
📖 《英国医学杂志》一项研究警告：全球 10 亿年轻人正面临听力受损的风险，罪魁祸首就是每天离不开的耳机和过大的音量。短短几段，告诉你多少分贝、听多久会伤耳，以及为什么听力一旦受损就再也回不来。
¶ 1

The British Medical Journal published a study which showed that 1 billion young people were at risk of hearing loss.

《英国医学杂志》发表的一项研究显示，10 亿年轻人正面临听力损失的风险。

¶ 2

Researchers shared their analysis of data from 2000 to 2021 on unsafe listening practices. If the volume is too loud for too long, headphones, a concert, or even a restaurant could cause irreversible damage. Young people are complaining about persistent ringing in their ears, although their hearing tests look normal. However, several years later, their hearing reduces.

研究人员分享了他们对 2000 年至 2021 年间不安全听音习惯的数据分析。如果音量过大、时间过长，耳机、音乐会，甚至餐厅都可能造成不可逆的损伤。年轻人抱怨耳朵里持续有嗡嗡声，尽管他们的听力测试结果看起来正常。然而，几年之后，他们的听力就会下降。

irreversible /,iri'vә:sәbl,-sib-/ a. 不可逆的；不可改变的；不能倒置的；不能翻转的；不能倒转的；不可撤销的e.g. If you forget to save the file, the loss is irreversible.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用，语气严肃，指无法挽回、无法恢复；日常口语较少用，口语多说 can't be undone。
persistent /pә'sistәnt/ a. 固执的；坚持的；持续的；作用持久的e.g. He has had a persistent cough for three weeks.💡 场合：正式和日常都常用，指持续不断、老是不好的状态，多用于咳嗽、疼痛、问题、噪音等，略带负面色彩。
damage /'dæmidʒ/ n. 损害；伤害e.g. Drinking too much can damage your liver.💡 场合：日常和新闻都常用，可作动词也可作名词；作动词指损坏、伤害身体或东西，语气中性偏正式。
volume /'vɒljum/ n. 册；卷；体积；容量；大量；许多；份量；音量e.g. I always keep the volume low when I listen to music at night.💡 场合：日常口语最常用，指电视、手机、音响的音量；也用于正式场合指“音量、响度”。
ringing /'riŋiŋ/ a. 响亮的；明白的；清脆的；干脆的e.g. After the loud concert, I heard a ringing in my ears all night.💡 场合：日常口语常用，指耳朵里的嗡嗡声（耳鸣）或电话、门铃的铃声；说耳鸣时常说 ringing in the ears。
complain about 短语 抱怨；埋怨e.g. My neighbor often complains about the noise from upstairs.💡 场合：日常口语最常用，指抱怨某事；新闻里也常用，语气中性，略带负面情绪。
¶ 3

The loud sounds damage sensory cells in the cochlea, the snail-looking part in the inner ear. Once these cells die, you can’t get them back. 85 decibels sound like a blender, 100 decibels sound like a subway train, and 120 decibels sound like a concert or a siren.

巨大的声响会损伤耳蜗中的感觉细胞，耳蜗是内耳里形似蜗牛的部分。这些细胞一旦死亡，就再也无法恢复。85 分贝听起来像一台搅拌机，100 分贝像一列地铁，120 分贝像一场音乐会或一声警笛。

sensory /'sensәri/ a. 知觉的；感觉的；传递感觉的e.g. The baby learns about the world through sensory play, like touching sand and water.💡 场合：医学、教育和科普文章常用，指与感觉、感官有关的；日常口语较少用。
decibel /'desibel/ n. 分贝e.g. A normal conversation is about 60 decibels.💡 场合：科普和新闻常用，是计量声音大小的单位；日常口语提到噪音、音量时会用到。
¶ 4

According to experts, 85 decibels during a 40-hour work week is the maximum safe limit. Just 2.5 hours of 92 decibels can damage your ears. However, only one episode of intense noise exposure can lead to lifelong hearing loss.

据专家说，在每周 40 小时的工作时间里，85 分贝是最高安全上限。仅仅 2.5 小时的 92 分贝就能损伤耳朵。然而，仅仅一次强烈的噪声接触就可能导致终身听力损失。

exposure /ik'spәuʒә/ n. 暴露；揭发；揭露e.g. Too much exposure to the sun can burn your skin.💡 场合：新闻、健康科普和正式场合常用，指长时间接触某种有害的东西（噪音、阳光、辐射、病毒等），语气偏正式。
lifelong /'laiflɒŋ/ a. 终身的；毕生的e.g. Reading is a lifelong habit that can change your life.💡 场合：新闻和正式书面常用，指持续一生的、终身的；日常口语也可用，语气比 all my life 更正式一点。
intense /in'tens/ a. 非常的；强烈的；紧张的；热情的e.g. The intense heat in summer makes it hard to work outside.💡 场合：日常和新闻都常用，指强烈的、剧烈的（光、热、噪音、感情、竞争等），语气比 strong 更强。
episode /'episәud/ n. 插曲；插话；有趣的事件；一段情节e.g. I watched a new episode of my favorite show last night.💡 场合：日常口语常指电视剧的一集；新闻和正式场合也指一段经历、一次事件（如一次生病、一场风波），语气中性。
maximum /'mæksimәn/ n. 极点；最大量；极大e.g. This bag can hold a maximum of 10 kilograms.💡 场合：日常和正式场合都常用，指最大量、最高限度；常和 speed、temperature、limit 搭配。
lead to 短语 导致；引起e.g. Too much screen time can lead to poor sleep.💡 场合：新闻、学术和日常都常用，指导致某种结果（多指不好的结果），比 cause 更正式一点。
according to 短语 据……所说；根据e.g. According to my teacher, the exam will be easy this time.💡 场合：新闻、正式和日常都常用，用于引用消息来源或某人的说法，语气中性。
¶ 5

Difficult words: irreversible (impossible to change or return to a previous condition), persistent (continue over a long period of time), exposure (affected by something).

生词：irreversible（不可逆转的，不可能改变或恢复到从前状态的），persistent（持续的，在很长一段时间内持续存在的），exposure（接触，受到某种事物的影响）。

be at risk of 短语 面临……的风险；有……的危险e.g. People who smoke are at risk of heart disease.💡 场合：新闻、健康科普和正式讨论常用，指面临某种不好的可能；日常口语也可用，语气比 may be in danger 更正式一点。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. If you forget to save the file, the loss is ____.
✓ irreversible　不可逆的；不可改变的；不能倒置的；不能翻转的；不能倒转的；不可撤销的
If you forget to save the file, the loss is irreversible.
如果你忘了保存文件，这个损失就无法挽回了。
2. ____ my teacher, the exam will be easy this time.
✓ according to　据……所说；根据
According to my teacher, the exam will be easy this time.
据我的老师说，这次考试会很简单。
3. Drinking too much can ____ your liver.
✓ damage　损害；伤害
Drinking too much can damage your liver.
喝太多酒会损伤肝脏。
4. Too much screen time can ____ poor sleep.
✓ lead to　导致；引起
Too much screen time can lead to poor sleep.
看屏幕时间太长会导致睡眠变差。
5. The baby learns about the world through ____ play, like touching sand and water.
✓ sensory　知觉的；感觉的；传递感觉的
The baby learns about the world through sensory play, like touching sand and water.
宝宝通过感官游戏认识世界，比如摸沙子和水。
6. This bag can hold a ____ of 10 kilograms.
✓ maximum　极点；最大量；极大
This bag can hold a maximum of 10 kilograms.
这个包最多能装 10 公斤。
7. Too much ____ to the sun can burn your skin.
✓ exposure　暴露；揭发；揭露
Too much exposure to the sun can burn your skin.
过多接触阳光会晒伤皮肤。
8. I always keep the ____ low when I listen to music at night.
✓ volume　册；卷；体积；容量；大量；许多；份量；音量
I always keep the volume low when I listen to music at night.
我晚上听音乐时总是把音量调得很低。
9. Reading is a ____ habit that can change your life.
✓ lifelong　终身的；毕生的
Reading is a lifelong habit that can change your life.
阅读是能改变人生的终身习惯。
10. I watched a new ____ of my favorite show last night.
✓ episode　插曲；插话；有趣的事件；一段情节
I watched a new episode of my favorite show last night.
昨晚我看了最喜欢的剧的最新一集。
11. A normal conversation is about 60 ____.
✓ decibels（decibel 的变形）　分贝
A normal conversation is about 60 decibels.
正常说话大约是 60 分贝。
12. People who smoke ____ heart disease.
✓ are at risk of（be at risk of 的变形）　面临……的风险；有……的危险
People who smoke are at risk of heart disease.
吸烟的人有患心脏病的风险。
13. My neighbor often ____ the noise from upstairs.
✓ complains about（complain about 的变形）　抱怨；埋怨
My neighbor often complains about the noise from upstairs.
我的邻居经常抱怨楼上的噪音。
14. He has had a ____ cough for three weeks.
✓ persistent　固执的；坚持的；持续的；作用持久的
He has had a persistent cough for three weeks.
他咳嗽已经持续三周了。
15. The ____ heat in summer makes it hard to work outside.
✓ intense　非常的；强烈的；紧张的；热情的
The intense heat in summer makes it hard to work outside.
夏天的酷热让人很难在户外工作。
