1. One billion meals thrown away every day

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-10-19 · 原文链接
📖 联合国环境规划署发布最新报告：全球每天竟浪费掉超过10亿份食物，而与此同时还有数百万人在挨饿。更惊人的是，食物浪费贡献了全球8%-10%的温室气体排放，其中家庭是"头号浪费大户"。值得读，因为减少浪费是每个人都能马上做到的环保行动。
¶ 1

More than one billion meals are thrown away worldwide every day, despite famine and millions of people starving. The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) recently reported that households are responsible for 60 per cent of food waste, followed by restaurants and food services at 28 per cent, and shops at 12 per cent. The Maldives has the highest estimated household food waste per person, at around 260 kilograms annually. Syria, Seychelles, Mexico and Tunisia also recorded high levels. Globally, food waste accounts for 8 to 10 per cent of greenhouse gas emissions. As discarded food decomposes in landfills, it releases the dangerous greenhouse gas methane, which accelerates global warming.

尽管全球仍有饥荒、数百万人在挨饿，世界各地每天仍有超过10亿份食物被扔掉。联合国环境规划署（UNEP）最近报告称，家庭要为60%的食物浪费负责，其次是餐馆和餐饮服务（28%），商店占12%。马尔代夫的人均家庭食物浪费量估计最高，每年约260公斤。叙利亚、塞舌尔、墨西哥和突尼斯的浪费量也很高。在全球范围内，食物浪费占温室气体排放的8%到10%。被丢弃的食物在垃圾填埋场分解时会释放危险的温室气体甲烷，加速全球变暖。

starving a. 挨饿的；饥饿的e.g. After hiking all day, I was absolutely starving.

methane /'meθein/ n. 甲烷；沼气e.g. Methane from cows is one source of greenhouse gas.

throw away 短语 扔掉，丢弃e.g. I never throw away leftover rice; I always turn it into fried rice.
¶ 2

Individuals, businesses and governments can all take practical steps to reduce food waste. People should plan meals, prepare shopping lists, and purchase only what they need. We should store ingredients correctly and freeze surplus portions. We should also turn leftovers into new dishes and compost inedible waste. Restaurants and supermarkets also have a huge part to play in reducing food loss. They can donate surplus food to communities in need. Martin Krause, Director of the Climate Change Division at UNEP, said: "Every meal rescued, shared or never wasted is food put to better use." Reducing waste would save money, improve food security and help curb climate change.

个人、企业和政府都可以采取实际行动来减少食物浪费。人们应该规划用餐、准备购物清单，只买需要的东西。食材要妥善储存，多余的可以冷冻。我们还应该把剩饭变成新菜式，并把不能吃的厨余堆肥。餐馆和超市在减少食物损耗上也大有作为，他们可以把多余的食物捐给有需要的人。联合国环境规划署气候变化司司长马丁·克劳斯说："每一份被挽救、被分享或从未被浪费的食物，都被更好地利用了。"减少浪费既能省钱，又能改善粮食安全，还能帮助减缓气候变化。

surplus /'sә:plәs/ n. 剩余；过剩；盈余e.g. We had surplus apples, so I gave some to my neighbors.

2. Nobel Medicine Prize Awarded for Brain Research

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-10-07 · 原文链接
📖 今年诺贝尔医学奖颁给了"光遗传学"的开创者：三位科学家用光来控制脑细胞，让人第一次能精准"开关"神经元。奖金120万美元，由美国的德塞罗斯与德国的黑格曼、纳格尔共享。值得读，因为光遗传学正带来治疗自闭症、精神分裂症甚至遗传性失明的新希望。
¶ 1

Three scientists have won the Nobel Prize in medicine for developing a way to use light to control brain cells, helping researchers understand how the brain works.

三位科学家因开发出用光控制脑细胞的方法而获得诺贝尔医学奖，这项方法帮助研究人员了解大脑是如何工作的。

a way to do sth 短语 做某事的方法、途径e.g. Keeping a daily list is a simple way to stay organized.
¶ 2

American Karl Deisseroth and Germans Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel will share the $1.2 million prize.

美国科学家卡尔·德塞罗斯以及德国的彼得·黑格曼和格奥尔格·纳格尔将共享120万美元奖金。

¶ 3

The breakthrough began with Hegemann's research into how a type of green algae swims toward light. Working with Nagel, he discovered a protein that produces an electrical signal when illuminated by blue light.

这项突破始于黑格曼对一种会向光游动的绿藻的研究。他与纳格尔合作发现了一种蛋白质，在蓝光照射下会产生电信号。

breakthrough /'breikθru:/ n. 突破e.g. Her new novel was a major breakthrough in her career.
algae /'ælgi:/ pl. 藻类；海藻e.g. The pond was covered with green algae in summer.
illuminated a. 被照明的；受启发的；加了灯饰的；加了彩饰的e.g. The whole street was illuminated by colorful lights.
¶ 4

This offered a way to study the brain, where nerve cells, called neurons, communicate using electrical signals. These cells do not normally respond to light.

这为研究大脑提供了一条新路：大脑中的神经细胞（即神经元）靠电信号交流，而这些细胞通常对光没有反应。

¶ 5

Deisseroth inserted the gene for the algae protein into selected neurons in living mice. This gave the cells instructions to make the protein themselves, allowing him to activate them by shining light on them through thin optical fibers implanted in the brain.

德塞罗斯把这种藻类蛋白的基因插入活体小鼠的特定神经元中，等于给这些细胞下达了制造该蛋白质的"指令"，让他可以通过植入大脑的细光纤，用光束激活这些细胞。

selected /si'lektid/ a. 挑选出来的；精选的e.g. He selected a quiet corner and started reading.
implanted [医] 植入的e.g. The chip was implanted under the skin of the dog.
activate /'æktiveit/ vt. 使活动；使激活；正式建成e.g. You need to activate the card before using it.
¶ 6

That work helped establish optogenetics: using light and genetic techniques to control cells. Scientists can now switch particular neurons on or off while animals perform tasks.

这项工作奠定了光遗传学的基础：用光和基因技术来控制细胞。科学家现在可以在动物执行任务时，把特定的神经元"打开"或"关掉"。

switch on/off 短语 打开/关掉（电源、开关）e.g. You can switch the lights on or off with a tap on the wall.
¶ 7

By observing changes in the animals' behavior, researchers can learn what those cells do and how they work together. For example, Deisseroth used light to wake sleeping mice, showing how particular nerve cells control wakefulness.

通过观察动物行为的变化，研究人员可以了解这些细胞的功能以及它们如何协同工作。比如，德塞罗斯曾用光唤醒熟睡的小鼠，证明了特定神经细胞控制着清醒状态。

¶ 8

"This is using light to control things, to make things happen, and to do it in a very precise way," Deisseroth told The Associated Press.

德塞罗斯在接受美联社采访时说："这是用光来控制、让事情发生，而且方式极其精准。"

precise /pri'sais/ a. 精确的；严谨的；明确的e.g. She gave me precise directions to her new apartment.
¶ 9

Optogenetics is mainly a research tool, but discoveries made using it could lead to treatments. Identifying the cells involved in a particular symptom could make it possible to target them with medicines or other methods. Researchers are already exploring possible treatments for autism and schizophrenia based on these findings.

光遗传学目前主要是研究工具，但由此获得的发现可能带来新的疗法。找出与某种症状相关的细胞，就可能用药物或其他方法精准地针对它们。研究人员已经在探索基于这些发现治疗自闭症和精神分裂症的可能。

identifying n. 识别；标识；标识关系e.g. Identifying the real cause took the doctor two weeks.
target /'tɑ:git/ n. 文中为动词：针对e.g. Our ads target young parents with kids under five.
symptom /'simptәm/ n. 症状；征候；征兆e.g. A bad cough is a common symptom of the flu.
lead to 短语 导致，带来（结果）e.g. Too much coffee late at night can lead to trouble falling asleep.
based on 短语 基于，根据e.g. The movie is based on a true story from a small town.
make it possible to 短语 使……成为可能e.g. Phone alarms make it possible to never oversleep again.
¶ 10

The technique itself is also being tested as a way to restore some vision in people with an inherited form of blindness. Researchers make cells in the retina, at the back of the eye, sensitive to light. Patients then wear special glasses that send light to activate those cells.

这项技术本身也在被测试，能否恢复遗传性失明患者的部分视力。研究人员让视网膜（眼睛后部的组织）中的细胞对光敏感，患者再戴上特制眼镜，把光信号发送过去激活这些细胞。

inherited /in'heritid/ a. 遗传的；继承权的；通过继承得到的e.g. He inherited his blue eyes from his mother.
retina /'retinә/ n. 视网膜e.g. The image is formed on the retina of the eye.
