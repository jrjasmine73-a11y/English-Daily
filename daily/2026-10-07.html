1. A shark in a canal

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-06 · 原文链接
📖 韩国釜山一条3.5米长的鲨鱼被困在狭窄的港口运河里超过两周，救援队用50米长的大网想把它“赶”回大海，渔网却总被水下礁石挂住，第二次救援也失败了。这条鲨鱼反而成了当地明星，吸引了约60万人前来围观。专家怀疑城市的噪音和灯光干扰了它的方向感——读这篇还能学到 herd（驱赶）、snag（挂住）等地道动词用法。
¶ 1

In Busan, South Korea, a 3.5-meter shark has been stuck in a narrow port canal for over two weeks after a second rescue attempt failed.

在韩国釜山，一条3.5米长的鲨鱼在第二次救援尝试失败后，已被困在狭窄的港口运河里超过两周。

be stuck in 短语 被困在……中e.g. We were stuck in traffic for two hours.
¶ 2

Rescuers tried using a massive 50-meter net to gently herd the animal back out toward the open ocean; however, the operation had to be called off when the net kept snagging on underwater rocks. The shark, believed to be a bronze whaler, has turned into a major local celebrity, and it drew roughly 600,000 visitors to the canal area. Marine experts think noisy surroundings and city lights might be confusing the shark’s senses and make it hard for the animal to navigate out on its own.

救援人员试图用一张巨大的50米长渔网，温和地把这条鲨鱼“赶”回开阔的海域；然而渔网不断被水下礁石挂住，行动只好叫停。这条鲨鱼据信是一条铜鲨，如今成了当地的大明星，运河一带吸引了约60万名游客。海洋专家认为，嘈杂的环境和城市灯光可能干扰了鲨鱼的感官，使它难以独自找到出路。

herd … back toward 短语 把……驱赶回……e.g. Rescuers used a massive net to gently herd the animal back out toward the open ocean.
call off 短语 取消；叫停e.g. They had to call off the match because of the storm.
snag on 短语 被……勾住、挂住e.g. My sweater snagged on the fence.
¶ 3

Rescuers have now positioned the net to keep the animal close to the sea. They hope changing tides will help it swim home. Meanwhile, researchers note that rising sea temperatures are driving significantly more large sharks toward South Korea’s coastlines.

救援人员现在已把渔网布设在靠近大海的一侧，希望潮汐变化能帮它游回家。与此同时，研究人员指出，海水温度上升正把越来越多的大型鲨鱼“推”向韩国海岸线。

¶ 4

Difficult words: herd (to gather and guide animals or people to move together in a specific direction), snag (to catch or get caught on something sharp or rough).

生词：herd（把动物或人聚集起来，引导他们朝特定方向一起移动），snag（被尖锐或粗糙的东西勾住、挂住）。

draw (visitors) 短语 吸引（游客）e.g. The shark drew roughly 600,000 visitors to the canal area.

2. Is rejection good for us?

BBC Learning English · 6 Minute English · 2026-10-07 · 原文链接
📖 BBC《6 分钟英语》本期聊一个网络新潮流——“拒绝疗法”（rejection therapy）：故意去找陌生人搭话、主动“求拒绝”，据说能练胆量、涨自信。节目里 BBC 记者汉娜真的去街头找人搭讪、借开出租车、试睡商场样板床，还请心理学家聊了聊年轻人的完美主义问题。对话体、语速友好，适合跟读；顺手学会 spike（激增）、socially anxious（社交焦虑）等表达。
¶ 1

GeorgieHello and welcome to 6 Minute English from BBC Learning English. I'm Georgie.

乔治：大家好，欢迎收听 BBC Learning English 的《6 分钟英语》，我是乔治。

¶ 2

NeilAnd I'm Neil. Don't forget you can find vocabulary, definitions and a worksheet for free on our website, bbclearningenglish.com.

尼尔：我是尼尔。别忘了，我们网站 bbclearningenglish.com 上有免费的词汇表、释义和练习题。

¶ 3

GeorgieSo Neil, today we're talking about rejection. So, rejection means saying no to someone or refusing to accept their idea or suggestion. Neil, how do you feel about rejection?

乔治：尼尔，今天我们聊“被拒绝”。Rejection 就是别人对你说不，或者拒绝接受你的想法和建议。尼尔，你怎么看待被拒绝这件事？

¶ 4

NeilWell, nobody likes rejection. But I think as you get older, you get used to it. And probably it's quite a good thing to experience rejection, even if it doesn't feel nice.

尼尔：嗯，没人喜欢被拒绝。但我觉得年纪越大，就越习惯它。经历被拒绝可能是件好事，哪怕当时感觉并不好受。

¶ 5

GeorgieYeah, I agree. We can't always get what we want, can we?

乔治：没错，我同意。我们不可能事事如愿，对吧？

¶ 6

NeilWe can't, no. In this podcast, we'll be learning about a new internet trend called rejection therapy and how people are using it to help them to be more confident. OK. Let's start with our quiz question, Georgie. Lots of famous people have faced rejection in their lives before they became successful, but which of these things did not happen to the animator Walt Disney before he became successful? Was it: b) his first business ran out of money, or c) his first full length film was a failure?

尼尔：是的，不可能。在本期节目里，我们会了解一个叫“拒绝疗法”的网络新潮流，以及人们如何用它来变得更自信。好，我们先来做每周小测验，乔治。很多名人在成功之前都经历过被拒绝，但动画大师沃尔特·迪士尼在成名之前，哪一件事并没有发生在他身上？是：a）他丢了画家的工作，b）他的第一家公司资金耗尽，还是 c）他的第一部动画长片遭遇惨败？

rejection therapy 短语 拒绝疗法（主动求被拒绝以练胆量的潮流）e.g. Rejection therapy is a social media trend where people record themselves talking to strangers.
¶ 7

GeorgieI've got no idea, but I'm going to go with a) he lost his job as an artist.

乔治：我完全没概念，但我选 a）他丢了画家的工作。

¶ 8

NeilOK. Well, we'll find out the answer at the end of the programme.

尼尔：好，节目最后我们再揭晓答案。

¶ 9

GeorgieOK Neil. So, most people don't like being rejected, but one thing to try might be something called rejection therapy. It's a social media trend where people record themselves talking to strangers and sometimes being rejected.

乔治：好，尼尔。大多数人都不喜欢被拒绝，但有个办法可以试试，叫“拒绝疗法”。这是一种社交媒体潮流：人们把自己和陌生人搭话、有时被拒绝的过程拍下来。

¶ 10

NeilOK. That sounds quite interesting, but let's find out how it works in practice. Hannah Gelbart, host of the BBC World Service podcast What in the World, had a go at the trend. Let's see how she got on.

尼尔：好，听起来挺有意思，但我们来看看实际操作中是什么样的。BBC 国际台播客《What in the World》的主持人汉娜·盖尔巴特亲自体验了这个潮流。我们来看看她的表现如何。

¶ 11

Hannah GelbartHey, can I come sit down at your table?

汉娜：嗨，我可以坐到你们这桌吗？

¶ 12

StrangerYeah.

路人：可以啊。

¶ 13

Hannah GelbartHey, how's it going?

汉娜：嗨，最近怎么样？

¶ 14

StrangerGood, thank you.

路人：挺好的，谢谢。

¶ 15

Hannah GelbartHow's your day gone? Good afternoon. How are you doing? Can I drive your cab? Can I? Can I get behind the wheel?

汉娜：今天过得怎么样？下午好。你还好吗？我能开一下你的出租车吗？我可以吗？能让我坐到驾驶座上试试吗？

¶ 16

Cab DriverDon't be silly!

出租车司机：别开玩笑了！

¶ 17

Hannah GelbartDon't be silly...! Coming somewhere else to try and face my fears... maybe do a bit of shopping. I was wondering, can I have a nap in one of these beds? Can I just try it out? Wow – I didn't expect that one to be a yes!

汉娜：“别开玩笑”……！换个地方继续挑战自己、直面恐惧……也许去逛逛街。我在想，我能在这些床上小睡一会儿吗？就试一下？哇——没想到这个居然答应了！

¶ 18

GeorgieWell, it seems like people were pretty nice to Hannah in those examples.

乔治：嗯，看起来这些例子里，人们对汉娜都挺友善的。

¶ 19

NeilYes. And she talks with psychologist Claudia Hammond about how she felt during the experiment.

尼尔：是的。她还和心理学家克劳迪娅·哈蒙德聊了聊实验过程中自己的感受。

¶ 20

Hannah GelbartAnd actually, walking up to the first person, I felt my adrenaline really, really spiking. I'm not a socially anxious person – I think I'm really confident. But that first thing really scared me. And once I'd done one, the second one came easier and the third one came even easier. And you know, by the fourth time I didn't even mind it whether they said yes or no.

汉娜：其实，走向第一个人的时候，我感觉肾上腺素真的在飙升。我不是个社交焦虑的人——我觉得自己挺自信的。但第一次真的把我吓到了。做完一次之后，第二次就容易多了，第三次更容易。你知道吗，到了第四次，我根本不在乎他们说 yes 还是 no 了。

socially anxious 短语 社交焦虑的（与人交谈会紧张的）e.g. He feels socially anxious at big parties.
¶ 21

GeorgieHannah says that when she walked up to the first stranger in the video, she felt her adrenaline spiking. If something spikes, it increases sharply.

乔治：汉娜说，走向视频里第一个陌生人时，她感到肾上腺素飙升（adrenaline spiking）。Spike 就是急剧上升的意思。

¶ 22

NeilHannah is not generally a socially anxious person. Someone who is socially anxious is nervous or worried about speaking to other people.

尼尔：汉娜平时并不是个社交焦虑的人。Socially anxious 指的是和别人说话时会紧张、担心的人。

¶ 23

GeorgieEach time Hannah tried to speak to a new person, she says, it came easier. Come easier is an informal way to say something is easier to achieve.

乔治：汉娜说，每次尝试和新的陌生人说话，都会变得更容易。Come easier 是“更容易做到”的非正式说法。

come easier 短语 变得更容易（非正式）e.g. Once I had done one, the second one came easier.
¶ 24

NeilNow, this social media trend is called rejection therapy, but it's not a proper form of therapy. It hasn't been tested.

尼尔：这个社交媒体潮流叫“拒绝疗法”，但它并不是正规的治疗方式，也没有经过科学验证。

¶ 25

GeorgieAnd there are some criticisms of the videos because they involve filming your interactions with strangers, which people might not want.

乔治：这些视频也受到一些批评，因为它们涉及拍摄你和陌生人的互动，而对方可能并不愿意被拍。

¶ 26

NeilBut people who post the videos say they boost their confidence. So, why do people want to ask for rejection?

尼尔：但发布视频的人说，这能提升他们的自信。那么，人们为什么想主动“求拒绝”呢？

¶ 27

GeorgiePsychologist Claudia Hammond has some ideas, which she shared with Hannah on the BBC World Service podcast What in the World.

乔治：心理学家克劳迪娅·哈蒙德有一些想法，她在 BBC 国际台播客《What in the World》里和汉娜分享过。

¶ 28

Claudia HammondWhat we do know is that there is an increase in perfectionism. People are more worried now, younger people, about how other people are judging them. And so, I wonder whether that might make people more shy of speaking to strangers – worried that the people will, you know, not appraise them in a good way, and so doing something like this to try to counter that might feel like a positive step to people, that they're trying to do something. Also, they're fun to watch.

克劳迪娅·哈蒙德：我们确实知道，完美主义在增加。现在人们更担心了，尤其是年轻人，担心别人怎么评判自己。所以我在想，这会不会让人们更不敢和陌生人说话——担心别人不会给自己好评，而做这样的事来对抗这种心态，对人们来说可能感觉是积极的一步，觉得自己在努力做点什么。而且，这些视频看着也挺有意思的。

perfectionism 短语 完美主义e.g. There is an increase in perfectionism among young people.
¶ 29

NeilClaudia says there's an increase in perfectionism among young people. Perfectionism is the feeling that everything you do needs to be perfect.

尼尔：克劳迪娅说，年轻人中的完美主义在增加。Perfectionism 就是觉得自己做的每件事都必须完美的那种心态。

¶ 30

GeorgieYoung people are also worried about how people will appraise them, which means judge them.

乔治：年轻人也担心别人会如何评价（appraise）自己，appraise 就是评判的意思。

¶ 31

NeilDeliberately looking for rejection might be an extreme way to become more confident, but it can lead to surprising discoveries.

尼尔：故意去寻找被拒绝，可能是个变得更自信的极端办法，但也可能带来意想不到的发现。

¶ 32

GeorgieYes, and Hannah found that most strangers she spoke to were friendly and happy to help, so she didn't really experience rejection much at all.

乔治：没错，汉娜发现，她搭话的大多数陌生人都很友好、乐于帮忙，所以她其实并没有怎么体验到被拒绝。

¶ 33

NeilOK. Georgie, I think it's time now for the answer to my quiz question. I asked you which rejection Walt Disney didn't experience before becoming successful?

尼尔：好，乔治，现在该揭晓小测验的答案了。我问的是，沃尔特·迪士尼在成功之前没有经历过哪种拒绝？

¶ 34

GeorgieYes, and I went for a) I don't think he lost his job as an artist.

乔治：对，我选了 a）我觉得他没有丢过画家的工作。

¶ 35

NeilWell, actually, the answer was c) his first full length film, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, was not a failure. It was a huge success.

尼尔：其实答案是 c）他的第一部动画长片《白雪公主和七个小矮人》并没有惨败——它取得了巨大成功。

¶ 36

GeorgieWow! That was his first film? That's really impressive.

乔治：哇！那是他的第一部电影？真了不起。

¶ 37

NeilIt is.

尼尔：确实。

¶ 38

GeorgieOK. Let's recap the vocabulary we learned in this programme, starting with rejection, which means saying no or refusing an idea or suggestion.

乔治：好，我们来回顾一下本期学到的词汇，先从 rejection 开始：对别人说不，或拒绝某个想法、建议。

¶ 39

NeilThe verb spike means increase suddenly.

尼尔：动词 spike 意为突然激增。

spike 短语 （数量、情绪）突然激增e.g. Walking up to the first stranger, I felt my adrenaline really spiking.
¶ 40

GeorgieSomeone who is socially anxious is nervous or worried about speaking to other people.

乔治：Socially anxious 指的是和别人说话时会紧张、担心的人。

¶ 41

NeilIf something comes easier, it's easier to achieve.

尼尔：Come easier 意为更容易做到。

¶ 42

GeorgiePerfectionism is the feeling that everything you do needs to be perfect.

乔治：Perfectionism 是觉得自己做的每件事都必须完美的心态。

¶ 43

NeilAnd if you appraise someone, you judge them.

尼尔：Appraise someone 就是评判某人。

¶ 44

GeorgieOnce again, our six minutes are up, but you can test what you've learned with the free worksheet on our website, bbclearningenglish.com.

乔治：6 分钟又过去了，你可以在我们网站 bbclearningenglish.com 上用免费练习题检验学习成果。

NeilThank you for joining us and goodbye.

尼尔：感谢收听，再见。

¶ 46

GeorgieGoodbye. What do you think of the new version of 6 Minute English? Email us at learningenglish@bbc.co.uk.
