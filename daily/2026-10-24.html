1. A religious leader in court

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-10-23 · 原文链接
📖 肯尼亚一名“末日传道者”及其 29 名追随者涉嫌造成 191 人死亡，其中几乎全是儿童，法院已经开始宣判。关键信息点：他在森林营地里让信徒绝食“更快见到耶稣”，下令先饿死儿童、再饿死妇女、最后饿死男子。值得读：这是一起震惊世界的集体死亡案的标志性审判，deliver a verdict / face charges / plead not guilty 都是法庭新闻高频表达。
¶ 1

A judge in Kenya has begun delivering a verdict in a landmark mass murder trial involving doomsday preacher Paul Mackenzie and 29 of his followers.

肯尼亚一名法官已经开始对一起具有里程碑意义的大规模谋杀案作出判决，涉案的是“末日传道者”保罗·麦肯齐及其 29 名追随者。

verdict /'vә:dikt/ n. 裁决；判决；判断性意见；定论；结论e.g. The court will announce the verdict tomorrow.
landmark /'lændmɑ:k/ n. 陆标；划时代的事；地界标e.g. It was a landmark moment in her career.
trial /'traiәl/ n. 审判；试验；艰苦；麻烦事；考验e.g. The trial lasted for two weeks.
doomsday /'du:mzdei/ n. 世界末日e.g. He believed doomsday was coming soon.
preacher /'pri:tʃә/ n. 传道士；讲道者；牧师e.g. The preacher spoke about kindness.
deliver a verdict 短语 作出判决/宣判e.g. The jury will deliver its verdict tomorrow morning.
be scattered throughout 短语 散布在……各处e.g. His books are scattered throughout the room.
¶ 2

The group faces charges over the deaths of 191 people, almost all of whom were children.

这个团伙被指控造成 191 人死亡，其中几乎全部是儿童。

face charges 短语 面临指控e.g. The driver faces charges for running a red light.
¶ 3

Mackenzie established a remote camp in a forest, where he convinced hundreds of devotees that the end of the world was approaching. He instructed his followers to starve themselves to death so they could meet Jesus faster. He ordered that children be starved first, followed by women, and finally men. Police later uncovered hundreds of decomposed bodies in shallow mass graves scattered throughout the forest.

麦肯齐在森林里建立了一个偏僻营地，让数百名信徒相信世界末日即将到来。他指示信徒们绝食而死，这样就能更快见到耶稣。他下令先饿死儿童，再饿死妇女，最后饿死男子。警方后来在森林各处的浅浅的集体坟墓中发现了数百具腐烂的尸体。

uncover /.ʌn'kʌvә/ vt. 揭露；揭开；暴露；脱帽致敬e.g. The reporter uncovered a big secret.
decomposed /.di:kәm'pәuzd/ a. 已腐烂的；已分解的e.g. After a month, the food was fully decomposed.
¶ 4

Despite the horrific allegations and extensive physical evidence, Mackenzie and his co-defendants have pleaded not guilty, and claimed that no one was forced to die. Grieving families who lost entire households are waiting for the court’s verdict, and they hope it will finally bring closure.

尽管指控骇人听闻、物证确凿，麦肯齐与共同被告均拒不认罪，并声称没有人是被迫赴死的。失去整个家庭的悲痛家属们正在等待法院的判决，希望这一切能终于有个了结。

horrific /hɒ'rifik/ a. 令人毛骨悚然的；感到恐惧的e.g. They survived a horrific car crash.
extensive /ik'stensiv/ a. 广的；广泛的；多方面的e.g. He has extensive experience in teaching.
grieving /ɡri:vɪŋ/ v. 感到悲痛；伤心( grieve的现在分词 ); 使…伤心e.g. The grieving mother cried all day.
plead not guilty 短语 拒不认罪/作无罪答辩e.g. He pleaded not guilty to the robbery charge.
bring closure 短语 让事情有个了结/使心结释怀e.g. Writing that letter finally brought me closure.
¶ 5

Difficult words: preacher (a person who gives religious speeches), devotee (a strong believer or follower), allegation (a statement claiming that someone has done something wrong or illegal, made without proof yet).

难词：preacher（布道者：发表宗教演讲的人），devotee（虔诚的信徒或追随者），allegation（指控：声称某人做了错事或违法之事的说法，尚未证实）。

devotee /.devә'ti:/ n. 爱好者；献身者；虔诚的宗教信徒e.g. She is a true devotee of classical music.
allegation /.æli'geiʃәn/ n. 断言；主张；申辩e.g. The company denied the allegation.

2. Australia bans AI songs from official music charts

Breaking News English · Level 6 · 2026-09-07 · 原文链接
📖 澳大利亚唱片业协会（ARIA）宣布：纯 AI 生成的歌曲禁止进入官方音乐排行榜，AI 辅助创作的仍可入围。关键信息点：导火索是一首 AI 翻唱版麦当娜金曲《Like a Prayer》，在榜 16 周后引发强烈争议。值得读：AI 与人类创作的边界之争是当下最热的科技文化话题，intrusion / backlash / livelihood 都是地道高频词。
¶ 1

The fight against the intrusion of artificial intelligence (AI) into music continues. The Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA) has banned songs that have been "wholly" created with AI from entering the country's official music charts. There has been a recent controversy over an AI version of the 1989 Madonna hit "Like a Prayer". The cover version of the tune made considerable use of AI technology. The song spent 16 weeks in the Australian top 20. It also created a backlash among music purists and the general public. ARIA declared that "wholly AI-generated" music will be banned from the charts, while "AI-assisted" music will still be eligible for inclusion.

抵制人工智能（AI）入侵音乐领域的斗争仍在继续。澳大利亚唱片业协会（ARIA）已禁止“完全”由 AI 创作的歌曲进入该国官方音乐排行榜。最近，一首 AI 版的麦当娜 1989 年金曲《Like a Prayer》引发了争议。这个翻唱版本大量使用了 AI 技术。这首歌在澳大利亚排行榜前 20 名中停留了 16 周。它也在音乐纯粹主义者和普通大众中引发了强烈反对。ARIA 宣布，“完全由 AI 生成”的音乐将被禁止上榜，而“AI 辅助创作”的音乐仍有资格入围。

intrusion /in'tru:ʒәn/ n. 闯入；侵扰e.g. He felt the call was an intrusion on his time.
controversy /'kɒntrәvә:si/ n. 论争；辩论；论战；争论e.g. The new policy caused a big controversy.
considerable /kәn'sidәrәbl/ a. 相当的；可观的；重要的e.g. She saved a considerable amount of money.
backlash /'bæklæʃ/ n. 后冲；强烈反对e.g. The price rise caused a backlash from customers.
purist /'pjuәrist/ n. 纯粹主义者e.g. He is a coffee purist; he never adds sugar.
declare /di'klєә/ v. 宣布；声明；申报；断言e.g. The school declared a day off.
eligible /'elidʒәbl/ a. 有资格当选的；合格的e.g. Are you eligible to vote?
inclusion /in'kluʒәn/ n. 包含；内含物e.g. The inclusion of photos makes the book lively.
be eligible for 短语 有资格获得……e.g. You are eligible for a free gift this weekend.
create a backlash 短语 引发强烈反对/反弹e.g. The new rule created a backlash among students.
¶ 2

ARIA CEO Annabelle Herd said the music charts must protect artists and their livelihoods. She said there should be a legitimate use of AI, but not to the extent that it excludes human creativity. She commented on the new rules, saying: "These changes reflect our intent to remain dynamic and promote the human nature of artistry in what is, to say the least, a rapidly developing space." Josh Fawaz, the artist who created the AI Madonna cover, acknowledged his use of AI on his version of the track. He defended his use of AI as a "tool" in creating music. He also called ARIA's new rules "a great move". He wrote: "I understand that AI in music isn't for everyone and that's fine."

ARIA 首席执行官表示，音乐排行榜必须保护艺人及其生计。她说，应该正当使用 AI，但不能到排挤人类创造力的地步。她在评论新规时说：“这些变化体现了我们的意图：在这个可以说发展迅猛的领域里，保持灵活应变，弘扬艺术的人性本质。”创作这首 AI 版麦当娜翻唱的艺人承认在自己的版本中使用了 AI。他为自己把 AI 当作创作音乐的“工具”做了辩护，还称 ARIA 的新规是“明智之举”。他写道：“我理解 AI 音乐并不适合所有人，这没关系。”

livelihood /'laivlihud/ n. 生计；营生；生活e.g. Fishing is their only livelihood.
legitimate /li'dʒitimәt/ a. 合法的；正当的；婚生的e.g. He has a legitimate reason to be late.
acknowledge /әk'nɒlidʒ/ vt. 承认；告知收悉；答谢；报偿e.g. He acknowledged his mistake.
reflect /ri'flekt/ vt. 反射；反映；招致；深思e.g. Your clothes reflect your personality.
