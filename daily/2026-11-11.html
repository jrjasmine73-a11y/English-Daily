1. 'Godzilla Minus Zero' Breaks Box Office Record

Engoo Daily News · Level 9 · 2026-11-10 · 原文链接
📖 日本怪兽片《哥斯拉 Minus Zero》北美首周末票房夺冠，刷新外语真人电影开画纪录。更值得注意的是，它和印度史诗片《罗摩衍那》同周大卖，说明非英语电影正在成为北美影院的新宠。读这篇可以顺便学一波票房新闻的常用表达。
¶ 1

Japanese monster movie Godzilla Minus Zero topped the North American box office over the weekend, earning {{prn:$31.6 million|thirty one point six million dollars}} and setting a new opening record for a live-action foreign-language film.

日本怪兽电影《哥斯拉 Minus Zero》上周末登顶北美票房，收入 3160 万美元，创下真人外语电影首映票房新纪录。

monster /'mɒnstә/ n. 怪物；恶人；巨物e.g. My little son is afraid of the monster in the cartoon.💡 场合：日常口语、儿童话题常用；也可以开玩笑形容食量大的人 a monster eater，带调侃。
top the box office 短语 登顶票房榜e.g. The new cartoon topped the box office in its first weekend.💡 场合：新闻报道、影评常用；口语里说一部电影“最卖座”也可以用。
set a new record 短语 创下新纪录e.g. Our school team set a new record in the 400-meter relay this year.💡 场合：新闻、体育报道高频；日常口语也常说，语气中性偏积极。
¶ 2

Takashi Yamazaki's sequel to 2023's Godzilla Minus One is set in Japan after World War II. It continues one of cinema's longest-running franchises, with Godzilla recently celebrating his 72nd birthday.

这部电影是山崎贵执导的 2023 年《哥斯拉-1.0》的续集，故事背景设定在二战后的日本。它延续了影史上最长寿的系列电影之一，哥斯拉最近刚庆祝了 72 岁生日。

sequel /'si:kwәl/ n. 继续；续集；后果e.g. I can't wait for the sequel — the first film ended with a big surprise.💡 场合：影迷、书迷日常口语高频；正式影评也用。
franchise /'fræntʃaiz/ n. 公民权；特权；特许经营权；免赔额e.g. This cartoon has become a huge franchise with films, toys and games.💡 场合：娱乐新闻、商业报道常用；日常口语多指电影系列，商业语境指连锁加盟。
¶ 3

The film broke the previous opening record for a live-action foreign-language movie, held by 2004's Hero, which earned $17.9 million, and added to the trend of international, non-English productions drawing American audiences to cinemas.

该片打破了此前由 2004 年《英雄》保持的真人外语电影首映纪录（1790 万美元），也延续了国际非英语制作吸引美国观众走进影院的趋势。

trend /trend/ n. 趋势；倾向；走向e.g. More and more parents follow the trend of reading English picture books with kids.💡 场合：新闻和日常口语都高频；说“赶时髦”可说 follow the trend。
¶ 4

Godzilla Minus Zero also earned about $17 million in Japan, where it opened at the same time as in North America.

《哥斯拉 Minus Zero》在日本也收获了约 1700 万美元，与北美同期上映。

¶ 5

"This historic opening shows how deeply audiences around the world connect with Godzilla and with Takashi Yamazaki's vision," said Koji Ueda, president of Toho Global, the film's producer.

该片制片方东宝全球公司总裁植田浩治表示："这次具有历史意义的首映表明，全球观众与哥斯拉、与山崎贵导演的创作理念产生了多么深的共鸣。"

¶ 6

The success follows that of Godzilla Minus One, which won an Oscar for best visual effects and became the highest-grossing Japanese live-action film in the US and Canada despite a modest budget under $15 million.

这次成功延续了《哥斯拉-1.0》的辉煌——后者曾获奥斯卡最佳视觉效果奖，并以不到 1500 万美元的不高预算成为美国和加拿大票房最高的日本真人电影。

modest /'mɒdist/ a. 谦逊的；羞怯的；端庄的；适度的e.g. We had a modest dinner at home — just noodles and eggs.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻常用；形容人是“谦虚的”，形容数量是“不多的”，别只记一个意思。
be well received 短语 受到好评e.g. Her first English speech was well received by the whole class.💡 场合：正式、书面语气较强，多用于报道作品、演讲、政策的反响；口语更常说 people really liked it。
¶ 7

Godzilla Minus Zero, made for less than $20 million, is expected to surpass its predecessor's record earnings of $57.1 million. The film has been well received by both critics and audiences, earning a 90% positive rating on Rotten Tomatoes.

《哥斯拉 Minus Zero》的制作成本不到 2000 万美元，预计将超越前作 5710 万美元的票房纪录。影片口碑很好，影评人和观众都喜欢，在烂番茄上获得了 90% 的好评率。

predecessor /.predi'sesә/ n. 前任；先辈；前身e.g. The new phone is much faster than its predecessor.💡 场合：正式书面、新闻报道常用；日常口语更常说 the old one / the last one。
critic /'kritik/ n. 批评家；鉴定家e.g. Even the strictest critics praised her first show.💡 场合：娱乐新闻常用；日常口语说“爱挑毛病的人”可用 a critic，略带贬义。
¶ 8

Another international film, the Hindi-language epic Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, also had a strong opening weekend. The first part of the two-film story, based on Indian mythology, earned $8.7 million in North America and $32 million in India. It is the most expensive Indian film ever made.

另一部国际电影、印地语史诗片《罗摩衍那：传奇崛起》首周末表现也很强劲。这部两部曲的第一部改编自印度神话，在北美收获 870 万美元，在印度收获 3200 万美元。它是有史以来制作成本最高的印度电影。

epic /'epik/ n. 史诗；叙事诗e.g. Our drive to the grassland was a real epic adventure.💡 场合：日常口语和影评常用，形容规模宏大、历时很长的事；作名词指史诗作品。
mythology /mi'θɒlәdʒi/ n. 神话e.g. These stories come from Chinese mythology, and children love them.💡 场合：文化、教育话题常用；日常聊天提到神话故事时也用。
¶ 9

Both Godzilla and Ramayana were made for IMAX, a cinema format that uses larger screens and advanced sound and picture technology. Godzilla Minus Zero earned $21.3 million worldwide from IMAX screenings, accounting for 34% of its total box-office earnings.

《哥斯拉》和《罗摩衍那》都是为 IMAX 制作的——IMAX 是一种使用更大银幕和先进音画技术的影院放映格式。《哥斯拉 Minus Zero》全球 IMAX 票房达 2130 万美元，占其总票房的 34%。

format /'fɒ:mæt/ n. 开本；版式；形式；格式e.g. The teacher changed the test format this term.💡 场合：职场、学习场景高频；既指文件格式，也指活动、节目的形式。
screening /'skri:niŋ/ [医] 筛选；筛分(如普查癌、结核等病)；萤光屏检查e.g. The 7 o'clock screening was already sold out.💡 场合：影院、电影话题常用；医疗语境指“筛查”，注意别混淆。
account for 短语 占（比例）；是……的原因e.g. The child's classes account for a third of our family spending.💡 场合：新闻、经济报道和正式书面语高频；日常口语说 make up 更随意。
¶ 10

The film's success could either boost excitement or set a high bar for the next Hollywood, English-language Godzilla movie, Godzilla x Kong: Supernova, which is due in March.

这部电影的成功既可能为下一部好莱坞英语版哥斯拉电影《哥斯拉大战金刚：超新星》（定于明年三月上映）造势，也可能给它立下一个很高的标杆。

boost /bu:st/ n. 推进；吹捧e.g. A short walk after lunch always boosts my energy.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻都高频；既可作动词也可作名词，语气积极。
set a high bar 短语 立下很高的标准/标杆e.g. Her perfect score set a high bar for the rest of us.💡 场合：日常口语和职场常用，带“后来者很难超越”的意味；bar 本义是横杆，引申为标准。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. The teacher changed the test ____ this term.
✓ format　开本；版式；形式；格式
The teacher changed the test format this term.
老师这学期改了考试的题型。
2. Her perfect score ____ for the rest of us.
✓ set a high bar　立下很高的标准/标杆
Her perfect score set a high bar for the rest of us.
她满分的成绩给我们其他人立下了一个很高的标杆。
3. My little son is afraid of the ____ in the cartoon.
✓ monster　怪物；恶人；巨物
My little son is afraid of the monster in the cartoon.
我的小儿子害怕动画片里的怪兽。
4. Our school team ____ in the 400-meter relay this year.
✓ set a new record　创下新纪录
Our school team set a new record in the 400-meter relay this year.
今年我们校队在 400 米接力中创下了新纪录。
5. The 7 o'clock ____ was already sold out.
✓ screening　筛选；筛分(如普查癌、结核等病)；萤光屏检查
The 7 o'clock screening was already sold out.
晚上七点那场放映的票已经卖完了。
6. This cartoon has become a huge ____ with films, toys and games.
✓ franchise　公民权；特权；特许经营权；免赔额
This cartoon has become a huge franchise with films, toys and games.
这部动画片已经发展成一个大系列，有电影、玩具和游戏。
7. Our drive to the grassland was a real ____ adventure.
✓ epic　史诗；叙事诗
Our drive to the grassland was a real epic adventure.
我们开车去草原的旅程真是一场史诗般的冒险。
8. I can't wait for the ____ — the first film ended with a big surprise.
✓ sequel　继续；续集；后果
I can't wait for the sequel — the first film ended with a big surprise.
我等不及想看续集了——第一部结尾留了个大悬念。
9. These stories come from Chinese ____, and children love them.
✓ mythology　神话
These stories come from Chinese mythology, and children love them.
这些故事来自中国神话，孩子们都爱听。
10. The new phone is much faster than its ____.
✓ predecessor　前任；先辈；前身
The new phone is much faster than its predecessor.
新手机比上一代快多了。
11. More and more parents follow the ____ of reading English picture books with kids.
✓ trend　趋势；倾向；走向
More and more parents follow the trend of reading English picture books with kids.
越来越多的家长开始跟风陪孩子读英文绘本。
12. The child's classes ____ a third of our family spending.
✓ account for　占（比例）；是……的原因
The child's classes account for a third of our family spending.
孩子的兴趣班占了全家开销的三分之一。
13. A short walk after lunch always ____ my energy.
✓ boosts（boost 的变形）　推进；吹捧
A short walk after lunch always boosts my energy.
午饭后散一会儿步总能让我恢复精力。
14. Her first English speech ____ by the whole class.
✓ was well received（be well received 的变形）　受到好评
Her first English speech was well received by the whole class.
她的第一次英语演讲受到了全班同学的好评。
15. We had a ____ dinner at home — just noodles and eggs.
✓ modest　谦逊的；羞怯的；端庄的；适度的
We had a modest dinner at home — just noodles and eggs.
我们只在家简单吃了一顿晚饭——只有面条和鸡蛋。
