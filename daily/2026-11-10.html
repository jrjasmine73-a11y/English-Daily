1. What happens if you do not brush your teeth for a year

News in Levels · Level 3 · 2026-11-09 · 原文链接
📖 一年不刷牙，口腔里会发生什么？这篇文章按时间线讲了从牙菌斑、牙龈炎到牙齿松动脱落的连锁反应，更提醒我们：口腔细菌可能进入血液，加重心脏病、糖尿病甚至阿尔茨海默病。短小却触目惊心，值得一读，顺便学学口腔健康相关的地道表达。
¶ 1

If you stop oral hygiene, the balance between helpful bacteria and harmful pathobionts in your mouth quickly breaks down.

如果你停止口腔清洁，口中有益细菌与有害致病菌之间的平衡很快就会被打破。

hygiene /'haidʒi:n/ n. 卫生保健；卫生学；保健法e.g. Good hygiene keeps you healthy.💡 场合：日常口语和健康科普常用，多与 oral、personal 连用。
break down 短语 （平衡、计划等）被打破；崩溃e.g. Their plan broke down after the first week.💡 场合：日常口语和新闻报道都常用，指计划、关系、健康等逐渐出问题或崩溃。
¶ 2

Within just a few days, harmful bacteria multiply and create a sticky layer called dental plaque. This plaque irritates your gums, which can cause bleeding and inflammation, an early stage of disease called gingivitis. Within a month, the bacteria produce strong acids that eat away at tooth enamel and create painful cavities. If left untreated for several months, gingivitis worsens into periodontitis, which damages soft gum tissue and causes gums to pull away from your teeth.

短短几天内，有害细菌就会大量繁殖，形成一层叫作牙菌斑的黏性薄膜。牙菌斑刺激牙龈，可能导致出血和发炎，这是牙龈炎——疾病的早期阶段。一个月内，细菌会产生强酸，腐蚀牙釉质，形成疼痛的龋洞。如果几个月都不处理，牙龈炎会恶化为牙周炎，损害柔软的牙龈组织，导致牙龈从牙齿上萎缩脱离。

multiply /'mʌltiplai/ v. 繁殖；乘；增加e.g. Germs multiply quickly in warm places.💡 场合：科普和日常口语都常用，也可指数量成倍增加。
plaque /plɑ:k/ n. 匾；饰板；名牌；勋章；胸襟饰物；血小板e.g. The dentist removed the plaque from my teeth.💡 场合：多用于牙科和健康语境，看牙时常听到。
irritate /'iriteit/ vt. 激怒；使发怒；使兴奋；使发炎e.g. Smoke irritates my eyes.💡 场合：日常口语常用，既指刺激身体部位，也指惹人烦。
inflammation /.inflә'meiʃәn/ n. 怒火；发火；燃烧；发炎e.g. Rest helps reduce the inflammation in her knee.💡 场合：医学和健康科普常用，偏正式书面语体。
enamel /i'næml/ n. 珐琅；瓷釉e.g. Too much soda can damage your tooth enamel.💡 场合：牙科和健康语境专用词，日常口语较少单独出现。
eat away at 短语 腐蚀；逐渐侵蚀e.g. Stress slowly eats away at his confidence.💡 场合：书面和口语都可用，既指酸、锈等物理腐蚀，也常用于抽象事物被逐渐消耗。
pull away from 短语 从……脱离；退开e.g. She pulled away from the crowd and walked home alone.💡 场合：日常口语常用，既可指身体上的离开，也可指情感或立场上的疏远。
worsen into 短语 恶化为e.g. A small cold can worsen into pneumonia if you ignore it.💡 场合：正式书面和新闻语体常用，描述疾病、局势等由轻变重。
left untreated 短语 如果不处理；置之不理e.g. Left untreated, the problem will only get worse.💡 场合：健康科普和新闻报道常用，偏正式书面语体，提醒忽视后果。
¶ 3

After a year without cleaning, chronic infection destroys the underlying bone and causes teeth to wobble and fall out. Even worse, damaged gums allow harmful bacteria to enter your bloodstream. From there, these microbes can travel through the body and worsen serious health conditions, including heart disease, diabetes, and memory disorders like Alzheimer’s.

一年不清洁，慢性感染会破坏牙齿下方的骨骼，导致牙齿松动脱落。更糟的是，受损的牙龈会让有害细菌进入血液。从那里，这些微生物可以游遍全身，加重严重的健康问题，包括心脏病、糖尿病以及阿尔茨海默病这类记忆障碍。

chronic /'krɒnik/ a. 慢性的；习惯性的e.g. He suffers from chronic back pain.💡 场合：医学和新闻报道常用，形容长期反复的疾病或问题，偏正式。
infection /in'fekʃәn/ n. 传染；影响；传染病e.g. Wash the cut to prevent infection.💡 场合：日常口语和医学语境都常用。
wobble /'wɒbl/ n. 摆动；摇晃；不稳定；抖动e.g. The table wobbles when I lean on it.💡 场合：日常口语常用，形容摇晃、不稳，也可形容声音发颤。
bloodstream n. 血流e.g. Sugar quickly enters the bloodstream after you eat sweets.💡 场合：健康科普常用，偏正式书面语体。
¶ 4

Difficult words: oral hygiene (keeping your mouth clean and free of disease by regular brushing and flossing), pathobiont (a potentially disease-causing microorganism that normally lives harmlessly in the body unless the balance is disrupted), inflammation (when part of the body becomes red, swollen, hot, and often painful as a reaction to injury or infection), enamel (the hard, shiny outer surface layer of a tooth).

生词：oral hygiene（通过定期刷牙和使用牙线保持口腔清洁、无疾病）、pathobiont（一种可能致病的微生物，平时在体内无害地共存，一旦平衡被打破就可能致病）、inflammation（身体某部位因受伤或感染而变红、肿胀、发热，常伴有疼痛的反应）、enamel（牙齿坚硬光滑的外层表面）。

cavity /'kæviti/ n. 洞；空穴；腔e.g. I have a small cavity in my back tooth.💡 场合：日常口语常用，看牙时的高频词；也有“空洞、洞穴”的一般义。

📝 语义小测

根据英文语境，选出填空处最合适的单词或短语（15 题）
1. Germs ____ quickly in warm places.
✓ multiply　繁殖；乘；增加
Germs multiply quickly in warm places.
细菌在温暖的地方繁殖得很快。
2. Sugar quickly enters the ____ after you eat sweets.
✓ bloodstream　血流
Sugar quickly enters the bloodstream after you eat sweets.
吃甜食后，糖分很快进入血液。
3. She ____ the crowd and walked home alone.
✓ pulled away from（pull away from 的变形）　从……脱离；退开
She pulled away from the crowd and walked home alone.
她脱离人群，独自走回家。
4. The table ____ when I lean on it.
✓ wobbles（wobble 的变形）　摆动；摇晃；不稳定；抖动
The table wobbles when I lean on it.
我一靠桌子它就晃。
5. He suffers from ____ back pain.
✓ chronic　慢性的；习惯性的
He suffers from chronic back pain.
他长期受背痛困扰。
6. Good ____ keeps you healthy.
✓ hygiene　卫生保健；卫生学；保健法
Good hygiene keeps you healthy.
良好的卫生习惯让你保持健康。
7. Rest helps reduce the ____ in her knee.
✓ inflammation　怒火；发火；燃烧；发炎
Rest helps reduce the inflammation in her knee.
休息有助于减轻她膝盖的炎症。
8. Stress slowly ____ his confidence.
✓ eats away at（eat away at 的变形）　腐蚀；逐渐侵蚀
Stress slowly eats away at his confidence.
压力慢慢侵蚀着他的自信。
9. A small cold can ____ pneumonia if you ignore it.
✓ worsen into　恶化为
A small cold can worsen into pneumonia if you ignore it.
小感冒如果不重视，可能恶化成肺炎。
10. I have a small ____ in my back tooth.
✓ cavity　洞；空穴；腔
I have a small cavity in my back tooth.
我后槽牙上有一个小龋洞。
11. ____, the problem will only get worse.
✓ left untreated　如果不处理；置之不理
Left untreated, the problem will only get worse.
如果置之不理，问题只会越来越糟。
12. The dentist removed the ____ from my teeth.
✓ plaque　匾；饰板；名牌；勋章；胸襟饰物；血小板
The dentist removed the plaque from my teeth.
牙医清除了我牙齿上的牙菌斑。
13. Too much soda can damage your tooth ____.
✓ enamel　珐琅；瓷釉
Too much soda can damage your tooth enamel.
喝太多汽水会损害牙釉质。
14. Smoke ____ my eyes.
✓ irritates（irritate 的变形）　激怒；使发怒；使兴奋；使发炎
Smoke irritates my eyes.
烟熏得我眼睛难受。
15. Wash the cut to prevent ____.
✓ infection　传染；影响；传染病
Wash the cut to prevent infection.
清洗伤口以防感染。
